# ECS-003-LIB-003

# Canonical Terminology Validation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Implement the authoritative validator governing canonical terminology across ECS-003 doctrine, registries, implementation artifacts, evidence, proof, traceability, and certification artifacts.

The Validator shall detect undefined, duplicated, conflicting, deprecated, ambiguous, or contextually inconsistent terminology.

No artifact shall receive release-ready status while a required canonical term remains unresolved.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every governed concept shall possess one canonical term.

Every canonical term shall possess:

* one deterministic identity;
* one authoritative definition;
* one governing domain;
* one status;
* one provenance record;
* one version and supersession lineage.

Synonyms shall not silently create separate constitutional meanings.

One term shall not possess conflicting active definitions.

Deprecated terminology shall not appear in active normative artifacts unless expressly preserved for historical reference.

Terminology shall never be validated solely by spelling similarity or word frequency.

Historical terminology shall never be overwritten.

---

# Objective

Create one deterministic engine capable of discovering, classifying, validating, reconciling, and publishing canonical ECS-003 terminology.

---

Accept authoritative:

* doctrine packages;

Every input shall possess:

Invalid or unreconciled authoritative inputs shall block release.

---

# Required Activities

## 1. Terminology Discovery

Discover defined and used terms from:

* explicit definition fields;
* terminology registries;
* headings and normative clauses;
* schemas;
* registry field names;
* identifiers;
* enumerations;
* traceability nodes and edges;
* proof and certification artifacts.

Do not rely solely on free-text frequency.

---

## 2. Term Classification

Classify every term as:

* CANONICAL_ACTIVE;
* CANONICAL_HISTORICAL;
* AUTHORIZED_SYNONYM;
* ABBREVIATION;
* DOMAIN_SPECIALIZATION;
* DEPRECATED;
* UNDEFINED;
* AMBIGUOUS;
* CONFLICTING;
* DUPLICATE;
* NON_NORMATIVE.

---

## 3. Canonical Term Record

For every canonical term define:

* term identity;
* canonical spelling;
* definition;
* governing domain;
* authority source;
* permitted contexts;
* prohibited contexts;
* authorized synonyms;
* abbreviations;
* predecessor term;
* superseding term;
* status;
* version;
* provenance.

---

## 4. Definition Validation

Verify:

* every active term has one authoritative definition;
* definitions are non-circular;
* definitions do not conflict;
* terms are not defined only through implementation behavior;
* authority and ownership terms preserve distinct meanings;
* evidence, proof, traceability, and certification terms remain distinct;
* office-specific specializations do not alter generic ECS-003 meaning.

Identify:

* missing definitions;
* circular definitions;
* conflicting definitions;
* duplicate definitions;
* scope ambiguity;
* unauthorized semantic expansion;
* unauthorized semantic narrowing.

---

## 5. Usage Validation

Validate every governed term occurrence against:

* canonical spelling;
* authorized synonym rules;
* domain;
* context;
* status;
* version;
* applicability;
* supersession.

Detect:

* deprecated active usage;
* unauthorized synonyms;
* inconsistent capitalization where meaningful;
* singular/plural identity conflicts;
* abbreviation collisions;
* cross-domain misuse;
* term substitution that changes constitutional meaning.

---

## 6. Critical Distinction Validation

Explicitly validate distinctions including:

```text
Authority ≠ Ownership
Ownership ≠ Custody
Observation ≠ Evaluation
Finding ≠ Alert
Alert ≠ Escalation
Acknowledgement ≠ Closure
Requirement ≠ Implementation Obligation
Verifier ≠ Execution
Evidence ≠ Proof
Proof ≠ Certification Verdict
Declared Traceability ≠ Execution-Derived Traceability
```

Any artifact that collapses a required distinction shall receive a conflicting-terminology finding.

---

## 7. Synonym and Abbreviation Governance

For every synonym or abbreviation verify:

* canonical target;
* permitted scope;
* permitted artifact classes;
* first-use expansion where required;
* absence of identity collision;
* absence of semantic drift.

Unauthorized synonyms and ambiguous abbreviations shall block release when they affect normative meaning.

---

## 8. Terminology Supersession

Verify:

* deprecated terms reference successors;
* successor terms reference predecessors;
* active artifacts use current terminology;
* historical artifacts preserve original language;
* no conflicting active successor exists;
* supersession chains remain complete.

---

## 9. Cross-Artifact Consistency

Compare terminology across:

* doctrine;
* requirements;
* implementation obligations;
* verifiers;
* evidence;
* proof;
* traceability;
* certification.

Identify inconsistent definitions or usage across artifact classes.

---

## 10. Reconciliation

Assign every discovered term one disposition:

* VALID_CANONICAL;
* VALID_SYNONYM;
* VALID_HISTORICAL;
* UNDEFINED;
* AMBIGUOUS;
* CONFLICTING;
* DUPLICATE;
* DEPRECATED_ACTIVE_USE;
* UNAUTHORIZED_SPECIALIZATION;
* UNRESOLVED.

Issue exactly one package disposition:

* READY_FOR_RELEASE;
* NOT_READY_FOR_RELEASE.

---

# Fail-Closed Rules

Refuse release when:

* a required term is undefined;
* an active term has conflicting definitions;
* a circular definition remains;
* a deprecated term controls active normative meaning;
* an unauthorized synonym changes meaning;
* an abbreviation collision remains;
* a critical constitutional distinction is collapsed;
* cross-artifact semantic inconsistency remains;
* deterministic validation fails;
* historical terminology lineage is incomplete.

Term presence alone shall never prove semantic consistency.
