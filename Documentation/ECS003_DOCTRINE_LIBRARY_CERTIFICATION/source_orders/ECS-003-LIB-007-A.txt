# ECS-003-LIB-007

# Traceability Graph Generator

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Establish the authoritative Traceability Graph Generator governing deterministic construction, validation, reconciliation, and publication of execution-derived ECS-003 traceability.

The Generator shall transform authoritative requirement, implementation, verifier, execution, evidence, proof, and certification registries into one complete bidirectional traceability graph.

The graph shall represent:

```text
Constitutional Authority
↔ Canonical Requirement
↔ Implementation Obligation
↔ Implementation Artifact
↔ Behavioral Verifier
↔ Fixture
↔ Execution
↔ Raw Evidence
↔ Normalized Evidence
↔ Finding
↔ Implementation Disposition
↔ Proof Object
↔ Certification Blocker
↔ Certification Verdict
```

The Generator shall establish traceability relationships without inventing missing participants or treating declared relationships as executed relationships.

Every generated node and edge shall preserve deterministic identity, provenance, status, and historical lineage.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every traceability node shall possess one canonical identity.

Every traceability edge shall possess one deterministic identity.

Every edge shall possess objective source evidence.

Every canonical requirement shall participate in complete bidirectional traceability.

Declared traceability shall remain distinguishable from execution-derived traceability.

Missing relationships shall remain explicitly unresolved.

Broken traceability shall fail closed.

Traceability shall never be inferred solely from:

* filenames;
* naming conventions;
* documentation;
* completion reports;
* manually maintained mappings;
* historical certification status.

Historical nodes and edges shall never be overwritten.

Corrections shall occur through versioning and supersession.

---

# Objective

Implement one deterministic engine capable of generating a complete ECS-003 traceability graph suitable for:

* implementation coverage;
* behavioral coverage;
* evidence coverage;
* proof coverage;
* certification-blocker analysis;
* independent reproduction;
* fail-closed certification.

## 4. Edge Status Classification

Assign every edge exactly one status:

* EXECUTION_DERIVED;
* OBJECTIVELY_DECLARED;
* REQUIRED;
* CONDITIONALLY_REQUIRED;
* NOT_APPLICABLE;
* BROKEN;
* CONTRADICTORY;
* UNRESOLVED.

Only `EXECUTION_DERIVED` edges may support completed behavioral or proof claims.

Declared edges may define obligations but shall not prove execution.

---

## 5. Bidirectional Traceability Validation

Verify forward and reverse traversal for every applicable requirement.

Forward traversal:

```text
Requirement
→ Implementation
→ Verifier
→ Execution
→ Evidence
→ Proof
→ Certification
```

Reverse traversal:

```text
Certification
→ Proof
→ Evidence
→ Execution
→ Verifier
→ Implementation
→ Requirement
→ Authority
```

Identify:

* missing nodes;
* missing edges;
* orphan nodes;
* dangling edges;
* circular relationships;
* contradictory relationships;
* duplicate relationships;
* cross-candidate contamination.

---

---

## 7. Historical Lineage

Preserve:

* node versions;
* edge versions;
* candidate lineage;
* evidence supersession;
* proof supersession;
* certification supersession;
* correction history.

No historical node or edge shall be deleted.

---

## 8. Graph Reconciliation

Verify:

* every applicable requirement appears;
* every requirement links to constitutional authority;
* every proven requirement links to executed verification;
* every proof links to admissible evidence;
* every verdict links to requirement-level proof or blockers;
* no orphan or contradictory active relationship remains;
* no unresolved required edge remains.

Issue exactly one disposition:

* READY_FOR_RELEASE;
* NOT_READY_FOR_RELEASE.

---

# Fail-Closed Rules

The Generator shall refuse release when:

* an authoritative input is invalid;
* identity collisions exist;
* required nodes are missing;
* required edges are missing;
* cross-references are unresolved;
* active contradictions remain;
* a proof lacks execution-derived lineage;
* a certification verdict lacks proof or blocker lineage;
* cross-candidate contamination exists;
* deterministic regeneration fails;
* historical lineage is incomplete.

The existence of a graph shall never constitute proof of complete traceability.
