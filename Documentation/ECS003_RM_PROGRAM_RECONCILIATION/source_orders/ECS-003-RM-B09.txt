# ECS-003-RM-B09

# Fail-Closed Certification Validation Program

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Fail-Closed Certification Validation Program establishes the authoritative methodology governing fail-closed certification validation under the Enterprise Constitutional Standard (ECS-003).

This Program exists solely to validate that the ECS-003 certification system correctly rejects constitutionally defective implementations while preserving acceptance of constitutionally compliant implementations.

The Program shall validate the certification system itself rather than the implementation being certified.

The constitutional architecture of the certified office shall be accepted as complete and authoritative before this Program begins.

The implementation inventory established under **ECS-003-RM-B01** shall be accepted as the authoritative implementation baseline.

The behavioral verification baseline established under **ECS-003-RM-B02** shall be accepted as the authoritative behavioral verification baseline.

The implementation candidate established under **ECS-003-RM-B03** shall be accepted as the authoritative implementation candidate.

The proof baseline established under **ECS-003-RM-B04** shall be accepted as the authoritative proof baseline.

The independent reproduction baseline established under **ECS-003-RM-B08** shall be accepted as the authoritative clean-room certification baseline.

Implementation modifications shall remain strictly limited to objectively verified certification-system deficiencies.

Every work order shall have one objective.

Every work order shall execute against one frozen implementation candidate.

Every work order shall preserve complete historical lineage.

Every work order shall preserve superseded behavioral evidence.

Every work order shall preserve superseded proof artifacts.

Every work order shall preserve superseded certification artifacts.

Every work order shall preserve superseded mutation artifacts.

No work order shall reinterpret constitutional doctrine.

No work order shall redefine implementation participation.

No implementation modification shall occur without corresponding objective execution evidence.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Certification shall fail closed.

No constitutionally defective implementation shall receive **UNCONDITIONAL_PASS**.

Every certification blocker shall originate exclusively from objective executable evidence.

Every certification blocker shall possess one constitutional justification.

Every certification blocker shall possess one deterministic disposition.

Every certification verdict shall be independently reproducible.

Every controlled mutation shall possess one deterministic identity.

Every controlled mutation shall be independently reproducible.

Every controlled mutation shall be independently reversible.

Every controlled mutation shall preserve complete historical lineage.

Every controlled mutation shall produce deterministic:

* behavioral evidence;
* proof divergence;
* traceability divergence;
* certification consequences.

False-positive certification shall never occur.

False-negative certification shall never occur.

Certification confidence shall originate exclusively from independently reproduced execution.

Certification confidence shall never originate from:

* documentation;
* implementation intent;
* metadata;
* completion reports;
* historical certification status;
* manual assertion.
