# ECS-003-RM-B06

# Behavioral Coverage and Dependency-Derived Discovery Remediation Program

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Behavioral Coverage and Dependency-Derived Discovery Remediation Program establishes the authoritative methodology governing remediation of implementation discovery, verifier discovery, fixture discovery, implementation participation, and behavioral coverage deficiencies identified during the independent reproduction audit under the Enterprise Constitutional Standard (ECS-003).

This Program exists solely to establish one authoritative dependency-derived behavioral verification population together with complete canonical constitutional requirement coverage.

The constitutional architecture of the certified office shall be accepted as complete and authoritative before this Program begins.

The implementation inventory established under **ECS-003-RM-B01** shall be accepted as the authoritative implementation baseline.

The behavioral verification baseline established under **ECS-003-RM-B02** shall be accepted as the authoritative behavioral verification baseline.

The implementation candidate established under **ECS-003-RM-B03** shall be accepted as the authoritative implementation candidate.

The proof baseline established under **ECS-003-RM-B04** shall be accepted as the current proof baseline.

The independent reproduction audit established under **ECS-003-RM-B05** shall be accepted as the authoritative deficiency baseline.

Implementation modifications shall remain strictly limited to objectively verified discovery and behavioral coverage deficiencies.

Every work order shall have one objective.

Every work order shall execute against one frozen implementation candidate.

Every work order shall preserve complete historical lineage.

Every work order shall preserve superseded behavioral evidence.

Every work order shall preserve superseded implementation discovery artifacts.

Every work order shall preserve superseded behavioral coverage artifacts.

No work order shall reinterpret constitutional doctrine.

No work order shall redefine implementation participation.

No implementation modification shall occur without corresponding objective execution evidence.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Behavioral coverage shall originate exclusively from canonical constitutional requirements.

Implementation participation shall originate exclusively from objective repository dependencies.

Verifier participation shall originate exclusively from objective repository dependencies.

Fixture participation shall originate exclusively from objective repository dependencies.

Behavioral coverage shall never be inferred from verifier inventories alone.

Every canonical constitutional requirement shall possess at least one independently discoverable executable behavioral verifier.

Every behavioral verifier shall possess one constitutional justification.

Every behavioral fixture shall possess one constitutional justification.

Every implementation participant shall possess one constitutional justification.

Repository-wide discovery shall be deterministic.

Repository-wide discovery shall be independently reproducible.

Behavioral coverage shall remain completely traceable to canonical constitutional requirements.

No manually maintained verifier population shall constitute authoritative discovery.

No manually maintained fixture population shall constitute authoritative discovery.

No constitutional requirement shall lack a behavioral verification disposition.
