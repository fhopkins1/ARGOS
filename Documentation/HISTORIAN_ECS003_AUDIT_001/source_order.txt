# HISTORIAN-ECS003-AUDIT-001

# Initial Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Conduct the initial Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 audit of the Historian Office.

The purpose of this audit is to determine whether the constitutional architecture governing the Historian Office is complete, deterministic, internally consistent, and sufficient to preserve immutable enterprise history for every constitutional workflow executed by the ARGOS Enterprise.

This audit shall not evaluate implementation quality.

This audit evaluates constitutional architecture only.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This audit derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine;
* Evidence Doctrine;
* Audit Doctrine;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine.

Nothing within this audit may modify constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Mission Under Audit

The Historian Office shall be evaluated against its constitutional mission:

> Preserve immutable enterprise history.

The Historian shall never:

* learn;
* infer;
* summarize;
* optimize;
* recommend;
* rank;
* predict;
* authorize;
* modify historical records.

The Historian shall function solely as the constitutional custodian of enterprise memory.

---

# Audit Scope

The audit shall determine whether the constitutional architecture completely governs:

* enterprise historical custody;

* enterprise information journeys;
* provenance preservation;
* historical ownership;
* historical lifecycle;
* historical interfaces;
* historical evidence;
* historical traceability;

* historical completeness.

The audit shall additionally determine whether the constitutional architecture fully supports deterministic enterprise learning without permitting historical corruption.
