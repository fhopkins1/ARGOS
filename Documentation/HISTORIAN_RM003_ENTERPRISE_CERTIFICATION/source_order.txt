# HISTORIAN-RM-003

# Historian Enterprise Certification Program

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This remediation program establishes the complete enterprise certification campaign for the Historian Office following successful completion of the constitutional remediation program and the implementation certification program.

The purpose of this series is to independently certify that the Historian Office satisfies all constitutional requirements for enterprise operation and can serve as the immutable historical foundation of the ARGOS Enterprise.

Successful completion of this program authorizes constitutional freeze of the Historian Office.

Future functional evolution shall occur exclusively through approved Modification Order (MO) campaigns.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This program derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine;
* HISTORIAN-RM-001;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002;
* HISTORIAN-MO-001;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine;
* Evidence Doctrine;
* Audit Doctrine.

Nothing within this program may alter constitutional architecture.

---

# Program Scope

This enterprise certification campaign shall verify:

* constitutional completeness;
* implementation completeness;
* enterprise integration;
* cross-office historical custody;
* enterprise-wide provenance;
* Enterprise Information Journey continuity;
* deterministic replay;
* Enterprise Learning readiness;
* counterfactual readiness;
* operational readiness;
* constitutional immutability.

This program certifies enterprise integration only.

No new constitutional behavior shall be introduced.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every certification order shall:

* verify observable enterprise behavior;
* require objective evidence;
* require deterministic reproduction;
* reject undocumented behavior;
* reject discretionary implementation;
* preserve immutable historical truth;
* authorize constitutional freeze only after complete certification.
