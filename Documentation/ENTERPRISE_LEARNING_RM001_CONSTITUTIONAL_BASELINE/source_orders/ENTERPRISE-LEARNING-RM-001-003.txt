## ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-003

### Constitutional Ownership Model

#### Purpose

Establish the constitutional ownership model governing every enterprise object managed by the Enterprise Learning Office.

This order defines ownership, lifecycle authority, custody, publication authority, and stewardship responsibilities for all learning assets while preserving strict constitutional separation from enterprise truth, historical preservation, workflow execution, operational decision making, and performance certification.

No enterprise object shall possess ambiguous or shared constitutional ownership.

---

### Constitutional Requirements

The Enterprise Learning Office shall possess exclusive constitutional ownership of:

* learning datasets;
* feature definitions;
* feature stores;
* hypotheses;
* experiments;
* experiment configurations;
* experiment results;
* trained models;
* model metadata;
* model versions;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty estimates;
* publication packages;
* learning evidence;
* publication lifecycle records.

Ownership shall be deterministic, exclusive, and independently auditable.

---

### Constitutional Ownership Principles

Every constitutional object shall possess exactly one constitutional owner.

Ownership shall define exclusive authority over:

* creation;
* modification;
* validation;
* publication;
* supersession;
* retirement;
* archival responsibility;
* lifecycle governance.

Ownership authority shall never be inferred through implementation behavior.

---

### Learning Dataset Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own:

* dataset definitions;
* dataset construction;
* dataset validation;
* dataset versioning;
* dataset quality metrics;
* dataset publication;
* dataset retirement.

The office shall not own the historical source records from which datasets are derived.

Historical records remain under the exclusive constitutional ownership of the Historian.

---

### Feature Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own:

* feature specifications;
* feature engineering;
* feature transformations;
* feature validation;
* feature documentation;
* feature versioning;
* feature retirement.

Feature lineage shall reference historical provenance without assuming ownership of that provenance.

---

### Hypothesis Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own:

* hypothesis creation;
* hypothesis definitions;
* hypothesis lifecycle;
* supporting rationale;
* experimental objectives;
* hypothesis retirement.

Hypotheses shall never constitute enterprise truth.

---

### Experiment Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own:

* experiment design;
* execution governance;
* experiment metadata;
* experiment results;
* statistical evaluation;
* experiment evidence;
* experiment lifecycle.

Experiment ownership shall not extend to operational workflow execution.

---

### Model Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own:

* model architecture;
* training processes;
* model parameters;
* validation results;
* deployment recommendations;
* published versions;
* retirement decisions.

Published models shall remain advisory artifacts and shall not authorize enterprise decisions.

---

### Learning Evidence Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall own evidence describing:

* dataset construction;
* feature generation;
* experiment execution;
* model training;
* validation activities;
* explainability generation;
* publication decisions.

Historical execution evidence remains the responsibility of the Historian.

---

### Publication Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall possess exclusive authority governing:

* publication approval;
* publication versioning;
* publication supersession;
* publication withdrawal;
* publication retirement.

Publication authority shall not include operational deployment authority unless constitutionally delegated to another office.

---

### Custody Responsibilities

The Enterprise Learning Office shall maintain constitutional custody of:

* learning artifacts;
* experiment artifacts;
* feature repositories;
* model repositories;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty artifacts;
* publication packages.

Custody shall preserve integrity, traceability, and version history throughout the lifecycle.

---

### Lifecycle Authority

The Enterprise Learning Office shall exclusively govern lifecycle transitions including:

* creation;
* validation;
* active publication;
* revision;
* supersession;
* retirement;
* archival.

Lifecycle transitions shall be deterministic and objectively verifiable.

---

### Ownership Exclusions

The Enterprise Learning Office shall not own:

* enterprise truth;
* historical records;
* provenance graphs;
* workflow execution;
* workflow authorizations;
* operational decisions;
* performance certification;
* audit certification;
* constitutional governance.

Ownership of these constitutional domains remains exclusively assigned to their respective enterprise offices.

---

### Ownership Verification

The implementation shall verify:

* every constitutional learning object has exactly one owner;
* ownership responsibilities are complete;
* ownership boundaries are non-overlapping;
* lifecycle authority aligns with ownership;
* custody aligns with ownership;
* publication authority aligns with ownership.

Any ownership ambiguity shall constitute a constitutional defect.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent constitutional review demonstrates that:

* every Enterprise Learning artifact possesses a single, deterministic constitutional owner;
* ownership, custody, lifecycle authority, and publication authority are completely defined;
* historical truth, provenance, workflow execution, operational decision making, and performance certification remain constitutionally outside Enterprise Learning ownership;
* ownership boundaries are complete, mutually exclusive, and independently auditable;
* no constitutional responsibility is duplicated, omitted, or ambiguously assigned.
