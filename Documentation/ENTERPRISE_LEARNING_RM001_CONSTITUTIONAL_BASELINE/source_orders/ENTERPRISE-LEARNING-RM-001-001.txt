# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-001

# Constitutional Mission Definition

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Establish the constitutional mission, authority, purpose, jurisdiction, and enterprise role of the Enterprise Learning Office.

The Enterprise Learning Office exists to transform immutable enterprise history into constitutionally governed learning hypotheses and validated knowledge capable of improving future enterprise performance.

The Enterprise Learning Office shall never directly modify enterprise behavior.

Enterprise improvement shall occur only through constitutional governance.

This order establishes the constitutional identity of the Enterprise Learning Office.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine;
* Evidence Doctrine;
* Audit Doctrine.

Nothing within this order may modify constitutional authority established elsewhere.

---

# Constitutional Mission

The constitutional mission of the Enterprise Learning Office is:

> **To transform immutable enterprise history into deterministic learning hypotheses, validated knowledge products, and evidence-based recommendations capable of improving future enterprise performance through constitutional governance.**

The Enterprise Learning Office shall never:

* create enterprise truth;
* modify historical records;
* alter provenance;
* authorize workflow execution;
* authorize trading;
* certify performance;
* modify enterprise behavior;
* implement enterprise improvements.

Enterprise Learning owns knowledge generation.

It does not own enterprise action.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Enterprise Learning Office exists to answer one constitutional question:

> **Given everything the enterprise has historically experienced, what experimentally supported knowledge can improve future enterprise behavior?**

Enterprise Learning shall never answer:

> **What shall the enterprise do immediately?**

Only the Commander possesses constitutional authority to authorize enterprise change.

---

# Constitutional Role

The Enterprise Learning Office occupies the constitutional position between:

* Historian; and
* Decision Laboratory.

The constitutional information flow shall remain:

```text
Historian
        ↓
Enterprise Learning
        ↓
Decision Laboratory
        ↓
Commander
        ↓
Modification Order
        ↓
Certified Enterprise
```

No constitutional shortcut shall exist.

Enterprise Learning shall never bypass the Decision Laboratory or Commander.

---

# Constitutional Inputs

Enterprise Learning may consume only constitutionally governed information including:

* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* historical custody records;
* provenance graphs;
* historical language;
* structured constitutional records;
* historical evidence;
* Performance Truth;
* Closed Position Truth;
* Monitoring observations;
* historical workflow artifacts.

Enterprise Learning shall not independently collect enterprise history.

Historical ownership remains exclusively with the Historian.

---

# Constitutional Outputs

Enterprise Learning may publish only constitutionally governed learning products including:

* learning hypotheses;
* validated knowledge products;
* feature definitions;
* causal hypotheses;
* predictive hypotheses;
* anomaly hypotheses;
* uncertainty analyses;
* learning evidence;
* recommendation packages for Decision Laboratory evaluation.

Enterprise Learning shall never publish:

* enterprise truth;
* workflow decisions;
* trade decisions;
* execution authority;
* constitutional amendments;
* certification decisions.

---

# Constitutional Authority Boundaries

Enterprise Learning possesses constitutional authority to:

* analyze historical information;
* construct learning datasets;
* generate learning hypotheses;
* conduct constitutionally authorized experiments;
* construct learning models;
* evaluate historical patterns;
* publish learning products.

Enterprise Learning shall not possess authority to:

* alter enterprise history;
* alter provenance;
* alter Performance Truth;
* alter Closed Position Truth;
* alter historical custody;
* execute workflows;
* authorize risk;
* authorize trading;
* authorize deployment;
* certify implementation.

---

# Constitutional Separation

The Enterprise Learning Office shall remain constitutionally distinct from:

### Historian

Historian preserves immutable enterprise history.

Enterprise Learning consumes immutable enterprise history.

Historian shall never learn.

Enterprise Learning shall never preserve constitutional history.

---

### Decision Laboratory

Enterprise Learning generates hypotheses.

Decision Laboratory independently validates hypotheses.

Enterprise Learning shall never validate its own conclusions.

---

### Commander

Enterprise Learning recommends.

Commander authorizes.

Enterprise Learning shall never authorize enterprise change.

---

### Performance Truth

Performance Truth measures enterprise outcomes.

Enterprise Learning analyzes those outcomes.

Enterprise Learning shall never redefine enterprise performance.

---

### Workflow Execution

Workflow execution performs enterprise work.

Enterprise Learning observes completed enterprise work.

Enterprise Learning shall never participate in workflow execution.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following constitutional invariants are permanent.

Enterprise Learning:

* owns learning only;
* never owns enterprise truth;
* never owns historical custody;
* never owns provenance;
* never owns workflow execution;
* never owns enterprise decisions;
* never owns certification authority.

Enterprise Learning shall remain an advisory constitutional office.

This order is complete only when:

* the constitutional mission of the Enterprise Learning Office is explicitly defined;

* constitutional purpose is deterministic;
* constitutional role within the Enterprise Improvement Group is established;
