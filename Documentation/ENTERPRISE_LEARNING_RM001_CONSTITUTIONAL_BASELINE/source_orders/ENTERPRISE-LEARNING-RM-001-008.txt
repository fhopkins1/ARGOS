## ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-008

### Explainability Architecture

#### Purpose

Establish the constitutional architecture governing explainability for every learning product published by the Enterprise Learning Office.

The purpose of explainability is to ensure that every published learning artifact is transparent, reproducible, objectively supported by evidence, and suitable for independent evaluation without requiring knowledge of its internal implementation.

Explainability shall enable understanding of learning outputs without converting those outputs into constitutional truth or enterprise decisions.

---

### Constitutional Requirements

Every published learning product shall include constitutionally defined explainability information describing:

* supporting evidence;
* underlying assumptions;
* confidence;
* uncertainty;
* feature contribution;
* reproducibility status;
* publication metadata;
* applicable operating domain.

Explainability shall be generated deterministically and independently verifiable.

---

### Explainability Scope

This architecture shall govern explainability for:

* hypotheses;
* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly models;
* uncertainty estimates;
* feature definitions;
* experiment results;
* published learning recommendations.

No learning product may be published without its corresponding explainability artifacts.

---

### Supporting Evidence

Every published learning product shall reference objective supporting evidence including:

* datasets utilized;
* experiment identifiers;
* validation results;
* evaluation metrics;
* supporting historical references;
* applicable feature sets;
* publication evidence.

Supporting evidence shall remain traceable to immutable historical records maintained by the Historian.

---

### Assumption Documentation

The Enterprise Learning Office shall publish all material assumptions including:

* modeling assumptions;
* statistical assumptions;
* feature assumptions;
* environmental assumptions;
* operational assumptions;
* applicability constraints;
* known limitations.

Assumptions shall be immutable for each published version.

---

### Confidence Architecture

Every learning product shall publish quantitative confidence information describing:

* prediction confidence;
* model confidence;
* validation confidence;
* calibration quality;
* confidence interval where applicable;
* confidence derivation methodology.

Confidence values shall be reproducible from the certified learning process.

---

### Uncertainty Architecture

Every published learning product shall explicitly disclose uncertainty including:

* data uncertainty;
* model uncertainty;
* feature uncertainty;
* observational uncertainty;
* extrapolation uncertainty;
* environmental uncertainty;
* known unknowns.

Uncertainty shall never be suppressed to improve perceived model quality.

---

### Feature Contribution

The implementation shall publish deterministic descriptions of feature contribution including:

* influential features;
* feature importance;
* feature interactions where supported;
* feature relevance;
* feature availability;
* feature quality indicators.

Feature contribution shall be reproducible for equivalent model versions and equivalent inputs.

---

### Reproducibility Status

Every published learning product shall include reproducibility metadata describing:

* training reproducibility;
* dataset reproducibility;
* experiment reproducibility;
* validation reproducibility;
* implementation reproducibility;
* certification status;
* known reproducibility limitations.

Products lacking reproducibility certification shall clearly indicate that status.

---

### Explainability Provenance

Every explainability artifact shall include provenance identifying:

* originating experiment;
* originating model version;
* originating dataset version;
* feature version;
* publication version;
* evidence references;
* certification references.

Historical provenance shall remain exclusively owned by the Historian.

---

### Publication Requirements

No learning product shall be eligible for publication unless it possesses complete explainability documentation demonstrating:

* supporting evidence;
* documented assumptions;
* confidence;
* uncertainty;
* feature contribution;
* reproducibility status;
* publication provenance.

Incomplete explainability shall prevent publication.

---

### Constitutional Limitations

Explainability artifacts shall not:

* establish enterprise truth;
* authorize workflow execution;
* modify historical records;
* alter provenance;
* certify operational performance;
* issue enterprise decisions.

Explainability exists solely to support understanding and evaluation of published learning products.

---

### Failure Behavior

Publication shall fail closed upon detection of:

* missing explainability artifacts;
* undocumented assumptions;
* missing supporting evidence;
* incomplete confidence information;
* incomplete uncertainty disclosure;
* missing feature contribution;
* invalid reproducibility status;
* inconsistent provenance.

No partially explained learning product shall be published.

---

### Constitutional Verification

The implementation shall verify that:

* every published learning product possesses complete explainability;
* explainability is deterministic and reproducible;
* supporting evidence is traceable to immutable historical records;
* assumptions, confidence, uncertainty, and feature contribution remain version-controlled;
* explainability remains constitutionally separate from truth, decision-making, and operational authority.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent constitutional review demonstrates that:

* every published learning product includes complete explainability artifacts;
* supporting evidence, assumptions, confidence, uncertainty, feature contribution, and reproducibility status are objectively documented and independently verifiable;
* publication is prevented whenever explainability requirements are incomplete;
* historical provenance remains exclusively under Historian ownership;
* explainability improves transparency and reproducibility without granting Enterprise Learning constitutional authority over truth, history, workflow execution, or enterprise decisions.
