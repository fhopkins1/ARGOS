# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-006

# Learning Lifecycle Architecture

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Establish the complete constitutional lifecycle governing every learning artifact owned by the Enterprise Learning Office.

The purpose of this order is to ensure that every learning artifact progresses through a deterministic, observable, reproducible, and independently auditable lifecycle from creation through retirement.

The learning lifecycle shall preserve constitutional ownership, evidence, reproducibility, explainability, and enterprise governance.

This order governs learning lifecycle architecture only.

It shall not authorize learning-driven enterprise modification or autonomous deployment.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine;
* Evidence Doctrine;
* Audit Doctrine;
* ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001.

Nothing within this order authorizes modification of constitutional ownership or enterprise authority.

---

# Constitutional Mission

The Enterprise Learning Office shall govern the complete lifecycle of every learning artifact it constitutionally owns.

Every lifecycle transition shall be:

* deterministic;
* observable;
* reproducible;
* evidence-backed;
* independently auditable.

Learning artifacts shall never bypass constitutional lifecycle governance.

---

# Lifecycle Scope

The constitutional lifecycle governs:

* learning datasets;
* feature definitions;
* engineered features;
* learning hypotheses;
* experiment definitions;
* experiment results;
* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly models;
* uncertainty models;
* explainability artifacts;
* recommendation packages;
* publication records.

Each artifact shall possess an independent lifecycle.

---

# Lifecycle Principles

Every learning artifact shall:

* possess immutable identity;
* possess constitutional ownership;
* possess deterministic state transitions;
* preserve historical lineage;
* preserve supersession history;
* preserve evidence;
* preserve reproducibility;
* preserve explainability.

Artifacts shall never be silently replaced.

---

# Canonical Lifecycle

Every learning artifact shall progress through the following constitutional lifecycle:

```text id="wwj5qj"
Proposed
      ↓
Defined
      ↓
Prepared
      ↓
Experimented
      ↓
Validated
      ↓
Published
      ↓
Active
      ↓
Superseded
      ↓
Retired
      ↓
Archived
```

Every transition shall possess constitutional authorization and supporting evidence.

---

# Proposed

A proposed artifact represents a constitutionally recognized concept.

The Proposed state shall preserve:

* artifact identity;
* proposal source;
* originating hypothesis;
* constitutional owner;
* creation timestamp;
* originating Enterprise Information Journeys.

No artifact may advance without constitutional definition.

---

# Defined

The Defined state establishes:

* purpose;
* inputs;
* outputs;
* assumptions;
* intended use;
* evaluation criteria;
* reproducibility requirements.

Definitions shall be immutable after publication.

---

# Prepared

Prepared artifacts shall possess:

* complete datasets;
* feature specifications;
* dependency verification;
* reproducibility metadata;
* experiment configuration.

Preparation shall preserve deterministic execution.

---

# Experimented

Experiment execution shall preserve:

* execution environment;
* runtime configuration;
* dataset identity;
* feature identity;
* experiment identity;
* model configuration;
* execution evidence;
* execution outputs.

Experiments shall be independently reproducible.

---

# Validated

Validation shall independently verify:

* reproducibility;
* statistical validity;
* evidence completeness;
* explainability;
* constitutional compliance;
* confidence;
* uncertainty.

Enterprise Learning shall never validate its own work without constitutionally defined independent validation procedures.

---

# Published

Publication shall establish:

* immutable publication identity;
* publication timestamp;
* publication version;
* superseded publications;
* publication evidence;
* publication provenance.

Publication shall not constitute enterprise authorization.

---

# Active

Active artifacts may be consumed by constitutionally authorized enterprise offices.

Activation shall preserve:

* version identity;
* usage evidence;
* dependency tracking;
* publication lineage.

Active artifacts remain advisory.

---

# Superseded

Supersession shall preserve:

* successor identity;
* supersession authority;
* supersession rationale;
* supersession timestamp;
* complete historical lineage.

Historical artifacts shall never be deleted.

---

# Retired

Retirement shall:

* terminate future usage;
* preserve historical availability;
* preserve reproducibility;
* preserve provenance.

Retirement shall not destroy enterprise history.

---

# Archived

Archived artifacts shall remain:

* immutable;
* reproducible;
* historically accessible;
* constitutionally owned.

Archive status shall preserve complete historical reconstruction.

---

# Lifecycle Authority

Enterprise Learning owns lifecycle progression through:

* Proposed;
* Defined;
* Prepared;
* Experimented.

Decision Laboratory owns independent validation.

Publication shall require successful validation.

Commander remains the sole constitutional authority for enterprise adoption of learning-derived improvements.

Lifecycle ownership shall never bypass constitutional governance.

---

# Lifecycle Evidence

Every lifecycle transition shall generate:

* transition identity;
* transition timestamp;
* previous state;
* new state;
* authorizing authority;
* supporting evidence;
* provenance;
* reproducibility status;
* validation status.

Every transition shall be independently auditable.

---

# Lifecycle Determinism

Identical artifacts progressing under identical constitutional conditions shall produce identical lifecycle transitions.

Lifecycle execution shall not depend upon undocumented assumptions or manual intervention.

---

# Lifecycle Failure

Lifecycle progression shall fail closed whenever:

* required evidence is absent;
* validation is incomplete;
* provenance is incomplete;
* ownership is ambiguous;
* reproducibility cannot be demonstrated;
* constitutional authority is absent.

Failed artifacts shall never advance automatically.

---

# Historical Preservation

Every lifecycle event shall be preserved by the Historian as part of the corresponding Enterprise Information Journey.

Enterprise Learning shall own the artifact.

The Historian shall own the immutable historical record of the artifact.

---

# Constitutional Separation

The learning lifecycle shall never:

* modify enterprise truth;
* modify historical provenance;
* modify workflow execution;
* authorize enterprise behavior;
* certify implementation;
* replace Commander authority.

Learning lifecycle governance remains constitutionally advisory.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order is complete only when:

* every Enterprise Learning artifact possesses a deterministic constitutional lifecycle;
* every lifecycle transition possesses objective evidence;
* lifecycle ownership is unambiguous;
* validation remains constitutionally independent;
* publication remains distinct from enterprise authorization;
* supersession preserves complete historical lineage;
* retirement preserves reproducibility;
* archival preserves immutable historical reconstruction;
* lifecycle behavior remains completely auditable and deterministic.

Completion of this order establishes the constitutional lifecycle architecture of the Enterprise Learning Office and authorizes progression to **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-007 — Evidence and Provenance Architecture**.
