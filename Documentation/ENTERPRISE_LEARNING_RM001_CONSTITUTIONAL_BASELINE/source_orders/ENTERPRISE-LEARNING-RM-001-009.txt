## ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-009

# Constitutional Boundary Verification

## Purpose

This order establishes the constitutional verification framework ensuring that the Enterprise Learning Office operates exclusively within its constitutional authority and does not assume responsibilities constitutionally assigned to other enterprise offices.

The objective is to preserve the separation of learning, truth, decision making, workflow execution, historical preservation, performance certification, and enterprise governance.

No learning capability shall expand constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

Implement a complete constitutional boundary verification ensuring the Enterprise Learning Office cannot:

* create enterprise truth;
* modify historical records;
* alter provenance;
* alter historical custody;
* authorize workflow execution;
* authorize enterprise decisions;
* certify enterprise performance;
* execute workflows;
* own workflow decisions;
* own operational risk;
* publish constitutional doctrine;
* modify constitutional architecture.

Every prohibited responsibility shall be both architecturally impossible and behaviorally verifiable.

---

# Truth Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* consumes verified truth;
* learns from verified outcomes;
* publishes knowledge products;
* never establishes objective enterprise truth;
* never modifies certified truth records.

Enterprise Truth shall remain exclusively owned by the Performance Truth Office.

---

# Historical Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* retrieves historical information;
* preserves references to historical artifacts;
* consumes historical provenance;
* never modifies historical records;
* never modifies historical custody;
* never reconstructs or rewrites enterprise history.

Historical ownership shall remain exclusively with the Historian Office.

---

# Decision Authority Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* publishes recommendations;
* publishes hypotheses;
* publishes predictive outputs;
* publishes confidence estimates;
* publishes uncertainty estimates;
* never approves operational decisions;
* never rejects operational decisions;
* never issues enterprise commands.

Enterprise decision authority shall remain outside Enterprise Learning.

---

# Workflow Execution Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* never initiates workflows;
* never controls workflow execution;
* never assigns workflow ownership;
* never modifies workflow state;
* never issues execution authorizations.

Workflow execution authority shall remain with constitutionally authorized workflow owners.

---

# Performance Certification Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* consumes performance measurements;
* consumes certified outcomes;
* evaluates learning quality;
* validates model quality;
* never certifies enterprise performance;
* never publishes official performance truth.

Performance certification shall remain exclusively owned by the Performance Truth Office.

---

# Risk Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* consumes historical risk information;
* learns from historical exposure;
* produces knowledge products regarding observed risk behavior;
* never performs constitutional risk evaluation;
* never establishes operational risk limits;
* never authorizes risk acceptance.

Risk authority shall remain exclusively with the Risk Office.

---

# Governance Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* operates under constitutional doctrine;
* follows enterprise governance;
* cannot modify constitutional policies;
* cannot alter enterprise authority structures;
* cannot redefine constitutional ownership.

Governance authority shall remain with constitutionally authorized offices.

---

# Publication Boundary Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning publishes only:

* hypotheses;
* trained models;
* feature definitions;
* experiment results;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty estimates;
* model validation reports;
* learning evidence.

Published artifacts shall constitute knowledge products only.

Publication shall not create enterprise truth, operational directives, or constitutional authority.

---

# Runtime Boundary Enforcement

Implement deterministic runtime validation preventing Enterprise Learning from performing prohibited constitutional actions.

Every attempted boundary violation shall:

* be rejected before execution;
* generate constitutional violation evidence;
* preserve system integrity;
* produce deterministic failure behavior.

Boundary enforcement shall execute automatically.

---

# Runtime Evidence Generation

Every boundary verification shall generate immutable evidence including:

* verification identifier;
* evaluated constitutional boundary;
* attempted operation;
* requesting component;
* validation outcome;
* violated constitutional rule when applicable;
* enforcement action;
* timestamps;
* verification status.

Evidence shall be independently reproducible.

---

# Runtime Interfaces

Implement deterministic interfaces supporting:

* boundary validation;
* authority verification;
* ownership verification;
* publication validation;
* constitutional compliance evaluation;
* boundary audit retrieval;
* enforcement evidence retrieval.

All interfaces shall preserve constitutional separation.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

Enterprise Learning shall reject any operation attempting to:

* create or modify enterprise truth;
* alter historical records;
* alter provenance or custody;
* authorize workflow execution;
* issue enterprise decisions;
* certify enterprise performance;
* execute workflows;
* assume constitutional ownership belonging to another office;
* modify constitutional governance.

No prohibited operation shall be partially executed.

---

# Architectural Verification

The constitutional architecture review shall verify:

* complete separation of learning from truth;
* complete separation of learning from historical preservation;
* complete separation of learning from workflow execution;
* complete separation of learning from operational decision making;
* complete separation of learning from performance certification;
* complete separation of learning from constitutional governance;
* deterministic enforcement of all constitutional boundaries;
* independently auditable evidence for every boundary validation.

Successful verification shall demonstrate that the Enterprise Learning Office functions exclusively as the constitutional producer and publisher of enterprise knowledge products while preserving the authority, ownership, and responsibilities of every other constitutional office.
