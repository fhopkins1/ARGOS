# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-007

## Evidence and Provenance Architecture

### Objective

Establish the constitutional architecture governing learning evidence, experiment evidence, feature lineage, model provenance, and publication provenance within the Enterprise Learning Office.

This order shall define the distinction between:

* learning evidence;
* learning lineage;
* model provenance;
* publication provenance;
* historical provenance.

Enterprise Learning shall own evidence and lineage created through learning activity.

The Historian shall remain the exclusive constitutional owner of historical provenance and permanent historical custody.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The architecture shall:

* assign one constitutional owner to every evidence and provenance object;
* preserve immutable historical provenance;
* prevent Enterprise Learning from modifying historical records;
* preserve complete learning lineage;
* preserve model reproducibility;
* preserve publication traceability;
* distinguish derived learning relationships from historical facts;
* require deterministic identity and lifecycle governance;
* prohibit undocumented evidence;
* prohibit provenance reconstruction by assumption;
* fail closed whenever lineage or provenance cannot be established.

No learning product shall be published without sufficient supporting evidence and provenance.

---

# Evidence Architecture

Enterprise Learning shall own evidence generated through its constitutional learning activities.

Learning evidence shall demonstrate:

* what learning activity occurred;
* which inputs were used;
* which transformations were performed;
* which methods were executed;
* which outputs were produced;
* which validations were completed;
* which conclusions were supported;
* which limitations remained;
* whether the activity was reproducible.

Learning evidence shall support learning claims.

It shall not create historical truth or replace authoritative enterprise records.

---

# Learning Evidence Classes

Enterprise Learning shall govern the following evidence classes:

* dataset-construction evidence;
* dataset-validation evidence;
* feature-generation evidence;
* feature-validation evidence;
* hypothesis evidence;
* experiment evidence;
* training evidence;
* model-validation evidence;
* model-comparison evidence;
* uncertainty evidence;
* explainability evidence;
* reproducibility evidence;
* publication evidence;
* monitoring-response evidence;
* retirement evidence;
* supersession evidence.

Each evidence class shall possess a defined constitutional purpose and lifecycle.

---

# Canonical Learning Evidence Object

Every learning evidence object shall include:

* canonical evidence identifier;
* evidence classification;
* constitutional owner;
* producing workflow;
* producing activity;
* creation timestamp;
* source-object references;
* dataset references;
* feature references;
* hypothesis references where applicable;
* experiment references where applicable;
* model references where applicable;
* method identity;
* environment identity;
* configuration identity;
* result;
* interpretation;
* uncertainty;
* reproducibility status;
* custody status;
* Historian registration reference;
* lifecycle state.

Evidence lacking these minimum elements shall not support publication.

---

# Learning Evidence Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall possess lifecycle authority over learning evidence while that evidence remains within the active learning lifecycle.

Its authority shall include:

* evidence creation;
* evidence classification;
* evidence validation;
* evidence association;
* evidence qualification;
* evidence correction through supersession;
* evidence publication;
* evidence retirement.

Enterprise Learning shall not silently alter previously published evidence.

Corrections shall create new evidence objects linked through explicit correction or supersession relationships.

---

# Historical Custody of Learning Evidence

The Historian shall possess permanent historical custody of learning evidence accepted into the enterprise historical record.

Upon Historian acceptance:

* the historical copy shall become immutable;
* Enterprise Learning shall retain a reference rather than alteration authority;
* corrections shall be submitted as new historical artifacts;
* supersession shall preserve the original evidence;
* historical ordering shall remain owned by the Historian;
* historical provenance shall remain owned by the Historian.

Enterprise Learning shall not delete, rewrite, or reclassify the Historian’s preserved record.

---

# Experiment Evidence Architecture

Every governed experiment shall produce a complete experiment evidence set.

The evidence set shall include:

* experiment identity;
* associated hypothesis;
* experimental objective;
* dataset versions;
* feature versions;
* method or model version;
* control configuration;
* comparison configuration;
* environment identity;
* dependency identity;
* randomization controls;
* deterministic seeds where applicable;
* evaluation metrics;
* acceptance criteria;
* rejection criteria;
* raw outputs;
* transformed outputs;
* validation results;
* contradictory findings;
* uncertainty;
* final experiment disposition;
* reproducibility result.

Experiment conclusions unsupported by an experiment evidence set shall possess no publication authority.

---

# Negative and Inconclusive Evidence

Enterprise Learning shall preserve:

* failed experiments;
* rejected hypotheses;
* inconclusive results;
* contradictory results;
* null results;
* validation failures;
* reproducibility failures;
* model underperformance;
* data deficiencies.

Negative and inconclusive evidence shall not be discarded merely because it does not support publication.

Such evidence shall remain available to prevent repeated error, selective reporting, and unsupported learning claims.

---

# Feature Lineage Architecture

Enterprise Learning shall own the complete lineage of every governed feature.

Feature lineage shall describe the derivation of a learning feature from authorized source information.

Each feature lineage record shall include:

* feature identifier;
* feature version;
* semantic definition;
* source-record references;
* source-field references;
* transformation sequence;
* intermediate derived values or definitions;
* calculation logic;
* unit conversions;
* temporal alignment rules;
* aggregation rules;
* missing-value treatment;
* uncertainty treatment;
* validation evidence;
* dataset usage;
* experiment usage;
* model usage;
* publication usage.

Feature lineage shall be deterministic and reproducible.

---

# Feature Lineage Boundaries

Feature lineage shall begin with references to constitutionally authorized source records.

It shall not redefine or modify those source records.

Historical provenance shall establish:

* where the source record originated;
* when it entered enterprise custody;
* how it changed historically;
* which historical relationships governed it.

Feature lineage shall establish:

* how Enterprise Learning selected the source;
* how the source was transformed;
* how the resulting feature was used.

The two forms of provenance shall remain constitutionally distinct.

---

# Model Provenance Architecture

Enterprise Learning shall own model provenance for every governed model.

Model provenance shall establish the complete derivational history of the model, including:

* model identifier;
* model version;
* model class;
* intended purpose;
* originating hypothesis;
* training dataset versions;
* validation dataset versions;
* feature versions;
* architecture or method definition;
* parameter configuration;
* training configuration;
* environment identity;
* dependency identity;
* randomization controls;
* training execution identity;
* training evidence;
* validation evidence;
* comparison evidence;
* explainability evidence;
* uncertainty evidence;
* reproducibility evidence;
* publication state;
* retirement or supersession relationships.

A model without complete model provenance shall not be published.

---

# Model Artifact Integrity

Every published model shall be bound to:

* executable model artifact;
* model definition;
* parameter state;
* configuration state;
* dependency state;
* input schema;
* output schema;
* feature schema;
* validation record;
* cryptographic identity.

The published model identity shall change whenever a behaviorally material component changes.

Undocumented mutation of a published model is prohibited.

---

# Publication Provenance Architecture

Enterprise Learning shall own publication provenance for every learning product it publishes.

Publication provenance shall establish:

* what was published;
* which version was published;
* who possessed publication authority;
* which evidence supported publication;
* which assumptions applied;
* which limitations applied;
* which uncertainty was disclosed;
* which intended uses were authorized;
* which uses were prohibited;
* when publication occurred;
* which consumers received the product;
* whether the product was later restricted, withdrawn, retired, or superseded.

Publication provenance shall make every publication decision traceable to its supporting learning evidence.

---

# Publication Evidence Threshold

Before publication, the architecture shall verify the existence of:

* complete object identity;
* complete lifecycle state;
* supporting learning evidence;
* experiment evidence where applicable;
* feature lineage;
* model provenance where applicable;
* uncertainty disclosure;
* explainability artifacts;
* reproducibility status;
* Historian registration status;
* publication authority;
* intended-use definition;
* prohibited-use definition.

Failure of any mandatory publication requirement shall block publication.

---

# Historical Provenance Ownership

The Historian shall retain exclusive constitutional ownership of historical provenance.

Historical provenance shall govern:

* historical artifact origin;
* historical custody;
* historical ordering;
* historical derivation;
* historical correction;
* historical supersession;
* historical journey relationships;
* historical certification lineage;
* historical replay.

Enterprise Learning may read and reference historical provenance.

It shall not:

* create authoritative historical provenance;
* modify historical provenance;
* delete historical provenance;
* rewrite historical lineage;
* reorder historical events;
* replace Historian provenance with learning lineage;
* certify historical completeness.

---

# Provenance Reference Architecture

Learning evidence and lineage objects shall reference Historian-owned provenance through immutable identifiers.

A provenance reference shall include:

* Historian artifact identifier;
* Historian journey identifier;
* provenance node identifier;
* relevant provenance edge identifiers;
* custody status;
* source version or historical state;
* retrieval timestamp;
* provenance verification result.

References shall not duplicate authority.

The Historian record shall remain authoritative where a conflict exists between a learning reference and historical provenance.

---

# Derived Interpretation Boundary

Enterprise Learning may create derived interpretations of historical information.

Derived interpretations shall:

* remain separate from source records;
* identify the interpreting method;
* identify the source records;
* identify assumptions;
* identify uncertainty;
* identify limitations;
* possess their own learning-object identity;
* never alter historical provenance.

An interpretation shall not be represented as part of the historical record unless separately registered with the Historian as a new artifact.

---

# Provenance Graph Separation

The architecture shall maintain distinct but linked graph domains.

## Historian Provenance Graph

The Historian graph shall represent:

* historical custody;
* historical derivation;
* historical correction;
* historical supersession;
* enterprise information journeys;
* historical certification lineage.

## Enterprise Learning Lineage Graph

The Enterprise Learning graph shall represent:

* dataset derivation;
* feature derivation;
* hypothesis relationships;
* experiment relationships;
* model derivation;
* evidence support;
* publication support;
* retirement and supersession of learning products.

Cross-domain edges shall be references only.

Enterprise Learning shall not assert ownership over Historian graph nodes or edges.

---

# Evidence Support Relationships

The architecture shall define explicit relationships including:

* evidence supports hypothesis;
* evidence contradicts hypothesis;
* experiment tests hypothesis;
* dataset supplies experiment;
* feature derives from source;
* feature supplies dataset;
* dataset trains model;
* dataset validates model;
* evidence validates feature;
* evidence validates model;
* evidence supports publication;
* product supersedes product;
* evidence corrects evidence;
* publication references historical provenance.

Relationships shall be canonical, typed, and deterministic.

Free-text association alone shall not satisfy lineage requirements.

---

# Evidence Lifecycle

Learning evidence shall progress through deterministic lifecycle states including:

* Created;
* Registered;
* Under Validation;
* Validated;
* Qualified;
* Published;
* Restricted;
* Superseded;
* Retired;
* Invalidated.

Lifecycle transitions shall require defined authority and evidence.

Invalidated evidence shall remain historically preserved but shall no longer support new publication.

---

# Provenance Lifecycle

Learning lineage and model provenance records shall progress through states including:

* Initialized;
* Accumulating;
* Complete;
* Verified;
* Published;
* Superseded;
* Retired.

A provenance record shall not become Complete until every mandatory relationship has been resolved.

A learning product shall not become Published until its required provenance record is Verified.

---

# Correction Architecture

Evidence, lineage, and provenance errors shall be corrected through append-only constitutional mechanisms.

Correction shall:

* preserve the original object;
* create a correction object;
* identify the defect;
* identify the corrected representation;
* link original and correction;
* disclose publication impact;
* determine whether dependent products remain valid;
* register the correction with the Historian.

Direct overwrite of historically registered evidence is prohibited.

---

# Supersession Architecture

When a new evidence object, feature definition, model, or learning product replaces a prior object, supersession shall:

* preserve both objects;
* identify the predecessor;
* identify the successor;
* record the reason;
* record effective time;
* identify affected publications;
* identify affected consumers;
* preserve full lineage;
* register the relationship with the Historian.

Supersession shall not imply that the predecessor never existed.

---

# Evidence Quality Requirements

Learning evidence shall be evaluated for:

* relevance;
* completeness;
* integrity;
* reproducibility;
* traceability;
* methodological validity;
* scope alignment;
* uncertainty disclosure;
* contradiction disclosure;
* temporal applicability;
* independence where required.

Evidence quality status shall be explicit.

Low-quality evidence may be preserved but shall not support claims beyond its qualified scope.

---

# Reproducibility Architecture

Every learning evidence and provenance object supporting publication shall include a reproducibility status.

The status shall identify whether the object is:

* Not Evaluated;
* Reproducible;
* Reproducible with Declared Variance;
* Partially Reproducible;
* Not Reproducible;
* Reproducibility Failed.

Publication rules shall define which statuses are permitted for each learning-product class.

A nonreproducible product shall not be published as fully qualified.

---

# Custody Architecture

Working custody and historical custody shall remain distinct.

Enterprise Learning shall possess working custody over:

* active datasets;
* active feature definitions;
* active hypotheses;
* active experiments;
* active models;
* draft learning evidence;
* draft publication records.

The Historian shall possess permanent historical custody over accepted:

* dataset versions;
* feature versions;
* hypothesis states;
* experiment records;
* model versions;
* evidence objects;
* publication events;
* retirement events;
* supersession events.

Transfer to historical custody shall not transfer constitutional ownership of the learning object itself.

---

# Interface Requirements

## Historian Interface

The interface shall support:

* historical provenance retrieval;
* historical artifact verification;
* evidence registration;
* lineage registration;
* publication registration;
* correction registration;
* supersession registration;
* immutable custody acknowledgment.

## Performance Truth Interface

The interface shall provide authoritative performance records used as learning inputs.

Enterprise Learning shall reference those records without assuming performance-certification authority.

## Monitoring Interface

The interface shall provide drift, anomaly, degradation, and operational-observation records that may generate new learning evidence.

## Librarian Interface

The interface shall support cataloging and discovery of published evidence, lineage, and learning products without transferring ownership.

All interfaces shall preserve authority boundaries.

---

# Evidence and Provenance Validation

The architecture shall validate:

* evidence identity;
* evidence ownership;
* source references;
* lineage completeness;
* provenance completeness;
* relationship validity;
* model-artifact binding;
* publication-evidence binding;
* historical provenance references;
* custody status;
* lifecycle legality;
* correction integrity;
* supersession integrity;
* reproducibility status.

Validation shall be deterministic and independently verifiable.

---

# Failure Conditions

The architecture shall reject:

* orphaned evidence;
* evidence without producing activity;
* features without source lineage;
* models without training provenance;
* publications without supporting evidence;
* altered historical provenance;
* undocumented transformations;
* missing uncertainty;
* missing contradictory evidence;
* missing reproducibility status;
* broken Historian references;
* ambiguous custody;
* circular derivation;
* unauthorized correction;
* silent supersession;
* provenance claims unsupported by records.

Unresolved provenance failure shall prevent publication.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

When evidence or provenance cannot be established, Enterprise Learning shall:

* reject qualification;
* reject publication;
* preserve existing valid records;
* generate a constitutional finding;
* identify the missing or invalid relationship;
* prevent downstream reliance;
* submit failure evidence to the Historian;
* require remediation before reconsideration.

The office shall not infer missing lineage or provenance from convention.

---

# Independent Verification Requirements

Independent verification shall confirm:

* complete learning evidence ownership;
* complete experiment evidence;
* complete feature lineage;
* complete model provenance;
* complete publication provenance;
* immutable references to Historian provenance;
* correct custody separation;
* correction preservation;
* supersession preservation;
* deterministic lifecycle governance;
* publication blocking upon incomplete provenance;
* absence of historical-provenance authority within Enterprise Learning.

Verification shall examine constitutional definitions, object schemas, interfaces, lifecycle rules, repository implementation, and observable behavior.

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be complete only when:

* every learning evidence class possesses a defined owner and lifecycle;
* experiment evidence requirements are complete;
* every feature is traceable to authorized source records;
* every model is traceable to datasets, features, methods, configurations, and evidence;
* every publication is traceable to supporting evidence and authority;
* learning lineage remains distinct from historical provenance;
* historical provenance remains exclusively owned by the Historian;
* permanent historical custody remains with the Historian;
* corrections and supersessions preserve original records;
* incomplete evidence or provenance blocks publication;
* undocumented transformation is prohibited;
* all cross-office provenance references preserve authority boundaries;
* no unresolved evidence, lineage, provenance, or custody ambiguity remains.
