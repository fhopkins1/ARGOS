# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-002

## Constitutional Responsibility Definition

### Objective

Establish the complete constitutional responsibilities of the Enterprise Learning Office and define the exclusive boundaries governing its enterprise authority.

This order shall determine what Enterprise Learning owns, what it may perform, what it may publish, and what it is constitutionally prohibited from controlling.

Enterprise Learning shall convert governed historical and operational information into reproducible learning products.

It shall not create enterprise truth, make enterprise decisions, execute workflows, authorize actions, certify performance, or modify historical records.

---

# Constitutional Mission Alignment

Every Enterprise Learning responsibility shall directly support the constitutional mission established under **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-001**.

The office shall exist to:

* construct governed learning datasets;
* formulate testable hypotheses;
* conduct reproducible experiments;
* develop learning models;
* define learning features;
* generate learning evidence;
* publish qualified learning products;
* preserve the lifecycle and status of those products.

No responsibility shall be inferred solely from technical capability.

---

# Exclusive Constitutional Responsibilities

Enterprise Learning shall possess exclusive constitutional responsibility for:

* learning dataset governance;
* hypothesis governance;
* experiment governance;
* feature-definition governance;
* model-development governance;
* learning validation;
* learning evidence generation;
* learning-product publication;
* learning-product retirement;
* learning-product supersession;
* reproducibility qualification;
* explainability qualification;
* uncertainty disclosure.

These responsibilities shall not transfer decision authority, truth authority, execution authority, performance-certification authority, or historical custody to Enterprise Learning.

---

# Learning Dataset Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own the creation and lifecycle governance of learning datasets derived from constitutionally authorized source information.

Its responsibilities shall include:

* defining dataset purpose;
* defining dataset scope;
* identifying permitted source records;
* defining inclusion criteria;
* defining exclusion criteria;
* defining labels and targets;
* defining sampling rules;
* defining transformation rules;
* defining partitioning rules;
* identifying known deficiencies;
* assigning dataset versions;
* validating dataset reproducibility;
* publishing dataset qualification status;
* retiring or superseding datasets.

Enterprise Learning shall own the derived learning dataset.

It shall not own the underlying historical source records.

Historical custody, historical integrity, and historical provenance shall remain exclusively governed by the Historian.

---

# Dataset Constitutional Requirements

Every learning dataset shall possess:

* a canonical identifier;
* a defined learning purpose;
* a declared owner;
* a declared source scope;
* source-record references;
* inclusion and exclusion rules;
* transformation definitions;
* feature references;
* label definitions when applicable;
* known limitations;
* uncertainty disclosures;
* lineage references;
* version identity;
* lifecycle state;
* reproducibility status.

A collection of data shall not become a constitutional learning dataset until these requirements are satisfied.

---

# Hypothesis Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own the complete lifecycle of learning hypotheses.

Its responsibilities shall include:

* hypothesis registration;
* hypothesis formulation;
* hypothesis classification;
* assumption declaration;
* test-condition definition;
* falsification-condition definition;
* supporting-evidence identification;
* contradictory-evidence identification;
* experiment association;
* status determination;
* publication;
* rejection;
* retirement;
* supersession.

A hypothesis shall represent a testable learning proposition.

It shall not represent enterprise truth, an enterprise decision, or an execution instruction.

---

# Hypothesis Statuses

Every hypothesis shall possess a deterministic constitutional status, including:

* Proposed;
* Registered;
* Under Evaluation;
* Supported;
* Inconclusive;
* Rejected;
* Published;
* Retired;
* Superseded.

Status transitions shall be governed by defined evidence and lifecycle rules.

Enterprise Learning shall not present an untested or inconclusive hypothesis as established knowledge.

---

# Experiment Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own the constitutional design and execution governance of learning experiments.

Its responsibilities shall include:

* defining the experiment objective;
* identifying the hypothesis under examination;
* defining the experimental method;
* defining source datasets;
* defining features;
* defining controls;
* defining comparison groups;
* defining evaluation metrics;
* defining acceptance criteria;
* defining rejection criteria;
* defining reproducibility requirements;
* executing or invoking authorized experimental computation;
* collecting experiment evidence;
* recording results;
* determining experiment status.

Experiments shall evaluate learning propositions only.

They shall not authorize enterprise action or modify enterprise operational state.

---

# Experiment Integrity Requirements

Every experiment shall possess:

* a canonical identifier;
* a responsible owner;
* an associated hypothesis;
* defined input datasets;
* defined feature versions;
* defined model or method version;
* configuration identity;
* environment identity;
* evaluation criteria;
* randomization controls where applicable;
* deterministic seed policy where applicable;
* output artifacts;
* evidence records;
* reproducibility status;
* final disposition.

Undocumented experimentation shall possess no constitutional authority.

---

# Model Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own learning models created for enterprise learning purposes.

Model classes may include:

* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly-detection models;
* classification models;
* ranking models;
* clustering models;
* uncertainty models;
* representation models;
* explanatory models.

Enterprise Learning responsibilities shall include:

* model registration;
* model architecture definition;
* training governance;
* parameter governance;
* validation;
* comparison;
* explainability assessment;
* uncertainty assessment;
* reproducibility verification;
* publication;
* monitoring qualification;
* retirement;
* supersession.

A model shall remain a learning product.

It shall not become an enterprise decision maker solely because it generates predictions, scores, classifications, or recommendations.

---

# Model Qualification Responsibilities

Before publication, Enterprise Learning shall verify:

* intended use;
* prohibited use;
* training-data identity;
* validation-data identity;
* feature identity;
* model version;
* performance metrics;
* uncertainty;
* limitations;
* known failure modes;
* bias or representational deficiencies where applicable;
* explainability;
* reproducibility;
* environmental assumptions;
* dependency identity;
* publication readiness.

A technically executable model shall not be considered constitutionally publishable until qualification is complete.

---

# Feature Definition Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own every feature definition used in governed learning activity.

Feature responsibilities shall include:

* canonical naming;
* semantic definition;
* source-record mapping;
* calculation logic;
* transformation logic;
* unit definition;
* valid-range definition;
* temporal interpretation;
* missing-value treatment;
* uncertainty treatment;
* versioning;
* validation;
* retirement;
* supersession.

A feature definition shall be distinct from the historical source information from which it is derived.

---

# Feature Lineage Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall preserve learning lineage showing:

* source-record references;
* source-field references;
* transformation sequence;
* intermediate derived values;
* feature version;
* dataset usage;
* experiment usage;
* model usage;
* publication usage.

Enterprise Learning shall own learning lineage.

The Historian shall remain the exclusive owner of historical provenance and immutable historical custody.

Learning lineage shall reference historical provenance without modifying or replacing it.

---

# Learning Evidence Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own evidence produced by learning activity.

Learning evidence may include:

* dataset-validation evidence;
* feature-validation evidence;
* experiment evidence;
* training evidence;
* model-validation evidence;
* comparison evidence;
* uncertainty evidence;
* explainability evidence;
* reproducibility evidence;
* publication evidence;
* retirement evidence;
* supersession evidence.

Enterprise Learning shall ensure that learning evidence is sufficient to support the status and publication claims of each learning product.

---

# Learning Evidence Requirements

Every learning evidence artifact shall identify:

* the learning object supported;
* the behavior or conclusion demonstrated;
* the producing experiment or process;
* source dataset versions;
* feature versions;
* model versions;
* evaluation method;
* evaluation result;
* execution environment;
* execution timestamp;
* reproducibility status;
* custody reference;
* historical provenance references where applicable.

Learning evidence shall not be treated as historical provenance.

Historical preservation of learning evidence shall occur through the Historian interface.

---

# Publication Lifecycle Ownership

Enterprise Learning shall own the publication lifecycle of every learning product.

Learning products may include:

* hypotheses;
* qualified datasets;
* feature definitions;
* experiment conclusions;
* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly models;
* uncertainty estimates;
* explainability artifacts;
* learning advisories.

Publication responsibilities shall include:

* readiness determination;
* scope definition;
* intended-use definition;
* prohibited-use definition;
* evidence attachment;
* uncertainty disclosure;
* assumption disclosure;
* version assignment;
* publication authorization within the office;
* distribution status;
* retirement;
* withdrawal;
* supersession.

Publication shall communicate qualified knowledge.

It shall not authorize enterprise execution.

---

# Publication States

Every learning product shall possess a deterministic publication state, including:

* Draft;
* Under Review;
* Qualified;
* Published;
* Restricted;
* Withdrawn;
* Retired;
* Superseded.

Enterprise Learning shall define the evidence required for every state transition.

No unpublished product shall be represented as an approved enterprise learning product.

---

# Reproducibility Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall ensure that every published learning product possesses a declared reproducibility status.

Its responsibilities shall include:

* preserving executable methods;
* preserving dataset references;
* preserving feature definitions;
* preserving configurations;
* preserving model definitions;
* preserving evaluation procedures;
* preserving environment requirements;
* preserving expected outputs;
* preserving comparison tolerances;
* generating reproducibility evidence.

A product that cannot be reproduced shall not be published without explicit restricted status and disclosed limitation.

---

# Explainability Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall ensure that every published learning product contains explanation appropriate to its purpose and risk.

Explainability responsibilities shall include:

* identifying relevant features;
* identifying feature contributions where applicable;
* identifying assumptions;
* identifying causal limitations;
* identifying uncertainty;
* identifying known failure modes;
* distinguishing correlation from causation;
* identifying unsupported interpretations;
* defining valid and invalid use.

Explainability shall not be replaced by model-performance metrics alone.

---

# Uncertainty Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall explicitly characterize uncertainty associated with:

* source information;
* dataset construction;
* feature generation;
* experimental results;
* model estimates;
* model comparisons;
* generalization;
* causal claims;
* publication conclusions.

Uncertainty shall be preserved as part of the learning product rather than omitted from publication.

---

# Learning Product Monitoring Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall define the learning-specific monitoring requirements for published products.

These may include:

* input drift;
* feature drift;
* population drift;
* performance degradation;
* calibration degradation;
* uncertainty expansion;
* assumption invalidation;
* reproducibility loss;
* dependency obsolescence.

Enterprise Learning may evaluate whether a learning product remains qualified.

Monitoring Office authority over enterprise monitoring architecture shall remain unchanged.

---

# Retirement and Supersession Responsibility

Enterprise Learning shall retire or supersede learning products when:

* evidence becomes invalid;
* assumptions no longer hold;
* source conditions materially change;
* reproducibility is lost;
* superior products are published;
* defects are discovered;
* constitutional scope changes;
* continued use becomes misleading.

Retirement shall prohibit future authorized reliance while preserving historical existence.

Supersession shall preserve linkage between prior and replacement products.

No learning product shall be deleted to conceal prior publication.

---

# Required Enterprise Interfaces

Enterprise Learning shall perform its responsibilities through governed interfaces with:

* Historian for historical records, provenance references, journey retrieval, and historical custody;
* Commander for enterprise learning priorities and receipt of published learning products;
* Analyst for analytical products, interpretations, and comparison with learning outputs;
* Risk for risk-relevant learning products and uncertainty disclosures;
* Trader for nonbinding learning products permitted for governed operational consumption;
* Performance Truth for verified performance records and outcome measures;
* Monitoring for drift, degradation, anomaly, and operational-observation inputs;
* Librarian for publication discovery, cataloging, and governed access.

Interfaces shall not transfer constitutional ownership unless explicitly established by doctrine.

---

# Prohibited Responsibilities

Enterprise Learning shall not:

* create enterprise truth;
* certify historical truth;
* modify historical records;
* alter historical provenance;
* own historical custody;
* make enterprise decisions;
* own Decision Objects;
* command workflows;
* execute trades;
* authorize execution;
* issue risk authorization;
* certify performance truth;
* own operational monitoring;
* replace Analyst judgment;
* replace Commander authority;
* publish undocumented models;
* conceal uncertainty;
* convert correlation into an unsupported causal conclusion;
* silently alter published products;
* treat model output as self-executing authority.

Any implementation assigning these responsibilities to Enterprise Learning shall be constitutionally invalid.

---

# Separation from Truth

Enterprise Learning may derive hypotheses, estimates, patterns, forecasts, and qualified knowledge from authorized information.

It shall not declare that a learning output constitutes enterprise truth.

Truth-producing and truth-certifying authority shall remain with the constitutionally designated truth offices.

Learning products shall preserve references to truth records without absorbing their ownership.

---

# Separation from Decision Making

Enterprise Learning may publish:

* predictions;
* classifications;
* rankings;
* anomaly findings;
* causal assessments;
* uncertainty estimates;
* explanatory artifacts;
* qualified recommendations.

These outputs may inform enterprise decisions.

They shall not themselves constitute enterprise decisions.

Decision ownership shall remain with the constitutionally authorized decision owner.

---

# Separation from Workflow Execution

Enterprise Learning may initiate internal learning workflows necessary to perform its own constitutional responsibilities.

It shall not own or direct enterprise operational workflows outside its office.

A learning product shall never execute an enterprise action merely because a threshold, score, prediction, or anomaly condition has been reached.

Operational execution shall require the constitutionally authorized workflow and decision path.

---

# Separation from Historical Preservation

Enterprise Learning shall maintain working custody over active learning objects necessary to perform its responsibilities.

Permanent historical custody shall belong to the Historian.

Enterprise Learning shall submit constitutionally required learning artifacts, evidence, lifecycle events, and publication records for historical preservation.

It shall not modify those records after historical acceptance.

---

# Separation from Performance Certification

Enterprise Learning may use Performance Truth records to:

* construct labels;
* evaluate outcomes;
* train models;
* validate predictions;
* compare experiments;
* assess learning-product performance.

It shall not determine or certify the authoritative performance truth of enterprise activity.

Performance Truth shall remain the exclusive source of certified enterprise performance records.

---

# Responsibility Assignment Requirements

Every Enterprise Learning object shall identify:

* constitutional object type;
* exclusive owner;
* lifecycle authority;
* working custodian;
* historical custodian;
* publication authority;
* permitted consumers;
* prohibited consumers where applicable;
* evidence requirements;
* interface dependencies;
* retirement authority;
* supersession authority.

No object may exist with ambiguous constitutional ownership.

---

# Responsibility Conflict Resolution

When responsibility appears to overlap with another office, the implementation shall resolve the conflict by determining:

1. which office owns the underlying constitutional object;
2. which office owns the derived learning object;
3. which office possesses lifecycle authority;
4. which office possesses historical custody;
5. which office may publish the object;
6. which office may consume the object;
7. whether the output is knowledge, truth, evidence, a decision, or an execution instruction.

Unresolved responsibility conflicts shall fail closed and be escalated for constitutional resolution.

---

# Constitutional Verification

The implementation shall verify that Enterprise Learning possesses complete responsibility for:

* learning datasets;
* hypotheses;
* experiments;
* models;
* feature definitions;
* learning evidence;
* learning-product publication lifecycle.

It shall further verify that Enterprise Learning possesses no unauthorized responsibility for:

* enterprise truth;
* historical provenance;
* historical custody;
* enterprise decisions;
* operational execution;
* risk authorization;
* performance certification;
* workflow ownership outside the office.

Verification shall evaluate repository structure, runtime behavior, interface contracts, object ownership, and lifecycle authority.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be complete only when:

* every Enterprise Learning responsibility is explicitly defined;
* every governed learning object possesses one constitutional owner;
* dataset, hypothesis, experiment, model, feature, evidence, and publication responsibilities are complete;
* lifecycle authority is deterministic;
* publication authority is explicit;
* permanent historical custody remains with the Historian;
* truth authority remains outside Enterprise Learning;
* decision authority remains outside Enterprise Learning;
* operational execution authority remains outside Enterprise Learning;
* performance certification remains outside Enterprise Learning;
* no non-constitutional responsibility remains assigned to the office;
* all responsibility boundaries are independently verifiable;
* no unresolved constitutional ownership ambiguity remains.
