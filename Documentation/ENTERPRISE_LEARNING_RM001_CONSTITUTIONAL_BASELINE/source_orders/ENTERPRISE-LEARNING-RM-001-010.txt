# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-010

## Constitutional Architecture Review

### Objective

Conduct a complete constitutional architecture review of the Enterprise Learning Office.

This order shall determine whether the office possesses a complete, coherent, minimal, deterministic, and constitutionally separated architecture capable of governing enterprise learning without assuming authority belonging to truth, decision, execution, risk, performance, monitoring, or historical offices.

The review shall evaluate the architecture established under ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-001 through ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001-009.

## Mission Completeness Review

Verify that the Enterprise Learning Office possesses a complete and unambiguous constitutional mission.

The mission shall establish that Enterprise Learning exists to:

* construct learning datasets;
* define learning features;
* formulate hypotheses;
* conduct experiments;
* train and validate models;
* characterize uncertainty;
* generate explainability;
* publish learning products;
* preserve learning evidence;
* manage learning-product lifecycles;
* support authorized enterprise consumers.

Verify that the mission does not authorize the office to:

* create enterprise truth;
* own historical custody;
* alter provenance;
* make enterprise decisions;
* authorize execution;
* accept risk;
* execute trades;
* certify performance;
* own operational workflows outside learning.

Any ambiguity between learning and another constitutional authority shall constitute a review finding.

---

## Responsibility Completeness Review

Verify that every constitutional responsibility of Enterprise Learning is explicitly assigned.

The review shall determine whether responsibility exists for:

* dataset definition;
* dataset eligibility;
* dataset versioning;
* feature definition;
* feature validation;
* hypothesis management;
* experiment design;
* experiment execution;
* model development;
* model validation;
* uncertainty characterization;
* explainability generation;
* publication approval;
* consumer contract definition;
* model monitoring coordination;
* product suspension;
* product supersession;
* product retirement;
* learning evidence generation;
* reproducibility declaration.

The review shall identify:

* missing responsibilities;
* duplicate responsibilities;
* conflicting responsibilities;
* implied responsibilities;
* responsibilities without owners;
* responsibilities assigned outside constitutional authority.

Verify constitutional ownership for every object governed by Enterprise Learning.

The ownership review shall cover:

* learning datasets;

* feature definitions;
* feature versions;
* hypotheses;
* experiment definitions;
* experiment executions;
* experiment results;
* model definitions;
* trained model artifacts;
* validation results;
* uncertainty estimates;
* explainability artifacts;
* publication records;
* learning evidence;
* retirement records;
* supersession records.

For every object, verify:

* constitutional owner;
* lifecycle authority;
* creation authority;
* modification authority;
* validation authority;
* publication authority;
* retirement authority;
* custody boundary;
* historical handoff requirement.

No object shall possess ambiguous, shared, or undocumented ownership.

## Interface Completeness Review

Verify all constitutional interfaces between Enterprise Learning and:

* Historian;
* Commander;
* Analyst;
* Risk;
* Trader;
* Performance Truth;
* Monitoring;
* Librarian.

Each interface shall define:

* producing office;
* consuming office;
* object transferred;
* transfer authority;
* ownership before transfer;
* ownership after transfer;
* custody responsibility;
* validation responsibility;
* freshness requirement;
* uncertainty semantics;
* failure behavior;
* evidence requirement;
* prohibited authority transfer.

The review shall reject any interface that transfers constitutional authority implicitly.

---

## Historian Interface Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning may receive historical records from the Historian but may not:

* modify historical records;
* alter provenance;
* overwrite historical interpretations;
* assume historical custody;
* delete preserved learning products;
* redefine historical sequence.

Verify that Enterprise Learning provides the Historian with complete records of:

* datasets;
* features;
* hypotheses;
* experiments;
* models;
* validations;
* publications;
* suspensions;
* retirements;
* supersessions;
* learning evidence.

Historical provenance shall remain exclusively owned by the Historian.

---

## Commander Interface Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning may provide the Commander with:

* predictions;
* hypotheses;
* causal assessments;
* anomaly findings;
* uncertainty estimates;
* explainability artifacts;
* learning-derived recommendations.

Verify that Enterprise Learning cannot:

* own the Commander’s decision;
* issue commands;
* consume the decision baton;
* convert a model output into enterprise intent;
* bypass Commander authority.

The Commander shall retain decision ownership.

---

## Analyst Interface Review

Verify the constitutional distinction between analytical work and enterprise learning.

The review shall determine whether:

* the Analyst produces decision-specific analysis;
* Enterprise Learning produces reusable learning products;
* analytical conclusions do not become models without governed transition;
* model outputs do not replace Analyst judgment where analysis is constitutionally required;
* evidence ownership remains explicit;
* duplicated reasoning authority is prevented.

Ambiguous separation between analysis and learning shall generate a finding.

---

## Risk Interface Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning may provide Risk with:

* model outputs;
* anomaly findings;
* uncertainty;
* calibration results;
* model limitations;
* stress-response estimates.

Verify that Enterprise Learning cannot:

* approve risk;
* establish risk tolerance;
* authorize exposure;
* override Risk determinations;
* conceal model uncertainty from Risk.

Risk authority shall remain constitutionally independent.

---

## Trader Interface Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning may provide advisory learning products to the Trader only through constitutionally authorized interfaces.

Enterprise Learning shall not:

* issue trade instructions;
* select executable orders;
* authorize execution;
* alter trader mode;
* bypass Commander, Risk, or Authorization controls;
* directly control broker interaction.

Every learning product used by the Trader shall retain its advisory classification unless another constitutional office lawfully transforms it into an authorized decision.

---

## Performance Truth Interface Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning may consume certified performance records and publish learning interpretations derived from them.

Enterprise Learning shall not:

* create Performance Truth Records;
* modify certified performance;
* certify model success as enterprise performance truth;
* replace Performance Truth calculations;
* alter historical performance records.

Model evaluation and enterprise performance certification shall remain constitutionally distinct.

---

## Monitoring Interface Review

Verify that Monitoring may observe:

* model availability;
* model health;
* publication status;
* latency;
* drift indicators;
* interface failures;
* evidence-generation failures;
* declared operational thresholds.

Verify that Monitoring cannot:

* retrain models;
* redefine features;
* publish learning products;
* alter learning conclusions;
* assume Enterprise Learning ownership.

Enterprise Learning shall define learning-product health semantics while Monitoring retains monitoring authority.

---

## Librarian Interface Review

Verify that the Librarian may manage discoverability, indexing, and retrieval of published learning products without assuming:

* learning ownership;
* publication authority;
* validation authority;
* model interpretation authority;
* historical custody authority.

Enterprise Learning shall remain responsible for product meaning, restrictions, status, and consumer contracts.

* supersession behavior;
* retirement behavior;

Verify deterministic lifecycle architecture governing:

* dataset creation;
* dataset validation;
* dataset publication;
* feature generation;
* feature validation;
* hypothesis creation;
* experiment authorization;
* experiment execution;
* model training;
* model validation;
* publication;

Every lifecycle shall define:

* permitted states;
* transition authority;
* transition prerequisites;

No lifecycle state shall exist only by implementation convention.

---

## Evidence Architecture Review

Verify that every learning activity produces objective evidence sufficient to establish:

* what occurred;
* who or what performed it;
* which inputs were used;
* which version was involved;
* which assumptions applied;
* which outputs were produced;
* which validations occurred;
* which authority approved publication;
* which limitations remained;
* whether the result was reproducible.

Evidence shall demonstrate behavior rather than merely describe intent.

---

## Provenance Architecture Review

Verify the constitutional separation between:

* learning lineage owned by Enterprise Learning;
* historical provenance owned by the Historian.

Enterprise Learning may own operational lineage describing:

* dataset derivation;
* feature derivation;
* experiment inputs;
* model training inputs;
* validation relationships;
* publication dependencies.

The Historian shall own immutable preservation of those relationships as historical provenance.

Enterprise Learning shall not alter, delete, or redefine Historian-owned provenance.

---

## Explainability Completeness Review

Verify that every published learning product possesses an explanation appropriate to its intended consumer and risk.

The architecture shall require:

* supporting evidence;
* assumptions;
* confidence;
* uncertainty;
* feature contribution;
* known limitations;
* applicability scope;
* failure conditions;
* reproducibility status.

The review shall reject architectures permitting opaque publication without explicit constitutional authorization and controls.

---

## Constitutional Boundary Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning cannot:

* declare enterprise truth;
* modify history;
* alter historical provenance;
* own operational decisions;
* consume workflow authority belonging to another office;
* authorize execution;
* approve risk;
* direct trading;
* certify performance;
* suppress uncertainty;
* conceal product limitations;
* publish unsupported learning products.

Boundary enforcement shall exist through explicit architecture rather than institutional expectation.

---

## Abstraction Quality Review

Evaluate whether the architecture uses the correct constitutional abstractions.

Verify that:

* datasets are distinct from historical records;
* features are distinct from raw evidence;
* hypotheses are distinct from conclusions;
* experiments are distinct from operational workflows;
* models are distinct from decisions;
* predictions are distinct from truth;
* recommendations are distinct from commands;
* validation is distinct from certification;
* learning evidence is distinct from historical provenance;
* publication is distinct from authorization for operational use.

Improper abstraction shall generate a review finding.

---

## Constitutional Cohesion Review

Determine whether all responsibilities assigned to Enterprise Learning serve one coherent constitutional mission.

The review shall identify responsibilities that:

* do not contribute to enterprise learning;
* belong to another office;
* create operational authority;
* create historical authority;
* create truth authority;
* create performance authority;
* create decision authority.

Non-cohesive responsibilities shall be removed or reassigned through constitutional remediation.

---

## Constitutional Coupling Review

Evaluate whether Enterprise Learning depends excessively upon another office or creates excessive dependency elsewhere.

Verify that interfaces:

* transfer explicit objects;
* preserve office autonomy;
* avoid shared mutable ownership;
* avoid hidden assumptions;
* avoid circular authority;
* avoid direct execution coupling;
* preserve fail-closed behavior;
* remain independently testable.

Necessary constitutional coupling shall be explicit and minimal.

---

## Architectural Minimality Review

Verify that the architecture contains no unnecessary:

* product classes;
* ownership layers;
* approval layers;
* lifecycle states;
* interfaces;
* abstractions;
* authorities;
* evidence objects;
* duplicated responsibilities.

Minimality shall not be achieved by removing required constitutional guarantees.

The architecture shall be the simplest design capable of preserving complete learning governance.

---

## Architectural Determinism Review

Verify that identical constitutional conditions produce identical:

* ownership determinations;
* lifecycle permissions;
* publication eligibility;
* interface behavior;
* failure conclusions;
* boundary conclusions;
* retirement requirements;
* supersession requirements.

No architectural outcome shall depend upon undocumented discretion.

---

* missing constitutional definitions;
* ambiguous ownership;
* incomplete interfaces;
* lifecycle gaps;
* duplicated authority;
* contradictory requirements;
* excessive coupling;
* weak abstractions;
* non-deterministic rules;
* unenforced boundaries;

Each issue shall receive:

* unique finding identifier;
* affected order;
* constitutional consequence;
* severity;
* required remediation;
* disposition authority.

---

## Review Classification

Classify every finding as:

* Critical Constitutional Defect;
* Major Architectural Defect;
* Moderate Architectural Defect;
* Minor Clarification;
* Documentation Deficiency;
* No Defect.

Any unresolved Critical Constitutional Defect shall prohibit progression.

Any unresolved Major Architectural Defect shall require explicit constitutional disposition before progression.

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* the Enterprise Learning mission is complete and unambiguous;
* all constitutional responsibilities possess explicit owners;
* every learning object possesses complete ownership and lifecycle authority;
* all enterprise interfaces preserve constitutional boundaries;
* all learning-product classes are fully governed;
* all learning lifecycles are deterministic;
* evidence ownership is complete;
* historical provenance remains exclusively owned by the Historian;
* explainability requirements apply to every published learning product;
* truth, decision, execution, risk, trading, performance, monitoring, and historical authorities remain separate;
* the architecture demonstrates high constitutional cohesion;
* constitutional coupling is explicit and minimal;
* abstractions are correctly defined;
* no undocumented discretionary authority remains;
* all critical and major findings are resolved or constitutionally dispositioned;
* a formal readiness determination for ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001 has been issued.
