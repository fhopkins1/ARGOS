# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-003-003

# Independent Proof Lineage Validation

## Objective

Independently validate complete, deterministic, bidirectional proof lineage for every proof object regenerated under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-003-002**.

Validate the complete lineage chain:

```text
Constitution
        │
        ▼
Canonical Requirement
        │
        ▼
Implementation Obligation
        │
        ▼
Implementation Artifact
        │
        ▼
Verifier
        │
        ▼
Fixture
        │
        ▼
Execution
        │
        ▼
Evidence
        │
        ▼
Proof
```

This order shall determine whether every regenerated proof possesses complete and admissible lineage from constitutional authority through independently regenerated execution evidence.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur except where strictly required to confirm missing or ambiguous lineage evidence.

No mutation campaign shall occur.

No traceability architecture shall be regenerated under this order.

No certification verdict shall be issued.

---

## Authoritative Inputs

Accept as authoritative inputs:

* the constitutional architecture established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001**;
* the implementation candidate established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002**;
* the independently discovered implementation inventory established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-002**;
* the independently reproduced behavioral execution population;
* the regenerated Evidence Registry;
* the regenerated Evidence Identity Registry;
* the regenerated Evidence Provenance Registry;
* the regenerated Evidence Integrity Registry;
* the regenerated Proof Registry;
* the regenerated Requirement Proof Registry;
* the regenerated Proof Findings Registry.

Prior proof-lineage reports, historical traceability graphs, manually prepared mappings, previous certification conclusions, and inherited proof relationships shall not serve as authoritative lineage evidence.

---

## Lineage Model

Represent each proof lineage as a deterministic directed graph containing the following canonical node classes:

* CONSTITUTIONAL_AUTHORITY;
* CANONICAL_REQUIREMENT;
* IMPLEMENTATION_OBLIGATION;
* IMPLEMENTATION_ARTIFACT;
* VERIFIER;
* FIXTURE;
* EXECUTION;
* EVIDENCE;
* PROOF.

Represent lineage relationships using, at minimum:

* CONSTITUTION_GOVERNS_REQUIREMENT;
* REQUIREMENT_CREATES_OBLIGATION;
* OBLIGATION_BINDS_IMPLEMENTATION;
* IMPLEMENTATION_IS_VERIFIED_BY;
* VERIFIER_USES_FIXTURE;
* VERIFIER_AND_FIXTURE_PRODUCE_EXECUTION;
* EXECUTION_PRODUCES_EVIDENCE;
* EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_PROOF;
* PROOF_DISPOSES_REQUIREMENT.

Every node and relationship shall possess deterministic identity.

---

## Node Validation

For every lineage node, verify:

* canonical identity;
* node class;
* authoritative source;
* existence;
* uniqueness;
* ownership;
* provenance;
* content integrity;
* admissibility;
* current status.

Reject any node that is:

* absent;
* duplicated without justification;
* inferred from a summary;
* inherited from prior certification artifacts;
* manually synthesized;
* unsupported by authoritative source material;
* identified only by filename or directory location;
* represented solely by a completion report or registry declaration.

---

## Relationship Validation

For every lineage relationship, verify:

* originating node identity;
* destination node identity;
* relationship type;
* relationship authority;
* derivation method;
* provenance;
* admissibility;
* uniqueness;
* directionality;
* constitutional relevance.

A relationship shall not be accepted merely because both endpoint nodes exist.

Each relationship shall possess objective support establishing why the originating node validly connects to the destination node.

---

## Forward Lineage Validation

For every canonical constitutional requirement, traverse lineage forward through:

1. constitutional authority;
2. canonical requirement;
3. implementation obligation;
4. implementation artifact;
5. verifier;
6. fixture;
7. execution;
8. evidence;
9. proof.

Verify that every required stage exists and that each transition is supported by admissible provenance.

Identify:

* requirements without implementation obligations;
* obligations without implementation artifacts;
* implementation artifacts without verifiers;
* verifiers without fixtures where fixtures are required;
* verifiers or fixtures without executions;
* executions without evidence;
* evidence without proof;
* requirements without exactly one proof disposition.

A forward lineage path shall be complete only when it terminates in the proof object disposing the originating requirement.

---

## Backward Lineage Validation

For every proof object, traverse lineage backward through:

1. proof;
2. evidence;
3. execution;
4. fixture;
5. verifier;
6. implementation artifact;
7. implementation obligation;
8. canonical requirement;
9. constitutional authority.

Verify that every proof can be traced to:

* admissible regenerated evidence;
* an independently reproduced execution;
* the governing verifier;
* the governing fixture where applicable;
* the implementation artifact exercised;
* the implementation obligation tested;
* the exact canonical requirement disposed;
* the governing constitutional authority.

Any proof that cannot be traced completely backward shall be classified as an orphan or broken-lineage proof.

---

## Proof-Disposition Lineage Requirements

### PROVEN

A PROVEN disposition shall possess complete lineage through every applicable stage.

The supporting evidence shall:

* originate from an independently reproduced execution;
* be admissible;
* possess verified integrity;
* directly support the requirement;
* be produced by the identified verifier and fixture;
* exercise the identified implementation artifact;
* demonstrate the identified implementation obligation.

A PROVEN disposition shall fail lineage validation if any required stage is absent, inferred, inherited, aggregate, or unsupported.

### NOT_PROVEN

A NOT_PROVEN disposition shall possess lineage to objective evidence or findings demonstrating why proof could not be established.

Validate lineage through:

* canonical requirement;
* implementation obligation;
* affected implementation artifact;
* verifier or missing-verifier finding;
* fixture or missing-fixture finding;
* execution or execution-gap finding;
* evidence or evidence-gap finding;
* Proof Findings Registry entry;
* NOT_PROVEN proof object.

NOT_PROVEN shall not be treated as absence of lineage.

### NOT_APPLICABLE

A NOT_APPLICABLE disposition shall possess lineage to explicit constitutional authority establishing inapplicability.

Validate:

* the canonical requirement;
* the relevant constitutional scope rule;
* implementation-scope evidence;
* the written justification;
* the approving authority where defined;
* the NOT_APPLICABLE proof object.

Implementation absence, verifier absence, fixture absence, execution failure, or missing evidence shall not establish NOT_APPLICABLE lineage.

---

## Evidence-to-Proof Validation

For every evidence-to-proof relationship, verify:

* evidence identity exists in the Evidence Registry;
* evidence integrity is valid;
* evidence provenance is complete;
* evidence was regenerated under the current clean-room campaign;
* evidence directly supports the proof disposition;
* evidence belongs to the identified execution;
* execution belongs to the identified verifier and fixture;
* evidence has not been copied from prior certification packages;
* evidence has not been reconstructed from reports;
* evidence is not used circularly to validate itself.

Where one evidence artifact supports multiple requirements, require explicit constitutional justification demonstrating that the same execution independently establishes each requirement.

Unjustified evidence reuse shall produce a lineage finding.

---

## Implementation Lineage Validation

For every implementation artifact represented in proof lineage, verify that participation was established through dependency-derived discovery.

Do not accept participation inferred solely from:

* filenames;
* directory names;
* comments;
* documentation;
* naming conventions;
* manually maintained implementation inventories;
* prior traceability mappings.

Validate that each implementation artifact is connected to the relevant obligation through objective evidence such as:

* runtime invocation;
* imports;
* object construction;
* dependency relationships;
* persistence participation;
* schema participation;
* event production;
* event consumption;
* evidence generation.

---

## Verifier and Fixture Lineage Validation

For every verifier and fixture relationship, verify:

* verifier identity exists in the Verifier Registry;
* fixture identity exists in the Fixture Registry;
* the verifier actually consumed the fixture;
* the execution record identifies both;
* the raw execution evidence supports the relationship;
* the fixture state was applicable to the requirement;
* the verifier asserted the relevant behavior.

Framework collection, fixture declaration, parameter naming, or static proximity shall not independently establish participation.

---

## Execution Lineage Validation

For every execution represented in lineage, verify:

* deterministic execution identity;
* repository identity;
* environment identity;
* configuration identity;
* verifier identity;
* fixture identity;
* requirement identity;
* execution disposition;
* raw evidence references;
* integrity hashes.

Reject lineage to:

* planned but unexecuted verification;
* skipped verification;
* deselected verification;
* overwritten execution evidence;
* historical execution;
* execution lacking deterministic identity;
* execution whose raw evidence cannot be located or validated.

---

## Unique Proof Ownership

Verify that every proof object disposes exactly one canonical requirement.

Verify that every canonical requirement is disposed by exactly one proof object.

Identify:

* duplicate proof ownership;
* multiple proof objects disposing one requirement;
* one proof object disposing multiple unrelated requirements;
* proof objects without requirements;
* requirements without proof objects;
* conflicting proof dispositions.

Shared supporting evidence may be permitted only where constitutionally justified, but proof ownership shall remain requirement-specific.

---

## Circular-Lineage Detection

Detect and reject all circular lineage, including:

* proof supporting its own evidence;
* proof validating its own requirement mapping;
* registry declarations serving as evidence for the records they contain;
* traceability artifacts serving as proof of their own correctness;
* completion reports proving completion;
* inferred relationships justified by downstream proof;
* requirement mappings derived solely from existing proof objects;
* evidence admissibility established solely by the proof it supports.

Every accepted lineage path shall form an acyclic directed path from constitutional authority to proof.

---

## Orphan Detection

Identify every:

* orphan constitutional requirement;
* orphan implementation obligation;
* orphan implementation artifact;
* orphan verifier;
* orphan fixture;
* orphan execution;
* orphan evidence artifact;
* orphan proof object;
* orphan lineage relationship.

An orphan is any required node or relationship that cannot participate in a complete admissible forward or backward lineage path.

Non-participating artifacts shall be explicitly classified and justified rather than silently excluded.

---

* BROKEN_FORWARD_LINEAGE;
* BROKEN_BACKWARD_LINEAGE;
* ORPHAN_NODE;
* ORPHAN_RELATIONSHIP;
* DUPLICATE_PROOF_OWNERSHIP;
* CONFLICTING_PROOF_DISPOSITION;
* UNJUSTIFIED_EVIDENCE_REUSE;
* CIRCULAR_LINEAGE;
* NONDETERMINISTIC_LINEAGE;
* PROVENANCE_FAILURE;
* IDENTITY_CONFLICT;
* INTEGRITY_FAILURE.

Each defect shall produce an objective lineage finding.

---

## Deterministic Lineage Identity

Assign deterministic identities to lineage paths and relationships.

A lineage-path identity shall derive from, at minimum:

* constitutional authority identity;
* requirement identity;
* implementation obligation identity;
* implementation artifact identity;
* verifier identity;
* fixture identity or justified fixture-null identity;
* execution identity;
* evidence identity;
* proof identity.

Machine-specific paths, timestamps, random identifiers, and traversal order shall not independently determine canonical lineage identity.

## Reconciliation Requirements

Reconcile:

* constitutional requirements against proof objects;
* proof objects against evidence;
* evidence against executions;
* executions against verifiers and fixtures;
* verifiers against implementation artifacts;
* implementation artifacts against implementation obligations;
* implementation obligations against canonical requirements;
* canonical requirements against constitutional authority;
* forward lineage against backward lineage;
* lineage registry entries against source registries;
* lineage findings against every incomplete or invalid path.

Every discrepancy shall produce an explicit finding.

---

## Prohibited Actions

Do not:

* create missing implementation mappings manually;
* infer proof lineage from naming conventions;
* inherit prior lineage relationships;
* use prior traceability graphs as authoritative inputs;
* synthesize evidence-to-proof relationships;
* convert aggregate behavioral success into requirement-level lineage;
* treat registry presence as proof of validity;
* suppress orphan nodes;
* suppress circular relationships;
* merge unrelated requirements into one proof;
* alter proof dispositions to make lineage complete;
* modify implementation, verifier, fixture, execution, evidence, or proof artifacts;
* issue a certification verdict.

---

## Fail-Closed Requirements

This order shall fail closed when:

* any canonical requirement lacks exactly one proof object;
* any PROVEN proof lacks complete admissible lineage;
* any NOT_PROVEN proof lacks objective finding lineage;
* any NOT_APPLICABLE proof lacks constitutional justification lineage;
* any lineage node lacks deterministic identity;
* any required relationship lacks objective provenance;
* any orphan node or relationship remains unresolved;
* duplicate proof ownership exists;
* conflicting proof dispositions exist;
* unjustified evidence reuse exists;
* circular lineage exists;
* forward and backward lineage do not reconcile;
* lineage depends upon prior certification artifacts or manually assigned mappings.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall complete only when:

* every canonical requirement possesses exactly one proof object;
* every proof object possesses deterministic identity and unique requirement ownership;
* every PROVEN proof possesses complete forward and backward lineage to admissible regenerated execution evidence;
* every NOT_PROVEN proof possesses complete lineage to objective findings;
* every NOT_APPLICABLE proof possesses complete lineage to explicit constitutional justification;
* every lineage node and relationship possesses deterministic identity and provenance;
* every implementation artifact relationship originates from dependency-derived discovery;
* every verifier, fixture, execution, and evidence relationship is supported by objective records;
* all forward lineage reconciles with backward lineage;
* no orphan proof, evidence, execution, fixture, verifier, implementation artifact, obligation, or requirement remains;
* no duplicate, conflicting, inferred, inherited, aggregate, synthesized, or circular lineage remains;
* every lineage defect has been recorded in the Lineage Findings Registry;
* all required registries and reports have been generated deterministically;
* no implementation or regenerated evidence or proof artifact has been modified;
* no traceability-regeneration or certification verdict has been issued.
