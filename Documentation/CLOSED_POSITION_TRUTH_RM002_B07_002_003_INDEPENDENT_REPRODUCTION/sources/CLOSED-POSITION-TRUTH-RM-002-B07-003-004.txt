# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-003-004

# Independent Traceability Regeneration

## Objective

Regenerate the complete implementation traceability architecture exclusively from independently regenerated proof, independently regenerated evidence, and independently reproduced behavioral execution.

The purpose of this order is to demonstrate that every traceability relationship can be objectively reconstructed from clean-room execution without reliance upon historical traceability graphs, manually maintained mappings, prior certification artifacts, or inferred relationships.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur except where constitutionally required to regenerate missing evidence.

No mutation campaign shall occur.

No certification verdict shall be issued.

---

## Regeneration Activities

### Constitutional Traceability Reconstruction

Regenerate every constitutional relationship between:

* constitutional authority;
* canonical requirement;
* implementation obligation.

Verify that each relationship possesses:

* constitutional authority;
* deterministic identity;
* objective provenance;
* admissible ownership.

Identify every missing or ambiguous constitutional relationship.

---

### Implementation Traceability Reconstruction

Regenerate complete implementation traceability linking:

* implementation obligations;
* implementation artifacts;
* runtime participants;
* behavioral verifiers;
* behavioral fixtures.

Verify that implementation participation is supported exclusively by independently reproduced discovery evidence.

---

### Behavioral Traceability Reconstruction

Regenerate complete behavioral traceability connecting:

* verifiers;
* fixtures;
* executions;
* execution evidence;
* behavioral findings.

Verify that every execution possesses deterministic linkage to its governing implementation obligation.

Reject inferred or manually assigned behavioral relationships.

---

### Proof Traceability Reconstruction

Regenerate complete proof traceability linking:

* evidence;
* proof objects;
* canonical requirements.

Verify that every proof object possesses:

* admissible regenerated evidence;
* deterministic execution provenance;
* unique ownership;
* objective constitutional authority.

No proof shall exist without regenerated traceability.

---

### Forward and Backward Traceability Validation

For every regenerated relationship verify:

* complete forward traversal;
* complete backward traversal;
* deterministic identity;
* unique ownership;
* reproducible provenance.

Confirm that every endpoint can be reached in both directions without ambiguity.

---

* orphan verifiers;
* orphan fixtures;

## Completion Criteria

This order shall complete only when:

* complete forward traceability has been regenerated;
* complete backward traceability has been regenerated;
* every constitutional relationship possesses deterministic provenance;
* every implementation obligation is traceable to governing authority;
* every implementation artifact is traceable to its governing obligation;
* every verifier, fixture, execution, evidence, and proof possesses complete traceability;
* every orphan relationship has been identified and documented;
* every circular traceability relationship has been identified and classified;
* no inferred, inherited, manually assigned, or undocumented traceability relationship remains;
* complete traceability regeneration has been objectively demonstrated.

Successful completion of this order authorizes progression to:
