### ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002-008

# Knowledge Publication Runtime

## Purpose

Implement deterministic executable runtime behavior governing the publication of Enterprise Learning knowledge products.

The runtime shall ensure that every published learning artifact is reproducible, explainable, uncertainty-aware, fully traceable, constitutionally compliant, and explicitly advisory.

Published knowledge shall inform enterprise decision-making but shall never constitute enterprise truth or authorize enterprise action.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The Knowledge Publication Runtime shall:

* implement deterministic publication behavior;
* preserve constitutional ownership;
* publish only certified learning products;
* preserve complete provenance;
* generate objective behavioral evidence;
* preserve explainability;
* preserve uncertainty reporting;
* remain advisory-only;
* fail closed upon constitutional violations.

Publication authority shall never imply operational authority.

---

# Published Knowledge Products

The runtime shall support publication of:

* hypotheses;
* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly models;
* uncertainty reports;
* explainability reports.

No additional publication classes shall be introduced without constitutional authorization.

---

# Publication Eligibility Runtime

Before publication the runtime shall verify that every knowledge product possesses:

* unique identifier;
* constitutional ownership;
* successful behavioral certification;
* complete provenance;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty assessment;
* supporting evidence references;
* publication authorization.

Products failing any prerequisite shall not be published.

---

# Publication Package Runtime

Every publication package shall include:

* publication identifier;
* publication version;
* publication timestamp;
* knowledge product type;
* executive summary;
* methodology summary;
* supporting hypotheses;
* supporting experiments;
* supporting datasets;
* supporting features;
* supporting models;
* explainability references;
* uncertainty assessment;
* provenance references;
* behavioral evidence references;
* advisory statement.

Publication packages shall become immutable following release.

---

# Advisory Runtime

Every published product shall explicitly state:

* recommendations are advisory;
* predictions are probabilistic;
* uncertainty accompanies all conclusions;
* enterprise truth remains externally governed;
* operational authority remains outside Enterprise Learning.

Published knowledge shall never issue enterprise decisions.

---

# Explainability Integration

Every publication shall reference executable explainability artifacts including:

* feature importance;
* supporting observations;
* evidence references;
* competing explanations;
* model assumptions;
* confidence estimates;
* uncertainty sources.

Publications lacking explainability shall not be released.

---

# Uncertainty Runtime

Every publication shall disclose:

* confidence estimates;
* statistical uncertainty;
* model limitations;
* data limitations;
* feature limitations;
* experimental limitations;
* known biases;
* competing hypotheses.

Uncertainty disclosure shall never be optional.

---

# Version Management Runtime

The runtime shall manage:

* publication creation;
* publication revision;
* publication supersession;
* publication retirement;
* historical preservation.

Previous publications shall remain permanently accessible.

---

# Supersession Runtime

When newer knowledge supersedes existing publications the runtime shall:

* preserve historical versions;
* establish supersession relationships;
* identify replacement publications;
* preserve evidence continuity;
* prevent historical alteration.

Supersession shall never destroy historical knowledge.

---

# Distribution Runtime

The runtime shall distribute only immutable publication packages.

Recipients shall receive:

* publication content;
* explainability references;
* uncertainty reports;
* provenance references;
* evidence references;
* advisory notices.

Distribution shall preserve publication integrity.

---

# Constitutional Restrictions

The runtime shall prohibit publication of:

* enterprise decisions;
* operational commands;
* workflow authorizations;
* truth declarations;
* policy modifications;
* constitutional modifications;
* ownership transfers.

Knowledge publication shall never assume executive authority.

---

# Interface Requirements

The Knowledge Publication Runtime shall exchange information only through constitutional interfaces with:

* Hypothesis Runtime;
* Model Lifecycle Runtime;
* Explainability Runtime;
* Learning Provenance Runtime;
* Behavioral Evidence Runtime;
* Historian;
* Performance Truth.

No direct modification of external constitutional objects shall occur.

---

# Behavioral Evidence

Every publication shall generate immutable evidence documenting:

* publication eligibility verification;
* publication package generation;
* explainability verification;
* uncertainty verification;
* provenance verification;
* publication release;
* publication supersession;
* publication retirement.

Evidence shall support independent constitutional certification.

---

# Audit Requirements

The runtime shall demonstrate:

* deterministic publication behavior;
* complete publication traceability;
* immutable publication records;
* advisory-only operation;
* complete explainability integration;
* mandatory uncertainty disclosure;
* preserved provenance;
* independently certifiable behavioral evidence.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* every published knowledge product is deterministic and reproducible;
* every publication contains complete explainability and uncertainty information;
* no publication asserts enterprise truth or operational authority;
* publication lineage remains fully traceable;
* historical publications remain immutable after supersession;
* every publication emits independently certifiable behavioral evidence;
* independent auditors can certify publication behavior without developer interpretation.
