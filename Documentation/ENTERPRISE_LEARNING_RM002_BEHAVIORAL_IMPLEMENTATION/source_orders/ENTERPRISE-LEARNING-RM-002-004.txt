# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002-004

## Hypothesis Runtime

### Objective

Implement deterministic executable runtime behavior governing the complete constitutional lifecycle of enterprise learning hypotheses from creation through retirement while preserving advisory-only authority, scientific reproducibility, explainability, uncertainty reporting, and constitutional separation from Enterprise Truth.

No runtime behavior implemented under this order shall permit Enterprise Learning to establish, modify, or certify enterprise truth.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The runtime implementation shall execute deterministic behavioral workflows governing:

* hypothesis creation;
* hypothesis registration;
* supporting evidence association;
* competing hypothesis management;
* confidence estimation;
* uncertainty estimation;
* experimental validation;
* hypothesis revision;
* hypothesis supersession;
* hypothesis acceptance;
* hypothesis rejection;
* publication;
* retirement.

Every state transition shall be deterministic, observable, reproducible, explainable, and independently auditable.

---

# Runtime Lifecycle

Every hypothesis shall progress through deterministic constitutional states.

Minimum lifecycle states shall include:

* Proposed;
* Registered;
* Under Investigation;
* Evidence Collection;
* Experimental Validation;
* Confidence Assessment;
* Advisory Publication;
* Superseded;
* Rejected;
* Retired.

State transitions shall require constitutional validation before execution.

Illegal transitions shall fail closed.

---

# Hypothesis Registration

Runtime behavior shall register every hypothesis using immutable constitutional identifiers.

Registration shall record:

* hypothesis identifier;
* originating workflow;
* originating experiment;
* originating dataset versions;
* feature versions;
* model versions, when applicable;
* creation timestamp;
* originating operator;
* constitutional authority;
* initial assumptions.

Registration records shall be immutable.

---

# Supporting Evidence Association

Runtime behavior shall associate every hypothesis with objective supporting evidence.

Evidence associations shall include:

* experimental observations;
* statistical measurements;
* datasets;
* feature sets;
* generated models;
* reproducibility evidence;
* uncertainty measurements;
* explainability artifacts.

Unsupported hypotheses shall fail publication.

---

# Competing Hypothesis Management

Runtime behavior shall permit multiple competing hypotheses to exist simultaneously.

Implementation shall:

* preserve independent evidence;
* preserve independent confidence estimates;
* prevent forced convergence;
* prevent premature elimination;
* preserve historical competition.

Selection of one hypothesis shall never delete competing alternatives.

---

# Confidence Estimation

Runtime behavior shall generate deterministic confidence estimates using constitutionally approved methodologies.

Confidence calculations shall be reproducible.

Generated confidence shall include:

* statistical confidence;
* validation coverage;
* supporting observation count;
* reproducibility metrics;
* evidence completeness.

Confidence values shall never be manually overridden.

---

# Uncertainty Estimation

Runtime behavior shall calculate objective uncertainty measurements for every hypothesis.

Uncertainty shall identify:

* insufficient evidence;
* conflicting evidence;
* limited observations;
* feature instability;
* model uncertainty;
* sampling limitations;
* experimental limitations.

Uncertainty shall always accompany published confidence.

---

# Experimental Validation

Runtime behavior shall evaluate hypotheses only through constitutionally approved experimental execution.

Validation shall verify:

* reproducibility;
* statistical significance;
* repeatability;
* evidence consistency;
* deterministic execution.

Validation failures shall prevent advancement to publication.

---

# Hypothesis Revision

Runtime behavior shall support controlled hypothesis revision.

Every revision shall:

* create a new immutable version;
* preserve previous versions;
* preserve supporting evidence;
* preserve historical reasoning;
* preserve experimental history.

Previous versions shall never be overwritten.

---

# Acceptance Behavior

Acceptance shall indicate only that currently available evidence supports the hypothesis within documented confidence and uncertainty limits.

Acceptance shall never constitute:

* enterprise truth;
* operational authorization;
* constitutional policy;
* enterprise decision.

Accepted hypotheses shall remain advisory.

---

# Rejection Behavior

Rejected hypotheses shall remain permanently preserved.

Runtime behavior shall record:

* rejection rationale;
* supporting evidence;
* experimental results;
* uncertainty measurements;
* responsible workflow.

Rejected hypotheses shall remain historically reproducible.

---

# Supersession Behavior

Runtime behavior shall support hypothesis supersession without historical deletion.

Supersession shall preserve:

* predecessor relationships;
* successor relationships;
* supporting evidence;
* reasoning;
* experimental lineage.

Complete supersession chains shall remain reconstructable.

---

# Publication Behavior

Published hypotheses shall include:

* hypothesis statement;
* supporting evidence;
* confidence estimates;
* uncertainty estimates;
* competing hypotheses;
* explainability references;
* provenance references;
* constitutional disclaimer indicating advisory-only status.

Publication shall fail if any required artifact is absent.

---

# Constitutional Separation

Runtime behavior shall enforce complete separation between Enterprise Learning and Enterprise Truth.

Hypotheses shall never:

* establish truth;
* authorize action;
* modify enterprise state;
* approve workflows;
* replace operational decisions.

Runtime enforcement shall fail closed upon violation.

---

# Runtime Evidence Generation

Every hypothesis lifecycle event shall generate immutable behavioral evidence including:

* lifecycle transition;
* validation outcome;
* confidence calculation;
* uncertainty calculation;
* evidence association;
* publication event;
* supersession event;
* rejection event;
* retirement event.

Evidence shall support independent ECS-003 certification.

---

# Behavioral Verification

Implementation verification shall demonstrate deterministic execution for:

* hypothesis registration;
* lifecycle transitions;
* competing hypotheses;
* confidence estimation;
* uncertainty estimation;
* experimental validation;
* revision;
* acceptance;
* rejection;
* supersession;
* publication;
* retirement.

Verification shall demonstrate identical outputs for identical constitutional inputs.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* every constitutional hypothesis responsibility executes deterministically;
* lifecycle transitions are fully observable;
* confidence and uncertainty are objectively generated;
* competing hypotheses are constitutionally preserved;
* advisory-only authority is continuously enforced;
* enterprise truth remains constitutionally isolated;
* immutable behavioral evidence is generated for every lifecycle event;
* runtime behavior is independently reproducible;
* implementation satisfies Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 behavioral implementation requirements.
