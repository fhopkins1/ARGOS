### ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002-007

# Learning Provenance Runtime

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This implementation order establishes the executable runtime behavior governing learning provenance within the Enterprise Learning Office.

The purpose of this runtime is to ensure that every learning artifact possesses complete, immutable, deterministic, and independently certifiable provenance from its originating datasets through publication and retirement.

Learning provenance exists to explain how knowledge products were produced.

It does not establish enterprise truth, authorize operational decisions, or assume historical ownership.

This implementation governs runtime behavior only.

Nothing within this order modifies constitutional responsibilities established under:

* ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001;
* ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001.

# Runtime Objectives

The Learning Provenance Runtime shall execute deterministic behavior governing:

* learning provenance registration;
* dataset lineage;
* feature lineage;
* experiment lineage;
* hypothesis lineage;
* model lineage;
* publication lineage;
* provenance verification;
* provenance retrieval;
* provenance archival.

Every provenance record shall be immutable.

---

# Constitutional Ownership

The Enterprise Learning Office shall generate provenance describing learning activities.

The Historian Office shall remain the exclusive constitutional owner of enterprise historical records.

The Learning Provenance Runtime shall never:

* modify historical records;
* rewrite provenance history;
* certify enterprise truth;
* assume Historian authority.

Historical custody remains exclusively with the Historian.

---

# Provenance Lifecycle

Every provenance record shall progress through the following lifecycle:

1. Registration
2. Identity Assignment
3. Relationship Discovery
4. Dependency Verification
5. Lineage Construction
6. Evidence Association
7. Integrity Verification
8. Publication
9. Historical Preservation
10. Retrieval

Lifecycle progression shall be deterministic.

---

# Provenance Registration

The runtime shall register provenance for every:

* dataset;
* feature;
* experiment;
* hypothesis;
* model;
* explainability artifact;
* uncertainty report;
* publication.

No publishable learning artifact shall exist without provenance.

---

# Dataset Lineage

Dataset provenance shall record:

* originating sources;
* acquisition workflow;
* certification status;
* dataset version;
* preprocessing operations;
* validation evidence;
* publication history;
* retirement history.

Every dataset dependency shall remain traceable.

---

# Feature Lineage

Feature provenance shall record:

* originating datasets;
* transformation sequence;
* dependency graph;
* feature version;
* execution workflow;
* validation evidence;
* publication history.

Feature derivations shall remain completely reconstructable.

---

# Experiment Lineage

Experiment provenance shall record:

* experiment definition;
* participating datasets;
* generated features;
* execution configuration;
* runtime parameters;
* execution environment;
* observations;
* evidence references;
* completion status.

Every experimental result shall remain reproducible.

---

# Hypothesis Lineage

Hypothesis provenance shall record:

* originating experiments;
* supporting observations;
* competing hypotheses;
* confidence estimates;
* uncertainty assessments;
* acceptance history;
* rejection history;
* supersession history.

Hypothesis evolution shall remain observable.

---

# Model Lineage

Model provenance shall record:

* training datasets;
* feature versions;
* experiment references;
* training configuration;
* validation results;
* explainability reports;
* uncertainty reports;
* publication history;
* retirement history.

Every published model shall possess complete lineage.

---

# Publication Lineage

Publication provenance shall record:

* published artifact;
* publication identifier;
* publication timestamp;
* supporting evidence;
* originating lineage;
* publication workflow;
* publication version;
* publication status.

Every published artifact shall remain historically traceable.

---

# Relationship Graph

The runtime shall construct immutable relationships linking:

* datasets;
* features;
* experiments;
* hypotheses;
* models;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty reports;
* publications.

Relationship graphs shall remain deterministic.

---

# Provenance Integrity Verification

The runtime shall verify:

* dependency completeness;
* lineage continuity;
* relationship consistency;
* evidence availability;
* version consistency;
* ownership compliance;
* historical references.

Integrity failures shall fail closed.

---

# Provenance Retrieval

Authorized workflows shall retrieve provenance by:

* artifact identifier;
* dataset identifier;
* feature identifier;
* experiment identifier;
* hypothesis identifier;
* model identifier;
* publication identifier;
* lineage relationship.

Retrieved provenance shall remain read-only.

---

# Historical Preservation

Historical provenance shall:

* remain immutable;
* preserve all versions;
* preserve relationships;
* preserve evidence references;
* preserve execution identity;
* preserve publication history.

No provenance record shall be deleted.

---

# Runtime Evidence

Every provenance execution shall generate immutable evidence including:

* provenance identifier;
* artifact identifier;
* lineage graph identifier;
* dependency verification results;
* integrity verification results;
* evidence references;
* publication status;
* execution timestamps;
* runtime status.

Evidence shall support independent certification.

---

# Failure Handling

Execution shall fail closed upon detection of:

* missing lineage;
* broken dependencies;
* incomplete provenance;
* inconsistent versions;
* missing evidence;
* ownership violations;
* relationship corruption;
* historical modification attempts.

All failures shall generate complete diagnostic evidence.

---

# Runtime Interfaces

The Learning Provenance Runtime may consume:

* certified datasets;
* feature records;
* experiment records;
* hypothesis records;
* model records;
* publication records.

The runtime may publish:

* provenance records;
* lineage graphs;
* relationship metadata;
* provenance evidence;
* verification results.

The runtime shall never publish enterprise truth or operational decisions.

---

# Behavioral Evidence Requirements

Behavioral evidence shall demonstrate:

* deterministic provenance generation;
* complete lineage construction;
* immutable relationship graphs;
* integrity verification;
* constitutional ownership preservation;
* independent reproducibility;
* complete historical traceability.

Evidence shall satisfy Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 behavioral certification requirements.

---

# Completion Criteria

This implementation order shall be considered complete when:

* every learning artifact receives immutable provenance;
* dataset, feature, experiment, hypothesis, model, and publication lineage execute deterministically;
* relationship graphs are complete and reproducible;
* provenance integrity verification executes automatically;
* constitutional ownership remains preserved between the Enterprise Learning Office and the Historian Office;
* behavioral evidence is generated for every provenance operation; and
* independent auditors can reconstruct the complete lineage of every published learning artifact using only the submitted repository, execution procedures, and generated evidence.
