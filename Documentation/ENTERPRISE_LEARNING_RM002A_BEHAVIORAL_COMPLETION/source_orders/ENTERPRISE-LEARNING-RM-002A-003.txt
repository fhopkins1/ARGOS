### **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002A-003**

## Baseline Equivalence Implementation Campaign

### Purpose

This order implements the executable runtime capability required to perform deterministic comparison between regenerated behavioral artifacts and their declared certification baselines.

The objective is to demonstrate that independently regenerated outputs are objectively equivalent to approved certification baselines under Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-004.

No behavioral capability shall be modified by this order.

---

## Implementation Requirements

Implement a deterministic **Baseline Equivalence Engine** capable of comparing every regenerated certification artifact against its declared baseline.

The implementation shall support comparison of:

* behavioral evidence;
* certification evidence;
* implementation reports;
* explainability reports;
* provenance reports;
* reproducibility reports;
* runtime summaries;
* audit artifacts;
* completion evidence;
* machine-readable certification outputs.

Every comparison shall execute automatically.

No manual comparison shall be required.

---

## Canonical Baseline Registry

Implement a canonical baseline registry.

Every registered baseline shall possess immutable metadata including:

* baseline identifier;
* artifact identifier;
* originating implementation;
* certification version;
* generation timestamp;
* producing workflow;
* evidence hash;
* schema version;
* provenance identifier;
* approval status.

Unregistered baselines shall fail closed.

---

## Artifact Identity Verification

Implement executable verification that regenerated artifacts match declared certification artifacts.

Verification shall include:

* identifier equality;
* artifact classification;
* workflow ownership;
* producing runtime;
* schema identity;
* evidence identity.

Identity failures shall terminate certification.

---

## Structural Equivalence

Implement deterministic structural comparison verifying:

* required fields;
* object hierarchy;
* collection completeness;
* mandatory sections;
* schema conformance;
* serialization consistency.

Structural inequality shall produce objective failure evidence.

---

## Normalized Equivalence

Implement normalization before comparison.

Normalization shall remove constitutionally permitted nondeterministic elements including:

* ordering differences;
* formatting differences;
* insignificant whitespace;
* canonical serialization differences;
* approved metadata normalization.

Normalization shall never alter behavioral meaning.

---

## Deterministic Equivalence

Implement executable verification that regenerated behavioral outputs are deterministically equivalent to certification baselines.

Verification shall include:

* behavioral outputs;
* execution decisions;
* explainability content;
* provenance relationships;
* evidence lineage;
* workflow execution sequence;
* certification conclusions.

Equivalent executions shall produce equivalent behavioral meaning.

---

## Permitted Variance Verification

Implement constitutional variance rules.

Permitted variance shall be explicitly declared.

Variance shall never conceal:

* behavioral differences;
* ownership changes;
* evidence loss;
* provenance modification;
* certification outcome changes.

Undeclared variance shall fail certification.

---

## Difference Classification

Implement deterministic classification of detected differences.

Every detected difference shall be classified as one of:

* identical;
* normalized equivalent;
* permitted variance;
* behavioral variance;
* evidence variance;
* provenance variance;
* certification variance;
* structural failure.

Every classification shall generate supporting evidence.

---

## Equivalence Evidence Generation

Generate objective comparison evidence including:

* compared artifacts;
* baseline identifier;
* regenerated identifier;
* normalization actions;
* comparison methodology;
* detected differences;
* equivalence determination;
* supporting evidence;
* execution timestamp;
* responsible workflow.

Evidence shall be immutable.

---

## Failure Handling

Comparison shall fail closed whenever:

* baseline is missing;
* artifact identity differs;
* structural validation fails;
* normalization fails;
* unauthorized variance exists;
* behavioral equivalence cannot be established;
* evidence integrity is violated;
* provenance cannot be verified.

Failures shall prevent certification progression.

---

## Runtime APIs

Implement deterministic runtime interfaces for:

* baseline registration;
* baseline retrieval;
* artifact comparison;
* normalized comparison;
* equivalence determination;
* variance reporting;
* evidence generation;
* certification status retrieval.

Every interface shall be fully deterministic.

---

## Audit Logging

Every comparison execution shall generate immutable audit records including:

* requesting workflow;
* baseline selected;
* regenerated artifact;
* comparison stages executed;
* normalization operations;
* detected differences;
* equivalence result;
* certification decision.

Audit records shall support complete replay.

---

## Required Evidence

Successful implementation shall generate evidence demonstrating:

* canonical baseline registration;
* deterministic artifact comparison;
* structural equivalence verification;
* normalized equivalence verification;
* permitted variance enforcement;
* behavioral equivalence determination;
* immutable comparison evidence;
* fail-closed enforcement;
* replay reproducibility;
* independent execution reproducibility.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* every certification artifact can be compared against a declared baseline;
* comparison behavior is fully deterministic;
* normalization is constitutionally controlled;
* permitted variance is explicitly enforced;
* unauthorized differences fail closed;
* objective equivalence evidence is generated automatically;
* comparison results are independently reproducible;
* audit replay reproduces identical comparison outcomes;
* all evidence supports independent ECS-004 reproducibility certification.
