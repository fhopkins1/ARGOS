EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B02-003

# Exit Decision Lifecycle Constitution

## Objective

Establish the complete constitutional lifecycle governing every Exit Decision object state, transition, authority boundary, evidence requirement, failure behavior, recovery behavior, and reconciliation requirement.

This order shall define the deterministic lifecycle model governing how Exit Decision objects are created, evaluated, decided, authorized, issued, executed, completed, corrected, superseded, cancelled, expired, and archived.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every lifecycle state shall possess one constitutional meaning.

Every transition shall possess one governing authority.

Every lifecycle mutation shall preserve historical lineage.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A lifecycle state shall represent one deterministic condition.

A transition shall represent one authorized movement between states.

No object shall exist in multiple contradictory lifecycle states.

No transition shall occur without:

* authorized initiating authority;
* satisfied preconditions;
* required evidence;
* valid state transition path.

No lifecycle transition shall create ownership over another office's truth domain.

No Exit Decision lifecycle state shall imply ownership of:

* market truth;
* risk truth;
* authorization truth;
* execution truth;
* position truth;
* settlement truth;
* historical truth;
* performance truth.

A lifecycle record shall preserve:

* predecessor state;
* successor state;
* transition authority;
* transition evidence;
* transition timestamp;
* transition rationale.

---

# Lifecycle State Registry

## Created

### Purpose

Represents creation of an Exit Decision object.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* valid creation request exists;
* initiating identity is established;
* required context is available.

### Required Evidence

* creation event;
* creator identity;
* creation timestamp;
* initial request context.

### Prohibited Conditions

Created shall not represent:

* valid evaluation;
* recommendation;
* decision;
* authorization;
* execution authority.

---

## Received

### Purpose

Represents receipt of an Exit Decision request or candidate input.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* object identity exists;
* receipt source is known.

### Required Evidence

* receipt record;
* source lineage;
* input reference.

### Prohibited Conditions

Receipt shall not imply admissibility.

---

## Validating

### Purpose

Represents validation of object completeness and admissibility requirements.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* received object exists;
* validation process initiated.

### Required Evidence

* validation record;
* validation criteria;
* validation outcome.

### Failure Behavior

Invalid objects shall transition to:

```text
Rejected
```

---

## Admitted

### Purpose

Represents acceptance that the Exit Decision request satisfies minimum entry requirements.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* validation successful;
* required inputs available.

### Required Evidence

* admissibility record;
* accepted input inventory.

### Prohibited Conditions

Admission shall not equal approval.

---

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Required Evidence

### Prohibited Conditions

Exit Decision Office.

Exit Decision Office.

Authorizations Office.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Required Evidence

* recipient identity;
* transmission record.

---

## Executing

Represents active execution by authorized execution authority.

### Authority

Trader Office.

### Preconditions

### Required Evidence

### Prohibited Conditions

---

### Authority

### Required Evidence

## Completed

### Purpose

### Authority

### Preconditions

* reconciliation performed.

### Required Evidence

* completion record;
* reconciliation evidence.

### Prohibited Conditions

Completion shall not create closed-position truth.

# Exceptional Lifecycle States

## Deferred

### Purpose

Represents temporary suspension of decision progression.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* insufficient information;
* unresolved condition;
* required dependency unavailable.

### Required Evidence

* deferral rationale;
* reopening criteria.

### Restrictions

Deferred decisions cannot execute.

---

## Rejected

### Purpose

Represents refusal to advance an Exit Decision object.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Preconditions

* invalid condition;
* insufficient admissibility;
* failed criteria.

### Required Evidence

* rejection reason;
* rejection evidence.

### Restrictions

Rejected decisions cannot silently reactivate.

---

## Cancelled

### Purpose

Represents termination of an active decision.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office or authorized cancellation authority.

### Required Evidence

* cancellation rationale;
* cancellation authority.

### Restrictions

Cancellation preserves history.

---

## Expired

### Purpose

Represents a decision whose validity period has ended.

### Authority

Exit Decision lifecycle authority.

### Required Evidence

* expiration condition;
* expiration timestamp.

### Restrictions

Expired decisions cannot execute.

---

## Corrected

### Purpose

Represents issuance of a corrected decision version.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Required Evidence

* correction reason;
* prior version reference;
* correction lineage.

### Restrictions

Correction shall never overwrite prior decisions.

---

## Superseded

### Purpose

Represents replacement by a newer decision version.

### Authority

Exit Decision Office.

### Required Evidence

* predecessor reference;
* successor reference;
* supersession rationale.

### Restrictions

Superseded decisions remain historically accessible.

---

## Archived

### Purpose

Represents final historical retention state.

### Authority

Historian.

### Required Evidence

* archival record;
* lineage confirmation.

# Invalid Transition Registry

The following transitions are constitutionally prohibited:

* Created → Executing;
* Received → Executed;
* Evaluating → Authorized;
* Recommended → Executing;
* Decided → Executing without authorization;
* Expired → Executing;
* Cancelled → Executing;
* Rejected → Executing;
* Superseded → Executing;
* Archived → Active lifecycle state.

No implementation may permit these transitions.

---

# Replay and Recovery Constitution

Lifecycle replay shall preserve:

* original transition order;
* original authority;
* original evidence;
* original timestamps;
* original lineage.

Recovery shall:

* restore the last valid lifecycle state;
* reject invalid state mutation;
* preserve incomplete transitions;
* require reconciliation after interruption.

No recovery process may erase historical lifecycle states.

---

# Duplicate Transition Constitution

Duplicate transitions shall:

* be detected;
* preserve original transition;
* generate reconciliation evidence;
* prevent unauthorized state mutation.

A repeated transition shall never create a second authoritative lifecycle event.
