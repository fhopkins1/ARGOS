# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B02-002

# Exit Decision Object Ownership and Custody Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional ownership, custody, mutation authority, correction authority, reconciliation authority, and evidence custody for every Exit Decision object and every governed object attribute.

This order exists solely to eliminate ambiguity regarding who owns Exit Decision objects, who maintains custody, who may alter object state, who may correct errors, who reconciles conflicting records, and who preserves historical evidence.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every object shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every object shall possess one constitutional custodian.

Every mutation authority shall have one constitutional source.

Every correction authority shall have one constitutional source.

Every reconciliation authority shall have one constitutional source.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Ownership shall not be inferred from consumption.

Custody shall not be inferred from execution.

Mutation authority shall not be inferred from visibility.

Read authority shall not imply ownership.

Correction authority shall not imply original authorship.

Reconciliation authority shall not imply truth ownership.

Exit Decision shall own only Exit Decision truth.

Exit Decision shall never own:

* market truth;
* risk truth;
* authorization truth;
* broker truth;
* position truth;
* settlement truth;
* performance truth;
* historical truth.

Every object attribute shall have a defined authority boundary.

Every attribute containing externally owned truth shall remain externally governed.

# 3. Custody Constitution

Establish custody boundaries for all Exit Decision information.

Define:

* active object custody;
* immutable evidence custody;
* historical custody;
* archival custody;
* replay custody.

Custody responsibilities shall be separated from:

* Risk;
* Authorizations;
* Trader;
* Broker;
* Position Registry;
* Historian.

Historian shall maintain historical custody.

Exit Decision shall maintain decision-domain custody.

No office shall silently assume custody of another office's records.

---

# 4. Mutation Authority Registry

Establish the authoritative mutation authority model.

For each object define:

* who may create;
* who may update;
* who may correct;
* who may cancel;
* who may supersede;
* who may archive.

Explicitly prohibit:

* Risk modifying Exit Decision objects;
* Trader modifying Exit Decision objects;
* Broker modifying Exit Decision objects;
* Position Registry modifying Exit Decision objects;
* Historian modifying active decision objects.

Historian shall preserve historical records, not modify decision truth.

---

# 5. Correction Authority Registry

Define correction governance.

Every correction shall require:

* authorized correction authority;
* correction reason;
* correction evidence;
* predecessor reference;
* correction timestamp;
* reconciliation record.

Corrections shall preserve:

* original state;
* original identity;
* original lineage;
* correction history.

Silent correction shall be prohibited.

---

# 6. Reconciliation Authority Registry

Define reconciliation ownership for conflicts involving:

* duplicate decisions;
* conflicting decision versions;
* stale decisions;
* missing evidence;
* inconsistent lifecycle states;
* external reference mismatches.

Reconciliation shall determine:

* conflict owner;
* resolution authority;
* evidence requirement;
* final recorded outcome.

Reconciliation shall not permit unauthorized mutation of external truth domains.

# 8. Conflict Identification Registry

Identify and resolve:

## Split Ownership

Identify objects with multiple claimed owners.

Assign:

* one constitutional owner;
* supporting consumers;
* evidence responsibilities.

---

## Circular Custody

Identify custody relationships where:

* Office A requires Office B;
* Office B requires Office A;
* neither possesses final custody.

Eliminate circular custody.

---

## Implicit Ownership

Identify cases where ownership is assumed through:

* data access;
* execution participation;
* reporting responsibility;
* reconciliation activity.

Convert implicit ownership into explicit constitutional ownership.

---

## Mutation Authority Conflict

Identify conflicting mutation rights.

Assign one authoritative mutation source.
