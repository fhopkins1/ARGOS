EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B02-004

# Decision Correction, Supersession, Expiration, and Historical Integrity Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional governance model for all Exit Decision modifications, corrections, supersessions, expirations, cancellations, and historical lineage preservation activities.

This order shall define how Exit Decision records may change while preserving immutable historical truth, deterministic lineage, auditability, and constitutional separation of authority.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify Exit Decision implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not perform certification activity.
* Not permit historical records to be overwritten.
* Not permit corrected decisions to erase prior decisions.
* Not permit supersession to destroy predecessor lineage.
* Not permit cancellation to silently reactivate.
* Not permit expiration to be bypassed.
* Not permit Exit Decision to mutate truth owned by another office.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Decision Correction Constitution

Establish correction as:

> A controlled constitutional process that creates a new authoritative decision state while preserving the complete historical record of the prior state.

A correction shall never modify the original decision record.

A correction shall create:

* correction identity;
* correction reason;
* correction authority;
* correction evidence;
* corrected decision reference;
* predecessor relationship;
* correction timestamp;
* reconciliation record.

---

# 2. Correction Authority Registry

Define:

## Exit Decision Office

Owns:

* correction initiation;
* correction rationale;
* correction request;
* decision-state reconciliation.

Does not own:

* position correction;
* risk correction;
* authorization correction;
* execution correction;
* historical record mutation.

---

## Risk Office

Owns correction of:

* risk interpretation;
* risk constraints;
* risk-state inputs.

Risk corrections shall not automatically modify prior Exit Decisions.

---

## Authorizations Office

Owns correction of:

* authorization validity;
* authorization lifecycle;
* authorization expiration;
* authorization revocation.

Authorization correction shall not rewrite decision history.

---

## Position Registry Office

Owns correction of:

* position identity;
* position state;
* position reconciliation.

Position correction shall not alter decision lineage.

---

## Historian Office

Owns:

* historical custody;
* immutable lineage preservation;
* audit reconstruction.

Historian shall not alter operational truth.

---

# 3. Correction Lifecycle Constitution

Define correction lifecycle:

```text
Correction Requested
        ↓
Correction Evaluated
        ↓
Correction Authorized
        ↓
Correction Created
        ↓
Correction Reconciled
        ↓
Correction Archived
```

Every correction transition shall require:

* initiating authority;
* evidence basis;
* predecessor reference;
* successor reference;
* reconciliation confirmation.

---

# 4. Supersession Constitution

Establish supersession as:

> The creation of a new Exit Decision that replaces the operational applicability of a prior decision while preserving the prior decision as historical truth.

A superseded decision:

* remains immutable;
* remains queryable;
* remains auditable;
* remains part of lineage;
* cannot return to active authority without a new authorized decision.

---

# 5. Supersession Authority Registry

Define:

Exit Decision owns:

* decision supersession creation;
* supersession rationale;
* successor decision linkage.

Authorizations owns:

* authorization applicability determination.

Trader owns:

* execution-state handling of superseded execution requests.

Historian owns:

* preservation of supersession lineage.

No office may delete or conceal a superseded decision.

---

# 6. Supersession Lineage Requirements

Every supersession shall contain:

* predecessor decision identity;
* successor decision identity;
* supersession reason;
* initiating authority;
* effective transition time;
* evidence supporting replacement;
* reconciliation status.

The lineage chain shall remain complete:

```text
Original Decision
        ↓
Correction / Supersession Event
        ↓
Replacement Decision
        ↓
Final Historical Record
```

---

# 7. Expiration Constitution

Establish expiration as:

> The constitutional termination of decision applicability due to elapsed validity, changed conditions, or invalidated decision context.

Expiration shall:

* preserve the decision record;
* preserve rationale;
* preserve evidence;
* prevent unauthorized execution;
* create an expiration record.

Expired decisions shall not:

* authorize execution;
* restart automatically;
* bypass authorization review.

---

# 8. Expiration Authority Registry

Define:

Exit Decision owns:

* decision validity period;
* decision expiration determination.

Authorizations owns:

* authorization expiration.

Trader owns:

* execution prevention when expired authority is detected.

Historian owns:

* expiration record custody.

---

# 9. Cancellation Constitution

Establish cancellation as:

> The controlled termination of an Exit Decision before completion due to invalidation, withdrawal, conflict, or changed conditions.

Cancellation shall preserve:

* original decision;
* cancellation reason;
* cancellation authority;
* cancellation timestamp;
* evidence;
* lifecycle history.

Cancelled decisions shall never silently return to executable status.

---

# 10. Historical Integrity Constitution

Establish the following principles:

Every Exit Decision historical record shall be:

* immutable;
* append-only;
* lineage-preserving;
* independently traceable;
* reconstructable.

Historical records shall contain:

* creation event;
* modification events;
* correction events;
* supersession events;
* expiration events;
* cancellation events;
* completion events.

No historical record shall be overwritten.

---

# 11. Predecessor and Successor Registry

Every decision relationship shall define:

## Predecessor

The prior decision record from which a new decision derives.

## Successor

The decision record replacing or extending prior applicability.

Every successor shall reference its predecessor.

Every predecessor shall remain preserved.

No orphan decisions shall exist.

---

# 12. Reconciliation Constitution

After any:

* correction;
* supersession;
* expiration;
* cancellation;

the following reconciliation shall occur:

## Exit Decision

Verify:

* decision lineage;
* lifecycle state;
* applicability.

## Authorizations

Verify:

* authorization validity;
* execution eligibility.

## Trader

Verify:

* execution request alignment.

## Position Registry

Verify:

* position state consistency.

## Historian

Verify:

* historical completeness.
