# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-001

# Closed Position Truth Office Independent ECS-003 Constitutional, Implementation, and Proof Audit

## Purpose

Perform the first independent ECS-003 audit of the Closed Position Truth Office.

Determine whether the Closed Position Truth Office possesses:

* complete constitutional authority;
* deterministic office boundaries;
* complete canonical closed-position truth object definitions;
* complete settlement and closure doctrine;
* complete lifecycle governance;
* complete ownership and custody doctrine;
* complete reconciliation doctrine;
* complete historical integrity doctrine;
* complete evidence obligations;
* complete requirement-level traceability;
* dependency-derived implementation participation;
* executable behavioral verification;
* sufficient implementation evidence;
* independently reproducible proof;
* certification readiness.

The audit shall determine whether Closed Position Truth can serve as the authoritative enterprise source of truth for completed positions.

The audit shall distinguish:

* position closure truth;
* execution truth;
* broker truth;
* settlement truth;
* performance truth;
* historical truth.

No constitutional doctrine shall be modified.

No implementation behavior shall be modified.

No remediation shall occur under this order.

No PASS shall be inferred from:

* documentation;
* metadata;
* source inspection;
* aggregate test counts;
* registry declarations;
* completion reports;
* self-issued readiness declarations.

# Audit Principles

Closed Position Truth shall be evaluated against the delivered repository and evidence packages.

Every conclusion shall be independently derived.

Constitutional conclusions shall originate from authoritative constitutional artifacts.

Implementation conclusions shall originate from dependency-derived implementation discovery and executable verification.

Proof conclusions shall originate from raw execution evidence.

Implementation participation shall never be determined exclusively through:

* filenames;
* directory names;
* naming conventions;
* manually curated inventories;
* historical execution lists;
* documentation references;
* keyword matching.

Every finding shall possess:

* unique identity;
* governing requirement;
* affected artifact;
* objective evidence;
* severity;
* classification;
* disposition;
* remediation recommendation.

The audit shall fail closed.

Missing, stale, contradictory, synthetic, circular, non-reproducible, or incomplete evidence shall prevent unconditional certification.
