## HISTORIAN-RM-002A-008

### Enterprise Learning Readiness Runtime

#### Purpose

Implement deterministic runtime interfaces that allow the Enterprise Learning capability to retrieve complete historical information from the Historian Office without granting authority to modify historical records or perform learning within the Historian itself.

The Historian shall preserve and expose immutable historical truth while remaining constitutionally independent of all learning, optimization, inference, and model-training activities.

---

### Constitutional Requirements

The implementation shall provide deterministic runtime behavior for:

* learning retrieval requests;
* authorization validation;
* historical query execution;
* journey reconstruction;
* contextual information retrieval;
* uncertainty retrieval;
* counterfactual retrieval;
* response assembly;
* evidence generation;
* retrieval completion.

The Historian shall never evaluate, rank, optimize, or learn from retrieved information.

---

### Enterprise Learning Interface

The runtime shall expose deterministic retrieval interfaces for:

* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* constitutional artifacts;
* provenance graphs;
* historical custody records;
* workflow execution history;
* decision history;
* authorization history;
* performance truth references;
* audit evidence;
* historical reconstruction outputs.

Interfaces shall be read-only.

---

### Journey Retrieval

The implementation shall support deterministic retrieval of:

* complete journeys;
* partial journeys;
* journey timelines;
* journey participants;
* workflow transitions;
* historical states;
* lifecycle history;
* associated evidence.

Retrieved journeys shall be complete and immutable.

---

### Decision Retrieval

The implementation shall retrieve:

* accepted decisions;
* rejected decisions;
* competing alternatives;
* decision rationale;
* supporting evidence;
* authorizations;
* dependencies;
* execution outcomes.

Decision ordering shall remain historically accurate.

---

### Uncertainty Retrieval

The implementation shall preserve and retrieve:

* unresolved questions;
* incomplete evidence;
* confidence assessments;
* missing information classifications;
* deferred decisions;
* conflicting observations;
* ambiguity records.

Historical uncertainty shall never be removed or rewritten.

---

### Information Deficiency Retrieval

The implementation shall retrieve records describing:

* missing evidence;
* unavailable information;
* incomplete dependencies;
* failed retrievals;
* unavailable authorizations;
* historical gaps;
* constitutional limitations.

Deficiencies shall remain part of the historical record.

---

### Causal Chain Retrieval

The implementation shall reconstruct deterministic causal relationships including:

* originating events;
* triggering conditions;
* workflow dependencies;
* authorization chains;
* decision sequences;
* resulting actions;
* downstream consequences.

Causal reconstruction shall be reproducible.

---

### Historical Context Retrieval

The implementation shall retrieve contextual information including:

* surrounding events;
* related workflows;
* historical environment;
* predecessor artifacts;
* successor artifacts;
* enterprise state;
* constitutional state at execution time.

Context shall preserve original historical conditions.

---

### Authorization Validation

Prior to retrieval, the implementation shall verify:

* requesting office identity;
* constitutional authority;
* access permissions;
* retrieval scope;
* requested historical domain.

Unauthorized retrieval requests shall fail closed.

---

### Read-Only Enforcement

The implementation shall ensure retrieval operations cannot:

* modify artifacts;
* alter provenance;
* change custody;
* rewrite historical records;
* generate historical corrections;
* create new constitutional records.

Retrieval shall have no side effects.

---

### Response Assembly

Responses shall contain only:

* requested historical information;
* immutable constitutional records;
* associated provenance;
* supporting evidence;
* retrieval metadata;
* authorization evidence.

No inferred or synthesized historical facts shall be introduced by the Historian.

---

### Failure Behavior

The runtime shall fail closed upon detection of:

* invalid authorization;
* nonexistent journey;
* corrupted historical references;
* inconsistent provenance;
* incomplete custody chain;
* invalid retrieval request;
* response integrity failure.

Failed retrievals shall generate objective evidence.

---

### Runtime Evidence

Execution shall generate evidence demonstrating:

* retrieval request receipt;
* authorization verification;
* query execution;
* artifact retrieval;
* journey reconstruction;
* uncertainty retrieval;
* causal chain reconstruction;
* response generation;
* response delivery;
* retrieval completion or failure.

Evidence shall be immutable and independently reproducible.

---

### Deterministic Verification

Verification shall demonstrate that:

* identical retrieval requests produce identical outputs;
* historical ordering remains unchanged;
* retrieved artifacts remain immutable;
* causal chains are reproducible;
* uncertainty records remain intact;
* read-only enforcement is preserved under all execution paths.

---

### Enterprise Learning Separation Verification

The implementation shall verify that the Historian:

* performs no inference;
* performs no optimization;
* performs no statistical analysis;
* performs no model updates;
* performs no autonomous decision-making;
* performs no learning.

Its sole constitutional responsibility is deterministic preservation and retrieval of historical truth.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent certification demonstrates that:

* Enterprise Learning can deterministically retrieve all constitutionally authorized historical information;
* journeys, decisions, uncertainties, deficiencies, causal chains, and contextual records are retrieved completely and reproducibly;
* all retrieval operations are strictly read-only;
* unauthorized requests fail closed;
* retrieval behavior produces independently certifiable evidence;
* the Historian remains constitutionally separated from all learning and inference functions.
