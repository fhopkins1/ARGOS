## HISTORIAN-RM-002A-012

# Behavioral Completion Review

## Purpose

This order establishes the final behavioral completion review for the HISTORIAN-RM-002A remediation campaign.

Its purpose is to independently verify that every constitutional responsibility assigned to the Historian Office now possesses complete, observable, deterministic, and certifiable runtime behavior suitable for re-entry into ECS-003 independent certification.

This review evaluates behavioral completeness only.

No constitutional architecture shall be modified.

---

# Review Authority

The Behavioral Completion Review shall be conducted under the authority of:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine;
* Enterprise Audit Doctrine;
* Enterprise Evidence Doctrine;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;
* HISTORIAN-RM-001;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002;
* HISTORIAN-RM-003;
* HISTORIAN-MO-001;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002A.

---

# Review Objectives

The review shall determine whether every constitutional capability now possesses:

* executable runtime behavior;
* observable execution;
* deterministic behavior;
* objective evidence generation;
* fail-closed operation;
* independent reproducibility;
* complete certification coverage.

No constitutional responsibility may remain architecture-only.

---

# Behavioral Inventory Verification

Verify that every constitutional responsibility defined by:

* HISTORIAN-RM-001;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002;
* HISTORIAN-RM-003;
* HISTORIAN-MO-001;

has corresponding executable runtime behavior.

Each responsibility shall map to one or more implemented behaviors.

No constitutional requirement may remain unmapped.

---

# Runtime Coverage Review

Verify runtime implementation for:

* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* artifact registration;
* historical custody;
* provenance generation;
* provenance graph construction;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* language preservation;
* missing-information preservation;
* Enterprise Learning interfaces;
* counterfactual support;
* behavioral evidence generation.

Coverage shall be complete.

---

# Behavioral Traceability Review

Verify traceability from:

* constitutional requirement;
* implementation component;
* runtime execution;
* emitted evidence;
* certification artifact;
* audit result.

Every execution shall be fully traceable.

---

# Evidence Completeness Review

Verify that each implemented behavior produces:

* execution evidence;
* ownership evidence;
* provenance evidence;
* custody evidence;
* validation evidence;
* failure evidence;
* certification evidence.

Evidence shall be immutable, reproducible, and independently verifiable.

---

# Determinism Review

Verify that repeated execution under identical inputs produces identical:

* artifacts;
* journey histories;
* provenance graphs;
* custody chains;
* replay outputs;
* evidence;
* certification results.

Non-deterministic behavior shall constitute review failure.

---

# Fail-Closed Review

Verify that every runtime capability fails closed upon detection of:

* missing artifacts;
* corrupted history;
* invalid custody;
* provenance inconsistencies;
* authorization failures;
* replay failures;
* certification failures;
* evidence corruption.

Failure handling shall itself generate certification evidence.

---

# Interface Review

Verify behavioral correctness of all interfaces with:

* Commander;
* Seeker;
* Analyst;
* Risk;
* Trader;
* Performance Truth;
* Librarian;
* Enterprise Learning;
* Enterprise Audit.

Interface behavior shall preserve constitutional ownership and historical integrity.

---

# Independent Reproduction Review

Execute multiple independent clean-room reproductions of:

* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* provenance graphs;
* custody chains;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* counterfactual retrieval.

Equivalent outputs shall be produced without reliance upon implementation knowledge.

---

# Certification Suite Review

Verify that the behavioral certification suite:

* covers every runtime behavior;
* validates deterministic execution;
* validates evidence generation;
* validates fail-closed behavior;
* validates reproducibility;
* detects implementation regressions.

Coverage shall be complete and objectively measurable.

---

# Gap Analysis

Identify any remaining deficiencies including:

* uncovered constitutional behaviors;
* incomplete runtime implementation;
* missing evidence;
* missing traceability;
* insufficient determinism;
* certification deficiencies;
* replay inconsistencies;
* unresolved audit findings.

Every identified gap shall be classified by severity and constitutional impact.

---

# Readiness Assessment

Determine whether the Historian Office demonstrates:

* complete runtime implementation;
* complete behavioral coverage;
* deterministic execution;
* objective evidence generation;
* independent reproducibility;
* constitutional compliance;
* certification readiness.

The assessment shall recommend one of:

* Ready for ECS-003 Independent Certification;
* Ready with Minor Corrective Actions;
* Remediation Required;
* Certification Denied.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* every constitutional responsibility possesses verified executable runtime behavior;
* all runtime behaviors produce objective certification evidence;
* deterministic execution has been independently demonstrated;
* fail-closed operation has been verified for every behavioral domain;
* complete constitutional traceability has been established;
* no unresolved behavioral certification gaps remain; and
* the Historian Office has received a formal recommendation either authorizing or denying re-entry into **HISTORIAN-ECS003-AUDIT-002** based solely upon objective behavioral evidence.
