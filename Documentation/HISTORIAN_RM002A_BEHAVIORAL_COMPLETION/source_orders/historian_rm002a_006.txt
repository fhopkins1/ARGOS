### HISTORIAN-RM-002A-006

# Deterministic Replay Runtime

## Purpose

This order establishes the executable runtime responsible for deterministic replay of Enterprise Information Journeys managed by the Historian Office.

The objective is to guarantee that any historical execution can be reproduced from preserved historical artifacts without modification, omission, interpretation, or nondeterministic behavior.

Replay exists solely to reconstruct constitutional execution exactly as originally recorded.

It shall not alter enterprise history.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The Deterministic Replay Runtime shall:

* replay complete Enterprise Information Journeys;
* reconstruct historical execution state;
* restore historical custody;
* restore provenance relationships;
* restore historical evidence;
* reproduce constitutional outputs;
* preserve historical ordering;
* generate replay evidence.

Replay shall be entirely read-only.

---

# Replay Scope

The runtime shall support deterministic replay of:

* Workflow Execution Tokens;
* Decision Objects;
* Prompt Contracts;
* Audit Records;
* Performance Truth Records;
* Historical Records;
* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* Provenance Graphs;
* custody assignments;
* constitutional metadata.

No historical artifact may be modified during replay.

---

# Replay Initialization

Prior to replay the runtime shall verify:

* journey identity;
* replay request authorization;
* historical completeness;
* artifact availability;
* provenance graph integrity;
* custody continuity;
* evidence availability.

Replay shall fail closed if any prerequisite cannot be satisfied.

---

# Replay Reconstruction

The runtime shall reconstruct:

* historical timeline;
* artifact creation order;
* ownership transitions;
* custody transitions;
* provenance evolution;
* dependency relationships;
* correction history;
* supersession history.

Reconstruction shall follow original historical ordering.

---

# Artifact Restoration

Each replayed artifact shall be restored with:

* original identifier;
* original timestamps;
* constitutional owner;
* historical version;
* immutable content;
* provenance relationships;
* custody status.

No replay-generated identifiers shall replace historical identifiers.

---

# Provenance Replay

The runtime shall replay:

* node creation;
* edge creation;
* derivation chains;
* dependency graphs;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* certification lineage.

The reconstructed graph shall be constitutionally equivalent to the preserved graph.

---

# Historical State Replay

Replay shall reconstruct:

* enterprise state;
* workflow state;
* historical decisions;
* historical authorizations;
* historical constraints;
* historical uncertainties;
* historical information availability.

Historical state shall match preserved evidence.

---

# Custody Replay

Replay shall reconstruct:

* custody assignment;
* custody transfer;
* custody validation;
* custody continuity;
* custody verification;
* custody evidence.

Historical custody shall remain immutable.

---

# Evidence Replay

Replay shall reproduce:

* historical evidence;
* audit evidence;
* certification evidence;
* behavioral evidence;
* provenance evidence;
* custody evidence.

Evidence reproduced during replay shall remain traceable to original historical artifacts.

---

# Deterministic Validation

The runtime shall verify that replay produces identical:

* artifact identities;
* execution ordering;
* ownership assignments;
* provenance graphs;
* custody chains;
* historical outputs;
* certification evidence.

Any deviation shall terminate replay.

---

# Replay Failure Detection

The runtime shall detect:

* missing artifacts;
* duplicate artifacts;
* corrupted history;
* invalid provenance;
* broken custody;
* ordering violations;
* evidence inconsistencies;
* nondeterministic reconstruction.

Failures shall produce objective diagnostic evidence.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

Replay shall terminate immediately upon detection of:

* missing historical records;
* provenance inconsistencies;
* custody violations;
* evidence corruption;
* unauthorized replay requests;
* constitutional validation failures.

Partial replay shall not be certified.

---

# Runtime Evidence

The runtime shall generate evidence recording:

* replay initiation;
* replay authorization;
* artifact restoration;
* provenance reconstruction;
* custody reconstruction;
* historical state reconstruction;
* validation results;
* replay completion.

Evidence shall be immutable.

---

# Runtime Metrics

The Historian shall measure:

* replay duration;
* artifacts replayed;
* provenance nodes restored;
* provenance edges restored;
* custody records restored;
* evidence records restored;
* replay failures;
* deterministic equivalence rate.

Metrics shall support independent certification.

---

# Behavioral Verification

Certification testing shall verify:

* complete journey replay;
* deterministic artifact restoration;
* provenance replay;
* custody replay;
* historical state replay;
* evidence replay;
* fail-closed behavior;
* reproducible replay outputs.

Each test shall produce objective certification evidence.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* Enterprise Information Journeys are replayed deterministically;
* historical state is reconstructed without deviation;
* provenance graphs are faithfully restored;
* custody history is reproduced completely;
* historical evidence is regenerated identically;
* replay outputs are constitutionally equivalent to original execution;
* fail-closed behavior is demonstrated under fault conditions;
* independent certification confirms deterministic replay across repeated executions.
