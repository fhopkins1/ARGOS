### HISTORIAN-RM-002A-011

# Behavioral Certification Suite

## Purpose

Develop an independent behavioral certification suite capable of objectively verifying every runtime behavior implemented under the Enterprise Information Journey Behavioral Completion Program.

Certification shall evaluate observable runtime behavior rather than implementation structure, source code, or architectural intent.

---

## Constitutional Requirements

The certification suite shall verify:

* observable execution;
* deterministic behavior;
* constitutional compliance;
* immutable history preservation;
* ownership preservation;
* custody preservation;
* provenance integrity;
* fail-closed behavior;
* evidence generation;
* independent reproducibility.

Every certification result shall be independently repeatable.

---

## Certification Scope

The certification suite shall verify runtime behavior for:

* Enterprise Information Journey lifecycle;
* artifact registration;
* historical custody;
* provenance generation;
* provenance graph construction;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* language preservation;
* missing-information preservation;
* Enterprise Learning interfaces;
* counterfactual retrieval;
* behavioral evidence generation.

No implemented runtime capability shall remain uncertified.

---

## Behavioral Test Categories

Implement certification tests for:

* positive execution;
* negative execution;
* boundary conditions;
* invalid input handling;
* fail-closed operation;
* deterministic equivalence;
* concurrency behavior;
* lifecycle transitions;
* recovery behavior;
* evidence completeness.

Each behavioral category shall execute independently.

---

## Deterministic Verification

Verify that repeated execution using identical:

* repositories;
* inputs;
* timestamps;
* constitutional configuration;
* historical state;

produces identical:

* outputs;
* historical state;
* provenance graphs;
* custody chains;
* evidence artifacts;
* certification results.

Any nondeterministic behavior shall constitute certification failure.

---

## Evidence Verification

Verify every certified behavior generates:

* execution evidence;
* ownership evidence;
* custody evidence;
* provenance evidence;
* lifecycle evidence;
* dependency evidence;
* certification evidence;
* immutable audit records.

Missing evidence shall fail certification.

---

## Fail-Closed Certification

Verify that runtime behavior rejects:

* invalid artifacts;
* duplicate registrations;
* broken custody chains;
* invalid provenance relationships;
* unauthorized reconstruction;
* unauthorized replay;
* corrupted repositories;
* incomplete journeys;
* inconsistent historical state.

Every rejection shall be deterministic and generate certification evidence.

---

## Independent Reproducibility

Verify that an independent certification environment can reproduce:

* runtime execution;
* historical reconstruction;
* replay execution;
* provenance graphs;
* custody verification;
* evidence generation;
* certification outcomes.

Independent reproduction shall require no implementation-specific knowledge.

---

## Mutation Validation

Introduce controlled mutations including:

* missing artifacts;
* corrupted custody;
* invalid provenance edges;
* altered timestamps;
* duplicate identifiers;
* broken dependencies;
* unauthorized ownership;
* incomplete journeys;
* modified evidence.

The Historian shall detect every constitutional violation and fail closed.

---

## Certification Metrics

Measure:

* behavioral coverage;
* certification pass rate;
* deterministic equivalence rate;
* evidence completeness;
* replay equivalence;
* reconstruction accuracy;
* provenance integrity;
* custody integrity;
* mutation detection rate;
* fail-closed success rate.

Metrics shall be objective and reproducible.

---

## Certification Evidence Package

Generate an immutable certification package containing:

* certification configuration;
* repository version;
* constitutional version;
* executed behavioral tests;
* runtime observations;
* deterministic comparison results;
* evidence inventory;
* mutation results;
* reproducibility verification;
* certification verdict.

The certification package shall itself become part of the Enterprise Information Journey.

---

## Certification Acceptance Criteria

Behavioral certification shall succeed only when:

* every required runtime behavior executes successfully;
* every execution is observable;
* all outputs are deterministic;
* evidence is complete and immutable;
* custody remains continuous;
* provenance remains valid;
* all mutations fail closed;
* independent reproduction succeeds without deviation.

Failure of any criterion shall result in certification failure.

---

## Certification Outputs

Upon successful completion, the suite shall issue:

* Enterprise Information Journey Behavioral Certification;
* Historical Custody Behavioral Certification;
* Provenance Behavioral Certification;
* Provenance Graph Behavioral Certification;
* Historical Reconstruction Behavioral Certification;
* Deterministic Replay Behavioral Certification;
* Language Preservation Behavioral Certification;
* Enterprise Learning Readiness Behavioral Certification;
* Counterfactual Runtime Behavioral Certification;
* Behavioral Evidence Certification;
* Historian Behavioral Completion Certification.

---

## Final Certification Requirement

Successful completion of this certification suite shall establish that every runtime capability introduced under **HISTORIAN-RM-002A** has been independently verified as executable, observable, deterministic, fail-closed, reproducible, and objectively certifiable in accordance with Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003.
