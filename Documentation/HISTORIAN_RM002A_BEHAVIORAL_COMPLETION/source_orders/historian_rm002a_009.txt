### HISTORIAN-RM-002A-009

# Counterfactual Runtime Support

## Purpose

This order establishes the executable runtime behavior required for deterministic retrieval and reconstruction of constitutional counterfactual scenarios using immutable historical information preserved by the Historian Office.

The Historian shall enable counterfactual analysis by preserving and reconstructing historical information only. It shall not generate hypotheses, evaluate alternatives, perform simulations, or recommend decisions.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

Implement deterministic runtime support for retrieval and reconstruction of:

* alternative evidence;
* alternative decisions;
* alternative authorizations;
* alternative execution paths;
* alternative risk assessments;
* alternative monitoring outcomes;
* alternative broker interactions;
* alternative trade executions;
* alternative exits;
* alternative Enterprise Information Journeys;
* TYPHON training scenarios.

Every reconstruction shall originate exclusively from constitutionally preserved historical information.

---

# Counterfactual Request Runtime

Implement runtime behavior that:

* receives a counterfactual request;
* validates requester authority;
* validates requested scope;
* resolves referenced historical objects;
* verifies historical completeness;
* initiates deterministic reconstruction.

Unauthorized requests shall fail closed.

---

# Historical Information Resolution

Implement deterministic resolution of all historical information required for reconstruction, including:

* workflow execution records;
* Decision Objects;
* Performance Truth Records;
* Authorization records;
* Risk records;
* monitoring records;
* broker records;
* execution evidence;
* provenance graphs;
* Enterprise Information Journeys.

Every resolved object shall retain its original historical identity.

---

# Counterfactual Reconstruction Runtime

Implement deterministic reconstruction of requested historical alternatives by assembling preserved historical records without modification.

Reconstruction shall preserve:

* execution ordering;
* temporal relationships;
* provenance relationships;
* ownership;
* custody;
* evidence references;
* historical context.

No reconstructed information shall alter immutable historical records.

---

# Alternative Path Retrieval

Implement runtime retrieval of historical branches including:

* accepted alternatives;
* rejected alternatives;
* abandoned alternatives;
* interrupted workflows;
* superseded decisions;
* failed authorizations;
* cancelled executions;
* rollback sequences.

All retrieved branches shall preserve their original constitutional state.

---

# Historical Context Preservation

Every reconstructed scenario shall include:

* originating workflow;
* originating journey;
* initiating events;
* triggering evidence;
* responsible offices;
* constitutional authorities;
* dependency relationships;
* temporal ordering;
* uncertainty records;
* information deficiencies.

Context shall be identical to the preserved historical record.

---

# Provenance Preservation

Every reconstructed counterfactual shall maintain complete provenance including:

* source artifacts;
* derivation lineage;
* dependency graph;
* supersession history;
* custody history;
* evidence lineage;
* certification lineage.

Provenance shall never be regenerated independently of preserved history.

---

# TYPHON Training Support

Implement deterministic retrieval interfaces supporting historical reconstruction for Enterprise training environments.

Support retrieval of:

* historical market conditions;
* enterprise state;
* available information;
* office outputs;
* decision alternatives;
* rejected actions;
* uncertainties;
* resulting enterprise behavior.

The Historian shall provide historical information only.

Simulation and evaluation remain outside Historian authority.

---

# Reconstruction Integrity Verification

Verify every reconstructed scenario for:

* historical completeness;
* provenance continuity;
* custody continuity;
* deterministic ordering;
* artifact integrity;
* evidence integrity;
* identity preservation;
* reproducibility.

Any integrity failure shall terminate reconstruction.

---

# Runtime Interfaces

Implement deterministic interfaces supporting:

* counterfactual request;
* scenario retrieval;
* historical branch retrieval;
* alternative decision retrieval;
* alternative evidence retrieval;
* journey reconstruction;
* provenance retrieval;
* historical context retrieval;
* replay preparation.

All interfaces shall return immutable historical information only.

---

# Runtime Evidence Generation

Every counterfactual operation shall generate immutable evidence including:

* request identifier;
* requesting authority;
* reconstructed scenario identifier;
* referenced journey;
* referenced artifacts;
* reconstruction timestamps;
* retrieved historical branches;
* provenance references;
* custody verification;
* integrity verification;
* completion status;
* failure reason when applicable.

Evidence shall be independently reproducible.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Historian shall reject:

* reconstruction of nonexistent history;
* modification of historical records;
* incomplete historical reconstruction;
* broken provenance;
* broken custody chains;
* unresolved historical dependencies;
* unauthorized reconstruction requests;
* unverifiable historical artifacts.

No incomplete or unverifiable reconstruction shall be returned.

---

# Behavioral Verification

The behavioral certification suite shall verify:

* deterministic counterfactual request processing;
* deterministic historical retrieval;
* deterministic reconstruction;
* preservation of provenance;
* preservation of custody;
* preservation of historical context;
* reproducible reconstruction outputs;
* TYPHON retrieval readiness;
* evidence generation;
* fail-closed enforcement.

Behavioral certification shall verify observable runtime behavior through independent execution and reproducible certification evidence.
