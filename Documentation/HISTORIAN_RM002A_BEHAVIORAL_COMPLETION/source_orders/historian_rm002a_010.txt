# HISTORIAN-RM-002A-010

## Behavioral Evidence Generation

### Objective

Implement deterministic runtime generation, preservation, validation, and certification of behavioral evidence for every executable capability introduced under the Enterprise Information Journey Behavioral Completion Program.

Behavioral evidence shall objectively demonstrate that constitutional behavior was executed, observed, reproduced, and independently verifiable during runtime.

Evidence generation shall occur automatically during execution and shall never rely upon manual intervention.

---

## Constitutional Requirements

The implementation shall generate independently certifiable behavioral evidence for:

* Enterprise Information Journey lifecycle;
* historical custody;
* provenance generation;
* provenance graph construction;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* language preservation;
* missing-information preservation;
* Enterprise Learning retrieval;
* counterfactual retrieval.

Behavioral evidence shall objectively demonstrate constitutional execution rather than implementation intent.

---

## Runtime Evidence Generation

Implement automatic generation of immutable behavioral evidence whenever constitutional runtime behavior occurs.

Evidence generation shall execute synchronously with constitutional operations.

Behavior shall never complete without corresponding evidence.

Failure to generate evidence shall fail closed.

---

## Evidence Identity

Every behavioral evidence record shall include:

* immutable evidence identifier;
* execution identifier;
* workflow identifier;
* Enterprise Information Journey identifier;
* historical artifact identifier;
* constitutional capability exercised;
* producing office;
* producing component;
* execution timestamp;
* evidence classification.

Evidence identifiers shall be globally unique.

## Behavioral Verification

Verify through independent execution that:

* every constitutional behavior generates evidence;
* evidence is immutable;
* evidence is deterministic;
* evidence is reproducible;
* evidence supports historical reconstruction;
* evidence supports deterministic replay;
* evidence supports provenance validation;
* evidence supports independent certification;
* missing evidence causes fail-closed behavior.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* every runtime capability introduced by HISTORIAN-RM-002A automatically emits immutable behavioral evidence;
* evidence is complete, deterministic, and independently reproducible;
* every evidence record is fully traceable through custody, provenance, and certification lineage;
* reconstruction and replay reproduce equivalent evidence across independent executions;
* all integrity validation succeeds under valid execution conditions;
* missing, inconsistent, duplicated, or corrupted evidence causes immediate fail-closed behavior;
* the generated behavioral evidence is sufficient to support independent ECS-003 certification without reliance on implementation internals.
