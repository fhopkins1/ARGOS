### HISTORIAN-RM-002A-005

# Historical Reconstruction Runtime

## Purpose

Implement deterministic runtime behavior allowing the Historian Office to reconstruct complete Enterprise Information Journeys from constitutionally preserved historical artifacts without modifying historical truth.

Historical reconstruction shall reproduce historical reality exactly as it existed at any selected constitutional point in time.

---

## Constitutional Requirements

The runtime shall reconstruct:

* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* workflow execution history;
* decision history;
* authorization history;
* evidence history;
* custody history;
* provenance history;
* historical system state.

No reconstructed information may be fabricated.

Unknown information shall remain explicitly unknown.

---

## Reconstruction Inputs

Support reconstruction from:

* Enterprise Information Journey identifiers;
* historical timestamps;
* Workflow Execution Tokens;
* Decision Objects;
* Audit Records;
* Performance Truth Records;
* Prompt Contracts;
* provenance graphs;
* custody records;
* constitutional snapshots.

Every reconstruction request shall resolve to a deterministic reconstruction scope.

---

## Runtime Reconstruction Pipeline

Implement deterministic execution for:

1. request validation;
2. authorization verification;
3. historical snapshot identification;
4. artifact discovery;
5. custody verification;
6. provenance traversal;
7. dependency reconstruction;
8. chronological ordering;
9. state reconstruction;
10. completeness verification;
11. reconstruction certification;
12. immutable evidence generation.

Execution order shall be deterministic.

---

## Dependency Reconstruction

Reconstruct:

* parent relationships;
* child relationships;
* workflow dependencies;
* decision dependencies;
* authorization dependencies;
* evidence dependencies;
* external references;
* supersession chains.

All dependency resolution shall preserve constitutional history.

---

## Temporal Reconstruction

Support reconstruction for:

* any historical timestamp;
* workflow start;
* workflow completion;
* decision creation;
* decision approval;
* execution boundaries;
* historical certification events;
* constitutional freeze points.

Temporal reconstruction shall produce identical results for identical timestamps.

---

## State Reconstruction

Reconstruct complete historical state including:

* active workflows;
* pending workflows;
* completed workflows;
* historical ownership;
* constitutional responsibilities;
* execution status;
* authorization state;
* historical evidence inventory.

State reconstruction shall preserve historical consistency.

---

## Historical Consistency Verification

Verify:

* chronological consistency;
* ownership continuity;
* custody continuity;
* provenance integrity;
* dependency integrity;
* constitutional compliance;
* immutable history preservation.

Any inconsistency shall terminate reconstruction.

---

## Completeness Verification

Verify reconstruction includes:

* every required artifact;
* every required dependency;
* every required custody record;
* every required provenance relationship;
* every required evidence object;
* every required constitutional reference.

Incomplete reconstruction shall fail closed.

---

## Deterministic Behavior

Repeated reconstruction using identical:

* inputs;
* repository state;
* historical timestamp;
* constitutional version;

shall produce:

* identical reconstructed history;
* identical reconstructed state;
* identical dependency graph;
* identical certification evidence.

No nondeterministic reconstruction behavior is permitted.

---

## Failure Handling

Detect and reject:

* missing artifacts;
* corrupted artifacts;
* broken custody chains;
* invalid provenance;
* dependency cycles;
* timestamp inconsistencies;
* unauthorized reconstruction requests;
* repository corruption.

Failures shall generate objective diagnostic evidence.

---

## Runtime Evidence

Generate immutable evidence recording:

* reconstruction request;
* reconstruction scope;
* artifacts discovered;
* dependencies resolved;
* custody verification;
* provenance verification;
* completeness verification;
* reconstructed state hash;
* reconstruction duration;
* certification outcome.

Evidence shall itself become part of the Enterprise Information Journey.

---

## Runtime Metrics

Measure:

* reconstruction success rate;
* reconstruction latency;
* artifacts reconstructed;
* dependency resolution count;
* completeness percentage;
* custody verification success;
* provenance verification success;
* deterministic replay equivalence.

Metrics shall support independent ECS-003 certification.

---

## Certification Requirements

Certification shall demonstrate:

* complete historical reconstruction;
* deterministic execution;
* complete dependency reconstruction;
* immutable historical preservation;
* constitutional consistency;
* reproducible outputs;
* fail-closed behavior;
* independently verifiable reconstruction evidence.

Successful completion certifies the Historian Office's Historical Reconstruction Runtime as constitutionally executable, observable, deterministic, and ready for independent ECS-003 behavioral certification.
