# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002A-012

# Complete Mutation Coverage and Certification Gate Remediation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This remediation order establishes the final mutation-coverage and certification-gate correction required to complete the Behavioral Completion Program for the Enterprise Learning Office.

Its purpose is to eliminate the remaining ECS-004 certification deficiency identified during execution of the authoritative clean-room certification workflow.

The authoritative workflow successfully completed repository verification, behavioral regeneration, evidence validation, baseline comparison, focused testing, and certification-report generation.

However, the workflow executed only eight of the sixteen mutations declared by the authoritative ECS-004 mutation inventory and incorrectly permitted a final `PASS` disposition.

This order shall require complete mutation execution, exact inventory reconciliation, and fail-closed certification gating.

It shall not establish new constitutional capabilities.

# Constitutional Mission

The mission of this remediation is to ensure that the authoritative ECS-004 certification workflow cannot emit `PASS` unless every mutation declared by the authoritative mutation inventory has been:

* discovered;
* executed;
* observed;
* reconciled;
* evidenced;
* verified to fail closed.

Mutation completeness shall be established through executable evidence rather than declaration.

# Authoritative Mutation Inventory

The authoritative ECS-004 mutation inventory shall contain exactly sixteen required mutation classes unless a later constitutional order explicitly modifies that inventory.

The required mutations shall include:

1. missing dataset;
2. altered dataset hash;
3. missing feature lineage;
4. missing experiment evidence;
5. invalid hypothesis uncertainty;
6. invalid model provenance;
7. missing explainability;
8. unauthorized publication;
9. operational authority assignment;
10. attempted establishment of enterprise truth;
11. altered evidence;
12. missing evidence;
13. invalid evidence schema;
14. nondeterministic execution;
15. repository tampering;
16. dependency drift.

Each mutation shall possess a unique immutable identifier.

---

# Mutation Implementation Requirements

Implement executable behavior for every mutation declared by the authoritative inventory.

Each mutation implementation shall define:

* mutation identifier;
* constitutional requirement challenged;
* target artifact or behavior;
* mutation procedure;
* expected failure code;
* expected failure classification;
* required evidence;
* cleanup procedure;
* restoration verification.

No mutation may be represented solely by documentation, configuration, or an inventory entry.

---

# Mutation Discovery

The certification runtime shall independently discover all mutation implementations.

Discovery shall not depend upon a manually maintained execution count.

The runtime shall compare:

* mutation identifiers declared by the authoritative inventory;
* mutation identifiers implemented;
* mutation identifiers discovered;
* mutation identifiers executed;
* mutation identifiers evidenced.

Any mismatch shall fail certification.

---

# Mutation Execution

The authoritative clean-room command shall execute all sixteen mutations during every certification run.

For each mutation, the runtime shall record:

* execution identifier;
* mutation identifier;
* target behavior;
* pre-mutation integrity state;
* applied mutation;
* observed result;
* observed failure code;
* expected failure code;
* evidence artifact;
* restoration result;
* final mutation disposition.

Mutations shall execute in deterministic isolation.

One mutation shall not alter the result of another.

---

# Expected-Failure Verification

A mutation shall pass verification only when:

* the mutation executes successfully;
* the affected behavior rejects the mutated condition;
* the observed failure matches the expected constitutional failure;
* objective failure evidence is generated;
* the original state is restored;
* post-restoration verification succeeds.

A process crash, missing dependency, malformed test harness, or unrelated exception shall not satisfy expected-failure verification.

---

# Unexpected-Pass Enforcement

Any mutation that produces successful runtime behavior where constitutional rejection is expected shall be classified as an unexpected pass.

One unexpected pass shall force the overall certification status to `FAIL`.

Unexpected passes shall not be:

* suppressed;
* waived;
* downgraded;
* omitted;
* classified as warnings;
* excluded after execution.

---

# Inventory Reconciliation Gate

Before final certification, the runtime shall calculate:

* mutations declared;
* mutations implemented;
* mutations discovered;
* mutations executed;
* expected failures observed;
* unexpected passes;
* execution errors;
* missing evidence records.

Certification shall require:

* sixteen mutations declared;
* sixteen mutations implemented;
* sixteen mutations discovered;
* sixteen mutations executed;
* sixteen expected failures observed;
* zero unexpected passes;
* zero execution errors;
* zero missing mutation evidence records.

Any deviation shall prevent `PASS`.

---

# Mutation Evidence

Every mutation shall generate immutable machine-readable evidence containing:

* mutation identifier;
* governing constitutional requirement;
* repository identity;
* execution environment;
* target identity;
* mutation input;
* expected result;
* observed result;
* expected failure code;
* observed failure code;
* evidence digest;
* restoration status;
* final disposition.

Mutation evidence shall validate against an authoritative schema.

Invalid mutation evidence shall fail certification.

---

# Mutation Summary Report

Generate a machine-readable mutation summary containing:

* authoritative inventory size;
* implementation count;
* discovery count;
* execution count;
* expected-failure count;
* unexpected-pass count;
* error count;
* missing-evidence count;
* per-mutation dispositions;
* aggregate mutation status.

The aggregate status shall be `PASS` only when all required reconciliation conditions are satisfied.

---

# Certification Gate Remediation

Modify the authoritative ECS-004 certification runtime so that final certification is calculated from all required gates.

The workflow shall not emit `PASS` unless all of the following pass:

* repository verification;
* repository provenance verification;
* dependency verification;
* behavioral regeneration;
* evidence generation;
* evidence-schema validation;
* baseline equivalence comparison;
* focused test execution;
* complete mutation execution;
* mutation inventory reconciliation;
* completion review;
* certification-report validation.

The mutation gate shall consume the authoritative mutation summary directly.

A hard-coded mutation count or manually asserted completion status is prohibited.

---

# Certification Status Rules

The authoritative runtime shall emit only:

* `PASS`;
* `FAIL`;
* `INCOMPLETE`.

It shall emit `PASS` only when every required gate completes successfully.

It shall emit `FAIL` when:

* any mutation unexpectedly passes;
* an observed failure does not match its expected failure;
* any mutation evidence is invalid;
* any baseline comparison fails;
* any focused test fails;
* any constitutional violation remains;
* any certification report misrepresents observed execution.

It shall emit `INCOMPLETE` when:

* a required mutation is missing;
* a required mutation is not discovered;
* a required mutation is not executed;
* required evidence is absent;
* execution cannot complete because of an unavailable declared dependency.

---

# Residual Git-Discovery Remediation

Identify every Git-discovery command reachable during the authoritative certification workflow and its focused test suite.

For each reachable command:

* remove the dependency where unnecessary;
* replace it with repository-contained identity behavior where required;
* or explicitly classify and verify the approved fallback behavior.

The clean-room execution shall not emit uncontrolled:

```text
fatal: not a git repository
```

messages.

Repository identity shall remain derivable without a `.git` directory.

Residual Git-discovery behavior shall not affect:

* execution outcome;
* evidence content;
* repository identity;
* mutation results;
* certification disposition.

---

# Completion Criteria

This remediation shall be considered complete only when:

* all sixteen authoritative mutations are implemented;
* all sixteen mutations are independently discovered;
* all sixteen mutations execute through the authoritative workflow;
* all sixteen produce the expected constitutional failures;
* no mutation unexpectedly passes;
* no mutation execution error remains;
* every mutation generates valid evidence;
* mutation inventory reconciliation passes exactly;
* residual Git-discovery warnings are eliminated or constitutionally controlled;
* the certification workflow fails closed on incomplete mutation coverage;
* the machine-readable and human-readable reports accurately reflect observed results;
* the authoritative clean-room command completes without manual intervention.

Successful completion of this order authorizes one final independent **Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-004 Clean-Room Certification Audit**.

No further remediation campaign shall be required unless that audit discovers a new constitutional or implementation deficiency outside the scope of this order.
