CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02-004

# Historical Integrity Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional doctrine governing immutable Closed Position Truth, correction lineage, supersession lineage, provenance, archival integrity, historical retrieval, and auditability.

This order shall ensure that every constitutionally finalized Closed Position Truth object remains permanently preserved, independently traceable, and reconstructable without historical overwrite or destructive mutation.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not perform implementation certification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not alter constitutional authority established under CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01.
* Not permit finalized truth objects to be modified.
* Not permit correction through overwrite.
* Not permit supersession to destroy predecessor records.
* Not permit archival activity to reduce historical accessibility or auditability.

Historical truth shall remain immutable.

Corrections shall occur only through successor records.

Supersession shall preserve complete lineage.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Immutability Doctrine

Establish that every constitutionally finalized Closed Position Truth object shall be immutable.

Finalized objects shall not permit:

* field replacement;
* destructive amendment;
* identity reuse;
* deletion;
* silent reclassification;
* retroactive state mutation;
* alteration of provenance;
* alteration of evidence lineage.

Immutability shall apply to:

* Closed Position Records;
* Closure Determinations;
* Closure Validation Results;
* Closure Evidence Records;
* Settlement Verification Records;
* Reconciliation Records;
* Residual Position Resolutions;
* Realized Outcome Records;
* Correction Records;
* Supersession Records;
* Historical Closure Records;
* Archival Records.

---

# 2. Historical Truth Finalization

Define constitutional finalization as the transition after which a canonical truth object becomes permanently historical and may no longer be mutated.

Finalization shall require:

* canonical identity;
* constitutional owner;
* valid lifecycle state;
* complete provenance;
* required evidence;
* reconciliation status;
* applicable settlement disposition;
* historical timestamp;
* lineage status.

No object lacking required evidence shall become finalized truth.

---

# 3. Correction Doctrine

Establish correction as:

> The creation of a new authoritative successor object that corrects a prior truth record while preserving the prior record unchanged.

A correction shall never alter the predecessor object.

Every correction shall include:

* Correction Record identity;
* predecessor identity;
* successor identity;
* corrected truth element;
* correction reason;
* correction authority;
* correction evidence;
* correction timestamp;
* effective applicability;
* reconciliation status.

A correction shall not erase the fact that the predecessor was once authoritative.

---

# 4. Correction Authority

Closed Position Truth owns correction authority only for truth elements constitutionally owned by the Closed Position Truth Office.

Closed Position Truth shall not correct:

* broker-owned facts;
* Position Registry truth;
* Trader execution records;
* authorization truth;
* risk truth;
* market evidence;
* Performance Truth analytics.

When an upstream source is corrected, Closed Position Truth shall determine whether a constitutionally governed successor Closed Position Truth object is required.

Upstream correction shall never directly mutate Closed Position Truth history.

---

# 5. Supersession Doctrine

Establish supersession as:

> The constitutional replacement of an authoritative Closed Position Truth object by a successor object while preserving the predecessor as immutable historical truth.

Every supersession shall define:

* predecessor object;
* successor object;
* supersession reason;
* supersession authority;
* supersession evidence;
* effective time;
* lineage relationship;
* reconciliation result.

A superseded object shall:

* remain retrievable;
* remain auditable;
* remain immutable;
* retain its original identity;
* retain its original evidence;
* retain its original historical status.

---

# 6. Version Identity Constitution

Every canonical version shall possess a unique identity.

Version identity shall distinguish:

* original record;
* corrected record;
* superseding record;
* archived representation.

Versioning shall not overwrite prior identities.

Every successor shall reference its immediate predecessor.

Every predecessor shall identify its successor when one exists.

No two authoritative versions shall share the same canonical identity.

---

# 7. Historical Lineage Constitution

Establish complete lineage:

```text
Original Closed Position Truth
        ↓
Correction or Supersession Event
        ↓
Successor Closed Position Truth
        ↓
Subsequent Successor, if applicable
        ↓
Historical Archive
```

Lineage shall preserve:

* origin;
* predecessor relationships;
* successor relationships;
* correction events;
* supersession events;
* effective applicability periods;
* governing authority;
* supporting evidence.

No orphan successor shall exist.

No broken predecessor chain shall exist.

---

# 8. Provenance Constitution

Every historical object shall possess complete provenance identifying:

* originating office;
* originating event;
* source objects;
* source authorities;
* evidence identities;
* creation authority;
* creation time;
* reconciliation basis;
* settlement basis;
* correction or supersession basis, where applicable.

Provenance shall be append-only.

Provenance shall not be inferred after finalization.

Missing required provenance shall prohibit finalization.

---

# 9. Archival Doctrine

Archival shall preserve authoritative historical truth without changing ownership, meaning, identity, or lineage.

Archival eligibility shall require:

* finalized lifecycle state;
* complete evidence;
* complete provenance;
* completed reconciliation;
* resolved correction status;
* resolved supersession status;
* no pending constitutional action.

Archival shall not:

* delete source records;
* collapse versions;
* remove evidence;
* remove provenance;
* alter identity;
* convert authoritative truth into analytical truth.

---

# 10. Archival Authority

Closed Position Truth owns determination of archival eligibility for its canonical objects.

Historian owns historical custody after constitutionally authorized archival transfer.

Custody transfer shall not transfer constitutional ownership.

Historian shall preserve:

* object identity;
* authoritative status;
* lineage;
* provenance;
* evidence references;
* retrieval capability;
* auditability.

---

# 11. Historical Retrieval Constitution

Every historical object shall remain retrievable by:

* canonical identity;
* closed position identity;
* predecessor identity;
* successor identity;
* correction identity;
* supersession identity;
* closure timestamp;
* archival identity.

Retrieval shall distinguish:

* currently applicable truth;
* prior authoritative truth;
* corrected truth;
* superseded truth;
* archived truth.

Historical retrieval shall never present multiple versions as simultaneously current.

---

# 12. Historical Auditability Constitution

Historical audit reconstruction shall permit independent determination of:

* why truth was created;
* what evidence supported it;
* which authority created it;
* when it became authoritative;
* whether it was later corrected;
* whether it was later superseded;
* which version is currently applicable;
* whether archival custody is complete.

Auditability shall not depend solely on:

* completion reports;
* metadata summaries;
* manually asserted lineage;
* synthetic reconstruction;
* implementation logs lacking constitutional evidence.

---

# 13. Destructive Action Prohibitions

Explicitly prohibit:

* historical overwrite;
* record deletion;
* destructive correction;
* identity reuse;
* predecessor replacement;
* lineage truncation;
* provenance alteration;
* evidence detachment;
* mutable archival records;
* silent successor substitution;
* reopening of constitutionally closed positions.

A closed position shall remain historically closed.

A correction may change the authoritative description of closure.

A correction shall not erase the historical closure event.

---

# 14. Historical Reconciliation

For every correction, supersession, or archival action, verify:

* predecessor preservation;
* successor validity;
* authority validity;
* evidence completeness;
* lineage completeness;
* applicability determination;
* archival custody status;
* historical retrieval integrity.

Unresolved reconciliation shall prohibit successor finalization or archival completion.
