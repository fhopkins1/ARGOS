CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02-003

# Canonical Lifecycle Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional lifecycle governance for every canonical object owned by the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall define the complete state model governing proposed closure objects, validation objects, reconciliation objects, settlement-verification objects, exception objects, authoritative Closed Position Truth objects, correction objects, supersession objects, and archival objects.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional doctrine only.

Every lifecycle state shall possess one constitutional meaning.

Every lifecycle transition shall possess one governing authority.

Every transition shall require constitutionally admissible evidence.

No transition shall create authority outside the Closed Position Truth Office boundaries established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01**.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Lifecycle progression shall never be inferred.

Execution completion shall not equal constitutional closure.

Settlement completion shall not equal constitutional closure.

Zero remaining quantity shall not independently equal constitutional closure.

Analytical confidence shall not equal constitutional eligibility.

A canonical Closed Position Record shall be created only after every required closure condition has been satisfied.

No authoritative truth object shall possess a provisional, degraded, uncertain, or partially authoritative state.

Before authoritative creation, an object may represent an unresolved closure process.

After authoritative creation, the Closed Position Record shall remain immutable.

Correction shall create a successor object.

Supersession shall preserve the predecessor.

Archival shall preserve ownership, identity, provenance, and lineage.

A constitutionally closed position shall never transition back into an open-position state.

---

# Canonical Lifecycle Scope

This order governs lifecycle participation for:

* Closed Position Record;
* Closed Position Identity;
* Closure Determination;
* Closure Validation Result;
* Closure Evidence Record;
* Settlement Verification Record;
* Reconciliation Record;
* Residual Position Resolution;
* Realized Outcome Record;
* Correction Record;
* Supersession Record;
* Closure Exception Record;
* Historical Closure Record;
* Archival Record.

Each object shall participate only in lifecycle states consistent with its constitutional purpose.

Supporting objects shall not independently create authoritative Closed Position Truth.

---

# Authoritative Lifecycle Model

The constitutional closure lifecycle shall be:

```text
Proposed
↓
Pending Closure Validation
↓
Pending Reconciliation
↓
Pending Settlement Verification
↓
Eligible for Closure
↓
Constitutionally Closed
↓
Archived
```

Exceptional and historical branches shall include:

```text
Pending Closure Validation
→ Exception

Pending Reconciliation
→ Exception

Pending Settlement Verification
→ Exception

Exception
→ Pending Closure Validation

Exception
→ Pending Reconciliation

Exception
→ Pending Settlement Verification

Constitutionally Closed
→ Corrected
→ Superseded
→ Archived
```

A corrected or superseding record shall enter the authoritative lifecycle as a new canonical object.

The predecessor shall remain constitutionally closed and historically immutable.

# Lifecycle State Registry

## Proposed

### Purpose

Represent a candidate closure process that has been initiated but has not yet passed constitutional closure validation.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Entry Requirements

* valid candidate position identity;
* identifiable initiating event;
* constitutional basis for closure evaluation;
* initial provenance record.

### Required Evidence

* candidate identity;
* initiating authority;
* initiation timestamp;
* source references.

### Permitted Transitions

* Proposed → Pending Closure Validation;
* Proposed → Exception.

### Prohibited Meaning

Proposed shall not mean:

* execution complete;
* reconciliation complete;
* settlement verified;
* eligible for closure;
* constitutionally closed.

---

## Pending Closure Validation

### Purpose

Represent active validation of the constitutional prerequisites for closure.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Required Validation

* position identity is authoritative;
* execution evidence is complete;
* execution activity is within scope;
* required source provenance exists;
* no unresolved identity conflict exists.

### Required Evidence

* Closure Validation Result;
* source inventory;
* validation findings;
* contradiction record where applicable.

### Permitted Transitions

* Pending Closure Validation → Pending Reconciliation;
* Pending Closure Validation → Exception.

### Prohibited Transitions

* Pending Closure Validation → Eligible for Closure;
* Pending Closure Validation → Constitutionally Closed.

---

## Pending Reconciliation

### Purpose

Represent reconciliation of execution facts, position state, quantities, duplicates, corrections, and relevant upstream records.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office for closure reconciliation, while upstream offices retain ownership of their source facts.

### Required Reconciliation

* execution population reconciled;
* duplicate executions resolved;
* execution corrections resolved;
* position quantity reconciled;
* conflicting source facts resolved;
* zero remaining quantity established or residual quantity routed to resolution.

### Required Evidence

* Reconciliation Record;
* authoritative execution references;
* Position Registry references;
* discrepancy disposition;
* quantity reconciliation.

### Permitted Transitions

* Pending Reconciliation → Pending Settlement Verification;
* Pending Reconciliation → Exception.

### Prohibited Transitions

* Pending Reconciliation → Constitutionally Closed;
* Pending Reconciliation → Archived.

---

## Pending Settlement Verification

### Purpose

Represent verification that all constitutionally required settlement conditions are satisfied or that an explicit constitutional settlement exemption applies.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office for closure eligibility determination.

Broker or other designated settlement authority shall retain ownership of settlement facts.

### Required Verification

* settlement status identified;
* settlement facts possess authoritative provenance;
* settlement discrepancies resolved;
* exemption authority established where settlement is not required.

### Required Evidence

* Settlement Verification Record;
* settlement source references;
* exemption record where applicable;
* settlement discrepancy disposition.

### Permitted Transitions

* Pending Settlement Verification → Eligible for Closure;
* Pending Settlement Verification → Exception.

### Prohibited Transitions

* Pending Settlement Verification → Constitutionally Closed without eligibility determination.

---

## Eligible for Closure

### Purpose

Represent the determination that every constitutional condition required for authoritative Closed Position Truth creation has been satisfied.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Required Conditions

* closure validation succeeded;
* execution reconciliation completed;
* zero remaining position quantity confirmed;
* residual quantity resolved;
* settlement requirements satisfied or constitutionally exempt;
* required realized-outcome inputs reconciled;
* no unresolved contradiction remains;
* evidence lineage is complete.

### Required Evidence

* Closure Determination;
* completed Reconciliation Record;
* Settlement Verification Record;
* authoritative position-state reference;
* closure evidence package.

### Permitted Transitions

* Eligible for Closure → Constitutionally Closed;
* Eligible for Closure → Exception if a previously hidden contradiction is discovered before authoritative creation.

### Restrictions

Eligibility shall not itself constitute authoritative Closed Position Truth.

---

## Constitutionally Closed

### Purpose

Represent the existence of authoritative enterprise truth that the identified position has satisfied every constitutional closure condition.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Entry Requirements

* valid Eligible for Closure state;
* authoritative Closed Position Record created;
* unique canonical identity assigned;
* closure timestamp established;
* closure reason established;
* realized outcome established within constitutional scope;
* provenance and evidence lineage completed.

### Required Evidence

* authoritative Closed Position Record;
* authoritative creation event;
* closure evidence references;
* final reconciliation result;
* final settlement-verification result;
* Position Registry zero-quantity reference.

### Constitutional Effect

The position is authoritative closed-position truth within the enterprise.

### Restrictions

A Constitutionally Closed record shall not:

* reopen;
* return to a pending state;
* be mutated;
* be deleted;
* be silently replaced;
* lose predecessor or successor lineage.

### Permitted Transitions

* Constitutionally Closed → Archived;
* Constitutionally Closed → Corrected through creation of a successor correction object;
* Constitutionally Closed → Superseded through creation of a successor authoritative record.

The original record itself shall not change state by field mutation.

---

## Exception

### Purpose

Represent a closure process that cannot lawfully progress because one or more required constitutional conditions are absent, contradictory, unresolved, or inadmissible.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Exception Conditions

* missing execution evidence;
* conflicting broker facts;
* incomplete reconciliation;
* unresolved duplicate execution;
* nonzero residual quantity;
* incomplete settlement;
* stale evidence;
* identity mismatch;
* incomplete provenance;
* failed evidence integrity;
* unresolved authority conflict.

### Required Evidence

* Closure Exception Record;
* affected object identity;
* exception category;
* source references;
* responsible resolving authority;
* reopening conditions.

### Permitted Transitions

Exception may return only to the appropriate unresolved lifecycle state after the constitutional defect is resolved:

* Exception → Pending Closure Validation;
* Exception → Pending Reconciliation;
* Exception → Pending Settlement Verification.

### Restrictions

Exception shall not represent degraded authoritative truth.

An Exception object shall not become Constitutionally Closed without re-entering and completing all required lifecycle stages.

---

## Corrected

### Purpose

Represent that an error affecting a constitutionally closed record has been established and that a successor correction process has been initiated.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Entry Requirements

* demonstrable error exists;
* authoritative correcting evidence exists;
* correction authority is valid;
* predecessor identity is preserved.

### Required Evidence

* Correction Record;
* correction rationale;
* predecessor reference;
* correcting evidence;
* correction authority.

### Constitutional Effect

Corrected shall not mutate the predecessor.

A new candidate successor shall pass all applicable validation and reconciliation requirements before becoming authoritative.

### Permitted Transition

* Corrected → Superseded.

---

## Superseded

### Purpose

Represent that a successor Closed Position Record has become the current authoritative record while the predecessor remains immutable and historically accessible.

### Governing Authority

Closed Position Truth Office.

### Entry Requirements

* valid successor record exists;
* successor satisfies applicable closure requirements;
* predecessor-successor relationship is explicit;
* supersession rationale is recorded;
* only one current authoritative record exists.

### Required Evidence

* Supersession Record;
* predecessor identity;
* successor identity;
* supersession authority;
* supersession timestamp;
* supporting evidence.

### Restrictions

Supersession shall not:

* erase the predecessor;
* modify the predecessor;
* create parallel current authority;
* reopen the position.

### Permitted Transition

* Superseded → Archived.

---

## Archived

### Purpose

Represent permanent historical custody of a finalized Closed Position Truth object and its complete lineage.

### Governing Authority

Historian for custody.

Closed Position Truth shall retain constitutional ownership of the truth domain.

### Entry Requirements

* archival eligibility established;
* authoritative lifecycle complete;
* unresolved correction state absent;
* unresolved supersession state absent;
* evidence lineage complete;
* integrity verification complete;
* retention classification established.

### Required Evidence

* Archival Record;
* custody-transfer record;
* integrity verification;
* complete lineage references.

### Restrictions

Archived records shall not:

* return to active lifecycle states;
* be mutated;
* be deleted through ordinary lifecycle action;
* lose provenance or lineage.

# Prohibited Transition Registry

The following transitions are constitutionally prohibited:

* Proposed → Constitutionally Closed;
* Pending Closure Validation → Constitutionally Closed;
* Pending Reconciliation → Constitutionally Closed;
* Pending Settlement Verification → Constitutionally Closed;
* Exception → Constitutionally Closed;
* Exception → Archived as authoritative truth;
* Constitutionally Closed → Proposed;
* Constitutionally Closed → Pending Closure Validation;
* Constitutionally Closed → Pending Reconciliation;
* Constitutionally Closed → Pending Settlement Verification;
* Constitutionally Closed → open-position state;
* Archived → active lifecycle state;
* Superseded → current authoritative status without a new constitutional supersession event.

No implementation may create an equivalent bypass under alternate terminology.

# Duplicate Prevention Constitution

Every canonical object shall possess a deterministic identity sufficient to prevent duplicate authoritative truth.

Before authoritative Closed Position Truth creation, the Office shall verify:

* no authoritative Closed Position Record already exists for the same canonical closure identity;
* no concurrent candidate can create parallel truth;
* no duplicate execution population has been counted;
* no duplicate lifecycle event has altered eligibility.

A duplicate request or duplicate transition shall not create a second authoritative object.

Duplicate activity shall produce:

* duplicate detection evidence;
* reference to the existing authoritative event or object;
* deterministic no-op or reconciliation disposition.

---

# Idempotency Constitution

Repeated processing of the same constitutionally identical input shall produce the same lifecycle result.

Idempotent reprocessing shall not:

* create a duplicate Closed Position Record;
* create duplicate correction lineage;
* create duplicate supersession lineage;
* change an authoritative timestamp;
* create a second execution or reconciliation path;
* reactivate an Exception without resolving evidence.

Idempotency shall preserve the first valid authoritative result.

---

# Replay Constitution

Replay shall reproduce the original constitutional lifecycle outcome from the same admissible evidence population and governing doctrine version.

Replay shall preserve:

* event order;
* object identities;
* authority identities;
* source evidence;
* lifecycle decisions;
* exception outcomes;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage.

Replay shall not:

* create new authoritative truth;
* reopen a closed position;
* alter archived history;
* convert previously inadmissible evidence into admissible evidence without a new constitutional event;
* create duplicate lifecycle transitions.

---

# Recovery Constitution

Recovery after interruption shall restore the last constitutionally established lifecycle state.

Recovery shall:

* identify the last accepted transition;
* preserve incomplete work as non-authoritative;
* reject partial authoritative creation;
* revalidate unresolved dependencies;
* require reconciliation before further progression;
* preserve all evidence generated before interruption.

Recovery shall never infer successful completion from incomplete processing.

---

# Correction Behavior Constitution

Correction shall begin only after authoritative error evidence exists.

Correction shall:

* preserve the predecessor object;
* create a Correction Record;
* create a candidate successor identity;
* repeat all constitutionally affected validation steps;
* preserve unchanged source lineage;
* reconcile changed facts;
* create a successor Closed Position Record only after eligibility is re-established.

The predecessor shall remain historically valid as the record that was authoritative during its original period.

---

# Supersession Behavior Constitution

A successor shall become authoritative only after:

* correction requirements are satisfied;
* successor validation is complete;
* successor reconciliation is complete;
* successor settlement requirements are satisfied or exempt;
* predecessor-successor lineage is complete;
* no parallel authoritative successor exists.

Supersession shall change current-authority designation through a constitutional event.

It shall not mutate either record.

---

# Archival Eligibility Constitution

A canonical object shall become eligible for archival only when:

* its lifecycle purpose is complete;
* required evidence is retained;
* provenance is complete;
* correction and supersession status is resolved;
* no unresolved Exception affects its authority;
* integrity verification succeeds;
* retention classification is assigned.

Archival eligibility shall not permit destruction of operational or historical lineage.
