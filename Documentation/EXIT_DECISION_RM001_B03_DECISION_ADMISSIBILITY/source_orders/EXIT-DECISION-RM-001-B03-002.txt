# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B03-002

# Decision Evaluation and Recommendation Constitution

## Objective

Establish constitutional governance for Exit Decision evaluation, recommendation generation, and decision formation.

This order exists solely to define the separation between analytical assessment, exit-condition evaluation, recommendation authority, and final decision authority.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every evaluation shall possess one governing authority.

Every recommendation shall possess one governing authority.

Every decision shall possess one governing authority.

Every rationale shall possess one evidence lineage.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Evaluation shall not equal recommendation.

Recommendation shall not equal decision.

Decision shall not equal authorization.

Authorization shall not equal execution.

Execution shall not equal settlement.

Settlement shall not equal closed-position truth.

Exit Decision shall evaluate conditions.

Exit Decision shall not create enterprise truth owned by another office.

Exit Decision shall consume:

* authorized observations;
* admissible analytical inputs;
* valid risk constraints;
* valid authorization state;
* valid position references;
* approved operational context.

Exit Decision shall never:

* replace Analyst authority;
* replace Risk authority;
* replace Authorization authority;
* replace Trader authority;
* replace Broker authority;
* replace Position Registry authority.

Every decision shall contain:

* admissibility basis;
* evaluation basis;
* decision rationale;
* authority source;
* evidence lineage.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Evaluation Authority Constitution

Establish the authority model governing Exit Decision evaluation.

Define:

* evaluation owner;
* evaluation creator;
* evaluation inputs;
* evaluation outputs;
* evaluation constraints;
* evaluation evidence;
* evaluation correction authority;
* evaluation reconciliation authority.

Exit Decision shall own Exit Decision evaluations.

Exit Decision evaluation shall determine:

* whether exit conditions are satisfied;
* whether decision criteria are met;
* whether a recommendation or decision is appropriate.

Exit Decision evaluation shall not:

* alter source truth;
* modify risk assessments;
* modify authorization state;
* modify position state.

---

# 2. Evaluation Input Governance

Define authorized evaluation inputs.

Inputs shall include where applicable:

* position identity;
* position state;
* risk conditions;
* authorization conditions;
* monitoring conditions;
* analytical assessments;
* market context;
* temporal validity;
* operational constraints;
* emergency conditions.

For every input define:

* source owner;
* source custodian;
* admissibility requirement;
* freshness requirement;
* validation authority;
* failure behavior.

No evaluation input shall be accepted without defined ownership.

---

# 3. Evaluation Output Constitution

Define evaluation outputs.

Evaluation outputs may include:

* exit condition assessment;
* condition satisfaction state;
* constraint assessment;
* decision readiness assessment;
* confidence assessment;
* evidence sufficiency assessment.

Evaluation outputs shall not become:

* authorization objects;
* execution instructions;
* broker commands;
* position updates.

---

# 4. Analytical Assessment Separation Constitution

Establish separation between Analyst outputs and Exit Decision outputs.

Define:

## Analyst Authority

Analyst owns:

* analytical models;
* analytical interpretations;
* analytical findings;
* analytical evidence.

## Exit Decision Authority

Exit Decision owns:

* evaluation of whether exit criteria are met;
* recommendation generation;
* final decision issuance.

Exit Decision shall consume analytical outputs.

Exit Decision shall not replace analytical ownership.

Analytical assessment shall not automatically create an exit decision.

---

# 5. Exit Condition Evaluation Registry

Establish the canonical exit condition evaluation model.

For every exit condition define:

* condition identity;
* condition source;
* evaluation authority;
* evaluation criteria;
* required evidence;
* satisfaction threshold;
* failure behavior;
* expiration behavior.

Exit conditions may include:

* risk-triggered exit;
* operational-triggered exit;
* authorization-triggered exit;
* Commander-directed exit;
* time-based exit;
* emergency exit.

No exit condition shall create execution authority.

---

# 6. Recommendation Constitution

Establish recommendation governance.

Define:

* recommendation owner;
* recommendation creator;
* recommendation content;
* recommendation evidence;
* recommendation limitations;
* recommendation lifecycle.

A recommendation may contain:

* proposed action;
* supporting rationale;
* evaluated conditions;
* confidence;
* limitations;
* required dependencies.

A recommendation shall not:

* authorize execution;
* compel Trader action;
* bypass Risk controls;
* bypass Authorization controls;
* mutate enterprise truth.

---

# 7. Decision Formation Constitution

Establish final Exit Decision governance.

Define:

* decision authority;
* decision criteria;
* decision evidence;
* decision rationale;
* decision limitations;
* decision lifecycle.

A final Exit Decision shall require:

* admissible inputs;
* valid evaluation;
* complete rationale;
* required evidence;
* authorized decision authority.

A decision shall not:

* execute a trade;
* create authorization;
* replace execution systems;
* alter positions;
* modify historical records.

---

# 8. Decision Factor Registry

Establish canonical decision factors.

Define governance for:

## Exit Conditions

Determine:

* whether exit criteria are satisfied;
* whether conditions justify action.

---

## Risk Conditions

Determine:

* whether Risk-owned constraints permit decision progression.

Risk remains the owner of risk truth.

---

## Authorization Conditions

Determine:

* whether valid authorization exists;
* whether authorization remains active.

Authorizations remains the owner of authorization truth.

---

## Operational Constraints

Determine:

* operational limitations;
* execution dependencies;
* system constraints.

---

## Timing Constraints

Determine:

* validity windows;
* expiration conditions;
* temporal limitations.

---

## Emergency Conditions

Determine:

* emergency classification;
* emergency decision requirements;
* emergency evidence requirements.

Emergency conditions shall not eliminate governance requirements.

---

# 9. Decision Rationale Constitution

Establish rationale governance.

Every decision rationale shall define:

* decision basis;
* evaluated conditions;
* supporting evidence;
* rejected alternatives;
* applicable constraints;
* uncertainty factors;
* decision limitations.

Decision rationale shall preserve:

* transparency;
* traceability;
* historical lineage.

Rationale correction shall preserve original rationale history.

---

# 10. Prohibited Authority Registry

Explicitly prohibit:

* recommendations executing trades;
* evaluations creating authorizations;
* decisions bypassing Risk;
* decisions bypassing Authorizations;
* decisions modifying positions;
* decisions issuing broker instructions;
* analytical outputs becoming execution commands;
* Trader execution changing Exit Decision ownership.

This order shall complete only when:

* evaluation authority is deterministic;
* evaluation inputs possess defined ownership;
* evaluation outputs possess defined boundaries;
* analytical ownership remains preserved;
* recommendation authority is deterministic;
* recommendation cannot execute;
* recommendation cannot authorize;
* decision authority is deterministic;
* decision rationale requirements are defined;
* Risk authority remains preserved;
* Authorization authority remains preserved;
* Trader execution authority remains preserved;
* no decision formation ambiguity remains;
* no recommendation authority ambiguity remains.
