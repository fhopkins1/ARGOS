EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B03-004

# Emergency Decision, Override, and Exception Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional governance model for exceptional Exit Decision conditions requiring accelerated evaluation, emergency decision pathways, temporary overrides, or exception handling.

This order shall define emergency authority, override authority, exception governance, evidence requirements, limitations, reconciliation obligations, and precedence rules while preserving constitutional ownership boundaries.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify Exit Decision implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not perform certification activity.
* Not create unrestricted emergency authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to assume Risk authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to assume Authorization authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to assume Trader authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to assume Broker authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to mutate Position Registry truth.
* Not permit emergency conditions to eliminate historical preservation.

Emergency authority shall accelerate governance.

Emergency authority shall not expand constitutional ownership.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Emergency Decision Constitution

Establish an emergency decision as:

> A constitutionally bounded Exit Decision pathway activated when delay introduces unacceptable enterprise exposure and ordinary decision timing cannot satisfy required response objectives.

Emergency decisions shall require:

* defined emergency condition;
* identified initiating authority;
* evidence basis;
* affected decision object;
* affected enterprise domains;
* emergency justification;
* expiration condition;
* reconciliation requirement.

---

# 2. Emergency Condition Registry

Define admissible emergency conditions including:

* immediate unacceptable exposure;
* rapidly deteriorating position condition;
* loss of required decision window;
* critical operational failure;
* unavailable ordinary decision pathway;
* material conflict requiring immediate protective action.

Emergency conditions shall not include:

* convenience;
* bypass of normal governance;
* avoidance of authorization requirements;
* avoidance of risk review;
* preference for execution speed.

---

# 3. Emergency Authority Ownership

## Exit Decision Office

Owns:

* emergency decision initiation;
* emergency decision rationale;
* emergency evaluation;
* emergency decision issuance.

Does not own:

* emergency authorization issuance;
* emergency execution;
* emergency broker communication;
* emergency position mutation;
* emergency risk acceptance.

---

## Commander Office

Owns:

* enterprise-level emergency prioritization;
* strategic intent during conflicting objectives;
* escalation resolution when enterprise priorities conflict.

Commander shall not:

* directly execute trades;
* bypass authorization ownership;
* replace execution custody.

---

## Risk Office

Owns:

* emergency risk evaluation;
* emergency risk constraints;
* emergency risk acceptance where constitutionally permitted.

Risk authority remains independent from Exit Decision authority.

---

## Authorizations Office

Owns:

* emergency authorization validation;
* emergency authorization issuance;
* emergency authorization expiration.

Emergency decision issuance shall not equal authorization.

---

## Trader Office

Owns:

* emergency execution handling;
* emergency execution sequencing;
* execution-state reconciliation.

Trader shall not create emergency decisions.

---

## Broker Office

Owns:

* emergency broker communication;
* broker acknowledgment;
* external execution state.

Broker shall not determine emergency intent.

---

# 4. Override Constitution

Establish:

> An override is a controlled constitutional exception allowing a higher-authority decision to temporarily supersede a lower-priority operational constraint without transferring ownership of that constraint.

An override shall:

* identify overridden condition;
* identify overriding authority;
* define scope;
* define duration;
* define evidence basis;
* define expiration;
* define reconciliation requirements.

---

# 5. Override Limitations

No override shall permit:

* Exit Decision overriding Risk truth;
* Exit Decision overriding Authorization truth;
* Exit Decision executing trades;
* Trader overriding authorization requirements;
* Broker overriding enterprise decisions;
* Position Registry being mutated outside Position Registry authority;
* Historian records being altered.

Overrides shall modify applicability.

Overrides shall not modify ownership.

---

# 6. Emergency Evidence Constitution

Every emergency decision shall preserve:

* triggering event;
* evidence sources;
* decision rationale;
* participating authorities;
* dissenting positions;
* approvals;
* authorization state;
* execution state;
* final reconciliation outcome.

No emergency decision shall exist without evidence lineage.

---

# 7. Emergency Expiration Constitution

Every emergency authority shall possess:

* activation time;
* expiration time;
* scope limitation;
* termination condition;
* reconciliation requirement.

Emergency authority shall automatically terminate upon:

* expiration;
* completion;
* cancellation;
* replacement;
* resolution of emergency condition.

Emergency authority shall not become ordinary authority.

---

# 8. Exception Governance Registry

Define exception categories:

## Decision Exception

Owned by:

Exit Decision.

Examples:

* unavailable evaluation input;
* incomplete decision context;
* conflicting decision evidence.

---

## Risk Exception

Owned by:

Risk.

Examples:

* temporary risk constraint adjustment;
* emergency risk acceptance.

---

## Authorization Exception

Owned by:

Authorizations.

Examples:

* emergency authorization issuance;
* accelerated authorization review.

---

## Execution Exception

Owned by:

Trader and Broker.

Examples:

* emergency execution routing;
* execution recovery.

---

# 9. Emergency Precedence Matrix

| Domain               | Primary Authority | Emergency Resolution Authority |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Enterprise intent    | Commander         | Commander                      |
| Exit evaluation      | Exit Decision     | Exit Decision                  |
| Risk constraints     | Risk              | Risk                           |
| Authorization state  | Authorizations    | Authorizations                 |
| Execution handling   | Trader            | Trader                         |
| Broker communication | Broker            | Broker                         |
| Position truth       | Position Registry | Position Registry              |
| Historical truth     | Historian         | Historian                      |

No emergency pathway may transfer ownership between domains.

---

# 10. Emergency Reconciliation Constitution

Following every emergency decision, reconciliation shall verify:

## Exit Decision

Verify:

* emergency rationale;
* decision lineage;
* emergency expiration;
* decision applicability.

## Risk

Verify:

* risk state;
* risk constraint application;
* risk exception handling.

## Authorizations

Verify:

* authorization validity;
* authorization consumption;
* authorization expiration.

## Trader

Verify:

* execution alignment;
* execution lifecycle;
* execution outcome.

## Broker

Verify:

* external execution state;
* broker acknowledgment.

## Position Registry

Verify:

* position truth.

## Historian

Verify:

* complete historical record.
