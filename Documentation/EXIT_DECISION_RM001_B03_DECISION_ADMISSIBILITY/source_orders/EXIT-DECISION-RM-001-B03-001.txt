# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B03-001

# Decision Admissibility Constitution Order

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional requirements governing whether information, conditions, and dependencies are admissible for Exit Decision evaluation.

This order shall define the admissibility boundary protecting Exit Decision from invalid, incomplete, stale, contradictory, unauthorized, or insufficient inputs.

This order shall not modify implementation behavior.

This order shall not define executable decision logic.

This order shall not perform behavioral verification.

This order shall not generate implementation proof.

This order shall not execute certification activity.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

## 1. Establish Decision Admissibility Constitution

Create the authoritative constitutional model determining whether Exit Decision may evaluate a potential exit condition.

Establish that:

```text
Observation
→ Admissibility
→ Evaluation
→ Recommendation
→ Decision
```

No evaluation shall occur without successful admissibility determination.

Admissibility shall determine whether required information is:

* available;
* authoritative;
* valid;
* current;
* complete;
* internally consistent;
* constitutionally permitted.

---

# 2. Establish Admissibility Authority

Define the authority responsible for determining Exit Decision input admissibility.

The constitution shall establish:

* admissibility owner;
* admissibility decision authority;
* admissibility evidence authority;
* admissibility reconciliation authority.

Admissibility authority shall not:

* create position truth;
* create risk truth;
* create authorization truth;
* replace source offices.

Admissibility shall validate available truth without becoming the owner of that truth.

---

# 3. Establish Position Identity Admissibility Requirements

Define requirements for position identity inputs.

The registry shall require:

* unique position identity;
* authoritative Position Registry source;
* valid ownership reference;
* valid lifecycle state;
* absence of unresolved identity conflict.

Exit Decision shall reject evaluation when:

* position identity is missing;
* position identity is ambiguous;
* position identity conflicts exist;
* position ownership cannot be established.

---

# 4. Establish Position State and Quantity Admissibility Requirements

Define requirements for position state information.

The constitution shall establish:

* acceptable position states;
* acceptable quantity representations;
* quantity authority;
* state freshness requirements;
* reconciliation requirements.

Exit Decision shall not independently calculate or replace position truth.

Position state ownership remains with Position Registry.

---

# 5. Establish Risk State Admissibility Requirements

Define admissibility requirements for Risk inputs.

The registry shall establish:

* approved Risk source;
* risk-state identity;
* risk-state validity;
* risk-state freshness;
* unresolved risk conflict handling.

Exit Decision shall consume Risk truth.

Exit Decision shall not:

* modify Risk truth;
* replace Risk evaluation;
* override Risk ownership.

---

# 6. Establish Authorization State Admissibility Requirements

Define authorization admissibility requirements.

The constitution shall establish:

* authorization identity;
* authorization source;
* authorization validity;
* authorization expiration;
* authorization revocation state;
* authorization scope.

Exit Decision shall reject evaluation when:

* authorization is missing;
* authorization is expired;
* authorization is revoked;
* authorization scope is insufficient.

Exit Decision shall not create authorization authority.

---

# 7. Establish Monitoring and Analytical Input Admissibility

Define admissibility requirements for:

* Monitoring findings;
* Sentinel observations;
* Analyst outputs;
* market context;
* operational constraints.

For each input define:

* source authority;
* ownership;
* permitted use;
* evidence requirement;
* freshness requirement.

Observation shall not automatically become decision authority.

---

# 8. Establish Temporal Validity Requirements

Define temporal admissibility requirements.

The constitution shall establish:

* timestamp requirements;
* freshness windows;
* expiration rules;
* temporal ordering;
* stale-data handling.

Exit Decision shall reject inputs that exceed constitutional validity periods.

---

# 9. Establish Completeness Requirements

Define minimum information requirements required before evaluation.

The registry shall define:

* mandatory inputs;
* optional inputs;
* conditional inputs;
* missing-input behavior.

Incomplete information shall result in:

* rejection;
* deferral;
* escalation.

Incomplete information shall never silently become a valid decision basis.

---

# 10. Establish Source Authority and Independence Requirements

Define source admissibility requirements.

Every input shall possess:

* one authoritative source;
* one ownership authority;
* one custody authority;
* one evidence lineage.

The constitution shall prohibit:

* duplicate competing truth sources;
* unauthorized derived truth;
* circular dependency;
* self-validation.

---

# 11. Establish Contradiction Handling Constitution

Define handling of conflicting inputs.

The constitution shall establish:

* contradiction detection;
* contradiction classification;
* contradiction ownership;
* reconciliation pathway;
* escalation requirements.

Exit Decision shall not resolve ownership disputes by assuming authority.

---

# 12. Establish Confidence and Evidence Sufficiency Requirements

Define evidence sufficiency requirements.

Each admissible input shall possess:

* supporting evidence;
* provenance;
* confidence classification;
* integrity validation.

Insufficient evidence shall result in:

* rejection;
* deferral;
* escalation.

---

# 13. Establish Mandatory Rejection Registry

Create the admissibility rejection registry.

The registry shall define mandatory rejection conditions:

* missing position truth;
* stale position truth;
* invalid authorization;
* expired authorization;
* revoked authorization;
* contradictory risk state;
* unresolved identity mismatch;
* insufficient evidence;
* invalid provenance;
* unauthorized source;
* incomplete decision context.

For each rejection condition define:

* rejection authority;
* rejection evidence;
* rejection outcome;
* reconciliation requirement.

---

# 14. Establish Admissibility Evidence Registry

Create the evidence registry defining evidence required for admissibility.

The registry shall include:

* evidence identity;
* evidence source;
* evidence owner;
* evidence custodian;
* evidence timestamp;
* retention requirement.

Evidence shall preserve lineage through the complete Exit Decision lifecycle.

---

# 15. Establish Failure Behavior

Define deterministic behavior when admissibility fails.

Failure outcomes shall include:

* rejection;
* deferral;
* escalation;
* request for additional information.

Failure shall not:

* produce an invalid decision;
* authorize execution;
* bypass controls;
* mutate enterprise truth.

---

# 16. Establish Completion Report

Produce a completion report confirming:

* admissibility rules established;
* admissibility authority established;
* required inputs defined;
* rejection conditions defined;
* evidence requirements defined;
* failure behavior defined.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order is complete only when:

* every Exit Decision input possesses admissibility requirements;
* every input source possesses defined authority;
* every invalid input possesses deterministic rejection behavior;
* position truth ownership remains with Position Registry;
* risk truth ownership remains with Risk;
* authorization truth ownership remains with Authorizations;
* evidence lineage requirements are established;
* stale and contradictory information handling is deterministic;
* no admissibility ambiguity remains.

Successful completion authorizes progression to:

> **EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B03-002 — Decision Evaluation and Recommendation Constitution**.
