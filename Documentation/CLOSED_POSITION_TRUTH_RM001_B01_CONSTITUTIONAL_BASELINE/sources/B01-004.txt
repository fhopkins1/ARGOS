# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01-004

# Dependency and Truth-Source Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional dependency model and truth-source hierarchy governing the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall define every authoritative upstream dependency, downstream consumer, dependency direction, truth-source precedence rule, and conflict-resolution pathway required to derive constitutionally authoritative Closed Position Truth.

This order shall establish one deterministic truth derivation model for constitutionally closed positions.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not perform implementation certification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not permit Closed Position Truth to originate broker-owned facts.
* Not permit analytical outputs to become authoritative truth.
* Not permit conflicting truth sources to coexist as authoritative.
* Not permit authority sharing between constitutional offices.

Closed Position Truth shall derive enterprise truth.

Closed Position Truth shall not create upstream truth.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Constitutional Dependency Doctrine

Establish that every dependency shall possess:

* one constitutional owner;
* one dependency direction;
* one constitutional purpose;
* one authoritative interface;
* one reconciliation authority;
* one failure behavior.

No dependency shall exist without constitutional justification.

No dependency shall imply ownership.

Position Registry remains the authoritative owner of all position truth.

---

## Exit Decision Office

Provides:

* exit decision identity;
* decision rationale;
* decision lineage;
* decision authority.

Exit Decision shall not determine closure truth.

---

## Risk Office

* realized risk context;
* final risk disposition.

Risk shall not own Closed Position Truth.

# 5. Truth-Source Precedence Constitution

When conflicting information exists, constitutional precedence shall be:

1. Broker-originated execution facts
2. Execution reconciliation results
3. Position Registry reconciliation
4. Constitutional closure validation
5. Closed Position Truth
6. Performance Truth derivatives
7. Analytical interpretations

Derived records shall never supersede authoritative records.

Analytical conclusions shall never supersede constitutional truth.

---

# 6. Discrepancy Resolution Constitution

## Broker Discrepancies

When broker evidence conflicts:

* suspend truth creation;
* perform reconciliation;
* preserve evidence;
* prohibit authoritative closure.

---

## Reconciliation Failures

When reconciliation fails:

* prohibit closure;
* preserve intermediate evidence;
* generate reconciliation findings.

---

## Incomplete Settlement

When settlement remains constitutionally required:

* prohibit authoritative closure.

When settlement is constitutionally exempt:

* permit closure in accordance with governing doctrine.

---

## Duplicate Executions

Duplicate execution evidence shall:

* trigger reconciliation;
* prohibit duplicate truth creation;
* preserve all evidence.

---

## Stale Evidence

Stale evidence shall:

* fail admissibility;
* prohibit truth creation.

---

## Degraded Analytical Inputs

Analytical degradation shall:

* never create authoritative truth;
* never invalidate existing authoritative truth.
