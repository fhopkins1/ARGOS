# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01-001

# Constitutional Charter Order

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional identity of the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall define the constitutional purpose, mission, authority, responsibilities, limitations, success criteria, and failure conditions governing the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall establish the Office as the exclusive constitutional owner of authoritative enterprise truth concerning constitutionally closed trading positions.

This order shall not modify implementation behavior.

This order shall not perform behavioral verification.

This order shall not generate implementation proof.

This order shall not execute certification activity.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

## 1. Establish Constitutional Charter

Create the authoritative Constitutional Charter for the Closed Position Truth Office.

The Charter shall define:

* constitutional identity;
* constitutional purpose;
* constitutional mission;
* constitutional authority;
* constitutional jurisdiction;
* constitutional responsibilities;
* constitutional limitations;
* constitutional success criteria;
* constitutional failure conditions.

The Charter shall become the governing constitutional authority for the Office.

---

# 2. Establish Constitutional Purpose

Define the constitutional purpose of the Closed Position Truth Office.

The Office exists solely to:

* establish authoritative enterprise truth for constitutionally closed trading positions;
* preserve deterministic historical truth regarding closed positions;
* determine constitutional closure status using authorized enterprise evidence;
* maintain immutable records of authoritative closed-position truth;
* provide authoritative realized-position truth to downstream enterprise consumers.

The Office shall never own or determine truth outside its constitutional jurisdiction.

---

# 3. Establish Constitutional Mission

Define the constitutional mission of the Office.

The mission shall include:

* determining whether constitutional closure requirements have been satisfied;
* creating authoritative Closed Position Truth only after constitutional eligibility is established;
* preserving immutable historical records;
* recording corrections exclusively through constitutional supersession;
* supplying authoritative closed-position truth to authorized downstream offices.

The mission shall explicitly exclude trading, execution, authorization, market analysis, and performance analytics.

---

# 4. Establish Constitutional Authority

Create the Constitutional Authority Registry.

Define the Office's authority to:

* determine constitutional closure;
* create authoritative Closed Position Truth;
* maintain authoritative closed-position records;
* supersede prior Closed Position Truth records;
* archive constitutionally complete records;
* reject invalid closure attempts;
* defer truth creation pending required evidence;
* reconcile closure eligibility using authorized upstream truth.

The Office shall possess no authority to create or modify truth owned by another office.

---

# 5. Establish Constitutional Responsibility Registry

Create the Constitutional Responsibility Registry.

The Office shall be responsible for:

* constitutional closure determination;
* authoritative closed-position identity;
* closure timestamps;
* closure rationale;
* realized outcome truth assigned to Closed Position Truth;
* reconciliation status;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* archival eligibility;
* preservation of historical integrity.

Every responsibility shall possess exactly one constitutional owner.

---

# 6. Establish Constitutional Limitation Registry

Create the Constitutional Limitation Registry.

The Closed Position Truth Office is expressly prohibited from:

* owning open-position truth;
* issuing trading decisions;
* issuing exit decisions;
* issuing authorizations;
* routing orders;
* executing trades;
* modifying broker-owned facts;
* modifying Position Registry truth;
* modifying Risk truth;
* modifying Authorization truth;
* modifying Performance Truth;
* modifying Historian records;
* modifying market data.

The Office shall derive enterprise truth from authorized upstream evidence without assuming ownership of upstream truth.

---

# 7. Establish Constitutional Success Criteria

Define the constitutional conditions for successful Office operation.

Success shall require:

* deterministic closure determination;
* singular truth ownership;
* complete evidence lineage;
* deterministic reconciliation;
* immutable historical preservation;
* correction through supersession only;
* deterministic downstream truth publication;
* complete constitutional traceability.

Authoritative truth shall exist only after every constitutional prerequisite has been satisfied.

---

# 8. Establish Constitutional Failure Conditions

Define constitutional failure conditions.

Failure shall include, at minimum:

* creation of authoritative truth without required evidence;
* acceptance of contradictory authoritative truth;
* ambiguous ownership;
* shared constitutional authority;
* modification of immutable historical records;
* direct correction of historical truth;
* unauthorized reopening of closed positions;
* unauthorized mutation of upstream truth;
* unauthorized assumption of external authority.

Any constitutional failure shall prevent certification readiness.

---

# 9. Establish Constitutional Authority Boundaries

Confirm that the Closed Position Truth Office possesses authority only within its constitutional jurisdiction.

The Office may:

* determine constitutional closure;
* establish authoritative closed-position truth;
* preserve immutable historical records;
* supersede prior records through successor records;
* publish authoritative closed-position truth.

The Office shall not:

* determine trading intent;
* determine market direction;
* authorize execution;
* perform execution;
* determine broker truth;
* determine risk truth;
* determine authorization truth;
* determine performance analytics.

Authority shall never extend beyond constitutionally assigned responsibilities.
