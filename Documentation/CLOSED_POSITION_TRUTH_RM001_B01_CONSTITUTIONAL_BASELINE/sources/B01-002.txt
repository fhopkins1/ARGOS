# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01-002

# Office Boundary Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional boundaries between the Closed Position Truth Office and every participating enterprise office.

This order exists solely to define constitutional responsibility allocation, authority ownership, dependency boundaries, information exchange, and prohibited responsibilities for every enterprise relationship involving the Closed Position Truth Office.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional doctrine only.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every constitutional responsibility shall possess exactly one owner.

Every office boundary shall possess one governing authority.

Information exchange shall not transfer constitutional ownership.

Evidence consumption shall not transfer authority.

Dependency shall not imply ownership.

Custody shall not imply mutation authority.

Analytical interpretation shall never become authoritative truth.

Closed Position Truth shall consume upstream truth without modifying upstream truth.

Closed Position Truth shall publish authoritative closed-position truth without assuming downstream authority.

No office shall possess authority to mutate another office's authoritative truth unless explicitly established by constitutional doctrine.
