# Truth Ownership and Authority Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional ownership, custody, creation authority, correction authority, supersession authority, and archival authority for every authoritative truth element maintained by the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall define the complete single-owner governance model for Closed Position Truth and eliminate every ambiguity involving ownership, custody, mutation, correction, supersession, archival eligibility, or authority transfer.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional doctrine only.

Every truth element shall possess exactly one constitutional owner.

Every truth element shall possess exactly one constitutional source of authority.

Custody shall never imply ownership.

Evidence possession shall never imply truth authority.

---

# Constitutional Principles

The Closed Position Truth Office shall own only authoritative enterprise truth concerning constitutionally closed positions.

The Closed Position Truth Office shall not own:

* broker-originated facts;
* execution intent;
* execution instructions;
* authorization state;
* open-position truth;
* market truth;
* risk truth;
* historical custody;
* enterprise performance truth beyond constitutionally assigned realized outcomes.

Truth ownership shall be singular.

Authority shall never be shared.

Authoritative truth shall either exist or not exist.

Authoritative Closed Position Truth shall not possess provisional, degraded, probable, inferred, or partially authoritative states.

Historical truth shall remain immutable.

Corrections shall occur only through constitutionally governed successor records.

Supersession shall preserve predecessor identity and lineage.

No accepted truth record shall be overwritten, deleted, reopened, or silently reactivated.

---

# Closed Position Truth Ownership Constitution

The Closed Position Truth Office shall possess exclusive constitutional ownership of:

* authoritative closed-position identity;
* authoritative closure determination;
* authoritative closure timestamp;
* authoritative closure reason;
* authoritative realized outcome within assigned scope;
* authoritative reconciliation status supporting closure;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* archival eligibility determination.

Ownership shall mean authority to determine whether the relevant Closed Position Truth element constitutionally exists.

Ownership shall not include authority to modify upstream source facts.

# Ownership, Custody, and Authority Distinctions

## Ownership

Ownership means constitutional authority to define and govern an authoritative truth element.

## Custody

Custody means responsibility to preserve and make available an authoritative record.

Custody shall not permit alteration of owned truth.

## Creation Authority

Creation authority means authority to establish a truth record only after all constitutional conditions are satisfied.

## Mutation Authority

Mutation authority means authority to alter a non-authoritative object before final truth creation.

No authoritative Closed Position Truth record shall possess ordinary mutation authority.

## Correction Authority

Correction authority means authority to create a successor record that corrects a prior record while preserving the prior record.

## Supersession Authority

Supersession authority means authority to designate a valid successor as authoritative without deleting or altering its predecessor.

## Archival Authority

Archival authority means authority to accept eligible records into permanent historical custody.

# Upstream Truth Ownership Boundaries

## Broker Office

Broker shall retain exclusive ownership of:

* broker acknowledgements;
* broker fills;
* broker cancellations;
* broker rejections;
* broker-reported execution status;
* broker-originated settlement facts.

Closed Position Truth may consume these facts.

Closed Position Truth shall not alter, replace, reinterpret, or become the owner of Broker truth.

---

## Trader Office

Trader shall retain ownership of:

* execution workflow;
* execution instructions;
* execution lifecycle;
* execution management;
* execution-request lineage.

Closed Position Truth shall not own execution activity.

---

## Position Registry

Position Registry shall retain ownership of:

* open-position identity;
* current position quantity;
* current position state;
* position mutation;
* active position reconciliation.

Closed Position Truth shall use authoritative Position Registry truth when determining closure.

Closed Position Truth shall not mutate Position Registry records.

---

## Exit Decision Office

Exit Decision shall retain ownership of:

* exit evaluation;
* exit recommendation;
* exit decision;
* exit-decision rationale;
* exit-decision lineage.

Closed Position Truth shall not reinterpret or replace Exit Decision truth.

---

## Risk Office

Risk shall retain ownership of:

* risk state;
* risk limits;
* exposure calculations;
* risk constraints.

Risk truth shall not become part of Closed Position Truth ownership.

---

## Performance Truth Office

Performance Truth shall retain ownership of:

* enterprise performance measurement;
* portfolio performance;
* strategy performance;
* attribution;
* risk-adjusted performance;
* performance analytics.

Performance Truth may derive from Closed Position Truth.

Closed Position Truth shall not own downstream performance conclusions.

---

## Historian

Historian shall retain permanent historical custody.

Historian shall not:

* create Closed Position Truth;
* correct Closed Position Truth;
* supersede Closed Position Truth;
* change closure determinations.

# Truth Creation Authority

The Closed Position Truth Office may create authoritative truth only when:

* position identity is authoritative;
* execution evidence is complete;
* execution reconciliation is complete;
* remaining position quantity is zero;
* settlement requirements are satisfied or constitutionally exempt;
* evidence provenance is complete;
* no unresolved contradiction remains.

If any required condition is absent, authoritative truth shall not be created.

Absence of authoritative truth shall not be represented as degraded truth.

---

# Immutability Constitution

After authoritative creation, a Closed Position Truth record shall be immutable.

The following actions are prohibited:

* field-level mutation;
* deletion;
* reopening;
* silent replacement;
* retroactive editing;
* predecessor concealment;
* lineage destruction.

Operational convenience shall never justify mutation of authoritative truth.

---

# Correction Constitution

A correction shall be permitted only when:

* the prior record contains a demonstrable error;
* authoritative correcting evidence exists;
* correction authority is established;
* predecessor identity is preserved;
* successor identity is unique;
* correction rationale is recorded;
* historical custody is maintained.

A correction shall produce a new successor record.

The original record shall remain historically accessible.

---

# Supersession Constitution

Supersession shall:

* identify the predecessor;
* identify the successor;
* identify the governing authority;
* identify the supersession reason;
* preserve all prior evidence;
* establish which record is currently authoritative.

Supersession shall not:

* erase the predecessor;
* modify the predecessor;
* create parallel authoritative truth;
* permit the closed position to reopen.

Only one record in a supersession lineage may possess current authoritative status.

---

# Custody Constitution

The Closed Position Truth Office shall retain operational custody until archival eligibility is established.

Historian shall assume permanent custody after archival transfer.

Custody transfer shall preserve:

* record identity;
* ownership identity;
* evidence lineage;
* correction history;
* supersession history;
* integrity verification.

Transfer of custody shall not transfer constitutional ownership.

---

The Closed Position Truth Office shall not possess authority to:
