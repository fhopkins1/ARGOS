# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003-005

# Constitutional Cohesion Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Constitutional Cohesion Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to determine whether every constitutional responsibility assigned to the office contributes directly and necessarily to the office mission of producing authoritative, reproducible, and auditable enterprise performance truth.

This order shall assume that some responsibilities may have accumulated within the office through convenience, implementation history, interface proximity, or prior architectural compromise rather than constitutional necessity.

Every responsibility lacking direct constitutional cohesion shall be reassigned, removed, or formally justified.

# Campaign Objective

The objective of this audit is to determine whether the Performance Truth Office possesses maximum constitutional cohesion.

Maximum constitutional cohesion exists when:

* every responsibility directly serves the office mission;
* every responsibility depends upon the office’s unique constitutional authority;
* no responsibility exists merely because the office already possesses related data;
* no responsibility duplicates another office;
* no responsibility creates unnecessary authority;
* no responsibility expands the office beyond performance truth.

Responsibilities that fail this standard shall constitute architectural findings.

---

# Office Mission Baseline

The constitutional mission of the Performance Truth Office shall be treated as the authoritative cohesion baseline.

The office exists to:

* derive performance from authoritative enterprise truth;
* calculate performance deterministically;
* preserve performance history;
* publish canonical Performance Truth Records;
* support correction, revision, replay, reconciliation, and audit;
* maintain complete performance provenance.

The office shall not acquire unrelated business responsibilities.

---

# Responsibility Inventory

The auditor shall construct a complete inventory of every responsibility assigned to or performed by the Performance Truth Office.

The inventory shall include:

* constitutional responsibilities;
* implementation responsibilities;
* operational responsibilities;
* interface responsibilities;
* evidentiary responsibilities;
* reconciliation responsibilities;
* archival responsibilities;
* certification responsibilities;
* implied responsibilities;
* responsibilities performed without explicit assignment.

No responsibility may remain undiscovered.

---

# Responsibility Classification

Each responsibility shall be classified as:

* core constitutional responsibility;
* necessary supporting responsibility;
* shared enterprise responsibility;
* delegated responsibility;
* incidental implementation behavior;
* misplaced responsibility;
* duplicated responsibility;
* unsupported responsibility.

Classification shall be supported by constitutional evidence.

---

# Mission Contribution Test

Every responsibility shall be evaluated against the office mission.

The audit shall determine whether the responsibility:

* directly produces performance truth;
* protects performance truth;
* validates performance truth;
* preserves performance truth;
* exposes performance truth through a required interface;
* enables independent reproduction or audit.

A responsibility that does not satisfy at least one mission contribution shall require removal or reassignment.

---

# Unique Authority Test

The auditor shall determine whether each responsibility requires authority uniquely held by the Performance Truth Office.

A responsibility shall remain within the office only when:

* the office possesses the authoritative inputs;
* the office owns the resulting performance object;
* the office alone can perform the action without violating ownership;
* assignment elsewhere would create duplicated truth or divided authority.

Responsibilities not requiring unique Performance Truth authority shall be examined for reassignment.

---

# Proximity Fallacy Audit

The audit shall search for responsibilities assigned to the office merely because it possesses nearby data, logic, or interfaces.

Examples include responsibilities retained because:

* required inputs are already available;
* calculations are computationally similar;
* storage is shared;
* interfaces already exist;
* implementation is convenient;
* prior code placement created dependency.

Architectural proximity shall not establish constitutional ownership.

---

# Data Custody Versus Responsibility

The audit shall distinguish between:

* possessing data;
* preserving data;
* calculating from data;
* owning the underlying truth;
* governing the originating business event.

Data custody shall not create authority over upstream truth.

The Performance Truth Office shall not assume responsibility merely because it consumes or preserves another office’s records.

---

# Calculation Cohesion Audit

Every calculation performed by the office shall be evaluated to determine whether it produces or validates performance truth.

The audit shall identify calculations that instead belong to:

* Closed Position Truth;
* Risk;
* Broker;
* Trader;
* Monitoring;
* Decision Objects;
* enterprise bridges;
* external analytical offices.

Duplicated or misplaced calculations shall be eliminated or reassigned.

---

# Reconciliation Cohesion Audit

Every reconciliation responsibility shall be examined to determine whether the office:

* owns the reconciled result;
* merely participates in reconciliation;
* verifies performance-specific consistency;
* attempts to resolve upstream truth conflicts outside its authority.

The office may identify discrepancies affecting performance truth.

It shall not assume authority to alter another office’s truth.

---

# Evidence Cohesion Audit

Evidence responsibilities shall remain within the office only where they concern:

* performance calculation;
* performance publication;
* performance correction;
* performance revision;
* performance reconciliation;
* performance certification.

Evidence creation belonging to source offices shall remain with those offices.

Performance Truth shall reference and validate upstream evidence rather than recreate it.

---

# Historical Responsibility Audit

The auditor shall distinguish between performance-specific historical preservation and general enterprise archival custody.

The Performance Truth Office may own:

* historical Performance Truth Records;
* performance revisions;
* performance calculation contexts;
* performance replay evidence.

General enterprise history shall remain under Historian authority.

---

# Certification Responsibility Audit

The audit shall determine whether any certification responsibility improperly shifts audit authority into the Performance Truth Office.

The office may:

* generate certification evidence;
* expose replay capability;
* support independent verification.

The office shall not:

* certify itself;
* define its own final certification outcome;
* suppress adverse findings;
* control independent audit conclusions.

---

# Interface-Derived Responsibility Audit

Every interface shall be examined for responsibilities introduced through contract design rather than constitutional mission.

The audit shall identify interfaces that cause the office to:

* validate another office’s internal behavior;
* maintain another office’s lifecycle;
* normalize another office’s truth;
* compensate for missing upstream guarantees;
* perform bridge logic internally.

Responsibility leakage through interfaces shall be eliminated.

---

# Shared Responsibility Audit

Where a responsibility appears shared, the audit shall determine:

* the singular constitutional owner;
* participating offices;
* custody boundaries;
* decision authority;
* evidence obligations;
* escalation path.

Shared participation shall not create shared ownership.

---

# Responsibility Chain Analysis

Each responsibility shall be traced through:

* trigger;
* authoritative input;
* decision authority;
* execution authority;
* object ownership;
* evidence generation;
* resulting output;
* downstream consumer.

The analysis shall reveal hidden transfers, duplicated decisions, and unclear accountability.

---

# Responsibility Necessity Test

For every responsibility, the auditor shall ask whether the office could fulfill its mission without performing it.

A responsibility shall be considered constitutionally necessary only when its removal would:

* prevent production of performance truth;
* break performance provenance;
* prevent deterministic replay;
* violate performance ownership;
* prevent required reconciliation;
* prevent independent audit.

Responsibilities failing the necessity test shall be candidates for removal or reassignment.

---

# Responsibility Reassignment Analysis

For every misplaced responsibility, the audit shall identify:

* proper constitutional owner;
* required interface changes;
* ownership transfer implications;
* evidence implications;
* lifecycle implications;
* migration requirements;
* backward-compatibility effects.

Reassignment shall preserve all certified guarantees.

---

# Responsibility Removal Analysis

Responsibilities may be removed when they are:

* redundant;
* unnecessary;
* implementation-specific;
* unsupported by doctrine;
* duplicated elsewhere;
* obsolete following prior modifications.

Removal shall include proof that no constitutional guarantee depends upon the responsibility.

---

# Cohesion Conflict Analysis

The audit shall identify responsibilities that create conflicting objectives within the office.

Conflicts may include:

* truth production versus self-certification;
* calculation versus upstream truth correction;
* publication versus reconciliation ownership;
* historical preservation versus general archival control;
* evidence generation versus evidence adjudication.

Conflicting responsibilities shall be separated.

---

# Architectural Cohesion Metrics

The audit shall produce objective cohesion measures including:

* percentage of responsibilities directly tied to mission;
* number of duplicated responsibilities;
* number of unsupported responsibilities;
* number of shared ownership ambiguities;
* number of interface-derived responsibilities;
* number of responsibilities requiring reassignment;
* number of responsibilities removed;
* number of responsibilities constitutionally justified.

Metrics shall support comparison against the prior architecture baseline.

---

# Constitutional Modifications

Every validated cohesion defect shall produce a constitutional modification specifying:

* affected responsibility;
* existing assignment;
* corrected assignment;
* revised authority;
* revised ownership;
* revised interfaces;
* revised evidence obligations;
* revised lifecycle obligations;
* migration and compatibility requirements.

No responsibility reassignment shall occur informally.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every office responsibility directly serves the constitutional mission;
* every responsibility possesses a singular constitutional owner;
* data proximity does not establish authority;
* custody does not establish ownership;
* the office does not duplicate another office’s truth or calculations;
* shared participation never creates shared ownership;
* certification authority remains independent of the office;
* every retained responsibility is constitutionally necessary;
* every removed or reassigned responsibility preserves existing guarantees.

Violation of any invariant constitutes an architectural hardening finding.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Constitutional Cohesion Audit shall issue a failing determination whenever:

* responsibilities remain undocumented;
* responsibilities lack a constitutional owner;
* duplicated authority remains;
* misplaced responsibilities remain unresolved;
* responsibility leakage persists through interfaces;
* the office governs truth owned by another office;
* certification authority is not independent;
* responsibility reassignment would weaken a certified guarantee;
* cohesion findings lack objective resolution.

The hardened architecture shall not be approved while material cohesion defects remain.

# Acceptance Criteria

This Modification Order shall be considered complete when independent architectural review demonstrates that:

1. every responsibility performed by the Performance Truth Office has been discovered and constitutionally classified;
2. every retained responsibility directly and necessarily contributes to the office mission;
3. no responsibility remains assigned merely because of data proximity, implementation convenience, historical placement, or interface design;
4. no responsibility duplicates another office’s authority, truth, calculation, lifecycle, evidence, or certification function;
5. every shared activity possesses singular ownership and explicit participation boundaries;
6. every misplaced, unsupported, duplicated, or unnecessary responsibility has been reassigned, removed, or constitutionally disproven;
7. all resulting architectural modifications preserve deterministic behavior, ownership, evidence, replay, reconciliation, and fail-closed guarantees;
8. objective cohesion measures demonstrate improvement over the prior constitutional architecture baseline; and
9. the Performance Truth Office possesses a maximally cohesive constitutional responsibility set that cannot be materially simplified or improved without weakening constitutional guarantees.
