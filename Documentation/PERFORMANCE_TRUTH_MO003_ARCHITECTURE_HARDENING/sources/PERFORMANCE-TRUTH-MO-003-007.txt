# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003-007

# Enterprise Dependency Minimization

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional architecture audit governing every external dependency required by the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether each dependency is constitutionally necessary, minimally scoped, explicitly governed, and incapable of introducing hidden authority, duplicated responsibility, circular coupling, or non-deterministic enterprise behavior.

The objective is not to maximize architectural isolation.

The objective is to eliminate unnecessary constitutional dependencies while preserving every guarantee required to produce authoritative, reproducible, evidence-backed Performance Truth.

Every remaining dependency shall possess explicit constitutional justification.

# Constitutional Principles

The audit shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Every dependency shall be constitutionally necessary.

2. Every dependency shall expose only the minimum information required.

3. Dependencies shall not transfer constitutional ownership.

4. Dependencies shall not duplicate business truth.

5. Dependencies shall not require hidden coordination.

6. Dependencies shall remain directionally explicit.

7. Circular constitutional dependencies are prohibited.

8. Dependency failure shall not produce discretionary behavior.

9. Dependency reduction shall not weaken evidence, traceability, or truth integrity.

10. Every retained dependency shall possess a documented constitutional justification.

---

# Dependency Definition

A constitutional dependency exists whenever the Performance Truth Office cannot satisfy a required constitutional behavior without relying upon:

* a fact owned by another office;
* an object owned by another office;
* an external validation;
* a workflow action;
* an evidence artifact;
* a storage or retrieval service;
* a certification decision;
* a temporal or coordination event.

Mere technical reuse shall not automatically constitute a constitutional dependency.

The audit shall distinguish constitutional necessity from implementation convenience.

---

The auditor shall produce a complete Dependency Inventory.

Each dependency shall identify:

* Dependency ID;
* dependency provider;
* dependency consumer;
* constitutional purpose;
* required object or fact;
* owner;
* direction;
* invocation conditions;
* lifecycle relevance;
* temporal requirements;
* evidence requirements;
* failure behavior;
* version constraints;
* substitutability;
* constitutional authority;

No dependency may remain implicit.

* Essential Truth Dependency;

Only constitutionally essential dependencies shall remain after closure unless a temporary exception is explicitly authorized.

---

# Necessity Test

For every dependency, the auditor shall ask:

* What constitutional guarantee requires it?
* What behavior becomes impossible without it?
* Can the same guarantee be satisfied through an existing canonical artifact?
* Does the dependency duplicate information already possessed?
* Does it exist only because of current implementation structure?
* Can it be removed without changing constitutional behavior?
* Can it be replaced with an immutable reference or evidence artifact?
* Does it force another office to perform Performance Truth responsibility?

A dependency that cannot pass this test shall be classified as unjustified.

---

# Dependency Minimality Test

For every necessary dependency, determine whether the Performance Truth Office receives more authority, information, state, or control than constitutionally required.

The audit shall minimize:

* fields exposed;
* object scope;
* invocation frequency;
* lifecycle coupling;
* synchronous coordination;
* write authority;
* correction authority;
* shared state;
* retained custody;
* implementation knowledge.

Minimum necessary access shall be preferred over broad interface access.

---

# Truth Dependency Audit

Verify every dependency on upstream truth, including:

* Decision Truth;
* Authorization Truth;
* Risk Truth;
* Broker Execution Truth;
* Closed Position Truth;
* Monitoring Truth;
* market-data truth;
* benchmark truth;
* Historical Truth.

For each dependency verify that:

* the upstream office owns the source fact;
* Performance Truth consumes rather than recreates the fact;
* source identity and version are explicit;
* provenance is preserved;
* correction behavior is defined;
* unavailable or contradictory truth fails closed.

---

# Workflow Dependency Audit

Determine whether Performance Truth depends upon Workflow Engine behavior beyond constitutionally necessary orchestration.

Verify that:

* workflow identity is sufficient to establish execution provenance;
* business calculations do not depend upon undocumented workflow internals;
* workflow execution order is explicit where material;
* workflow possession does not imply object ownership;
* replay does not require unavailable orchestration state.

Hidden workflow coupling shall be eliminated.

---

# Commander Dependency Audit

Determine whether Performance Truth requires Commander involvement for:

* calculation initiation;
* publication;
* correction;
* certification;
* reconciliation;
* recovery.

Commander involvement shall remain limited to constitutionally authorized command and consumption functions.

Performance Truth shall not depend upon Commander discretion for mathematical or truth determination.

---

# Sentinel Dependency Audit

Determine whether Performance Truth depends upon Sentinel for:

* event discovery;
* failure detection;
* wake behavior;
* dependency status;
* publication awareness.

Sentinel shall not become the sole source of business truth or historical provenance unless doctrine explicitly assigns that role.

Absence of Sentinel observation shall not silently redefine Performance Truth.

---

# Trader Dependency Audit

Verify whether Performance Truth consumes only the Trader-owned execution facts necessary for attribution and provenance.

Performance Truth shall not depend upon:

* undocumented Trader state;
* transient execution internals;
* inferred intent;
* mutable local caches;
* Trader-owned calculations that duplicate Performance Truth.

Any such dependency shall be removed or explicitly justified.

---

# Risk Dependency Audit

Verify that dependencies on Risk are limited to authoritative risk decisions, constraints, and relevant evidence.

Performance Truth shall not require Risk to calculate performance or classify performance outcomes.

Risk unavailability shall produce deterministic behavior without transferring risk authority to Performance Truth.

---

# Broker Dependency Audit

Verify that dependencies on Broker are limited to broker-owned execution and external transaction evidence required for reconciliation.

Where Closed Position Truth already constitutionally incorporates broker data, determine whether direct Broker dependency remains necessary.

Duplicate reliance on both Broker and Closed Position Truth shall be justified field by field.

---

# Monitoring Dependency Audit

Determine whether Monitoring inputs are constitutionally necessary to calculate performance or are used only to explain operational context.

The audit shall distinguish:

* performance-determining facts;
* reconciliation evidence;
* anomaly evidence;
* contextual observations.

Performance publication shall not depend upon nonmaterial monitoring observations unless doctrine explicitly requires them.

---

# Closed Position Truth Dependency Audit

Verify that Closed Position Truth remains the authoritative dependency for:

* completed position state;
* realized profit or loss;
* settlement state;
* residual exposure;
* closure corrections.

Determine whether every direct dependency on Trader, Broker, or Monitoring for these same facts can be eliminated in favor of Closed Position Truth references.

No simplification may remove independent reconciliation where constitutionally required.

---

# Historian Dependency Audit

Verify that Performance Truth depends upon Historian only for constitutional preservation, retrieval, and replay support.

Determine whether current-state calculations improperly require Historian availability where canonical current objects should suffice.

Historian shall not become a synchronous prerequisite unless historical evidence is constitutionally necessary for the operation being performed.

---

# Decision Object Dependency Audit

Verify that Performance Truth depends upon Decision Objects only where decision provenance, attribution, or intent comparison is constitutionally material.

Performance Truth shall not require complete decision internals where immutable decision identity and certified context are sufficient.

---

# Evidence Repository Dependency Audit

Verify that the Evidence Repository dependency is limited to:

* storage;
* retrieval;
* integrity verification;
* evidence availability.

Performance Truth shall not depend upon repository-specific organization, indexing conventions, or implementation details to interpret evidence.

Evidence meaning shall remain portable.

---

# Audit Office Dependency Audit

Verify that Audit Office dependency exists only for independent certification and constitutional audit functions.

Routine Performance Truth operation shall not depend upon active Audit Office approval unless explicit doctrine requires a certification gate.

The Audit Office shall not participate in business calculation or ordinary publication.

---

# Market-Data Dependency Audit

For every market-data dependency, verify:

* authoritative source;
* observation identity;
* timestamp semantics;
* version or correction behavior;
* normalization ownership;
* required precision;
* availability behavior;
* replay availability.

Performance Truth shall not depend upon live mutable market data when calculating historical results if immutable historical observations are required.

---

# Benchmark Dependency Audit

Verify that benchmark dependencies expose only:

* benchmark identity;
* benchmark definition;
* effective interval;
* authoritative values;
* version;
* evidence.

Performance Truth shall own benchmark comparison logic but not necessarily the benchmark source.

External benchmark unavailability shall not cause undocumented substitution.

---

# Bridge Dependency Audit

Examine every enterprise bridge connected to Performance Truth.

Verify that no bridge:

* owns business truth;
* performs hidden calculations;
* stores authoritative lifecycle state;
* resolves constitutional conflicts;
* duplicates evidence;
* introduces circular dependencies;
* creates hidden synchronization requirements.

Bridge behavior shall remain transportive, validating, or coordinating only to the extent explicitly authorized.

---

# Circular Dependency Analysis

Construct a directed constitutional dependency graph.

Search for cycles involving:

* Performance Truth;
* Closed Position Truth;
* Historian;
* Monitoring;
* Workflow Engine;
* Evidence Repository;
* Audit Office;
* certification state;
* correction workflows.

Every cycle shall be classified as:

* apparent but nonconstitutional;
* temporally ordered and safe;
* structurally necessary;
* hidden architectural coupling;
* prohibited circular dependency.

A cycle that prevents independent ownership, replay, or fail-closed resolution shall be eliminated.

---

# Dependency Direction Audit

Verify that every dependency has an explicit direction.

Examples include:

* Performance Truth consumes Closed Position Truth;
* Historian preserves Performance Truth;
* Audit Office certifies Performance Truth;
* Commander consumes certified Performance Truth.

Bidirectional interaction shall not be mistaken for bidirectional authority.

Where two offices exchange information, each direction shall be independently justified.

---

# Temporal Dependency Audit

Determine whether Performance Truth depends upon:

* exact event arrival order;
* synchronous responses;
* same-run completion;
* immediate evidence availability;
* immediate reconciliation;
* immediate historical persistence.

Every temporal dependency shall be explicit.

Where possible, replace timing assumptions with immutable identities, effective times, lifecycle states, and deterministic reconciliation.

---

# Availability Dependency Audit

For every dependency, define behavior when the provider is:

* unavailable;
* delayed;
* partially available;
* stale;
* inconsistent;
* partitioned;
* recovering;
* superseded.

No dependency failure may cause Performance Truth to invent substitute truth or proceed under uncertainty.

---

# Version Dependency Audit

Verify that every version-sensitive dependency identifies:

* provider version;
* schema version;
* object version;
* formula version;
* evidence version;
* compatibility rules;
* migration rules;
* rejection behavior.

Implicit compatibility shall be prohibited.

---

# Dependency Substitution Audit

Determine whether a dependency may be satisfied by:

* a canonical object;
* an immutable reference;
* a certified evidence package;
* a historical snapshot;
* an alternative certified provider.

Substitution shall be allowed only when constitutional equivalence is explicitly proven.

Implementation similarity shall not establish substitutability.

---

# Shared-State Audit

Search for shared mutable state between Performance Truth and external offices.

Shared mutable state shall be presumed unconstitutional unless:

* ownership remains singular;
* mutation authority is explicit;
* lifecycle control is deterministic;
* evidence is complete;
* concurrent behavior is defined.

Prefer immutable exchange and explicit references over shared mutable custody.

---

# Hidden Coordination Audit

Search for dependencies expressed through:

* polling;
* file placement;
* repository layout;
* naming conventions;
* timing assumptions;
* cache behavior;
* repeated retries;
* undocumented notifications;
* manual audit steps;
* operator knowledge.

Every hidden coordination mechanism shall either become explicit constitutional doctrine or be eliminated.

---

# Dependency Failure Behavior

For every retained dependency, define deterministic behavior for:

* missing input;
* invalid input;
* conflicting input;
* duplicate input;
* stale input;
* delayed input;
* corrupted input;
* provider unavailability;
* version incompatibility;
* unresolved reconciliation.

The affected operation shall fail closed whenever its constitutional guarantees cannot be satisfied.

---

# Dependency Reduction Experiments

The auditor shall attempt to remove, replace, or narrow each dependency.

For every experiment, measure constitutional effects upon:

* truth correctness;
* ownership;
* determinism;
* evidence completeness;
* traceability;
* replay;
* reconciliation;
* failure behavior;
* auditability.

A dependency shall be removed or narrowed whenever equivalent guarantees remain.

---

# Adversarial Dependency Testing

The auditor shall intentionally:

* suppress dependency responses;
* reorder dependency events;
* substitute stale versions;
* provide conflicting provider records;
* duplicate dependency messages;
* partition providers;
* alter bridge behavior;
* remove undocumented shared state;
* deny access to implementation-specific metadata.

Every dependency shall either continue constitutionally or fail closed.

---

# Constitutional Resolution Requirements

Every dependency defect shall result in one or more of the following:

1. Remove the dependency.

2. Narrow the dependency scope.

3. Replace shared state with immutable reference exchange.

4. Replace direct dependency with a canonical upstream truth.

5. Eliminate duplicated calculation or truth.

6. Define explicit version compatibility.

7. Define deterministic temporal behavior.

8. Define fail-closed dependency behavior.

9. Eliminate circular authority or coordination.

10. Prove the dependency is constitutionally necessary and minimal.

No dependency may be retained solely because current implementation uses it.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The architecture shall fail closed whenever:

* a required dependency cannot be identified;
* dependency ownership is ambiguous;
* dependency authority is circular;
* provider truth is contradictory;
* version compatibility cannot be established;
* dependency evidence is incomplete;
* temporal ordering is constitutionally material but unresolved;
* dependency substitution cannot be proven equivalent;
* shared mutable state has undefined authority;
* removal of a hidden dependency prevents deterministic reproduction.

No dependent Performance Truth result shall be published or certified while a material dependency defect remains unresolved.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold.

* Every dependency is constitutionally necessary and explicitly justified.
* Every dependency is minimal in scope.
* Dependencies never transfer ownership implicitly.
* Dependencies never duplicate authoritative truth.
* Circular constitutional authority does not exist.
* Shared mutable state does not create ownership ambiguity.
* Temporal coupling is explicit and deterministic.
* Provider failure produces fail-closed behavior.
* Dependency evidence is complete and reproducible.
* Performance Truth remains independently auditable despite external dependencies.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every constitutional dependency of the Performance Truth Office has been identified and classified.
2. Every retained dependency has explicit constitutional authority, purpose, direction, ownership, and failure behavior.
3. Every dependency has been challenged for removal, replacement, or scope reduction.
4. Unnecessary, duplicative, circular, hidden, and implementation-convenience dependencies have been eliminated.
5. Every remaining dependency exposes only the minimum constitutionally required information and authority.
6. Shared state, timing assumptions, version assumptions, and hidden coordination have been eliminated or explicitly governed.
7. Dependency failure, delay, contradiction, corruption, and incompatibility produce deterministic fail-closed behavior.
8. The directed dependency graph contains no unresolved circular authority, truth duplication, or ownership leakage.
9. Independent auditors can reconstruct and justify every dependency solely from constitutional doctrine and evidence.
10. No material dependency can be removed or further narrowed without reducing constitutional guarantees, and no unresolved enterprise dependency weakness remains.
