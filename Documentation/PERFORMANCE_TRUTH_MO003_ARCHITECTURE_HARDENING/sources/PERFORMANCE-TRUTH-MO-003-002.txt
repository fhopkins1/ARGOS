# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003-002

# Ownership Boundary Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional architecture audit governing ownership boundaries within the Performance Truth Office and across every enterprise interface connected to it.

The purpose of this order is to challenge every existing ownership assignment and determine whether constitutional ownership is singular, necessary, complete, non-overlapping, and aligned with the office’s actual mission.

The objective is not to confirm current ownership assignments.

The objective is to expose any instance in which ownership, custody, authority, control, responsibility, publication power, correction authority, or certification power has been assigned ambiguously, redundantly, incompletely, or to the wrong constitutional office.

Every discovered ownership defect shall result in constitutional clarification, reassignment, prohibition, or proof that the existing boundary is correct.

# Constitutional Principles

The audit shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Every constitutional object shall possess exactly one owner.

2. Every constitutional fact shall possess exactly one authoritative owner.

3. Ownership shall be distinct from custody, access, observation, validation, storage, and use.

4. Authority shall derive only from ownership or explicit constitutional delegation.

5. Custody shall never imply ownership.

6. Possession of evidence shall never imply authority over the truth evidenced.

7. Interface participation shall never create shared ownership.

8. Ownership shall remain continuous throughout every lifecycle.

9. No office shall silently acquire authority through implementation behavior.

10. Any unresolved ownership ambiguity shall fail closed.

# Ownership Concepts

For purposes of this audit, the following distinctions shall be constitutionally enforced.

## Ownership

The exclusive constitutional authority to define, create, validate, revise, supersede, certify, and govern a business object or fact within the limits of applicable doctrine.

## Custody

Temporary or persistent possession of an object or artifact without authority to alter its constitutional meaning.

## Access

Permission to read or consume an object without ownership authority.

## Observation

Authority to report an observed condition without authority to redefine the underlying object.

## Validation

Authority to determine whether an object satisfies specified requirements without acquiring ownership of that object.

## Certification

Authority to determine whether evidence demonstrates compliance without acquiring ownership of the certified object.

## Storage

Responsibility to preserve an object or artifact without authority to alter its meaning.

## Delegation

Explicit, limited authority granted by the constitutional owner for a defined action.

These concepts shall not be treated as interchangeable.

# Constitutional Resolution Requirements

Every ownership defect shall result in one or more of the following:

1. Assign singular ownership.

2. Reassign ownership to the constitutionally proper office.

3. Narrow delegated authority.

4. Clarify custody without ownership.

5. Prohibit unauthorized action.

6. Separate validation from ownership.

7. Separate certification from ownership.

8. Clarify lifecycle authority.

9. Clarify reconciliation authority.

10. Prove that the suspected defect is impossible under existing doctrine.

No ownership defect may be closed through implementation convention alone.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The constitutional architecture shall fail closed whenever:

* ownership cannot be determined;
* multiple offices claim the same authority;
* custody conflicts with ownership;
* delegated authority exceeds its scope;
* lifecycle ownership becomes undefined;
* correction authority is disputed;
* revision authority is disputed;
* certification authority is disputed;
* reconciliation cannot determine the authoritative owner;
* an interface implies ownership transfer without doctrine.

No disputed object or fact shall be published, revised, superseded, or certified until ownership is constitutionally resolved.

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold.

* Every constitutional object has exactly one owner.
* Every constitutional fact has exactly one authoritative owner.
* Custody never implies ownership.
* Storage never implies ownership.
* Validation never implies ownership.
* Certification never implies ownership.
* Interface participation never creates ownership.
* Ownership remains continuous throughout every lifecycle.
* Delegated authority is explicit, narrow, and auditable.
* Ownership ambiguity deterministically prevents constitutional action.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every object, field, fact, calculation, responsibility, and authority associated with Performance Truth has an explicit constitutional owner.
2. Ownership has been distinguished from custody, access, observation, validation, certification, storage, and delegation.
3. Every ownership boundary between Performance Truth and each dependent office has been independently challenged.
4. No duplicate, ownerless, misplaced, or implicitly transferred ownership remains.
5. Field-level ownership and source authority are explicit for every constitutionally material value.
6. Ownership remains singular and continuous throughout every lifecycle and workflow transition.
7. Correction, revision, reconciliation, publication, recovery, and certification authority are explicitly assigned.
8. Unauthorized ownership, mutation, publication, correction, and certification attempts fail closed.
9. Every discovered ownership defect has been resolved through constitutional modification or proven impossible.
10. Independent auditors can determine the authoritative owner and permitted authority for every constitutional object and fact without relying upon implementation convention or subjective interpretation.
