# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003-006

# Alternative Architecture Evaluation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the adversarial construction and evaluation of alternative constitutional architectures for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether the certified constitutional architecture is materially superior to credible alternative decompositions of responsibility, authority, ownership, interfaces, evidence, reconciliation, and lifecycle control.

The existing architecture shall not be presumed optimal merely because it is complete, certified, or operationally familiar.

Alternative architectures shall be constructed deliberately and evaluated against the same constitutional guarantees.

Any alternative that materially reduces complexity, strengthens ownership, improves determinism, simplifies interfaces, increases auditability, or reduces constitutional coupling without weakening guarantees shall generate a required constitutional modification.

---

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* construct credible alternative constitutional architectures;
* vary responsibility decomposition, office boundaries, bridge boundaries, truth ownership, lifecycle authority, and evidence custody;
* evaluate every alternative against the certified baseline;
* determine whether the baseline contains unnecessary offices, boundaries, dependencies, or responsibilities;
* determine whether responsibilities can be reassigned more coherently;
* determine whether interfaces can be reduced without losing constitutional completeness;
* identify architectures with stronger fail-closed behavior;
* identify architectures with simpler audit and replay;
* reject alternatives that weaken constitutional guarantees;
* adopt materially superior alternatives through formal constitutional modification.

---

# Evaluation Principle

The certified architecture shall remain authoritative unless an alternative is proven materially superior.

An alternative is materially superior only when it:

* preserves every constitutional guarantee;
* does not create new ambiguity;
* does not weaken fail-closed behavior;
* does not create shared or duplicated ownership;
* does not reduce evidence or traceability;
* does not reduce deterministic replay;
* provides measurable constitutional improvement.

Novelty alone shall not constitute improvement.

---

# Required Alternative Classes

The evaluation shall construct, at minimum, the following alternative classes.

## PT-AAE-001

### Consolidated Truth Architecture

Evaluate whether Performance Truth and one or more related truth offices could be constitutionally consolidated.

Potential consolidation targets include:

* Closed Position Truth;
* Historical Truth functions;
* performance-related reconciliation;
* performance evidence generation.

The evaluation shall determine whether consolidation:

* reduces interfaces;
* removes duplicated authority;
* simplifies correction propagation;
* creates excessive responsibility concentration;
* weakens separation of duties;
* creates competing truth classes.

Consolidation shall be rejected where it compromises singular truth ownership or independent verification.

---

## PT-AAE-002

### Narrow Performance Truth Architecture

Evaluate whether Performance Truth should own fewer responsibilities and consume more externally certified results.

Potential transfers include:

* benchmark governance;
* market-data normalization;
* attribution context;
* evidence storage;
* historical custody;
* certification state management;
* reconciliation execution.

The evaluation shall determine whether narrowing the office:

* improves cohesion;
* reduces coupling;
* weakens completeness;
* creates excessive external dependency;
* fragments lifecycle authority.

---

## PT-AAE-003

### Expanded Performance Truth Architecture

Evaluate whether responsibilities currently external to the office should move into Performance Truth.

Potential additions include:

* benchmark selection authority;
* calculation-context finalization;
* performance discrepancy ownership;
* revision-impact analysis;
* performance-specific evidence assembly.

Expansion shall be accepted only where the responsibility is inseparable from Performance Truth ownership and does not absorb external source truth.

---

## PT-AAE-004

### Bridge-Centric Architecture

Evaluate whether selected cross-office responsibilities should be assigned to certified enterprise bridges.

Potential bridge responsibilities include:

* identity correlation;
* schema translation;
* delivery-state management;
* boundary evidence;
* deterministic routing;
* reconciliation request transport.

No bridge-centric design may permit bridges to:

* create business truth;
* resolve semantic discrepancies;
* own canonical objects;
* control business lifecycles;
* certify office behavior.

---

## PT-AAE-005

### Event-Driven Architecture

Evaluate a constitutional design in which Performance Truth responds exclusively to certified enterprise events.

The evaluation shall examine:

* event ownership;
* event completeness;
* ordering;
* duplicate delivery;
* late arrival;
* replay;
* correction propagation;
* dependency recovery;
* publication eligibility.

Event-driven design shall not rely upon arrival order unless constitutionally guaranteed.

---

## PT-AAE-006

### Pull-Based Reconstruction Architecture

Evaluate a design in which Performance Truth reconstructs performance by retrieving authoritative source truth when required rather than receiving pushed updates.

The evaluation shall examine:

* source availability;
* retrieval consistency;
* historical reproducibility;
* source-version preservation;
* replay;
* dependency failure;
* snapshot stability.

Pull-based reconstruction shall fail if source state can change without preserved historical versions.

---

## PT-AAE-007

### Immutable Ledger Architecture

Evaluate a design based upon append-only constitutional events and derived immutable performance views.

The evaluation shall examine:

* event finality;
* correction semantics;
* supersession;
* reconstruction;
* storage ownership;
* event ordering;
* evidence;
* auditability;
* lifecycle simplicity.

Append-only design shall not erase the distinction between source events and derived Performance Truth.

---

## PT-AAE-008

### Partitioned Performance Architecture

Evaluate whether Performance Truth should be partitioned by:

* asset class;
* account;
* portfolio;
* enterprise instance;
* performance domain;
* reporting currency.

The evaluation shall determine whether partitioning:

* preserves one canonical truth hierarchy;
* prevents identity collisions;
* preserves cross-partition aggregation;
* avoids duplicated governance;
* supports deterministic reconciliation.

---

## PT-AAE-009

### Stateless Calculation Architecture

Evaluate a design in which calculations are stateless and all constitutional state resides in canonical records and evidence packages.

The evaluation shall examine:

* reproducibility;
* lifecycle control;
* revision behavior;
* performance;
* dependency burden;
* audit simplicity;
* hidden state elimination.

Stateless execution shall not eliminate constitutionally required state.

---

## PT-AAE-010

### Centralized Certification Architecture

Evaluate whether Performance Truth certification state should remain inside the office or be held exclusively by an independent certification authority.

The evaluation shall distinguish:

* object validation;
* office publication eligibility;
* audit certification;
* enterprise certification;
* constitutional freeze.

No architecture may permit self-certification.

---

# Evaluation Dimensions

## PT-AAE-011

### Constitutional Completeness

Determine whether the alternative governs every required constitutional behavior.

Any omitted responsibility shall constitute failure.

---

## PT-AAE-012

### Ownership Strength

Determine whether ownership is singular, explicit, complete, and aligned with authority.

---

## PT-AAE-013

### Determinism

Determine whether identical constitutional inputs produce identical outcomes without discretionary coordination.

---

### Interface Minimality

Measure whether the alternative reduces:

* interface count;
* exposed fields;
* ownership transfer;
* lifecycle transfer;
* coordination paths;
* failure paths.

Fewer interfaces shall not be preferred when they create overloaded or ambiguous contracts.

---

## PT-AAE-015

### Cohesion

Determine whether responsibilities assigned to each office directly support a unified constitutional mission.

---

## PT-AAE-016

### Coupling

Determine whether the alternative reduces:

* timing dependencies;
* shared calculations;
* shared lifecycle assumptions;
* duplicated truth;
* implicit coordination;
* dependency cycles.

---

## PT-AAE-017

### Fail-Closed Strength

Determine whether defects are detected earlier, contained more narrowly, and prevented from altering certified truth.

---

## PT-AAE-018

### Evidence and Traceability

Determine whether complete provenance, chain of custody, historical lineage, and certification evidence remain simpler and stronger.

---

## PT-AAE-019

### Replay and Auditability

Determine whether an independent auditor can reconstruct and reproduce constitutional behavior more directly.

---

## PT-AAE-020

### Scalability

Determine whether the alternative remains valid under increased volume, additional assets, distributed execution, and multiple enterprise instances.

---

## PT-AAE-021

### Modification Burden

Determine the constitutional, historical, interface, bridge, evidence, and re-certification cost of adopting the alternative.

Modification burden shall not excuse retention of a materially defective baseline.

# Comparative Architecture Matrix

Every alternative shall be compared against the certified baseline through a Comparative Architecture Matrix containing:

* evaluation dimension;
* baseline result;
* alternative result;
* objective evidence;
* constitutional advantage;
* constitutional disadvantage;
* introduced risk;
* removed risk;
* unresolved ambiguity;
* adoption recommendation.

No alternative may be accepted or rejected without a complete comparison.

---

# Constitutional Equivalence Test

Before superiority is evaluated, each alternative shall demonstrate constitutional equivalence.

The alternative shall preserve:

* all certified Performance Truth guarantees;
* singular ownership;
* deterministic lifecycle behavior;
* complete evidence;
* complete traceability;
* deterministic reconciliation;
* deterministic replay;
* fail-closed operation;
* historical preservation;
* independent auditability.

An alternative failing equivalence shall be rejected without superiority consideration.

---

# Superiority Test

A constitutionally equivalent alternative shall be considered materially superior only when it demonstrates one or more of the following without material disadvantage:

* fewer authoritative responsibilities;
* fewer constitutional interfaces;
* fewer dependency cycles;
* fewer duplicated validations;
* stronger ownership alignment;
* earlier failure detection;
* simpler reconciliation;
* simpler correction propagation;
* simpler proof construction;
* simpler independent replay;
* reduced architectural ambiguity;
* reduced long-term modification burden.

Subjective elegance shall not establish superiority.

---

# Alternative Disposition

Every evaluated alternative shall receive exactly one disposition.

## Adopt

The alternative is constitutionally equivalent and materially superior.

A constitutional modification campaign shall be initiated.

---

## Partially Adopt

Specific architectural elements are materially superior while the complete alternative is not.

Each accepted element shall generate a bounded Modification Record.

---

## Retain as Contingency

The alternative is not superior under current conditions but may become relevant under defined future conditions.

The triggering conditions shall be explicit.

---

## Reject

The alternative fails equivalence, introduces greater risk, or provides no material improvement.

The rejection rationale shall be preserved.

---

## Indeterminate

Available evidence cannot establish equivalence or superiority.

The alternative shall not be adopted and shall remain open pending further proof.

---

# Architectural Challenge Scenarios

Every alternative shall be tested against scenarios including:

* source truth correction after publication;
* conflicting source records;
* missing evidence;
* duplicate events;
* late-arriving events;
* partial workflow completion;
* bridge outage;
* Historian outage;
* Evidence Repository outage;
* multi-currency calculation;
* cross-account aggregation;
* parallel revisions;
* replay under historical schemas;
* multiple enterprise instances;
* distributed execution;
* ownership dispute;
* constitutional audit;
* constitutional freeze and later modification.

The alternative shall produce exactly one deterministic constitutional outcome for each scenario.

---

# Migration and Historical Integrity Review

For every adopted or partially adopted alternative, the audit shall determine:

* whether existing Performance Truth Records remain valid;
* whether object identities remain stable;
* whether ownership changes;
* whether historical records require migration;
* whether evidence packages remain complete;
* whether interfaces require versioning;
* whether bridges require replacement;
* whether prior certifications remain valid;
* whether bounded or complete re-certification is required.

No migration may silently alter historical meaning.

---

# Finding Classification

## Critical

The baseline architecture contains a defect capable of producing competing truth, ambiguous ownership, self-certification, or unsafe constitutional behavior.

Immediate modification is required.

---

## Major

A materially superior alternative exists and the baseline retains avoidable constitutional weakness.

Modification shall occur before architecture closure.

---

## Minor

An alternative offers bounded simplification or audit improvement without correcting a material constitutional defect.

Adoption may occur through approved modification.

---

## Observation

The alternative provides no current constitutional advantage but may inform future design.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth architecture shall fail closed whenever:

* an alternative reveals ambiguous ownership in the baseline;
* a required responsibility is omitted from every evaluated architecture;
* the baseline depends upon an unjustified circular dependency;
* an alternative cannot be compared because baseline architecture is undocumented;
* an adopted alternative lacks complete migration and re-certification requirements;
* ownership would become shared during transition;
* historical meaning could change without constitutional revision;
* constitutional equivalence cannot be demonstrated;
* superiority depends upon subjective preference;
* the disposition of a materially significant alternative remains unresolved.

No architectural transition shall enter operation until the adopted design has been constitutionally specified, independently certified, and authorized through Modification Order.

---

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

The certified baseline has been compared against complete credible alternatives.

No materially superior alternative remains unadopted.

Every alternative has received an evidence-supported disposition.

No unresolved Critical or Major architectural finding remains.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

The baseline remains constitutionally valid, but bounded Minor architectural improvements or contingency evaluations remain.

Each condition shall possess an approved owner, Modification Record, and completion requirement before Constitutional Architecture Closure.

---

## FAIL

The baseline contains material architectural weakness, or a materially superior alternative has been identified but not constitutionally adopted.

Architecture closure shall remain prohibited until modification and independent re-certification are complete.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. A complete certified Baseline Architecture Model has been produced.
2. Credible alternatives have been constructed for consolidation, narrowing, expansion, bridge use, event-driven operation, pull-based reconstruction, immutable ledgers, partitioning, stateless calculation, and certification authority.
3. Every alternative has been fully specified across responsibility, authority, ownership, lifecycle, interface, evidence, replay, reconciliation, failure, and audit dimensions.
4. Every alternative has undergone constitutional equivalence testing.
5. Every equivalent alternative has undergone objective superiority testing.
6. Every alternative has been challenged against normal, failure, correction, replay, scaling, and modification scenarios.
7. Every alternative has received exactly one final disposition.
8. Every adopted or partially adopted element has generated a complete Modification Record.
9. Migration, historical integrity, interface versioning, and re-certification impacts have been determined.
10. All evidence has been preserved within the Evidence Repository.
11. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
12. No materially superior constitutional architecture remains unexamined or unresolved.
