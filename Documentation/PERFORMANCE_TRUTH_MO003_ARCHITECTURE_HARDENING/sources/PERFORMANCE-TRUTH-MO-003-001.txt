# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003-001

# Responsibility Decomposition Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the adversarial audit of every constitutional responsibility assigned to the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether each responsibility presently assigned to the office belongs there constitutionally, whether any required responsibility is missing, whether any responsibility is duplicated elsewhere, and whether any responsibility should instead belong to another office or an enterprise bridge.

The existing decomposition shall not be presumed correct.

Every responsibility shall be treated as potentially misplaced until its relationship to the constitutional mission, authority, ownership, evidence, lifecycle, and enterprise interfaces has been independently justified.

No responsibility may remain within the Performance Truth Office solely because it is convenient, historically present, or technically adjacent.

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* discover every explicit and implicit responsibility currently performed by or attributed to the Performance Truth Office;
* identify every responsibility required by the office mission but not presently assigned;
* determine whether each responsibility belongs within the office;
* identify responsibilities duplicated across offices or bridges;
* identify responsibilities placed within Performance Truth that properly belong elsewhere;
* identify responsibilities placed elsewhere that constitutionally belong within Performance Truth;
* identify ownerless responsibilities;
* distinguish business authority from custody, validation, evidence preservation, and transport;
* eliminate convenience-based and implementation-driven decomposition;
* establish a hardened constitutional responsibility baseline.

---

# Constitutional Mission Test

Every responsibility assigned to the Performance Truth Office shall directly support one or more of the following mission elements:

* production of authoritative performance truth;
* preservation of performance truth;
* deterministic calculation of performance;
* constitutional publication of performance;
* constitutional correction or revision of performance;
* attribution of performance;
* reconciliation of performance with authoritative source truth;
* preservation of complete provenance;
* deterministic replay and audit reconstruction;
* certification of Performance Truth objects within the office’s authority.

A responsibility failing this test shall be presumed misplaced unless another explicit constitutional justification exists.

# Responsibility Classification

Every Responsibility Record shall receive exactly one classification.

## Core Office Responsibility

The responsibility is inseparable from the Performance Truth mission and requires Performance Truth authority or ownership.

It shall remain within the office.

---

## Supporting Office Responsibility

The responsibility supports Performance Truth but belongs constitutionally to another office with singular authority.

Performance Truth may consume its output but shall not own or duplicate it.

---

## Bridge Responsibility

The responsibility exists solely to transport, bind, translate, acknowledge, or reconcile across a certified boundary without creating business truth or independent authority.

It shall belong to a constitutionally defined enterprise bridge.

---

## Shared Infrastructure Responsibility

The responsibility provides non-business technical capability that may be shared without creating shared constitutional ownership.

Shared infrastructure shall not determine truth, authority, lifecycle, or certification.

---

## Duplicated Responsibility

The responsibility is authoritatively performed by more than one office or bridge.

The duplication shall be eliminated or reduced to non-authoritative defensive validation.

---

## Ownerless Responsibility

The responsibility is required but lacks a singular constitutional owner.

A constitutional owner shall be assigned.

---

## Misplaced Responsibility

The responsibility is currently assigned to an office whose mission, authority, or ownership does not justify it.

The responsibility shall be reassigned.

---

## Unnecessary Responsibility

The responsibility is not required to preserve constitutional guarantees.

It shall be removed.

---

## Indeterminate Responsibility

Available doctrine cannot determine the correct owner or necessity.

Indeterminate responsibilities shall fail closed pending constitutional resolution.

# Decomposition Tests

Each responsibility shall undergo all of the following tests.

## PT-RDA-001

### Mission Necessity Test

Determine whether the responsibility is necessary to fulfill the Performance Truth mission.

A responsibility is not necessary merely because the office currently performs it.

---

## PT-RDA-002

### Authority Test

Determine which office possesses the constitutional authority required to perform the responsibility.

No office may perform a responsibility whose authority belongs elsewhere.

---

## PT-RDA-003

### Ownership Test

Determine which office owns the canonical object or truth affected by the responsibility.

Responsibility shall ordinarily follow ownership unless constitutional doctrine explicitly separates them.

---

## PT-RDA-004

### Lifecycle Test

Determine which office controls the lifecycle transitions affected by the responsibility.

An office shall not control the lifecycle of externally owned truth.

---

## PT-RDA-005

### Truth-Origin Test

Determine whether the responsibility creates:

* source truth;
* derived truth;
* certification truth;
* historical truth;
* evidence;
* transport state.

The owner shall correspond to the truth class created.

---

## PT-RDA-006

### Evidence Test

Determine which office must produce and preserve evidence for the responsibility.

Evidence custody shall not be mistaken for business ownership.

---

## PT-RDA-007

### Cohesion Test

Determine whether the responsibility shares the same mission, objects, invariants, and failure behavior as the rest of Performance Truth.

Low-cohesion responsibilities shall be presumed misplaced.

---

## PT-RDA-008

### Coupling Test

Determine whether assigning the responsibility to Performance Truth creates unnecessary dependency upon another office or forces another office to depend upon Performance Truth improperly.

---

## PT-RDA-009

### Substitutability Test

Determine whether the responsibility could be moved to another office without changing constitutional meaning.

If so, the office possessing the stronger mission, authority, and ownership claim shall own it.

---

## PT-RDA-010

### Fail-Closed Test

Determine which office can most reliably detect failure before irreversible constitutional state change.

Failure ownership shall align with the authority capable of preventing unsafe operation.

---

## PT-RDA-011

### Auditability Test

Determine whether the assignment produces simpler, more objective, and more independently reproducible audit evidence.

Audit convenience alone shall not override constitutional ownership.

---

## PT-RDA-012

### Minimality Test

Determine whether the responsibility can be decomposed further without creating fragmented authority or whether it can be combined with related responsibilities without creating overlap.

# Authority, Ownership, Custody, and Performance Separation

The audit shall separately identify:

* who owns the business object;
* who possesses authority to change its state;
* who performs the operation;
* who stores the object;
* who stores the evidence;
* who transports the object;
* who verifies the result;
* who certifies the result.

These roles shall not be treated as equivalent.

Custody shall not imply ownership.

Execution shall not imply authority.

Validation shall not imply certification.

Transport shall not imply interpretation.

Evidence preservation shall not imply truth ownership.

---

# Bridge Assignment Standard

A responsibility may be assigned to an enterprise bridge only when it is limited to:

* transport;
* protocol binding;
* schema translation that preserves meaning;
* identity correlation;
* acknowledgment;
* delivery-state management;
* boundary evidence generation;
* deterministic routing.

A bridge shall not:

* calculate performance;
* decide truth;
* resolve business discrepancies;
* own canonical business objects;
* control business lifecycles;
* certify office behavior;
* reinterpret source values;
* select between competing constitutional authorities.

Responsibilities exceeding these limits shall belong to a constitutional office.

---

# Defensive Validation Standard

Multiple offices may validate the same received object defensively only when:

* one office remains the authoritative validator;
* defensive validation cannot create competing truth;
* rejection behavior is explicit;
* validation results remain independently traceable;
* downstream offices cannot override the authoritative owner;
* duplicated validation does not become duplicated responsibility.

Defensive duplication shall not be misclassified as shared ownership.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth Office shall fail closed whenever:

* a required responsibility lacks a constitutional owner;
* multiple offices claim authoritative responsibility;
* authority and ownership conflict;
* a bridge performs an unauthorized business responsibility;
* a responsibility cannot be connected directly to a constitutional mission;
* lifecycle authority is ambiguous;
* correction or publication authority is disputed;
* a missing responsibility prevents complete performance truth;
* reassignment would break evidence continuity;
* historical records may depend upon an unresolved responsibility defect;
* constitutional decomposition permits multiple valid outcomes;
* the receiving owner of a transferred responsibility has not accepted authority.

No affected Performance Truth object shall be created, published, revised, reconciled as complete, or certified until the responsibility defect is constitutionally resolved.
