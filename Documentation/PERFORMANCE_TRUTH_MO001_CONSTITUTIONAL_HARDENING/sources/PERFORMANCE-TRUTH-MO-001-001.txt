# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-001

# Constitutional Assumption Discovery

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the adversarial discovery, classification, and resolution of every implicit assumption contained within the certified constitutional specification of the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether any required constitutional behavior depends upon facts, conditions, interpretations, conventions, or dependency behavior that the Constitution does not explicitly guarantee.

The certified baseline shall not be presumed complete.

Every behavior necessary to produce, preserve, reconcile, revise, audit, or certify Performance Truth shall be treated as unsupported until its governing assumptions are identified and constitutionally justified.

Every discovered assumption shall be:

* explicitly specified as constitutional doctrine;
* demonstrated to follow necessarily from existing constitutional doctrine; or
* eliminated from the office architecture.

No unsupported assumption may remain within the hardened constitutional baseline.

---

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* identify every explicit and implicit assumption governing the office;
* determine where assumptions enter constitutional behavior;
* identify every artifact, lifecycle, calculation, interface, dependency, and certification decision affected by each assumption;
* test whether each assumption is guaranteed by existing doctrine;
* expose contradictions between assumptions and certified requirements;
* eliminate reliance upon informal convention;
* convert necessary assumptions into explicit constitutional requirements;
* remove assumptions that are unnecessary or unsafe;
* preserve backward compatibility with all valid certified behavior;
* establish permanent evidence of assumption resolution.

# Assumption Definition

For purposes of this order, a constitutional assumption is any condition treated as true, stable, complete, unique, available, ordered, compatible, or authoritative without an explicit constitutional guarantee establishing that condition.

Assumptions include, but are not limited to:

* presumed dependency availability;
* presumed uniqueness of identifiers;
* presumed completeness of evidence;
* presumed ordering of events;
* presumed stability of schemas;
* presumed mathematical conventions;
* presumed currency or unit consistency;
* presumed finality of external truth;
* presumed absence of duplicate inputs;
* presumed ownership clarity;
* presumed synchronization between offices;
* presumed reliability of timestamps;
* presumed deterministic iteration order;
* presumed compatibility of revisions;
* presumed retention of historical records;
* presumed certification validity;
* presumed equivalence between replay and original execution.

An assumption remains constitutional even when embedded only within implementation, documentation, test fixtures, operator practice, or bridge behavior.

---

# Discovery Method

The review shall examine every constitutional statement and ask:

1. What must already be true for this requirement to function?
2. Which office guarantees that condition?
3. Where is that guarantee explicitly established?
4. What occurs if the condition is false?
5. Is the failure detected before constitutional state changes?
6. Can two compliant implementations interpret the condition differently?
7. Can replay produce a different result because the assumption was not preserved?
8. Does certification evidence prove the condition or merely presume it?

Any requirement lacking deterministic answers shall generate an Assumption Candidate.

# Assumption Classification

Every Assumption Candidate shall receive exactly one classification.

## Explicitly Specified

The assumption is directly guaranteed by existing constitutional doctrine.

The governing doctrine and evidence shall be cited.

No modification is required unless the doctrine is ambiguous or incomplete.

---

## Indirectly Guaranteed

The assumption follows necessarily from one or more existing constitutional requirements.

The logical derivation shall be documented.

An indirect guarantee shall be accepted only when no constitutionally compliant interpretation can violate the assumption.

---

## Unsupported but Necessary

The assumption is required for constitutional behavior but is not presently guaranteed.

A constitutional modification shall be produced.

Affected behavior shall fail closed until the modification is certified.

---

## Unsupported and Unnecessary

The assumption is not constitutionally guaranteed and need not exist.

The architecture shall be modified to eliminate reliance upon it.

---

## Contradictory

The assumption conflicts with existing constitutional doctrine or another required assumption.

The conflict shall be resolved through formal constitutional modification.

---

## Indeterminate

Available doctrine and evidence cannot determine whether the assumption is valid.

Indeterminate assumptions shall be treated as unsupported and shall fail closed pending resolution.

---

# Assumption Dependency Analysis

For every Unsupported, Contradictory, or Indeterminate assumption, the review shall identify:

* upstream sources;
* downstream consumers;
* affected calculations;
* affected publications;
* affected revisions;
* affected reconciliations;
* affected evidence packages;
* affected certification decisions;
* affected historical records;
* affected bridges;
* potential propagation into other offices.

The analysis shall determine whether previously certified records relied upon the assumption.

Where historical reliance is possible, retrospective reconciliation shall be required.

---

# Adversarial Validation

Each assumption shall be challenged through at least one scenario in which the assumption is false.

The scenario shall determine whether:

* the condition is detected;
* detection occurs before publication;
* constitutional state remains unchanged;
* partial state is created;
* evidence records the failure;
* reconciliation is initiated;
* replay reproduces the same failure;
* recovery requires unauthorized discretion.

An assumption shall not be considered harmless merely because normal execution does not violate it.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth Office shall fail closed whenever:

* required behavior depends upon an unclassified assumption;
* an assumption is classified as Unsupported, Contradictory, or Indeterminate;
* the constitutional owner of a necessary condition cannot be identified;
* an indirect guarantee cannot be logically proven;
* assumption failure cannot be detected before publication;
* historical records may rely upon an unresolved assumption;
* required evidence cannot demonstrate assumption validity;
* assumption resolution would weaken a certified guarantee;
* multiple constitutional interpretations remain possible.

No affected Performance Truth Record shall be newly published, revised, reconciled as complete, or certified until the governing assumption is resolved.

---

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

Every discovered assumption has been classified and resolved.

All necessary assumptions are explicitly specified or necessarily guaranteed.

All unsupported assumptions have been constitutionalized or eliminated.

No unresolved Critical or Major assumption findings remain.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

No unsupported assumption presently threatens published Performance Truth, but bounded Minor findings remain.

Every condition shall possess an approved owner, remediation order, and completion requirement.

Conditional findings shall be resolved before Constitutional Hardening Closure.

---

## FAIL

One or more necessary assumptions remain unsupported, contradictory, indeterminate, ownerless, or capable of producing unsafe constitutional behavior.

Affected functions shall remain fail closed until remediation and independent verification are complete.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every requirement within the certified Performance Truth constitutional baseline has been reviewed for implicit assumptions.
2. Every discovered assumption has an immutable Assumption Candidate Record.
3. Every assumption has received exactly one constitutional classification.
4. Every indirect guarantee has been formally derived from existing doctrine.
5. Every unsupported necessary assumption has generated an explicit constitutional modification.
6. Every unsupported unnecessary assumption has been eliminated from required behavior.
7. Every contradictory or indeterminate assumption has been resolved or placed under fail-closed restriction.
8. Historical reliance upon defective assumptions has been assessed and reconciled where required.
9. Complete traceability exists between assumptions, requirements, objects, offices, bridges, evidence, and certification decisions.
10. All findings and resolutions have been preserved within the Evidence Repository.
11. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
12. No unsupported constitutional assumption remains within the hardened Performance Truth baseline.
