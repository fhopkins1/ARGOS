# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-009

# Enterprise Bridge Dependency Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Enterprise Bridge Dependency Audit governing every bridge connected to the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to identify and eliminate hidden responsibilities, duplicated calculations, ownership leakage, lifecycle leakage, truth duplication, circular dependency, and undocumented behavioral coupling across enterprise bridge boundaries.

The objective is not to confirm that existing bridges function.

The objective is to determine whether any bridge has acquired constitutional responsibility that belongs to an office, obscures the authoritative source of truth, or permits behavior beyond the minimum necessary to transfer constitutionally governed objects and evidence.

Every discovered weakness shall either:

* be demonstrated constitutionally impossible; or
* result in an approved constitutional modification.

# Audit Scope

This audit shall examine every bridge that:

* delivers inputs to Performance Truth;
* receives outputs from Performance Truth;
* transfers ownership involving Performance Truth;
* transports evidence involving Performance Truth;
* supports reconciliation involving Performance Truth;
* supports replay involving Performance Truth;
* communicates status or failure involving Performance Truth;
* participates indirectly in Performance Truth workflows.

The audit shall include registered, generated, runtime-discovered, dormant, recovery, administrative, and compatibility bridges.

No bridge shall be excluded because it is considered passive, internal, temporary, or implementation-specific.

---

# Bridge Audit Principle

A bridge shall possess no independent business authority.

A bridge may:

* validate transport eligibility;
* preserve object identity;
* preserve evidence identity;
* enforce interface contracts;
* transfer constitutionally authorized objects;
* transfer constitutionally authorized ownership;
* record bridge evidence;
* reject invalid transfer.

A bridge shall not:

* create business truth;
* reinterpret business truth;
* calculate performance;
* revise performance;
* reconcile truth independently;
* assign authority;
* invent lifecycle state;
* retain undeclared ownership;
* substitute for an office workflow.

Any bridge behavior exceeding constitutionally minimal transfer responsibility constitutes a hardening finding.

Any undiscovered or undocumented bridge constitutes constitutional weakness.

The discovered bridge inventory shall be reconciled against:

* the authoritative bridge registry;

* dependency manifests;
* runtime configuration;

The audit shall identify:

* registered bridges not implemented;

Every discrepancy shall produce a formal finding.

# Minimal Responsibility Audit

For every bridge, the auditor shall determine the minimum constitutional responsibility required to complete its transfer purpose.

The implemented and specified bridge responsibility shall be compared against that minimum.

The audit shall identify responsibility for:

* calculation;
* aggregation;
* normalization;
* attribution;
* correction;
* revision;
* reconciliation;
* publication;
* certification;
* lifecycle transition;
* ownership assignment;
* evidence interpretation.

Any such responsibility performed by a bridge shall require explicit constitutional authority or removal.

Convenience, latency reduction, compatibility, or implementation simplicity shall not justify expanded bridge authority.

---

# Hidden Responsibility Discovery

The auditor shall search for responsibilities performed implicitly through:

* message transformation;
* default field population;
* inferred state;
* silent normalization;
* conditional routing;
* retry logic;
* exception handling;
* compatibility translation;
* caching;
* batching;
* deduplication;
* bridge-local persistence.

Each discovered responsibility shall be classified as:

* constitutionally authorized bridge behavior;
* office-owned behavior incorrectly delegated to a bridge;
* unsupported behavior;
* duplicated behavior;
* behavior requiring constitutional modification.

No bridge responsibility shall remain implicit.

---

# Duplicated Calculation Audit

The auditor shall determine whether any bridge performs or reproduces calculations owned by the Performance Truth Office or another constitutional office.

The audit shall search for duplicated:

* performance calculations;
* interval calculations;
* cumulative calculations;
* benchmark calculations;
* normalization;
* attribution;
* currency conversion;
* precision adjustment;
* rounding;
* correction propagation;
* reconciliation comparison.

A bridge may validate that a supplied result conforms to a declared contract.

It shall not independently create a competing calculated value unless explicitly authorized by constitutional doctrine.

Duplicated calculations that can produce distinct outputs constitute constitutional failure.

---

# Ownership Leakage Audit

The auditor shall verify that ownership remains explicit and unique throughout every bridge interaction.

The audit shall attempt to identify:

* ownership retained after authorized transfer;
* ownership transferred without authority;
* ownership duplicated across bridge endpoints;
* temporary ownership lacking constitutional definition;
* ownership inferred from message location;
* ownership assigned by transport success;
* ownership lost during retry or recovery;
* bridge custody treated as business ownership.

Bridge custody shall never be interpreted as constitutional ownership.

Any period during which ownership cannot be determined uniquely constitutes a hardening finding.

---

# Lifecycle Leakage Audit

The auditor shall determine whether any bridge initiates, completes, or infers lifecycle transitions that belong to a constitutional office.

The audit shall challenge bridge behavior involving:

* creation;
* validation;
* acceptance;
* publication;
* correction;
* revision;
* supersession;
* rejection;
* archival;
* certification.

Transport events shall not silently become business lifecycle events.

A bridge acknowledgment shall not constitute constitutional acceptance unless explicitly defined.

A delivery failure shall not alter business state except through authorized workflow behavior.

---

# Truth Duplication Audit

The auditor shall search for duplicate or competing truth created through:

* bridge-local copies;
* transformed payloads;
* cached records;
* compatibility objects;
* replay artifacts;
* reconciliation shadows;
* retry records;
* dead-letter records;
* transport envelopes.

The audit shall determine whether each copy is:

* authoritative truth;
* non-authoritative evidence;
* transport representation;
* prohibited duplicate truth.

Every non-authoritative copy shall be explicitly marked and constitutionally incapable of becoming authoritative through reuse, replay, recovery, or publication.

---

# Transformation Audit

Every bridge transformation shall be independently reviewed.

The audit shall identify transformations involving:

* field renaming;
* type conversion;
* schema translation;
* unit conversion;
* precision conversion;
* timestamp conversion;
* identity mapping;
* enumeration mapping;
* default insertion;
* field omission;
* aggregation;
* decomposition.

Each transformation shall be:

* explicitly specified;
* deterministic;
* reversible where constitutionally required;
* evidence-producing;
* incapable of changing constitutional meaning.

Lossy or ambiguous transformation constitutes constitutional weakness.

# Identity Preservation Audit

Every bridge shall preserve the immutable identity of transported constitutional objects.

The audit shall challenge:

* identifier replacement;
* identifier truncation;
* namespace collision;
* identity regeneration;
* composite identity decomposition;
* version identity confusion;
* envelope identity substituted for object identity.

Bridge-local identifiers may supplement but shall never replace constitutional object identity.

# Temporal Boundary Audit

The auditor shall determine whether bridge behavior introduces temporal ambiguity through:

* delayed delivery;
* reordered delivery;
* duplicate delivery;
* concurrent delivery;
* retry;
* timeout;
* clock inconsistency;
* batching;
* replay;
* recovery.

Transport Time shall remain distinct from:

* Event Time;
* Effective Time;
* Observation Time;
* Calculation Time;
* Publication Time;
* Correction Time.

No bridge timestamp shall silently redefine constitutional business time.

---

# Ordering and Concurrency Audit

The auditor shall construct scenarios involving:

* messages arriving out of order;
* concurrent transfers of related objects;
* duplicate delivery;
* delayed acknowledgment;
* partial batch delivery;
* simultaneous corrections;
* concurrent replay and live operation.

The bridge architecture shall preserve deterministic constitutional behavior regardless of transport ordering.

Where ordering is constitutionally required, the bridge contract shall state and enforce that requirement explicitly.

---

# Retry and Idempotency Audit

The auditor shall verify bridge behavior under:

* retry after timeout;
* retry after partial delivery;
* duplicate acknowledgment;
* destination restart;
* source restart;
* bridge restart;
* uncertain delivery state.

Retry shall not produce:

* duplicate truth;
* duplicate ownership;
* duplicate lifecycle transition;
* duplicate publication;
* duplicate evidence interpreted as distinct business evidence.

Idempotency shall be tied to immutable constitutional identity rather than mutable transport state.

---

# Failure Boundary Audit

The auditor shall determine whether bridge failures remain confined to the bridge boundary.

The audit shall introduce:

* malformed payloads;
* invalid identities;
* unsupported versions;
* unavailable dependencies;
* corrupted evidence;
* authorization failure;
* ownership conflict;
* reconciliation conflict;
* partial persistence;
* transport interruption.

The bridge shall fail closed before invalid state reaches the receiving office.

Failure shall not authorize the bridge to repair, infer, or replace constitutional truth.

# Circular Dependency Audit

The auditor shall construct the complete dependency graph for every bridge connected to Performance Truth.

The audit shall identify:

* direct circular dependencies;
* indirect circular dependencies;
* reconciliation loops;

* correction propagation loops;
* readiness deadlocks;

A bridge topology shall not require Performance Truth output to establish the validity of its own authoritative input.

Every cycle shall be either constitutionally disproven as non-authoritative or eliminated.

# Bridge Bypass Audit

The auditor shall search for pathways that bypass an authoritative bridge or office interface.

Potential bypasses include:

* direct database access;
* shared mutable storage;
* administrative utilities;
* test hooks;
* compatibility endpoints;
* cache reads;
* reporting queries;
* recovery scripts;
* manual file exchange;
* internal service calls.

A bypass capable of affecting constitutional behavior constitutes certification failure unless explicitly authorized and governed as a constitutional interface.

---

# Bridge Substitution Audit

The auditor shall determine whether one bridge can be substituted for another without constitutional authorization.

The audit shall challenge:

* fallback routes;
* alternate transports;
* legacy interfaces;
* emergency pathways;
* test adapters;
* replay adapters.

Substitution shall not alter authority, ownership, evidence, ordering, or failure behavior.

Undocumented substitution is prohibited.

# Dormant and Legacy Bridge Audit

Dormant, deprecated, compatibility, and legacy bridges shall be independently assessed.

The audit shall verify that each such bridge is:

* constitutionally authorized;
* unreachable during normal operation unless required;
* incapable of bypassing current controls;
* version constrained;
* evidence-producing;
* subject to removal or explicit retention authority.

Uncontrolled dormant bridges constitute hidden enterprise exposure.

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every bridge possesses one explicit constitutional purpose;
* every bridge responsibility is minimal and documented;
* bridges do not create, reinterpret, or own business truth;
* constitutional ownership remains unique across every transfer;
* transport events do not silently create lifecycle transitions;
* immutable object identity survives every bridge interaction;
* all transformations preserve constitutional meaning;
* retries cannot duplicate constitutional effects;
* bridge custody is never treated as business ownership;
* every bridge interaction produces complete evidence;
* no bridge creates a circular authoritative dependency;
* no undocumented bridge or bypass pathway exists.

Violation of any invariant constitutes constitutional weakness.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent adversarial review demonstrates that:

1. every bridge connected directly or indirectly to the Performance Truth Office has been independently discovered and reconciled against the authoritative bridge registry;
2. every bridge possesses one explicit, minimal, and constitutionally authorized responsibility;
3. no bridge performs undocumented calculations, reconciliation, lifecycle transitions, ownership assignment, or truth creation;
4. constitutional ownership remains unique and continuously determinable throughout every bridge interaction;
5. no bridge-local copy, transformation, cache, retry artifact, or recovery artifact can become competing constitutional truth;
6. every transformation preserves immutable identity, constitutional meaning, temporal semantics, and complete provenance;
7. retries, concurrency, delayed delivery, reordering, and uncertain delivery states cannot create duplicate constitutional effects;
8. no circular authoritative dependency, hidden ownership coupling, undocumented bypass, or ungoverned bridge substitution remains;
9. every bridge failure produces deterministic fail-closed behavior and complete constitutional evidence;
10. every discovered weakness has been constitutionally disproven or converted into an approved modification requirement; and
11. the Performance Truth Office demonstrates stronger enterprise boundary integrity than its **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003** certified baseline.
