# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-004

# Truth Integrity Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Truth Integrity Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to aggressively challenge the constitutional assumption that the Performance Truth Office possesses a single, complete, deterministic, and immutable definition of "Performance Truth."

The objective of this audit is to discover every circumstance under which multiple constitutional interpretations of performance truth could simultaneously exist.

Every discovered ambiguity shall either:

* be proven constitutionally impossible; or
* result in constitutional modification.

The objective is constitutional falsification, not confirmation.
