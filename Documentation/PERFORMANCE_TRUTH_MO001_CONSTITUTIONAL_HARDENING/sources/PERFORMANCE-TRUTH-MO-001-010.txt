# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-010

# Certification Blind Spot Discovery

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Certification Blind Spot Discovery campaign for the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to assume that the completed ECS-003 certification campaign failed to inspect one or more constitutionally important properties and to identify every material dimension of correctness that may have escaped prior review.

This order shall not presume that prior certification evidence is complete merely because certification was granted.

Every omitted certification dimension shall either be proven constitutionally irrelevant or incorporated into the hardened certification baseline.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This Modification Order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003
* Enterprise Modification Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Truth Doctrine
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine

Nothing within this order may weaken previously certified constitutional guarantees.

---

# Campaign Objective

The objective of this campaign is to discover properties that:

* were not tested;
* were tested incompletely;
* were inferred rather than demonstrated;
* were obscured by test design;
* depended upon hidden assumptions;
* were excluded by certification scope;
* were represented by insufficient evidence;
* could fail outside the certified scenarios.

The campaign shall seek falsification rather than confirmation.

---

# Certification Scope Reconstruction

The independent reviewer shall reconstruct the complete scope of prior certification.

The reconstruction shall identify:

* every constitutional requirement reviewed;
* every implementation behavior tested;
* every interface exercised;
* every dependency represented;
* every failure mode injected;
* every replay scenario performed;
* every evidence package examined;
* every explicit exclusion;
* every implicit exclusion;
* every untested assumption.

Certification scope shall be derived from evidence rather than summary claims.

---

# Requirement-to-Test Coverage

Every constitutional requirement shall be mapped to one or more objective certification tests.

The mapping shall identify:

* requirement identifier;
* responsible certification order;
* test identifier;
* tested behavior;
* input conditions;
* expected result;
* generated evidence;
* observed result;
* coverage limitations.

A requirement without direct test coverage constitutes a certification blind spot unless non-testability is constitutionally justified.

---

# Invariant Coverage

Every constitutional invariant shall be independently mapped to evidence demonstrating:

* normal-state enforcement;
* failure-state enforcement;
* boundary-state enforcement;
* recovery-state enforcement;
* replay enforcement;
* cross-office enforcement.

Certification of ordinary behavior alone shall not establish invariant coverage.

---

# Negative-Space Analysis

The reviewer shall identify behavior that prior certification did not attempt to observe.

Negative-space analysis shall include:

* omitted inputs;
* omitted lifecycle states;
* omitted transition combinations;
* omitted dependency states;
* omitted interface sequences;
* omitted temporal arrangements;
* omitted evidence failures;
* omitted concurrency conditions;
* omitted recovery conditions;
* omitted scale conditions.

Absence from the certification suite shall not be interpreted as impossibility.

---

# Scenario Diversity Audit

Prior certification scenarios shall be examined for excessive similarity.

The audit shall determine whether certification relied upon:

* repeated use of identical fixtures;
* repeated use of identical workflows;
* narrow value ranges;
* single dependency configurations;
* single temporal orderings;
* single benchmark types;
* single correction paths;
* single publication paths.

Insufficient scenario diversity constitutes a certification weakness.

---

# Oracle Integrity Audit

Every prior certification decision shall be examined to determine whether the expected result was independently derived.

The audit shall reject circular validation in which:

* implementation output defines expected output;
* duplicated implementation logic defines the test oracle;
* generated evidence validates itself;
* a single dependency serves as both source and verifier;
* certification scripts reproduce the same defect as the implementation.

Every certification oracle shall possess independent constitutional authority.

---

# Evidence Sufficiency Audit

Certification evidence shall be examined for whether it proves the claimed property rather than merely documenting execution.

The reviewer shall distinguish between:

* proof of execution;
* proof of result;
* proof of causality;
* proof of ownership;
* proof of determinism;
* proof of completeness;
* proof of fail-closed behavior;
* proof of historical preservation.

Evidence that demonstrates only that a test ran shall not establish constitutional compliance.

---

# Sampling Blind Spot Audit

Where prior certification relied upon sampling, the reviewer shall determine:

* what population was sampled;
* how samples were selected;
* which classes were excluded;
* whether rare states were represented;
* whether boundary conditions were represented;
* whether failure states were represented;
* whether the sample can support the certification conclusion.

Unsupported extrapolation from sampled behavior is prohibited.

---

# Lifecycle Coverage Audit

The reviewer shall verify that certification exercised every legal lifecycle state and transition for every canonical object.

The audit shall include:

* creation;
* validation;
* publication;
* correction;
* revision;
* supersession;
* archival;
* replay;
* rejection;
* recovery.

Untested transitions constitute certification blind spots.

---

# Interface Sequence Audit

Certification shall be challenged using interface sequences that differ from nominal operation.

The reviewer shall examine:

* reordered valid messages;
* repeated messages;
* delayed messages;
* concurrent messages;
* stale messages;
* superseded messages;
* partially completed exchanges;
* recovery after interrupted exchanges.

Interface correctness shall not be inferred from isolated endpoint validation.

---

# Cross-Requirement Interaction Audit

The reviewer shall test whether individually certified requirements remain correct when combined.

Interaction analysis shall include:

* correction during reconciliation;
* replay during dependency degradation;
* publication during concurrent revision;
* attribution following late-arriving evidence;
* archival during certification;
* benchmark revision during cumulative calculation;
* ownership transfer during workflow interruption.

Certification of isolated requirements shall not establish correctness of their interaction.

---

# Recovery Coverage Audit

Every certified failure mode shall be examined for post-failure behavior.

The reviewer shall verify:

* state preservation;
* failure evidence;
* retry eligibility;
* recovery authorization;
* recalculation;
* reconciliation;
* publication eligibility;
* historical continuity.

A system that fails closed but cannot recover deterministically is not fully certified.

---

# Repeated-Failure Audit

The campaign shall test repeated and compound failures, including:

* the same failure occurring more than once;
* a second failure during recovery;
* evidence failure during failure reporting;
* dependency failure during reconciliation;
* archive failure during correction;
* replay failure following prior replay failure.

Single-failure certification shall not establish compound-failure correctness.

---

# Longitudinal Certification Audit

The reviewer shall determine whether prior certification demonstrated continued correctness across time.

The audit shall include:

* repeated operation;
* accumulated revisions;
* prolonged evidence retention;
* dependency upgrades;
* benchmark evolution;
* archival migration;
* modification lineage;
* certification renewal.

Point-in-time success shall not automatically establish long-term constitutional stability.

---

# Environmental Assumption Audit

The campaign shall identify environmental conditions assumed by prior certification, including:

* storage availability;
* clock behavior;
* process ordering;
* dependency latency;
* repository layout;
* configuration presence;
* identifier uniqueness;
* serialization stability;
* resource availability.

Every environmental assumption shall be made explicit and constitutionally governed where material.

---

# Human Procedure Audit

Where certification depended upon human action, the reviewer shall determine whether:

* instructions were complete;
* decisions were deterministic;
* discretion was constrained;
* evidence was preserved;
* authority was verified;
* repeatability was demonstrated.

Unbounded human discretion constitutes a certification blind spot.

---

# Toolchain Independence Audit

Certification conclusions shall be challenged using independent tooling where practicable.

The reviewer shall determine whether prior certification depended upon:

* a single test runner;
* a single parser;
* a single evidence generator;
* a single replay utility;
* a single repository packaging process;
* a single audit script.

A defect shared by the implementation and certification toolchain shall not remain undetected.

---

# Certification Boundary Audit

The reviewer shall identify properties excluded because they were assigned to another office, bridge, program, or future audit.

For every exclusion, the reviewer shall verify:

* constitutional ownership;
* receiving certification authority;
* actual test coverage;
* evidence availability;
* absence of coverage gaps between programs.

A property assigned elsewhere but certified nowhere constitutes a constitutional defect.

---

# Blind Spot Classification

Every discovered blind spot shall be classified as:

* constitutional omission;
* implementation omission;
* test omission;
* evidence omission;
* oracle weakness;
* scope exclusion;
* interaction omission;
* recovery omission;
* longitudinal omission;
* toolchain dependency.

Classification shall not replace remediation.

---

# Required Constitutional Additions

Where a blind spot reveals a missing constitutional property, this order shall produce a proposed constitutional addition specifying:

* new requirement;
* constitutional owner;
* affected objects;
* affected interfaces;
* required invariants;
* required evidence;
* required fail-closed behavior;
* required certification tests;
* backward-compatibility impact.

No constitutional addition becomes authoritative without approved modification authority.

---

# Certification Suite Expansion

Every validated blind spot shall produce one or more permanent certification additions.

Each addition shall define:

* target requirement;
* adversarial scenario;
* independent oracle;
* deterministic procedure;
* required evidence;
* acceptance criteria;
* regression inclusion.

New certification coverage shall become part of all future certification campaigns.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every constitutional requirement possesses objective certification coverage;
* every invariant is tested under normal, boundary, failure, and recovery conditions;
* every certification conclusion is supported by sufficient evidence;
* every test oracle is independent of the implementation under test;
* every certification exclusion has an identified and verified certification owner;
* every discovered blind spot produces remediation or constitutional disproof;
* every permanent certification addition enters future regression testing;
* certification scope is explicit and independently reconstructable.

Violation of any invariant constitutes a constitutional hardening finding.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall not retain hardened certification status whenever:

* material certification scope cannot be reconstructed;
* constitutional requirements lack objective test coverage;
* certification evidence is insufficient;
* test oracles are circular;
* exclusions create unowned certification gaps;
* discovered blind spots remain unresolved;
* new certification requirements have not been added to regression coverage;
* prior certification conclusions cannot be independently reproduced.

Certification confidence shall never substitute for demonstrated coverage.

---

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This Modification Order shall be considered complete when independent adversarial review demonstrates that:

1. the full scope and limitations of the prior ECS-003 certification campaign have been independently reconstructed;
2. every constitutional requirement and invariant possesses direct, objective, and reproducible certification coverage;
3. all negative-space, lifecycle, interface-sequence, interaction, recovery, compound-failure, and longitudinal dimensions have been examined;
4. certification evidence proves the claimed constitutional properties rather than merely recording test execution;
5. every certification oracle is independent and free from circular validation;
6. every excluded property has a verified constitutional owner and confirmed certification coverage;
7. every discovered blind spot has been remediated, incorporated into permanent regression certification, or constitutionally proven irrelevant;
8. no unresolved material certification omission remains; and
9. the Performance Truth Office demonstrates a materially stronger and more complete certification architecture than the **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003 Constitutional Freeze Baseline**.
