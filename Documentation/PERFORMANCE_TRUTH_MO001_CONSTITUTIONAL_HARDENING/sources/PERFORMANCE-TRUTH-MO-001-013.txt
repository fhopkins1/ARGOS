# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-013

# Constitutional Simplicity Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Constitutional Simplicity Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to challenge the constitutional specification under the assumption that unnecessary complexity, duplicated doctrine, redundant requirements, overlapping responsibilities, and excessive constitutional language may obscure rather than strengthen constitutional certainty.

The objective of this audit is not to reduce rigor.

The objective is to determine whether every constitutional requirement contributes unique constitutional value and whether equivalent guarantees can be achieved through a simpler constitutional specification.

Only simplification that preserves every certified constitutional guarantee is permitted.

# Audit Objectives

This audit shall determine whether constitutional doctrine contains:

* duplicated requirements;
* redundant doctrine;
* overlapping responsibilities;
* unnecessary abstraction;
* repetitive invariants;
* excessive terminology;
* unnecessary lifecycle complexity;
* unnecessary interface complexity;
* unnecessary certification complexity;
* hidden architectural complexity.

Every identified complexity shall either be proven constitutionally necessary or be eliminated through constitutional simplification.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Constitutional simplification shall:

* preserve deterministic behavior;
* preserve constitutional ownership;
* preserve historical truth;
* preserve traceability;
* preserve auditability;
* preserve fail-closed behavior;
* preserve backward constitutional compatibility.

Simplicity shall never weaken constitutional guarantees.

---

# MO-013-001 Constitutional Redundancy Audit

Review the complete constitutional specification.

Identify:

* duplicated doctrine;
* repeated constitutional requirements;
* repeated invariants;
* repeated certification rules;
* repeated ownership definitions.

Every duplicate shall either:

* be consolidated;
* become a constitutional reference;
* be justified as independently necessary.

---

# MO-013-002 Responsibility Simplification Audit

Determine whether responsibilities assigned to the Performance Truth Office are:

* constitutionally unique;
* unnecessarily duplicated;
* partially delegated without authority;
* shared without explicit governance.

Every responsibility shall possess one constitutional owner.

---

# MO-013-003 Object Model Simplification Audit

Review every canonical object.

Determine whether:

* multiple objects represent identical constitutional concepts;
* relationships are unnecessarily complex;
* lifecycle distinctions are artificial;
* object separation remains constitutionally justified.

Canonical objects shall remain minimal while preserving constitutional meaning.

---

# MO-013-004 Lifecycle Simplification Audit

Challenge every lifecycle state.

Determine whether:

* adjacent states can be merged;
* transitions are redundant;

* lifecycle complexity exceeds constitutional necessity.

Every remaining lifecycle state shall possess independent constitutional purpose.

# MO-013-005 Interface Simplification Audit

Review every constitutional interface.

Determine whether:

* interfaces duplicate information;
* ownership overlaps exist;

* equivalent guarantees can be achieved through fewer interfaces.

Interfaces shall remain constitutionally minimal.

* duplicate calculations;

Review certification doctrine.

# MO-013-010 Terminology Simplification Audit

Review constitutional terminology.

Identify:

* synonymous terms;
* inconsistent definitions;
* overlapping vocabulary;

* unnecessary terminology proliferation.

Each constitutional concept shall possess one canonical definition.

---

# MO-013-011 Dependency Simplification Audit

Review constitutional dependencies.

Determine whether:

* dependencies are duplicated;
* indirect dependencies duplicate direct dependencies;
* dependency ownership is unnecessarily distributed;
* bridge responsibilities can be reduced.

Dependency architecture shall remain minimal and deterministic.

---

# MO-013-012 Architectural Coupling Audit

Search for constitutional provisions that unnecessarily couple Performance Truth to implementation or enterprise architecture.

Determine whether constitutional guarantees remain valid under:

* implementation replacement;
* technology replacement;
* deployment changes;
* infrastructure changes.

Constitutional doctrine shall remain implementation-independent.

---

# MO-013-013 Simplification Equivalence Verification

For every proposed simplification, independently verify that:

* constitutional guarantees remain unchanged;
* deterministic behavior remains unchanged;
* evidence remains complete;
* traceability remains complete;
* historical integrity remains unchanged;
* replay remains deterministic;
* fail-closed behavior remains unchanged.

No simplification shall introduce behavioral regression.

---

# MO-013-014 Residual Complexity Review

Review every remaining element of constitutional complexity.

Each remaining complexity shall be classified as:

* constitutionally required;
* enterprise required;
* audit required;
* historical preservation required;
* certification required.

Complexity lacking constitutional justification shall be removed.

# Constitutional Hardening Requirements

Every identified unnecessary complexity shall result in one of the following:

* constitutional consolidation;
* doctrinal clarification;
* terminology standardization;
* ownership clarification;
* dependency reduction;
* lifecycle simplification;
* elimination of redundant constitutional requirements.

Every retained complexity shall possess explicit constitutional justification.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Constitutional Simplicity Audit shall immediately fail whenever:

* simplification weakens constitutional guarantees;
* ownership becomes ambiguous;
* deterministic behavior changes;
* evidence requirements become insufficient;
* provenance becomes incomplete;
* replay can no longer reproduce certified behavior;
* fail-closed guarantees are weakened;
* historical preservation is compromised;
* constitutional equivalence cannot be objectively demonstrated.

No constitutional simplification shall be adopted unless complete behavioral equivalence has been independently verified.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

* every constitutional requirement has been evaluated for necessity;
* every duplicated doctrine has been consolidated or formally justified;
* canonical objects, lifecycle states, interfaces, dependencies, evidence requirements, and certification activities have been reduced to their constitutionally minimal form where possible;
* every remaining complexity possesses explicit constitutional justification;
* independent equivalence testing demonstrates preservation of all certified guarantees;
* no simplification introduces constitutional, behavioral, or certification regression;
* the Performance Truth Office demonstrates the simplest constitutional specification capable of preserving every previously certified ECS-003 guarantee.
