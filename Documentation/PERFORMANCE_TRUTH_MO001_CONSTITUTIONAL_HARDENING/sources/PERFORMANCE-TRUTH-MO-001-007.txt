# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-007

# Cross-Truth Consistency Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the adversarial constitutional audit governing consistency between Performance Truth and every related enterprise truth domain.

The purpose of this order is to identify, construct, and resolve every scenario in which Performance Truth may disagree with Closed Position Truth, Decision Truth, Authorization Truth, Monitoring Truth, or Historical Truth.

The objective is not to assume that these truth domains are naturally consistent.

The objective is to prove that all cross-truth relationships are constitutionally governed, deterministically reconcilable, historically reproducible, and incapable of producing competing authoritative conclusions.

Every discovered inconsistency shall be resolved through explicit constitutional doctrine.

# Constitutional Principles

The audit shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Each truth domain shall retain exactly one constitutional owner.

2. No truth domain may silently redefine another truth domain.

3. Performance Truth shall derive from upstream constitutional truth without duplicating upstream authority.

4. Cross-truth disagreement shall produce deterministic constitutional behavior.

5. No office may select among conflicting truths through implementation discretion.

6. Every truth relationship shall possess explicit precedence and reconciliation rules.

7. Corrections shall propagate deterministically without destroying historical truth.

8. Cross-truth consistency shall be independently reproducible.

9. Unresolved disagreement shall prevent dependent publication and certification.

10. No competing authoritative enterprise history shall exist.

---

# Truth Domain Definitions

## Performance Truth

Represents the authoritative constitutional interpretation of enterprise performance derived from valid upstream truth, governed calculations, complete evidence, and certified publication.

## Closed Position Truth

Represents the authoritative constitutional state and realized outcome of positions that have completed the required closure and settlement lifecycle.

## Decision Truth

Represents the authoritative constitutional record of what enterprise decision was made, under what context, and by what authority.

## Authorization Truth

Represents the authoritative constitutional record of what action was permitted, prohibited, constrained, expired, revoked, or consumed.

## Monitoring Truth

Represents authoritative operational observations, detected conditions, anomalies, and execution-state evidence within Monitoring jurisdiction.

## Historical Truth

Represents the immutable constitutional record of prior states, events, revisions, corrections, evidence, and certified outcomes preserved by the Historian.

These domains shall remain distinct.

Consistency shall not be achieved by collapsing their ownership or meaning.

---

# Canonical Truth Hierarchy

The audit shall determine and verify the constitutional role of each truth domain in producing Performance Truth.

At minimum:

* Decision Truth shall establish intended enterprise action.
* Authorization Truth shall establish whether that action was constitutionally permitted.
* Closed Position Truth shall establish realized position outcome.
* Monitoring Truth shall establish relevant observed operational conditions.
* Historical Truth shall preserve the authoritative sequence and prior versions.
* Performance Truth shall calculate and publish certified performance derived from those upstream truths.

Performance Truth shall not overwrite, reinterpret, or replace upstream truth.

Where a calculation depends upon multiple truth domains, the dependency and precedence rules shall be explicit.

# Performance Truth and Closed Position Truth Audit

Attempt to construct scenarios involving:

* Performance Truth referencing an open position as closed;
* realized profit or loss differing between offices;
* settlement information arriving after publication;
* closure correction without performance correction;
* duplicate closed-position records;
* missing closure evidence;
* residual exposure after declared closure;
* different cost basis or proceeds;
* incompatible fee treatment;
* incompatible currency treatment.

Verify that:

* Closed Position Truth remains authoritative for realized position outcome;
* Performance Truth does not independently recreate closure truth;
* discrepancies prevent affected publication or certification;
* corrections propagate through new records and revisions;
* prior published history remains preserved.

---

# Performance Truth and Decision Truth Audit

Attempt to construct scenarios involving:

* performance attributed to the wrong Decision Object;
* executed activity without an authoritative decision;
* one decision linked to incompatible outcomes;
* duplicate decisions linked to one performance result;
* superseded decisions remaining active;
* missing decision evidence;
* decision intent differing from realized activity;
* performance calculation based on inferred intent.

Verify that:

* Decision Truth remains authoritative for decision identity and intent;
* Performance Truth records actual outcome without rewriting decision history;
* attribution distinguishes intended action from realized result;
* missing or contradictory decision truth fails closed where required;
* every applicable Performance Truth Record preserves deterministic decision provenance.

---

# Performance Truth and Authorization Truth Audit

Attempt to construct scenarios involving:

* profitable activity executed without valid authorization;
* expired authorization linked to performance;
* revoked authorization treated as valid;
* authorization scope differing from executed scope;
* authorization consumed more than once;
* missing authorization evidence;
* later authorization correction;
* conflicting authorization states.

Verify that:

* performance may describe realized outcome without legitimizing unauthorized action;
* authorization validity and performance outcome remain distinct constitutional facts;
* unauthorized activity is visibly classified and cannot be represented as constitutionally compliant performance;
* certification behavior is deterministic;
* authorization disagreement generates evidence and required escalation;
* Performance Truth never modifies Authorization Truth.

---

# Performance Truth and Monitoring Truth Audit

Attempt to construct scenarios involving:

* Monitoring reporting execution failure while performance reports success;
* missing operational events;
* duplicate observations;
* delayed anomaly detection;
* monitoring timestamps conflicting with workflow history;
* observed values differing from broker or closure evidence;
* monitoring correction after performance publication;
* missing health or dependency state.

Verify that:

* Monitoring Truth remains authoritative within its observation jurisdiction;
* observations do not automatically override transaction or closure truth outside that jurisdiction;
* contradictory observations trigger deterministic reconciliation;
* unresolved contradictions prevent affected certification where constitutionally material;
* late monitoring evidence causes governed correction or revision;
* all discrepancies remain auditable.

---

# Performance Truth and Historical Truth Audit

Attempt to construct scenarios involving:

* Historian records differing from current Performance Truth;
* missing historical revisions;
* reordered events;
* overwritten prior values;
* inconsistent replay results;
* absent evidence packages;
* duplicate historical identities;
* correction chains that cannot be reconstructed;
* current records referencing unavailable history.

Verify that:

* Historian preserves immutable historical truth;
* current Performance Truth identifies its current authoritative revision without deleting prior states;
* replay reconstructs the same cross-truth sequence;
* historical discrepancies fail closed;
* no office may silently repair historical records;
* corrections create new constitutional history.

---

# Multi-Truth Conflict Scenarios

The auditor shall construct scenarios involving three or more truth domains, including:

* valid decision, invalid authorization, completed trade, profitable closure;
* valid authorization, incorrect execution, corrected closure, stale performance publication;
* missing monitoring events, broker confirmation, incomplete historical preservation;
* conflicting closure and historical records with matching performance output;
* revised decision context after execution;
* delayed settlement combined with benchmark publication;
* duplicated workflow execution producing conflicting truth chains.

For every scenario, verify:

* constitutional authority;
* truth ownership;
* source precedence;
* reconciliation sequence;
* publication eligibility;
* certification eligibility;
* correction behavior;
* historical preservation;
* required evidence.

No conflict shall be resolved through implementation discretion.

# Truth Precedence Rules

For every identified conflict, doctrine shall specify:

* which office owns each disputed fact;
* whether facts are actually contradictory or merely different in scope;
* whether one truth may supersede another;
* required reconciliation authority;
* permitted provisional states;
* publication restrictions;
* certification restrictions;
* correction and revision consequences.

Truth precedence shall be field-specific and jurisdiction-specific.

No universal precedence rule may be assumed where truth domains govern different constitutional facts.

# Correction Propagation Requirements

When upstream truth is constitutionally corrected:

* the correction shall be independently verified;
* every dependent Performance Truth Record shall be identified;
* recalculation shall use the corrected upstream truth;
* a new correction or revision shall be created;
* prior records shall remain immutable;
* affected snapshots, intervals, metrics, attributions, and certifications shall be reevaluated;
* propagation evidence shall be preserved.

Silent correction propagation is prohibited.

---

* correction time;

Different timestamps shall not be treated as contradictory merely because they differ.

Their constitutional meaning, ordering requirements, and allowable lateness shall be explicit.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall fail closed whenever:

* a constitutionally material truth conflict remains unresolved;
* ownership of a disputed fact cannot be determined;
* source evidence is incomplete;
* truth versions cannot be reconciled;
* temporal ordering cannot be established;
* historical records cannot be reproduced;
* correction propagation cannot be completed;
* cross-truth provenance contains gaps.

Under fail-closed conditions:

* affected performance shall not be newly published;
* affected records shall not be certified;
* previously certified unaffected truth shall remain available;
* the conflict shall be preserved as constitutional evidence;
* no substitute truth shall be inferred.

---

# Mutation and Negative Testing

The auditor shall deliberately:

* alter truth-domain references;
* substitute incompatible versions;
* remove one truth source;
* duplicate truth records;
* reorder events;
* corrupt ownership metadata;
* introduce delayed corrections;
* create conflicting authorization states;
* create incompatible closure values;
* suppress historical revisions.

Every mutation shall either be detected deterministically or demonstrate a constitutional defect requiring modification.

---

# Constitutional Modification Requirements

Every discovered inconsistency shall result in one of the following:

1. A new explicit cross-truth relationship rule.

2. A field-specific ownership clarification.

3. A deterministic precedence rule.

4. A reconciliation requirement.

5. A correction-propagation requirement.

6. A temporal-semantics clarification.

7. A fail-closed publication or certification rule.

8. Proof that the apparent inconsistency is impossible under existing constitutional guarantees.

No finding may be closed solely because current implementation happens to behave correctly.
