# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-011

# Mutation Resistance Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Constitutional Mutation Resistance Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this audit is to determine whether every constitutionally significant mutation affecting Performance Truth is detected, contained, rejected, or constitutionally reconciled before it can alter certified enterprise truth.

Unlike conventional testing, this audit intentionally injects valid and invalid mutations into constitutional objects, evidence, dependencies, calculations, and interfaces to determine whether the constitutional architecture is genuinely resistant to corruption rather than merely correct during expected operation.

The objective is not to demonstrate successful execution.

The objective is to demonstrate that every unauthorized mutation inevitably results in deterministic detection and fail-closed behavior.

# Mutation Philosophy

Every constitutional guarantee shall be presumed vulnerable until an adversarial mutation demonstrates otherwise.

Every mutation shall answer the following questions:

1. Was the mutation detected?
2. Was the mutation detected before constitutional state changed?
3. Was the mutation localized?
4. Was evidence generated?
5. Was fail-closed behavior invoked?
6. Was replay deterministic?
7. Was reconciliation initiated?
8. Was recovery governed constitutionally?
9. Could a compliant implementation overlook the mutation?
10. Could enterprise truth be silently corrupted?

Only mutations that are consistently detected under independent reproduction shall be considered constitutionally resisted.

# Mutation Classification

Every mutation shall receive exactly one classification.

## Fully Resisted

The mutation is detected before constitutional state changes.

No certified truth is altered.

---

## Contained

The mutation enters processing but cannot escape into certified enterprise truth.

Recovery is deterministic.

---

## Recoverable Weakness

The mutation is detected after state change but constitutional recovery fully restores correctness.

A Modification Record shall be generated.

---

## Critical Vulnerability

The mutation alters certified enterprise truth without deterministic detection or fail-closed behavior.

Certification shall immediately fail.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* a mutation escapes detection;
* certified Performance Truth changes without constitutional authorization;
* immutable identities are altered undetected;
* ownership validation fails;
* evidence integrity cannot be verified;
* reconciliation accepts mutated truth incorrectly;
* replay diverges from the certified baseline;
* certification artifacts are modified without detection;
* restoration cannot reproduce the certified baseline;
* any Critical constitutional invariant is violated.

No affected Performance Truth object shall be published, revised, reconciled as complete, certified, or included within enterprise reporting until the mutation has been fully analyzed, contained, and constitutionally resolved.

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

All mutation categories have been exercised.

Every unauthorized mutation is either Fully Resisted or Contained.

No unresolved Critical Vulnerabilities remain.

A hardened constitutional mutation resistance baseline has been established.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

No mutation compromises certified enterprise truth, but one or more Recoverable Weaknesses remain.

Each weakness shall possess an approved Modification Record, constitutional owner, and remediation plan before Constitutional Hardening Closure.

---

## FAIL

One or more Critical Vulnerabilities permit unauthorized mutation to alter constitutional behavior or certified Performance Truth without deterministic detection and fail-closed response.

Affected functionality shall remain disabled or restricted until remediation and independent re-certification are complete.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every constitutional mutation surface has been identified and exercised.
2. Every mutation has been injected using a controlled and reproducible procedure.
3. Mutation detection, containment, replay, reconciliation, and recovery have been independently verified.
4. Every mutation has received exactly one constitutional classification.
5. Complete mutation coverage has been demonstrated across objects, interfaces, calculations, evidence, dependencies, replay, and certification artifacts.
6. Every confirmed vulnerability has generated a Modification Record and supporting evidence.
7. A complete Mutation Resistance Report has been produced and preserved.
8. All mutation evidence has been stored within the Evidence Repository.
9. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
10. No unresolved Critical Vulnerability remains capable of silently compromising certified Performance Truth.
