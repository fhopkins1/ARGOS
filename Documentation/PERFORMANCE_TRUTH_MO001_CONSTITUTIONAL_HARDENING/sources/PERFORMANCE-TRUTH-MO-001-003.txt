# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-003

# Mathematical Governance Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Mathematical Governance Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to challenge every mathematical operation governing Performance Truth under the assumption that hidden ambiguity, undefined numerical behavior, inconsistent mathematical semantics, or incomplete calculation governance may still exist despite prior constitutional certification.

This audit exists to invalidate mathematical assumptions rather than confirm existing implementation.

Every mathematical operation shall either be proven constitutionally deterministic or result in constitutional hardening.

The Mathematical Governance Audit shall determine whether every mathematical operation possesses:

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* mathematical precision is undefined;
* rounding behavior is ambiguous;
* aggregation produces non-deterministic results;
* normalization lacks constitutional governance;
* attribution possesses multiple valid interpretations;
* benchmark calculations cannot be reproduced;
* interval semantics are ambiguous;
* cumulative calculations diverge;
* correction propagation is incomplete;
* exceptional numerical conditions cannot be deterministically resolved;
* unit consistency cannot be demonstrated.

No mathematical result shall be published until deterministic constitutional governance has been established.

This order shall be considered complete when:

* every discovered mathematical ambiguity has been eliminated or formally resolved through constitutional doctrine;
* no implementation-defined mathematical behavior remains;
* the Performance Truth Office demonstrates a mathematically complete, deterministic, and constitutionally hardened calculation model.
