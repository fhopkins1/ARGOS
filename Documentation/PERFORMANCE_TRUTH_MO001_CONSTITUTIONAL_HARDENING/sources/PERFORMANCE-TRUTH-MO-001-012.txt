# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-012

# Enterprise Scalability Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional scalability hardening campaign for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this audit is to determine whether every constitutional guarantee established under the RM-series remains valid when the ARGOS Enterprise is subjected to substantially increased operational scale, historical depth, execution concurrency, enterprise partitioning, and distributed deployment.

The objective is not to optimize performance.

The objective is to prove that constitutional correctness, determinism, ownership, evidence integrity, replay capability, and fail-closed behavior remain invariant regardless of enterprise scale.

Every discovered scalability weakness shall either be demonstrated constitutionally impossible or result in a constitutional modification.

3. Deterministic execution, replay, ownership, evidence integrity, and historical preservation remain unchanged regardless of scale.
4. No enterprise partition or distributed deployment produces competing constitutional truth or ownership ambiguity.
5. Large-scale correction, revision, reconciliation, and certification campaigns preserve complete constitutional guarantees.
6. Multi-enterprise deployments remain constitutionally isolated and independently reproducible.
7. All scalability mutations either produce constitutionally identical behavior or deterministically fail closed.
8. Every discovered scalability weakness has been resolved through constitutional modification or proven impossible.
9. Independent auditors can reproduce all scalability findings solely from constitutional evidence and repository artifacts.
10. No unresolved scalability-related constitutional ambiguity remains, and the Performance Truth Office demonstrates constitutional correctness independent of enterprise size, execution concurrency, historical depth, or deployment architecture.
