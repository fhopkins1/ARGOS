# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001-008

# Adversarial Failure Analysis

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Adversarial Failure Analysis campaign for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to deliberately construct hostile, contradictory, incomplete, and pathological operating conditions in order to invalidate assumptions regarding the constitutional resilience of the Performance Truth Office. Every failure scenario shall be treated as if it represents a realistic enterprise condition until constitutionally disproven.

The objective is not to demonstrate successful operation under normal conditions.

The objective is to prove that the office always responds with deterministic, constitutional, fail-closed behavior when subjected to adversarial conditions.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* contradictory truth cannot be uniquely resolved;
* required evidence is missing or unverifiable;
* evidence integrity cannot be established;
* mathematical impossibility is detected;
* dependency failures invalidate constitutional guarantees;
* reconciliation cannot determine a unique authoritative result;
* historical immutability is threatened;
* deterministic replay cannot be reproduced;
* ownership becomes ambiguous;
* any adversarial condition prevents objective establishment of Performance Truth.

No Performance Truth Record shall be published, certified, revised, or reconciled while any unresolved adversarial condition exists.

This order shall be considered complete when:

* every defined adversarial scenario has been independently executed;
* every constitutional guarantee has been challenged under hostile conditions;
* all unsafe publication paths have been eliminated or proven impossible;
* deterministic fail-closed behavior has been demonstrated for every scenario;
* historical truth, evidence, provenance, and ownership remain preserved under attack;
* recovery procedures have been independently verified for every failure class;
* every discovered weakness has resulted in constitutional hardening or been formally disproven;
* no unresolved adversarial failure mode remains capable of compromising the constitutional integrity of the Performance Truth Office.
