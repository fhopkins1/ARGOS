# Hidden Responsibility Discovery

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional hardening campaign responsible for discovering every business responsibility performed by the Performance Truth Office that is not explicitly assigned by constitutional doctrine.

The objective of this order is to eliminate all implicit ownership, undocumented authority, hidden operational responsibilities, and architectural assumptions that survived the original constitutional certification campaign.

No business responsibility shall remain implicit.
