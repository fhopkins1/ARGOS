# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-002

# Independent Clean-Room Execution, Evidence Regeneration, Mutation Validation, and Final Certification Audit

## Purpose

Conduct a completely independent ECS-003 certification audit of the Performance Truth Office using only the submitted Repository Package and Evidence Package.

This audit shall determine whether the Performance Truth Office independently satisfies every constitutional, implementation, behavioral, and certification requirement necessary to receive final ECS-003 certification.

This audit shall assume that previous certifications, remediation campaigns, modification campaigns, and audit reports may all be incorrect.

No prior certification decision shall influence the outcome of this audit.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This audit derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-003

This audit possesses full constitutional authority to reject previous certification conclusions.

---

# Independence Requirements

The auditor shall behave as an independent constitutional auditor.

The auditor shall not:

* assume repository correctness;
* assume evidence correctness;
* assume previous PASS decisions are valid;
* infer missing implementation;
* infer intended behavior;
* complete unfinished implementation;
* modify source code;
* repair defects;
* rewrite tests;
* reinterpret constitutional requirements;
* substitute judgment for evidence.

Every conclusion shall be supported by independently verifiable evidence.

---

# Repository Discovery

Beginning with only the submitted Repository Package:

Independently determine:

* repository structure;
* implementation inventory;
* office boundaries;
* dependencies;
* configuration;
* generated artifacts;
* executable components;
* test infrastructure.

No repository knowledge may be imported from previous audits.

---

# Constitutional Verification

Verify complete implementation of every constitutional requirement established under:

* RM-001
* RM-002
* RM-003
* MO-001
* MO-002
* MO-003

Every constitutional requirement shall be independently evaluated.

Missing implementation shall constitute failure.

---

# Clean-Room Execution

Construct an independent execution environment using only documented repository information.

Execute the repository without relying upon:

* historical environments;
* undocumented dependencies;
* prior configuration;
* institutional knowledge;
* manual implementation assistance.

Failure to independently reproduce execution shall constitute a certification finding.

---

# Evidence Regeneration

Independently regenerate every required certification artifact including:

* implementation inventory;
* behavior inventory;
* traceability;
* ownership;
* reconciliation;
* dependency graph;
* lifecycle verification;
* interface verification;
* constitutional verification;
* certification reports.

Compare regenerated evidence against submitted evidence.

Every discrepancy shall be investigated.

---

# Behavioral Verification

Execute independent verification of:

* deterministic calculations;
* publication;
* correction;
* revision;
* aggregation;
* attribution;
* benchmark computation;
* reconciliation;
* replay;
* temporal behavior;
* ownership;
* evidence generation.

Behavior shall be demonstrated through execution.

Artifact existence alone shall not constitute proof.

---

# Mutation Validation

Independently perform mutation testing including mutations of:

* source code;
* evidence;
* truth records;
* calculations;
* timestamps;
* ownership;
* interfaces;
* dependencies;
* reconciliation inputs;
* certification artifacts.

Determine whether every mutation is detected through constitutional behavior.

Undetected mutations shall constitute certification failure.

---

# Fail-Closed Validation

Independently exercise fail-closed behavior under:

* missing evidence;
* corrupted evidence;
* contradictory evidence;
* duplicate evidence;
* dependency failures;
* incomplete workflows;
* invalid ownership;
* replay failures;
* reconciliation failures;
* temporal violations;
* configuration corruption.

Unsafe continuation shall constitute certification failure.

---

# Replay Verification

Independently verify deterministic replay.

Multiple independent executions shall reproduce identical:

* truth records;
* evidence;
* calculations;
* reconciliation;
* certification outputs.

Non-deterministic behavior shall constitute certification failure.

---

# Cross-Office Verification

Verify constitutional interaction with every dependent office.

Confirm:

* ownership preservation;
* interface correctness;
* dependency correctness;
* constitutional authority;
* evidence continuity;
* truth consistency.

No hidden architectural coupling shall be accepted.

---

# Evidence Sufficiency Review

Determine whether submitted evidence independently proves every constitutional requirement.

Evidence that merely suggests correctness shall not be accepted.

Every constitutional claim shall possess sufficient independent proof.

---

# Adversarial Review

Assume throughout the audit that:

* the repository may intentionally conceal defects;
* evidence may be incomplete;
* documentation may omit critical behavior;
* certification artifacts may contain unsupported conclusions.

Attempt to falsify every constitutional guarantee.

Failure to falsify shall strengthen confidence but shall not replace evidence.

---

# Certification Decision

Upon completion, issue one and only one certification decision:

* PASS
* FAIL

Conditional certification is prohibited.

If certification fails:

* identify every finding;
* identify constitutional authority for each finding;
* provide objective evidence supporting each finding;
* distinguish constitutional deficiencies from implementation deficiencies.

If certification passes:

State explicitly that the Performance Truth Office independently satisfies every applicable ECS-003 constitutional, implementation, behavioral, architectural, and certification requirement based solely upon independently reproduced evidence generated during this audit.

---
