# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-009

# Reconciliation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the complete constitutional reconciliation doctrine for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of reconciliation is to ensure that every published Performance Truth Record remains fully consistent with the authoritative constitutional records maintained throughout the ARGOS Enterprise.

Reconciliation is the constitutional process by which the Performance Truth Office continuously verifies that all published performance values remain mathematically, temporally, and evidentially consistent with their originating enterprise truth.

Whenever reconciliation cannot establish complete constitutional consistency, publication authority shall fail closed until the discrepancy is resolved through constitutionally authorized correction procedures.

---

# Constitutional Objectives

The reconciliation process shall ensure:

* enterprise truth remains internally consistent;
* every published performance value is reproducible;
* every calculation is supported by complete evidence;
* every dependency is constitutionally synchronized;
* historical performance remains immutable;
* revisions preserve historical lineage;
* discrepancies are detected deterministically;
* unresolved inconsistencies cannot become published constitutional truth.

---

# Constitutional Scope

The Performance Truth Office shall reconcile with the following constitutional offices:

* Closed Position Truth
* Broker
* Monitoring
* Historian
* Workflow Engine
* Decision Objects
* Evidence Repository
* Audit Office

Each reconciliation shall be independently verifiable and fully reproducible.

---

# Reconciliation Principles

Every reconciliation shall be:

* deterministic;
* repeatable;
* evidence-driven;
* complete;
* reproducible;
* version aware;
* immutable once completed;
* independently auditable.

No reconciliation may rely upon assumptions, estimates, inferred values, or implementation-specific behavior.

* certification state.

Each reconciliation shall produce exactly one constitutional outcome.

Fully Reconciled

## Outcome C

# Constitutional Discrepancy Handling

Every detected discrepancy shall generate a Reconciliation Exception containing:

* unique reconciliation identifier;
* discrepancy classification;
* affected Performance Truth Records;
* affected dependencies;
* severity classification;
* constitutional invariant violated;
* evidence references;
* detection timestamp;
* resolution status;
* responsible constitutional office.

No discrepancy may be silently ignored.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately suspend publication whenever:

* reconciliation cannot complete;
* any dependency is unavailable;
* evidence integrity fails;
* historical replay differs from published results;
* broker records disagree with Closed Position Truth;
* workflow identity cannot be verified;
* provenance is incomplete;
* duplicate constitutional identity is detected;
* certification prerequisites fail;
* any constitutional invariant is violated.

No unresolved discrepancy shall be published as constitutional performance truth.
