# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-005

# Office Interfaces

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the complete constitutional interface specification governing all interactions between the Performance Truth Office and every external office within the ARGOS Enterprise.

The purpose of these interfaces is to ensure that all exchanges of information, authority, ownership, and evidence occur through deterministic, auditable, and constitutionally authorized interactions while preserving strict ownership boundaries and complete traceability.

No interface defined herein grants authority to modify another office's constitutional responsibilities or ownership.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every constitutional interface shall:

* preserve constitutional ownership;
* expose only constitutionally authorized information;
* remain deterministic;
* be fully auditable;
* be replayable;
* be versioned;
* preserve immutable provenance;
* fail closed whenever constitutional guarantees cannot be satisfied.

Interfaces define constitutional behavior only.

Implementation technology, transport protocols, serialization formats, messaging systems, APIs, databases, and infrastructure remain outside the constitutional scope.

---

# General Interface Requirements

Every interface shall specify:

* producing office;
* consuming office;
* ownership before transfer;
* ownership after transfer;
* required inputs;
* required outputs;
* preconditions;
* postconditions;
* constitutional guarantees;
* required evidence;
* failure behavior.

No interface may bypass constitutional validation.

# Cross-Interface Constitutional Requirements

The following requirements apply to every interface:

* Ownership shall never transfer unless explicitly authorized by constitutional doctrine.
* Every exchanged object shall possess immutable identity.
* Every received object shall undergo constitutional validation.
* Every interface interaction shall generate auditable evidence.
* Every interaction shall be timestamped using constitutional temporal standards.
* Every interaction shall preserve complete provenance.
* Duplicate submissions shall be detected deterministically.
* Version identity shall be preserved throughout all exchanges.
* Interface failures shall never produce partial constitutional state.
* Interfaces shall be fully reproducible during deterministic replay.

---

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The Performance Truth Office shall reject any interface interaction when:

* required inputs are absent;
* object identity cannot be verified;
* ownership is ambiguous;
* certification prerequisites are unmet;
* evidence integrity cannot be established;
* timestamps violate constitutional temporal rules;
* provenance is incomplete;
* duplicate publication is detected;
* dependency certification has failed;
* reconciliation prerequisites are unsatisfied.

Rejected interactions shall not alter constitutional state.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered constitutionally complete when:

1. Every constitutional interface of the Performance Truth Office is explicitly defined.
2. Required inputs and outputs are specified for each interface.
3. Ownership before and after each interaction is unambiguous.
4. Constitutional guarantees are defined for every interface.
5. Interface preconditions and postconditions are deterministic.
6. Cross-interface invariants preserve ownership, provenance, and replayability.
7. Universal fail-closed behavior is defined for all interface failures.
8. No constitutional ambiguity remains regarding external interactions of the Performance Truth Office.
