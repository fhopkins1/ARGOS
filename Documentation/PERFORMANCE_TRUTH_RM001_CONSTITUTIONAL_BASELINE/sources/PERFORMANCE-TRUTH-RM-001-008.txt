# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-008

# Temporal Integrity

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the constitutional temporal governance of the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to ensure that every performance record, calculation, publication, correction, revision, and certification remains temporally consistent, historically reproducible, and constitutionally auditable throughout the lifetime of the enterprise.

Temporal integrity guarantees that historical performance truth can always be reconstructed exactly as it existed at any constitutional point in time while preserving the complete history of subsequent corrections and revisions.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* Truth Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* Historian Doctrine
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001

Nothing within this order may supersede constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

Every temporal attribute within the Performance Truth Office shall be:

* deterministic;
* immutable where constitutionally required;
* independently reproducible;
* evidence-backed;
* historically preservable;
* fully traceable;
* auditable;
* fail-closed upon violation.

No calculation or publication may rely upon ambiguous, inferred, or undocumented time values.

---

# Constitutional Temporal Model

Every constitutional performance object shall distinguish between multiple independent concepts of time.

Temporal dimensions shall never be conflated.

The constitutional temporal model shall include:

* Event Time
* Effective Time
* Observation Time
* Calculation Time
* Publication Time
* Correction Time
* Certification Time
* Archival Time

Each represents a unique constitutional event.

---

# Event Time

Event Time represents the time at which the underlying business event actually occurred.

Examples include:

* execution completion;
* position closure;
* benchmark publication;
* workflow completion;
* authorization issuance.

Event Time shall never be modified after constitutional acceptance.

---

# Effective Time

Effective Time defines the period during which constitutional truth is considered valid.

Effective Time governs:

* attribution;
* interval calculations;
* cumulative performance;
* benchmark applicability;
* historical replay.

Effective Time may differ from Event Time.

---

# Observation Time

Observation Time records when constitutional information first became available to the enterprise.

Observation Time shall support:

* late-arriving market data;
* delayed broker confirmations;
* external corrections;
* asynchronous workflow completion.

Observation Time shall never replace Event Time.

---

# Calculation Time

Calculation Time records when a performance calculation was executed.

Every calculation shall record:

* calculation start;
* calculation completion;
* calculation version;
* calculation context.

Calculation Time shall never redefine historical business truth.

---

# Publication Time

Publication Time records when a performance value becomes constitutionally available for enterprise consumption.

Publication shall occur only after:

* reconciliation completion;
* evidence validation;
* dependency validation;
* calculation verification;
* constitutional authorization.

Publication Time shall remain immutable.

---

# Correction Time

Correction Time records when a constitutional correction is accepted.

Correction Time shall not alter:

* Event Time;
* Effective Time;
* historical publication records.

Correction history shall remain permanently preserved.

---

# Certification Time

Certification Time records when constitutional certification was granted.

Certification Time shall identify:

* certification authority;
* certification version;
* certification evidence;
* certification decision.

Certification records shall remain immutable.

---

# Archival Time

Archival Time records when constitutional objects transition into historical preservation.

Archival shall never destroy constitutional history.

Archived objects shall remain available for replay and audit.

---

# Timestamp Requirements

Every constitutional performance object shall contain all applicable timestamps.

Each timestamp shall specify:

* timestamp type;
* value;
* originating authority;
* source evidence;
* time standard;
* precision.

Missing timestamps constitute constitutional failure.

---

# Time Standard

The enterprise shall operate under a single constitutional time standard.

All timestamps shall be recorded using that standard.

Time zone conversions may be performed only for presentation.

Internal constitutional processing shall never depend upon local time zones.

---

# Timestamp Precision

Timestamp precision shall be constitutionally defined.

All calculations shall preserve the original timestamp precision supplied by constitutional source truth.

Precision shall never be silently increased or reduced.

---

# Calculation Windows

Every performance calculation shall execute within an explicitly defined Calculation Window.

Each Calculation Window shall specify:

* beginning boundary;
* ending boundary;
* inclusion rules;
* exclusion rules;
* effective interval;
* benchmark interval;
* correction scope.

Implicit calculation windows are prohibited.

---

# Historical Replay

The Performance Truth Office shall support deterministic historical replay.

Replay shall reconstruct performance exactly as it existed using:

* constitutional source truth;
* historical calculation rules;
* historical benchmark versions;
* historical configuration;
* historical dependencies;
* historical corrections applicable to the selected replay point.

Replay shall produce identical results for identical replay parameters.

---

# Replay Points

Replay shall support reconstruction using any constitutional replay point.

Replay points include:

* original publication;
* post-correction publication;
* certification publication;
* historical enterprise state;
* specified effective date.

Replay parameters shall be explicit.

---

# Late-Arriving Information

Information may arrive after the applicable Event Time.

Examples include:

* delayed executions;
* delayed broker confirmations;
* corrected market data;
* corrected benchmark values;
* delayed workflow completion.

Late-arriving information shall initiate constitutional correction rather than historical modification.

---

# Historical Immutability

Published constitutional history shall never be overwritten.

When truth changes:

* new constitutional records shall be created;
* supersession relationships shall be established;
* prior records shall remain historically preserved;
* provenance shall remain complete.

Historical deletion is prohibited.

---

# Revision Governance

Every revision shall identify:

* superseded calculation;
* superseding calculation;
* revision authority;
* revision evidence;
* revision timestamp;
* affected intervals;
* affected reports;
* affected benchmarks.

Revision history shall remain immutable.

---

# Dependency Versioning

Historical calculations shall remain permanently associated with the dependency versions used during calculation.

Dependency changes shall never silently alter historical calculations.

New dependency versions shall require new calculations.

---

# Temporal Consistency

Every constitutional timestamp shall satisfy:

* chronological consistency;
* causal consistency;
* dependency consistency;
* workflow consistency;
* evidence consistency.

Impossible temporal relationships constitute constitutional failure.

---

# Temporal Validation

Before publication, the Performance Truth Office shall validate:

* timestamp completeness;
* chronological ordering;
* interval validity;
* benchmark validity;
* dependency timing;
* correction ordering;
* replay consistency.

Validation failure shall prohibit publication.

---

# Temporal Reconciliation

Temporal reconciliation shall verify consistency between:

* Performance Truth;
* Closed Position Truth;
* Workflow Execution;
* Historian;
* Broker;
* Monitoring;
* Decision Objects.

Temporal discrepancies shall initiate constitutional reconciliation.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* Event Time never changes.
* Effective Time is explicitly defined.
* Observation Time never replaces Event Time.
* Published history is immutable.
* Corrections create new constitutional records.
* Replay is deterministic.
* Calculation windows are explicit.
* Timestamp provenance is complete.
* Dependency versions remain historically preserved.
* Every publication possesses complete temporal evidence.

Violation of any invariant constitutes constitutional failure.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall refuse publication whenever:

* timestamps are missing;
* timestamps are ambiguous;
* chronological ordering is impossible;
* Event Time cannot be established;
* Effective Time is undefined;
* calculation windows overlap unconstitutionally;
* replay cannot reproduce historical results;
* dependency timing is inconsistent;
* correction ordering is invalid;
* temporal reconciliation fails.

No performance value shall be published when temporal integrity cannot be demonstrated.

---

* timestamp inventory;

* time standard;
* precision specification;
* calculation window definition;
* replay parameters;
* dependency versions;
* correction history;
* revision chain;
* temporal validation results;

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered constitutionally complete when independent audit demonstrates that:

1. every constitutional performance object contains complete and unambiguous temporal information;
2. Event Time, Effective Time, Observation Time, Calculation Time, Publication Time, Correction Time, Certification Time, and Archival Time are independently governed and never conflated;
3. every calculation executes within an explicitly defined Calculation Window;
4. deterministic historical replay reconstructs constitutional performance exactly for any authorized replay point;
5. late-arriving information is handled exclusively through constitutional correction and revision without modifying historical records;
6. all published performance history is immutable and fully versioned;
7. temporal reconciliation across dependent offices is deterministic and complete;
8. all temporal invariants are continuously enforced; and
9. every temporal violation results in deterministic fail-closed behavior.
