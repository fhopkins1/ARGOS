# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-004

# Performance Calculation Governance

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the constitutional governance governing every performance calculation performed by the Performance Truth Office.

The objective of this order is to ensure that every published performance value is deterministic, independently reproducible, fully attributable, mathematically auditable, and constitutionally traceable to authoritative enterprise truth.

No calculation may be influenced by implementation-specific behavior, execution timing, hidden state, or discretionary interpretation.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* Truth Doctrine
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001

Nothing within this order may supersede constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

Every performance calculation shall be:

* deterministic;
* reproducible;
* evidence-backed;
* attributable;
* temporally valid;
* mathematically complete;
* independently auditable;
* fail-closed upon constitutional violation.

No calculation may depend upon undocumented assumptions, mutable external state, implementation-specific behavior, execution order, floating ownership, or unverifiable inputs.

---

# Constitutional Performance Calculation Principles

The Performance Truth Office shall serve exclusively as the constitutional authority responsible for calculating enterprise performance metrics.

It shall never create business truth.

It shall derive performance exclusively from authoritative constitutional truth produced by constitutional source offices.

The Performance Truth Office shall perform computation only.

It shall never estimate, infer, interpolate, fabricate, predict, or substitute constitutional truth.

---

# Constitutional Source Truth

Every calculation shall derive exclusively from authoritative constitutional sources.

Permitted constitutional sources include:

* Closed Position Truth
* Workflow Execution Records
* Decision Objects
* Authorization Records
* Historian
* Broker-confirmed execution records
* Monitoring observations
* Enterprise configuration under constitutional authority

No other source shall be considered authoritative.

If multiple sources disagree, no calculation shall be published until reconciliation has completed successfully.

---

# Calculation Authority

The Performance Truth Office shall possess exclusive authority for:

* performance metric computation;
* cumulative performance computation;
* benchmark computation;
* attribution computation;
* interval computation;
* normalized performance computation;
* performance revision computation;
* performance correction propagation.

No other office may independently publish constitutional enterprise performance metrics.

---

# Deterministic Calculation

Every calculation shall produce identical output when supplied with:

* identical inputs;
* identical configuration;
* identical calculation rules;
* identical temporal scope.

Repeated execution shall produce identical results.

No calculation may depend upon:

* execution timing;
* thread scheduling;
* database ordering;
* randomization;
* non-deterministic iteration;
* implementation-defined arithmetic behavior.

---

# Calculation Inputs

Every calculation shall explicitly identify:

* calculation identifier;
* calculation version;
* calculation purpose;
* calculation timestamp;
* effective interval;
* source records;
* dependency versions;
* benchmark references;
* normalization parameters.

Implicit inputs are prohibited.

---

# Calculation Context

Every calculation shall execute within an immutable Calculation Context.

The Calculation Context shall uniquely define:

* enterprise configuration version;
* benchmark definitions;
* normalization rules;
* calculation algorithms;
* dependency versions;
* temporal boundaries;
* correction state.

The Calculation Context shall remain immutable following publication.

---

# Performance Aggregation

Aggregation shall be deterministic.

Aggregation rules shall explicitly define:

* aggregation scope;
* inclusion criteria;
* exclusion criteria;
* weighting rules;
* ordering rules;
* grouping rules;
* cumulative methodology.

Aggregation shall never silently discard records.

Excluded records shall remain constitutionally traceable.

---

# Attribution Governance

Every performance value shall identify the constitutional origin of its contribution.

Attribution shall identify:

* originating workflow;
* originating decision;
* originating position;
* originating strategy;
* originating authorization;
* originating execution;
* originating market event where applicable.

Attribution shall remain immutable following publication except through constitutional revision.

---

# Performance Normalization

Normalization shall be constitutionally defined.

Normalization may include:

* capital normalization;
* exposure normalization;
* interval normalization;
* benchmark normalization;
* currency normalization;
* portfolio normalization.

Normalization rules shall be deterministic.

No discretionary normalization is permitted.

---

# Benchmark Governance

Benchmarks shall constitute immutable constitutional reference standards.

Every benchmark shall define:

* benchmark identifier;
* benchmark source;
* benchmark methodology;
* benchmark interval;
* benchmark version;
* benchmark publication timestamp.

Benchmark revisions shall create new benchmark versions.

Historical benchmarks shall never be overwritten.

---

# Interval Computation

Every interval calculation shall explicitly define:

* start time;
* end time;
* effective time;
* included records;
* excluded records;
* correction state.

Intervals shall never overlap unless constitutionally authorized.

---

# Cumulative Computation

Cumulative performance shall specify:

* starting state;
* ending state;
* included intervals;
* excluded intervals;
* correction history;
* cumulative methodology.

Every cumulative calculation shall be reproducible from historical interval calculations alone.

---

# Correction Propagation

When constitutional truth changes, affected calculations shall be recomputed through deterministic correction propagation.

Propagation shall identify:

* originating correction;
* affected calculations;
* superseded calculations;
* revised calculations;
* affected benchmarks;
* affected reports.

Original published calculations shall remain historically preserved.

Revisions shall supersede rather than replace previous constitutional records.

---

# Mathematical Integrity

Every published value shall satisfy:

* complete mathematical derivation;
* deterministic arithmetic;
* unit consistency;
* dimensional consistency;
* reproducible intermediate calculations;
* complete evidence support.

Undefined mathematical operations are prohibited.

---

# Dependency Governance

Every calculation shall explicitly declare all constitutional dependencies.

Hidden dependencies are prohibited.

Dependency versions shall be recorded as constitutional evidence.

Dependency changes shall invalidate prior calculation certification until recalculation is completed.

---

# Publication Authority

Performance calculations may be published only after:

* source validation;
* evidence validation;
* reconciliation completion;
* dependency verification;
* calculation verification;
* attribution verification;
* temporal verification.

Incomplete calculations shall never be published.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* identical inputs produce identical outputs;
* every value possesses complete provenance;
* every value derives from constitutional source truth;
* every calculation possesses immutable context;
* every benchmark is versioned;
* every correction preserves historical truth;
* every aggregation is deterministic;
* every normalization rule is explicit;
* every published value is independently reproducible.

Violation of any invariant constitutes constitutional failure.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall refuse publication whenever:

* source truth is unavailable;
* reconciliation is incomplete;
* evidence is incomplete;
* attribution cannot be established;
* benchmark identity is ambiguous;
* normalization rules are undefined;
* dependency versions are inconsistent;
* mathematical integrity cannot be demonstrated;
* temporal boundaries are invalid;
* deterministic reproduction fails.

No estimated, inferred, or partially computed performance value may be published.

---

# Required Constitutional Evidence

The following evidence shall be produced for every calculation:

* calculation identifier;
* calculation context;
* source truth references;
* dependency inventory;
* benchmark references;
* normalization rules;
* aggregation methodology;
* attribution records;
* intermediate calculation trace;
* final computed values;
* validation results;
* publication authorization.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered constitutionally complete when independent audit demonstrates that:

1. every performance calculation derives exclusively from constitutional source truth;
2. deterministic replay produces identical outputs for identical inputs;
3. every calculation possesses a complete immutable Calculation Context;
4. aggregation, attribution, normalization, benchmark, interval, and cumulative computation rules are fully specified;
5. correction propagation is deterministic and preserves historical truth;
6. every published performance value is fully traceable to authoritative enterprise evidence;
7. no hidden assumptions, undocumented dependencies, discretionary calculations, or non-deterministic behavior remain;
8. all constitutional invariants are enforced; and
9. all constitutional violations produce deterministic fail-closed behavior.
