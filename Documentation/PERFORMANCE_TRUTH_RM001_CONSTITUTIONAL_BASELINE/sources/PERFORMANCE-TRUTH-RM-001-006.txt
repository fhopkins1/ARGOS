# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-006

# Evidence Requirements

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the complete constitutional evidence requirements governing every calculation, publication, correction, revision, aggregation, benchmark comparison, and certification decision performed by the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to ensure that every published performance value is independently reproducible, fully traceable, and constitutionally verifiable through immutable evidence. No performance information shall be published, certified, or relied upon without sufficient evidence to reproduce every computational and business decision that contributed to its creation.

This order governs constitutional evidence requirements only. Implementation mechanisms for storage, serialization, indexing, transport, or optimization remain outside the scope of this constitutional remediation program.

---

# Constitutional Principles

The evidence architecture shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Every published performance value shall be supported by constitutional evidence.

2. Evidence shall be immutable after publication.

3. Evidence shall be independently reproducible.

4. Evidence shall permit deterministic replay.

5. Evidence shall preserve complete provenance.

6. Every evidence artifact shall possess immutable identity.

7. Evidence shall never be modified to reflect historical corrections; corrections shall generate new evidence.

8. Missing evidence shall prevent publication.

9. Partial evidence shall never satisfy constitutional completeness.

10. All evidence shall remain historically accessible.

---

# Source Truth Evidence

Every published performance value shall reference immutable evidence identifying the authoritative source truth from which the value ultimately derives.

Source Truth Evidence shall include, as applicable:

* Closed Position Truth references
* Decision Object references
* Workflow Execution references
* Authorization references
* Risk decisions
* Broker execution references
* Monitoring observations
* Historian references

Source Truth Evidence shall identify every upstream constitutional dependency.

---

# Calculation Evidence

Every calculation shall produce immutable Calculation Evidence.

Calculation Evidence shall include:

* Calculation Identifier
* Formula Version
* Calculation Method
* Input References
* Output Values
* Intermediate Results (where constitutionally required)
* Execution Timestamp
* Execution Identity
* Calculation Context Reference

Calculation Evidence shall permit independent reproduction of identical outputs.

---

# Input Evidence

Every input consumed by a performance calculation shall be identifiable through immutable Input Evidence.

Input Evidence shall include:

* Input Identifier
* Input Source
* Effective Timestamp
* Acquisition Timestamp
* Validation Status
* Provenance Reference
* Integrity Verification

No calculation shall consume unidentified inputs.

---

# Dependency Evidence

Every dependency affecting calculation behavior shall be recorded.

Dependency Evidence shall include:

* Dependency Identity
* Dependency Version
* Dependency Owner
* Configuration Version
* Validation Status

Dependency substitutions shall produce new evidence.

---

# Temporal Evidence

Every calculation shall record constitutional temporal evidence.

Temporal Evidence shall include:

* Creation Time
* Effective Time
* Observation Time
* Publication Time
* Calculation Window
* Replay Window
* Historical Version

Temporal Evidence shall permit deterministic historical reconstruction.

---

# Attribution Evidence

Every published attribution shall possess evidence identifying the constitutional basis of the attribution.

Attribution Evidence shall include:

* Attribution Identifier
* Attribution Method
* Supporting Metrics
* Source Objects
* Calculation References
* Confidence Basis (if constitutionally applicable)

No attribution may rely upon undocumented reasoning.

---

Aggregation Evidence shall include:

* Inclusion Rules

Aggregation shall never conceal contributing records.

# Provenance Evidence

Every canonical object shall possess complete provenance.

Provenance Evidence shall establish an unbroken chain from:

* Published Performance Value
* Performance Metric
* Performance Truth Record
* Calculation Context
* Input Evidence
* Source Truth
* Workflow Execution
* Constitutional Evidence

No provenance chain may contain unidentified gaps.

---

# Evidence Completeness Requirements

Every published Performance Truth Record shall possess evidence demonstrating:

* source truth;
* calculation inputs;
* calculation outputs;
* calculation method;
* calculation context;
* temporal context;
* dependency versions;
* attribution;
* benchmark comparison (if applicable);
* aggregation (if applicable);
* reconciliation;
* certification status;
* workflow provenance.

Absence of any required evidence shall render the record constitutionally incomplete.

---

# Evidence Integrity Requirements

Every evidence artifact shall satisfy the following constitutional guarantees.

* Immutable identity
* Immutable contents after publication
* Historical preservation
* Deterministic reproducibility
* Complete provenance
* Version traceability
* Independent auditability
* Ownership accountability
* Referential integrity

---

# Evidence Retention Requirements

Constitutional evidence shall never be destroyed while any dependent constitutional record remains active or archived.

Superseded evidence shall remain permanently available for:

* historical replay;
* certification review;
* audit reconstruction;
* forensic investigation;
* constitutional verification.

Retention policies external to the Constitution shall not invalidate constitutional evidence requirements.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall fail closed whenever:

* required evidence is missing;
* evidence integrity cannot be verified;
* provenance is incomplete;
* dependencies cannot be identified;
* calculation evidence is unavailable;
* benchmark evidence cannot be reproduced;
* reconciliation evidence is absent;
* certification evidence is incomplete.

Under fail-closed conditions:

* publication shall be prohibited;
* certification shall be denied;
* downstream offices shall receive no published performance values;
* the failure shall be recorded as constitutional evidence.
