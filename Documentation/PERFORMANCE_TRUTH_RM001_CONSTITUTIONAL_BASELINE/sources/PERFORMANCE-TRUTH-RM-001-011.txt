# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-011

# Performance Truth Auditability

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the constitutional auditability requirements governing the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of auditability is to ensure that every published Performance Truth value, calculation, revision, certification, and historical state can be independently verified, deterministically reproduced, and constitutionally certified without reliance upon undocumented implementation knowledge or institutional memory.

Every constitutional assertion made by the Performance Truth Office shall be independently auditable.

---

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine
* Truth Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* Historian Doctrine

---

# Constitutional Principles

The Performance Truth Office shall satisfy the following auditability principles:

* independent verification;
* deterministic reproduction;
* complete transparency;
* constitutional reproducibility;
* evidence-based validation;
* immutable historical preservation;
* fail-closed certification.

No Performance Truth assertion shall depend upon trust alone.

# Constitutional Audit Requirement

Every published Performance Truth value shall be independently auditable.

Independent auditors shall be capable of verifying constitutional correctness using only:

* repository contents;
* constitutional doctrine;
* preserved evidence;
* deterministic replay procedures.

No undocumented knowledge may be required.

---

# Independent Reproducibility

Auditors shall be capable of independently reproducing:

* every calculation;
* every published metric;
* every benchmark comparison;
* every attribution result;
* every interval calculation;
* every cumulative performance calculation;
* every correction;
* every revision.

Independent reproduction shall produce identical constitutional results.

---

# Deterministic Replay

The Performance Truth Office shall support deterministic replay of every published result.

Replay shall reproduce:

* identical inputs;
* identical dependency versions;
* identical calculations;
* identical outputs;
* identical timestamps where constitutionally preserved;
* identical evidence relationships.

Replay shall not require external interpretation.

---

Auditors shall be capable of reconstructing the complete constitutional history of every Performance Truth Record.

Reconstruction shall include:

* originating workflow execution;
* Decision Objects;
* authorizations;

* market observations;
* broker activity;
* Closed Position Truth;

* calculation context;
* publication history;
* corrections;
* revisions;
* supersessions;
* archival history.

The complete constitutional lifecycle shall remain recoverable.

Auditors shall verify that:

* calculations are deterministic;
* algorithms are versioned;
* formulas are preserved;
* inputs are immutable;
* outputs are reproducible;
* normalization rules were correctly applied;
* benchmark calculations are reproducible;
* attribution calculations are reproducible.

# Historical Integrity Verification

Audit procedures shall verify that:

* published records remain immutable;
* revisions preserve historical truth;
* superseded records remain accessible;
* timestamps remain intact;
* archived records remain reproducible;
* no historical information has been overwritten.

Historical preservation shall be constitutionally enforced.

# Reconciliation Verification

* Closed Position Truth;

* Monitoring;
* Historian;
* Workflow Execution;
* Decision Objects;
* Authorization;
* Risk.

All reconciliation results shall be reproducible.

---

# Certification Readiness

A Performance Truth object shall be constitutionally eligible for certification only if:

* governance requirements are satisfied;
* required objects exist;
* lifecycle requirements are satisfied;
* calculations are deterministic;
* reconciliation succeeds;
* evidence is complete;
* provenance is complete;
* historical integrity is preserved;

Certification readiness shall be objectively verifiable.

* constitutional requirements evaluated;

# Constitutional Audit Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every published value is independently reproducible;
* every calculation is deterministically verifiable;
* every published value possesses complete evidence;
* every dependency relationship is auditable;
* every revision preserves audit history;
* every certification decision is independently reviewable;
* every audit artifact remains immutable after publication.

# Constitutional Audit Failures

The following constitute constitutional audit failures:

* inability to reproduce calculations;
* incomplete evidence;
* broken provenance;
* missing historical records;
* inconsistent reconciliation;
* undocumented algorithms;
* ambiguous ownership;
* non-deterministic behavior;
* incomplete certification records;
* missing audit artifacts.

Any constitutional audit failure shall prevent certification.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* deterministic replay cannot be completed;
* calculations cannot be independently reproduced;
* evidence cannot be verified;
* provenance is incomplete;
* historical reconstruction fails;
* reconciliation cannot be verified;
* certification readiness cannot be demonstrated;
* required audit artifacts are missing;
* constitutional compliance cannot be objectively established.

No Performance Truth object shall receive constitutional certification while any auditability requirement remains unsatisfied.
