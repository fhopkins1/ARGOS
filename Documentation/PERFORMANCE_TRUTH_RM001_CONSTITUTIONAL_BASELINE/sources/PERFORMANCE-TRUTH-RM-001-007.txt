# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-007

# Performance Truth Traceability

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the complete constitutional traceability requirements governing every Performance Truth value produced by the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of traceability is to ensure that every published performance value can be deterministically reconstructed from its originating enterprise events, calculations, evidence, and constitutional decisions. Every published metric shall possess complete provenance from origin through publication, certification, revision, and archival.

No Performance Truth shall exist without complete constitutional traceability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Performance Truth traceability shall satisfy the following principles:

* complete provenance;
* deterministic reconstruction;
* immutable lineage;
* reproducible calculations;
* complete ownership visibility;
* constitutional auditability;
* fail-closed validation.

Traceability exists to prove why every published performance value is correct.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The Performance Truth Office shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* provenance cannot be completed;
* dependency identity is unknown;
* calculation lineage is incomplete;
* evidence cannot be verified;
* historical reconstruction fails;
* workflow lineage is broken;
* publication lineage is ambiguous;
* certification lineage is incomplete;
* ownership cannot be established.

No Performance Truth value shall be published until complete constitutional traceability has been restored.
