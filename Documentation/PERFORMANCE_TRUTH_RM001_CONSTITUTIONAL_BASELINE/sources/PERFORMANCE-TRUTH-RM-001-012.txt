# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-012

# Constitutional Completeness Review

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the final constitutional review governing completion of the **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001** remediation program.

Its purpose is to independently verify that the constitutional specification for the Performance Truth Office is complete, internally consistent, free of ambiguity, and suitable to serve as the authoritative foundation for implementation certification.

This order certifies constitutional completeness only.

It does not certify implementation, software quality, repository completeness, behavioral correctness, clean-room reproducibility, or ECS-003 implementation compliance.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* Audit Doctrine
* Truth Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001

Nothing within this order may supersede constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The Constitutional Completeness Review shall independently determine whether the constitutional architecture of the Performance Truth Office is:

* complete;
* deterministic;
* internally consistent;
* non-contradictory;
* fully traceable;
* implementation-independent;
* auditable;
* certification-ready.

The review shall evaluate the constitutional specification only.

# Constitutional Readiness Determination

Upon completion of review, the Performance Truth Office shall receive one of the following constitutional determinations:

* **PASS** — Constitution is complete and suitable for implementation certification.
* **PASS WITH OBSERVATIONS** — Constitution is complete, with recommendations that do not affect constitutional correctness.
* **FAIL** — Constitutional deficiencies remain requiring remediation before implementation certification.

Only a **PASS** authorizes progression to the PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002 Implementation Certification Program.
