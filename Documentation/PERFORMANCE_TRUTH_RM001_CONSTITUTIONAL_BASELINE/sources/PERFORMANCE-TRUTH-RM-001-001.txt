# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001-001

# Office Governance and Constitutional Authority

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the complete constitutional governance of the Performance Truth Office.

The Performance Truth Office exists as the sole constitutional authority responsible for producing, maintaining, certifying, and preserving the authoritative performance history of the ARGOS Enterprise.

Its purpose is to transform validated enterprise evidence into deterministic, reproducible, auditable performance truth while preserving complete constitutional traceability from every published performance value back to its originating enterprise events.

This office shall never create, estimate, infer, or speculate upon performance information. All published performance truth shall derive exclusively from constitutionally authorized enterprise evidence.

---

# Constitutional Mission

The Performance Truth Office shall:

* receive constitutionally authorized enterprise evidence;
* calculate deterministic enterprise performance;
* publish immutable Performance Truth Records;
* preserve complete historical performance;
* maintain constitutional provenance;
* support enterprise auditing;
* support deterministic replay;
* support certification activities;
* provide enterprise performance to authorized consumers.

The office shall function exclusively as an objective recorder of enterprise performance and shall possess no authority to alter enterprise behavior.

---

# Constitutional Responsibilities

The Performance Truth Office is responsible for:

* producing Performance Truth Records;
* maintaining performance history;
* computing constitutional performance metrics;
* preserving calculation provenance;
* maintaining historical revisions;
* publishing certified performance values;
* supporting historical replay;
* supporting enterprise reconciliation;
* providing audit evidence;
* preserving immutable historical truth.

The office is not responsible for:

* trading decisions;
* workflow execution;
* authorization;
* market data acquisition;
* position management;
* risk management;
* broker communication;
* strategy selection;
* workflow scheduling.

---

# Constitutional Authority

The Performance Truth Office possesses exclusive constitutional authority to:

* calculate enterprise performance from constitutional evidence;
* publish authoritative enterprise performance;
* certify completed performance calculations;
* supersede published performance through constitutional correction procedures;
* reject incomplete or unverifiable calculations;
* suspend publication when constitutional guarantees cannot be satisfied.

No other office may publish enterprise performance as constitutional truth.

---

# Jurisdiction

The jurisdiction of the Performance Truth Office includes:

* realized enterprise performance;
* workflow performance;
* portfolio performance;
* account performance;
* benchmark comparison;
* historical performance;
* performance attribution;
* cumulative performance;
* interval performance;
* certification status of published performance.

The office has no jurisdiction over:

* unrealized market valuation unless constitutionally authorized;
* market prediction;
* strategy optimization;
* trade authorization;
* workflow ownership;
* market surveillance;
* execution control.

---

# Constitutional Ownership Boundaries

The Performance Truth Office owns exclusively:

* Performance Truth Records;

* Performance Benchmarks;

Ownership of source objects remains permanently with their originating constitutional offices.

# Constitutional Separation of Duties

The Performance Truth Office shall remain constitutionally independent from:

* execution;
* authorization;
* market acquisition;
* strategy;
* risk approval;
* workflow ownership;
* audit certification.

No office responsible for producing enterprise activity may certify its own performance.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The office shall refuse publication whenever:

* evidence is incomplete;
* provenance is incomplete;
* ownership is uncertain;
* dependencies are unavailable;
* calculations are non-deterministic;
* reconciliation is unresolved;
* certification prerequisites remain unsatisfied.

No partial performance truth shall ever be published.
