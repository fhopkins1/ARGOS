# HISTORIAN-IC-001

# Historian Implementation Closure and Enterprise Baseline Freeze

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Implementation Closure Order establishes the authoritative completion, stabilization, and constitutional freeze of the Historian Office following successful completion of its constitutional development, behavioral implementation, independent certification, and independent reproducibility certification programs.

The purpose of this order is to transition the Historian Office from an actively developed enterprise component into a constitutionally governed operational baseline.

Following implementation closure, the Historian Office shall be considered the authoritative enterprise subsystem responsible for immutable historical custody, provenance preservation, deterministic reconstruction, and Enterprise Information Journey management.

Future modifications shall occur only through formally authorized constitutional change.
