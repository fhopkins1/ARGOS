# HISTORIAN-MO-001

# Enterprise Information Journey Constitutional Hardening Campaign

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order series establishes the first constitutional hardening campaign for the Historian Office.

The purpose of this campaign is to challenge the constitutional architecture governing the **Enterprise Information Journey**, which constitutes the primary constitutional object of the Historian Office.

Rather than searching for implementation defects or certification weaknesses, this campaign searches for weaknesses in the constitutional definition, ownership, boundaries, completeness, provenance, and long-term utility of Enterprise Information Journeys.

Its objective is to determine whether the Enterprise Information Journey represents the smallest, simplest, most deterministic, and most complete immutable historical object capable of preserving the complete constitutional history of the ARGOS Enterprise.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This campaign derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Improvement Group Doctrine;
* HISTORIAN-RM-001;
* Enterprise Modification Doctrine.

Nothing within this campaign may weaken constitutional guarantees.

Every modification shall preserve immutable enterprise history.

---

# Campaign Scope

This campaign shall examine:

* Enterprise Information Journey identity;
* journey boundaries;
* journey ownership;
* journey completeness;
* provenance;
* historical graph architecture;
* constitutional minimality;
* language preservation;
* missing-information preservation;
* counterfactual readiness;
* Enterprise Learning readiness;
* long-term scalability.

The objective is to strengthen constitutional architecture rather than implementation.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every Modification Order shall:

* assume the Enterprise Information Journey is incomplete;
* search for omitted historical information;
* search for excessive historical information;
* search for duplicated custody;
* search for hidden ownership;
* search for architectural ambiguity;
* preserve determinism;
* preserve immutable historical truth.

No Modification Order shall authorize learning, recommendation, or enterprise modification.
