# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002-B06

# Determinism and Replay Verification

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the independent implementation certification campaign verifying that the Performance Truth Office executes deterministically and that every published constitutional result can be reproduced through independent replay.

The purpose of this certification is to demonstrate that identical constitutional inputs, calculation contexts, and evidence packages always produce identical Performance Truth outputs, regardless of execution time, execution environment, or auditor. Every published Performance Truth Record shall therefore be independently reproducible, historically reconstructable, and constitutionally auditable.

This order certifies implementation only.

It shall not redefine constitutional behavior established under **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001**.

---

* ARGOS Constitution

* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* Truth Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine

Where implementation differs from constitutional doctrine, constitutional doctrine shall prevail.

# Constitutional Principles

The implementation shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Identical constitutional inputs shall always produce identical outputs.

2. Published Performance Truth shall be reproducible indefinitely.

3. Historical replay shall never modify historical truth.

4. Replay shall reconstruct constitutional state without implementation-specific assumptions.

5. Determinism shall be independent of execution environment.

6. Every replay shall generate identical constitutional evidence.

7. Replay shall preserve immutable identities.

8. Replay shall preserve temporal semantics.

9. Replay shall preserve certification outcomes.

10. Non-deterministic implementation behavior is constitutionally prohibited.

# Negative Testing

The auditor shall intentionally introduce:

* reordered execution;
* repeated execution;
* replay after archival;
* replay after revisions;
* replay after corrections;
* delayed execution;
* independent repository reconstruction;
* interrupted replay;
* duplicate replay requests.

Implementation shall either:

* produce constitutionally identical results; or
* fail closed when constitutional guarantees cannot be maintained.

No undefined behavior shall occur.

10. No constitutional ambiguity remains regarding deterministic execution or replay behavior within the Performance Truth Office.
