# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002-B05

# Evidence and Traceability Verification

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This implementation certification order establishes the independent verification of evidence generation and traceability for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this certification is to verify that every published Performance Truth Record is completely supported by constitutionally required evidence and that every published performance value can be traced deterministically to its originating enterprise activities.

This order certifies implementation of evidence generation and traceability only.

It does not certify calculation correctness, interface behavior, deterministic replay, or fail-closed behavior except where those behaviors directly affect evidence integrity.

---

# Certification Objectives

The certification shall independently verify that:

* every published Performance Truth Record possesses complete constitutional evidence;
* complete provenance is preserved throughout the implementation;
* every published metric is independently reconstructable;
* every dependency is traceable;
* every historical revision preserves lineage;
* evidence packages are complete and immutable;
* no published performance value exists without constitutional provenance.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This certification derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001;
* Evidence Doctrine;
* Truth Doctrine;
* Audit Doctrine.

Implementation shall demonstrate conformance to constitutional evidence requirements without exception.

---

# Certification Scope

The certification shall verify evidence generation and traceability for:

* Performance Truth Records;

# Evidence Verification Categories

## PT-EVT-001

### Workflow Execution Traceability

Verify that every published Performance Truth Record is traceable to the originating Workflow Execution Token.

The certification shall verify:

* Workflow Execution Token identity;
* workflow ownership;
* workflow completion status;
* execution timestamps;
* execution evidence;
* workflow version;
* workflow certification state.

No published Performance Truth Record shall exist without a verified workflow lineage.

---

### Decision Traceability

Verify that every performance calculation requiring attribution references the appropriate Decision Object.

The certification shall verify:

* Decision Object identity;
* decision version;
* workflow association;
* strategy association;
* decision timestamp;
* decision evidence.

Every attribution shall remain independently reproducible.

---

# Failure Classification

Certification shall fail whenever:

* evidence is missing;
* provenance is incomplete;
* object identities cannot be verified;
* ownership boundaries are violated;
* historical lineage is broken;
* calculations cannot be traced;
* revisions are incomplete;
* evidence packages are incomplete;
* certification evidence is absent;
* traceability is non-deterministic.

---

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The implementation shall immediately fail certification whenever:

* any published Performance Truth Record lacks complete provenance;
* any required evidence package is incomplete;
* Workflow Execution lineage cannot be reconstructed;
* Decision Object attribution cannot be verified;
* authorization evidence is absent;
* market data lineage is incomplete;
* Closed Position Truth cannot be verified;
* calculation provenance cannot be reproduced;
* historical revisions are disconnected;
* certification evidence is missing.

No Performance Truth Record with incomplete evidence or traceability shall be considered implementation compliant.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every published Performance Truth Record is fully traceable to its originating enterprise activities.
2. Complete provenance has been independently verified for every constitutional evidence category.
3. All evidence packages are complete, immutable, and reproducible.
4. Workflow, decision, authorization, market data, and Closed Position Truth lineage have been verified.
5. Every calculation possesses complete mathematical provenance.
6. Every historical revision preserves an unbroken lineage.
7. A complete traceability matrix has been generated and verified.
8. All certification evidence has been preserved within the Evidence Repository.
9. All evidence and traceability verification procedures execute deterministically.
10. No constitutional ambiguity remains regarding evidence generation or traceability implementation within the Performance Truth Office.
