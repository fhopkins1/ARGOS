# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001A-B01

# Canonical Requirement Architecture Constitution

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Canonical Requirement Architecture Constitution establishes the authoritative constitutional requirement baseline governing the Closed Position Truth Office.

This series exists solely to eliminate every remaining constitutional deficiency identified during the ECS-003 Constitutional Reconciliation Audit concerning requirement architecture.

The constitutional governance established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01** is accepted as the authoritative governance baseline.

The canonical object and lifecycle model established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02** is accepted as the authoritative object baseline.

The closure, settlement, reconciliation, and realized outcome doctrine established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03** is accepted as the authoritative operational baseline.

The temporal, evidence, historical integrity, and traceability doctrine established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B04** is accepted as the authoritative certification baseline.

The constitutional reconciliation completed under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B05** is accepted as the authoritative constitutional assessment.

This order shall establish the complete canonical constitutional requirement architecture required by ECS-003.

No constitutional doctrine shall be modified.

No constitutional authority shall be changed.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order exists solely to establish deterministic constitutional requirements.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every constitutional obligation shall become exactly one canonical requirement.

Every canonical requirement shall represent exactly one constitutional obligation.

Aggregate requirements are prohibited.

Duplicate requirements are prohibited.

Implicit requirements are prohibited.

Derived requirements shall preserve lineage to their originating constitutional doctrine.

Every requirement shall possess:

* one canonical identifier;
* one constitutional owner;
* one originating doctrine;
* one governing authority;
* one governing object;
* one governing lifecycle obligation;
* one governing evidence obligation;
* one certification disposition.

Every requirement shall remain immutable after constitutional freeze.

Requirement identifiers shall remain permanently stable.

Requirement ownership shall never be shared.
