# HISTORIAN-RM-004-011

## Independent Reproduction Validation

### Objective

Execute and validate multiple independent clean-room reproductions of the complete Historian Office certification process.

This order shall determine whether equivalent repositories, equivalent documented procedures, and independently reconstructed environments produce constitutionally equivalent runtime behavior, evidence, provenance, replay results, and certification outcomes.

Independent reproduction shall be performed without developer assistance, previously generated evidence, implementation-specific knowledge, or reliance upon the original certification environment.

---

## Constitutional Requirements

Independent reproduction shall verify:

* deterministic runtime execution;
* deterministic behavioral outputs;
* deterministic evidence generation;
* deterministic provenance generation;
* deterministic historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* deterministic certification conclusions;
* fail-closed reproducibility behavior.

Certification shall be established through repeated execution rather than documentary comparison alone.

---

## Independent Reproduction Environments

Execute the complete certification process in multiple independently constructed clean environments.

Each environment shall be created exclusively from:

* the submitted repository;
* documented bootstrap instructions;
* declared dependency manifests;
* documented certification commands;
* supported execution-platform requirements.

Independent environments shall not share:

* prior execution outputs;
* generated evidence;
* local caches;
* mutable runtime state;
* hidden configuration;
* developer workspaces;
* undocumented credentials;
* implementation-specific setup knowledge.

---

## Minimum Reproduction Set

Perform no fewer than three complete independent reproductions.

The reproduction set shall include:

* one primary clean-room reproduction;
* one independently rebuilt equivalent-environment reproduction;
* one repeated reproduction after complete destruction of prior runtime state.

Where supported, execute an additional reproduction using a second constitutionally compatible platform or operating-system environment.

---

## Repository Identity Validation

Before each reproduction, verify:

* repository identity;
* repository version;
* source-tree completeness;
* manifest integrity;
* dependency-lock integrity;
* absence of generated certification artifacts;
* absence of untracked execution dependencies;
* absence of prior runtime state.

Each reproduction shall begin from the same certified source state.

---

## Environment Independence Validation

Verify that each environment is independently reconstructed.

Record:

* operating-system identity;
* interpreter identity;
* dependency versions;
* dependency hashes;
* configuration values;
* runtime variables;
* bootstrap outputs;
* environment fingerprint.

Equivalent environmental requirements shall produce equivalent execution behavior.

Undocumented environmental differences affecting certification shall fail closed.

---

## Complete Certification Execution

Each reproduction shall execute the complete certification surface, including:

* repository bootstrap;
* dependency verification;
* environment reconstruction;
* repository structural validation;
* runtime discovery;
* certification suite execution;
* behavioral verification;
* evidence regeneration;
* provenance regeneration;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* mutation validation;
* certification report generation.

Partial reproduction shall not satisfy this order.

---

## Deterministic Output Validation

Compare all constitutionally significant outputs across independent reproductions.

Verify equivalence of:

* behavioral results;
* lifecycle transitions;
* custody conclusions;
* provenance nodes;
* provenance edges;
* reconstruction outputs;
* replay outputs;
* certification findings;
* pass-and-fail conclusions;
* constitutional readiness determinations.

Equivalent execution inputs shall produce equivalent constitutional outputs.

---

## Evidence Reproducibility Validation

Verify that each independent reproduction regenerates complete certification evidence.

Compare:

* evidence classifications;
* execution sequences;
* artifact relationships;
* behavioral conclusions;
* traceability mappings;
* integrity results;
* certification lineage;
* evidence completeness.

Environment-specific metadata may differ only where explicitly designated non-semantic.

Previously submitted evidence shall not be consumed as an execution input.

---

## Provenance Reproducibility Validation

Verify equivalent regeneration of:

* provenance nodes;
* provenance edges;
* dependency relationships;
* derivation relationships;
* correction relationships;
* supersession relationships;
* certification lineage.

Canonical provenance structure shall remain equivalent across reproductions.

Missing, added, reordered, or altered constitutional relationships shall generate reproducibility findings.

---

## Replay Reproducibility Validation

Execute deterministic replay within every reproduction environment.

Verify:

* original-to-replay equivalence;
* cross-environment replay equivalence;
* historical-state equivalence;
* custody-state equivalence;
* provenance equivalence;
* evidence equivalence;
* certification-result equivalence.

Replay divergence shall fail closed.

---

## Certification Reproducibility Validation

Compare final certification outcomes across all independent reproductions.

Verify equivalence of:

* executed certification requirements;
* test inventory;
* pass results;
* expected fail results;
* mutation-detection results;
* unresolved findings;
* readiness conclusions;
* certification status.

Equivalent reproductions shall not produce contradictory certification outcomes.

---

## Canonical Comparison Model

Implement a deterministic comparison model distinguishing between:

* constitutionally significant outputs;
* reproducibility-significant outputs;
* permitted environmental metadata;
* nondeterministic but non-semantic metadata;
* prohibited execution divergence.

Permitted differences shall be explicitly declared, normalized, and justified.

Undeclared differences shall be treated as certification findings.

---

## Temporal Normalization

Normalize non-semantic temporal values where necessary for comparison.

Normalization may apply only to:

* execution timestamps;
* temporary file locations;
* process identifiers;
* environment-generated session identifiers;
* platform-specific non-semantic metadata.

Normalization shall never alter:

* behavioral sequence;
* lifecycle ordering;
* custody relationships;
* provenance relationships;
* evidence meaning;
* replay meaning;
* certification conclusions.

---

## Reproduction Divergence Detection

Implement automatic detection of divergence including:

* differing discovered component inventories;
* differing test inventories;
* differing execution order;
* missing evidence;
* altered provenance;
* replay mismatch;
* custody mismatch;
* reconstruction mismatch;
* mutation-detection inconsistency;
* certification-outcome inconsistency.

Every divergence shall generate a machine-readable finding.

---

## Divergence Classification

Classify each detected divergence as:

* permitted non-semantic variation;
* environment compatibility issue;
* dependency drift;
* execution nondeterminism;
* evidence nondeterminism;
* provenance nondeterminism;
* replay nondeterminism;
* certification nondeterminism;
* repository incompleteness;
* undocumented dependency;
* constitutional failure.

Unclassified divergence shall fail closed.

---

## Reproduction Failure Behavior

The certification process shall fail closed when:

* any required reproduction cannot execute;
* environments cannot be independently reconstructed;
* required evidence cannot be regenerated;
* provenance differs materially;
* replay outputs diverge;
* certification conclusions conflict;
* undocumented manual intervention is required;
* unexplained nondeterminism remains;
* comparison evidence is incomplete.

A successful execution in only one environment shall not satisfy independent reproducibility.

---

## Reproduction Evidence

Generate immutable evidence for each reproduction including:

* reproduction identifier;
* repository fingerprint;
* environment fingerprint;
* bootstrap record;
* runtime inventory;
* test inventory;
* execution record;
* regenerated evidence inventory;
* provenance inventory;
* replay result;
* mutation result;
* certification result;
* divergence findings;
* final reproduction conclusion.

Evidence shall permit an unrelated auditor to verify the comparison independently.

---

## Independent Reproduction Report

Produce a canonical Independent Reproduction Report containing:

* reproduction scope;
* environment descriptions;
* repository identity;
* execution procedures;
* component inventories;
* test inventories;
* behavioral results;
* evidence comparisons;
* provenance comparisons;
* replay comparisons;
* mutation comparisons;
* certification comparisons;
* permitted differences;
* detected divergences;
* unresolved findings;
* final reproducibility determination.

The report shall be generated automatically from runtime evidence.

---

## Negative Validation

Verify detection of controlled reproducibility failures including:

* dependency-version drift;
* missing repository file;
* altered configuration;
* hidden environment variable;
* reordered nondeterministic output;
* evidence omission;
* provenance alteration;
* replay corruption;
* mutation-test bypass;
* contradictory certification outcome.

Each injected divergence shall be automatically detected and classified.

Removal of the injected condition shall restore equivalent reproduction.

---

## Behavioral Verification

Verify through execution that:

* all required clean-room reproductions complete without developer assistance;
* each environment is independently reconstructed;
* equivalent inputs produce equivalent runtime behavior;
* regenerated evidence is constitutionally equivalent;
* regenerated provenance is constitutionally equivalent;
* reconstruction outputs are equivalent;
* replay outputs are equivalent;
* mutation detection is consistent;
* certification conclusions are equivalent;
* prohibited divergence causes fail-closed behavior;
* all reproduction evidence is independently verifiable.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* at least three complete independent clean-room reproductions have been executed;
* every reproduction begins from the same verified repository state without supplied evidence artifacts;
* every environment is reconstructed solely from documented repository instructions;
* all constitutional runtime capabilities execute successfully in every valid reproduction;
* regenerated behavioral evidence is constitutionally equivalent across reproductions;
* provenance graphs and certification lineage are equivalent across reproductions;
* historical reconstruction and replay produce equivalent outputs;
* mutation tests produce consistent detection results;
* final certification outcomes are equivalent;
* all prohibited differences are automatically detected and classified;
* unresolved nondeterminism causes certification failure;
* the Independent Reproduction Report provides sufficient objective evidence to support the HISTORIAN-RM-004-012 ECS-004 Readiness Review.
