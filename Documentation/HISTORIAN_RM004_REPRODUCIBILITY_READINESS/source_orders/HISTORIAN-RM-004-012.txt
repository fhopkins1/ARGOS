## HISTORIAN-RM-004-012

# ECS-004 Readiness Review

## Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive independent reproducibility readiness assessment to determine whether the Historian Office satisfies every prerequisite required to enter **HISTORIAN-ECS004-AUDIT-001 — Independent Reproducibility Certification Audit**.

The review shall determine whether certification has become an objectively reproducible property of repository execution rather than developer knowledge or previously generated evidence.

---

## Constitutional Requirements

The readiness review shall evaluate:

* repository reproducibility;
* environment reproducibility;
* dependency reproducibility;
* runtime reproducibility;
* evidence reproducibility;
* provenance reproducibility;
* reconstruction reproducibility;
* certification reproducibility;
* mutation resistance;
* clean-room auditor independence.

The review shall not evaluate constitutional architecture.

---

## Certification Scope

Conduct a comprehensive assessment of every capability introduced under:

* HISTORIAN-RM-001;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002;
* HISTORIAN-RM-002A;
* HISTORIAN-RM-003;
* HISTORIAN-RM-004.

Verify that each certified capability can be independently reproduced from source.

---

## Repository Assessment

Verify that the submitted repository contains:

* complete source code;
* executable certification suites;
* deterministic configuration;
* dependency manifests;
* bootstrap procedures;
* runtime documentation;
* certification assets;
* reproducibility instructions.

Reject repositories containing undocumented execution requirements.

---

## Environment Assessment

Verify independent reconstruction of:

* operating system assumptions;
* runtime environment;
* interpreter versions;
* libraries;
* package dependencies;
* environment variables;
* configuration files;
* executable tooling.

The certified environment shall be reproducible without developer assistance.

---

## Runtime Reproducibility Assessment

Verify deterministic execution of:

* repository bootstrap;
* runtime discovery;
* certification suite execution;
* historical reconstruction;
* deterministic replay;
* provenance generation;
* evidence regeneration;
* mutation validation.

Equivalent repositories shall produce equivalent runtime behavior.

---

## Evidence Assessment

Verify that every certification artifact is regenerated exclusively through runtime execution.

Confirm:

* evidence completeness;
* evidence integrity;
* evidence authenticity;
* evidence determinism;
* provenance consistency;
* custody continuity.

Previously supplied certification artifacts shall not influence regenerated certification.

---

## Certification Equivalence Assessment

Compare independently regenerated certification against submitted certification.

Verify equivalence of:

* behavioral conclusions;
* certification outcomes;
* historical reconstruction;
* replay results;
* provenance graphs;
* evidence inventories;
* constitutional compliance;
* mutation detection.

Any unexplained deviation shall generate an audit finding.

---

## Mutation Resistance Assessment

Verify automatic detection of controlled mutations including:

* repository omissions;
* dependency drift;
* configuration corruption;
* provenance corruption;
* custody corruption;
* replay corruption;
* evidence corruption;
* execution failures.

Every mutation shall produce deterministic fail-closed behavior.

---

## Auditor Independence Assessment

Verify that an unrelated auditor can successfully complete certification using only:

* the submitted repository;
* documented execution procedures;
* a clean execution environment.

No implementation-specific knowledge, undocumented commands, hidden credentials, or developer intervention shall be required.

---

## Reproducibility Metrics

Measure:

* repository reproducibility rate;
* bootstrap success rate;
* environment reconstruction success;
* certification execution success;
* evidence regeneration success;
* deterministic equivalence rate;
* mutation detection rate;
* fail-closed success rate;
* certification equivalence rate;
* independent reproduction success rate.

Metrics shall be objectively measurable and reproducible.

---

## Readiness Evidence

Generate immutable readiness evidence including:

* repository assessment report;
* environment assessment report;
* execution logs;
* reproducibility measurements;
* regenerated evidence inventory;
* equivalence analysis;
* mutation assessment;
* auditor execution report;
* readiness findings;
* final certification recommendation.

All readiness evidence shall become part of the Enterprise Information Journey.

---

## Readiness Decision Matrix

Classify readiness using one of the following constitutional outcomes:

* **READY** — All reproducibility requirements satisfied; no material findings remain.
* **READY WITH MINOR FINDINGS** — Minor documentation or evidence improvements remain, but they do not affect independent reproducibility.
* **CONDITIONALLY READY** — Correctable deficiencies remain that prevent unconditional certification.
* **NOT READY** — One or more constitutional reproducibility requirements are not satisfied.

The assigned classification shall be supported entirely by objective evidence.

---

## Exit Criteria

The Historian Office shall be authorized to enter **HISTORIAN-ECS004-AUDIT-001** only if all of the following are demonstrated:

* complete repository reproducibility;
* deterministic environment reconstruction;
* deterministic runtime execution;
* complete evidence regeneration;
* certification equivalence;
* successful mutation detection;
* fail-closed behavior under all tested failure conditions;
* independent clean-room reproduction without developer assistance.

Failure of any criterion shall prevent advancement to ECS-004 certification.

---

## Deliverables

Successful completion of this order shall produce:

* ECS-004 Readiness Assessment Report;
* Repository Reproducibility Verification Report;
* Environment Reconstruction Verification Report;
* Independent Reproduction Verification Report;
* Certification Equivalence Report;
* Mutation Resistance Assessment;
* Auditor Independence Validation Report;
* Final ECS-004 Readiness Decision.

---

## Certification Requirement

Successful completion of this order certifies that the Historian Office has demonstrated objective, deterministic, independently reproducible certification behavior across all constitutional capabilities established under **HISTORIAN-RM-001**, **HISTORIAN-RM-002**, **HISTORIAN-RM-002A**, **HISTORIAN-RM-003**, and **HISTORIAN-RM-004**, thereby authorizing commencement of **HISTORIAN-ECS004-AUDIT-001 — Independent Reproducibility Certification Audit**.
