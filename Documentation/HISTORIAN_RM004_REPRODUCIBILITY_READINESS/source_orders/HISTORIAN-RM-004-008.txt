# HISTORIAN-RM-004-008

## Certification Equivalence

### Objective

Implement deterministic comparison between independently regenerated Historian certification results and the submitted certification baseline.

The implementation shall determine whether clean-room execution produces constitutionally equivalent:

* behavioral conclusions;
* evidence;
* provenance;
* replay results;
* certification outcomes.

Certification equivalence shall be established through executable comparison rather than narrative assertion.

---

# Constitutional Requirements

The implementation shall:

* compare regenerated certification exclusively against an identified submitted baseline;
* preserve both baseline and regenerated artifacts immutably;
* distinguish exact identity from constitutional equivalence;
* apply deterministic comparison rules;
* detect all material divergence;
* generate objective equivalence evidence;
* prohibit discretionary auditor judgment within automated comparison;
* fail closed whenever equivalence cannot be established.

Equivalent certification shall not require byte-for-byte identity where nondeterministic metadata is constitutionally permitted.

---

# Certification Baseline Registration

The runtime shall register the submitted certification baseline before comparison.

The baseline record shall include:

* certification identifier;
* repository identity;
* repository revision;
* certification-suite version;
* execution-environment identity;
* evidence-package identity;
* provenance-package identity;
* replay-package identity;
* certification outcome;
* cryptographic hashes;
* submission timestamp.

An unidentified or internally inconsistent baseline shall be rejected.

---

# Regenerated Certification Registration

The clean-room certification execution shall produce and register an independent certification set containing:

* regenerated behavioral results;
* regenerated evidence;
* regenerated provenance;
* regenerated replay outputs;
* regenerated mutation results;
* regenerated certification findings;
* regenerated final outcome.

The regenerated set shall be linked to the clean-room execution that created it.

Previously submitted evidence shall not be reused as regenerated evidence.

---

# Comparison Domains

Certification equivalence shall be evaluated across the following domains:

1. repository identity;
2. certification scope;
3. behavioral coverage;
4. test discovery;
5. test execution;
6. behavioral conclusions;
7. evidence completeness;
8. evidence integrity;
9. provenance integrity;
10. replay equivalence;
11. mutation detection;
12. fail-closed behavior;
13. certification findings;
14. final certification outcome.

Each domain shall produce an independent comparison result.

---

# Exact Identity Comparison

Where constitutional determinism requires exact identity, the runtime shall compare:

* canonical artifact identifiers;
* repository revision;
* source hashes;
* test inventory;
* dependency lock identity;
* configuration identity;
* behaviorally significant serialized values;
* evidence hashes;
* provenance graph structure;
* replay state outputs;
* certification decision codes.

Any unexplained difference shall constitute divergence.

---

# Constitutional Equivalence Comparison

Where exact identity is not required, the runtime shall normalize only explicitly permitted nondeterministic fields.

Permitted normalization may include:

* execution timestamps;
* temporary filesystem paths;
* process identifiers;
* machine-specific identifiers;
* environment-specific path separators;
* nondeterministic ordering proven to be semantically irrelevant.

Normalization rules shall be:

* documented;
* machine-readable;
* version-controlled;
* deterministically applied;
* independently testable.

Normalization shall never conceal behaviorally material differences.

---

# Behavioral Conclusion Equivalence

The runtime shall verify that both certification sets reach equivalent conclusions for every tested capability.

Comparison shall include:

* pass outcomes;
* fail outcomes;
* expected rejection outcomes;
* mutation detection outcomes;
* replay outcomes;
* custody conclusions;
* provenance conclusions;
* evidence conclusions;
* lifecycle conclusions;
* final readiness conclusions.

A changed conclusion for any constitutional capability shall fail equivalence.

---

# Evidence Equivalence

Regenerated evidence shall be compared against submitted evidence for:

* artifact coverage;
* event coverage;
* identity consistency;
* ownership consistency;
* custody continuity;
* validation results;
* failure evidence;
* mutation evidence;
* certification lineage;
* cryptographic integrity.

Evidence shall be considered equivalent only when it supports the same constitutional conclusions through independently regenerated execution.

Missing evidence shall not be treated as equivalent.

---

# Provenance Equivalence

The implementation shall compare submitted and regenerated provenance for:

* node identity;
* edge identity;
* derivation relationships;
* dependency relationships;
* correction relationships;
* supersession relationships;
* certification lineage;
* custody relationships;
* graph completeness;
* graph integrity.

Equivalent provenance shall preserve the same historical and certification meaning.

Orphaned, missing, added, or altered material relationships shall fail equivalence.

---

# Replay Equivalence

The runtime shall compare deterministic replay results for:

* journey reconstruction;
* lifecycle reconstruction;
* historical state;
* artifact ordering;
* custody state;
* provenance state;
* missing-information state;
* language preservation;
* certification evidence;
* final replay outcome.

Replay equivalence shall require constitutionally equivalent state evolution and conclusions.

---

# Certification Outcome Equivalence

The final certification comparison shall verify equivalence of:

* certification status;
* certification scope;
* unresolved findings;
* exclusions;
* limitations;
* mutation results;
* reproducibility result;
* readiness determination.

A submitted PASS and regenerated FAIL shall never be equivalent.

A regenerated PASS with materially reduced scope shall never be equivalent to a full-scope submitted PASS.

---

# Divergence Classification

Every detected difference shall be classified as one of the following:

* Permitted Nondeterministic Difference;
* Nonmaterial Representation Difference;
* Evidence Difference;
* Provenance Difference;
* Replay Difference;
* Behavioral Difference;
* Scope Difference;
* Environment Difference;
* Certification Outcome Difference;
* Unclassified Difference.

Only the first two classifications may preserve equivalence.

Unclassified differences shall fail closed.

---

# Materiality Rules

A divergence shall be material when it affects:

* constitutional behavior;
* execution result;
* ownership;
* custody;
* provenance;
* replay;
* evidence completeness;
* mutation detection;
* fail-closed behavior;
* certification scope;
* certification outcome.

Materiality shall be determined by deterministic rules rather than auditor discretion.

---

# Runtime Comparison Sequence

The equivalence workflow shall execute in the following order:

1. register the submitted baseline;
2. validate baseline integrity;
3. register the regenerated certification set;
4. validate regenerated-set integrity;
5. validate scope equivalence;
6. apply authorized normalization;
7. compare behavioral conclusions;
8. compare evidence;
9. compare provenance;
10. compare replay;
11. compare mutation outcomes;
12. compare certification findings;
13. compare final certification outcomes;
14. classify divergences;
15. determine certification equivalence;
16. emit the equivalence report.

The comparison sequence shall be deterministic and fully automated.

---

# Failure Detection

The implementation shall detect:

* missing baseline artifacts;
* missing regenerated artifacts;
* baseline integrity failure;
* regenerated-set integrity failure;
* repository revision mismatch;
* certification-suite mismatch;
* reduced behavioral coverage;
* unauthorized normalization;
* evidence divergence;
* provenance divergence;
* replay divergence;
* mutation-result divergence;
* certification-scope divergence;
* outcome divergence;
* unexplained differences.

Each failure shall generate a deterministic audit finding.

---

# Fail-Closed Behavior

When equivalence cannot be established, the runtime shall:

* reject certification equivalence;
* preserve both certification sets;
* preserve all comparison artifacts;
* identify every divergence;
* prevent issuance of an equivalence certification;
* emit immutable failure evidence;
* preserve reproducibility records;
* require explicit remediation and re-execution.

The runtime shall not downgrade material divergences to warnings.

---

# Mutation Validation

The equivalence comparator shall be tested using controlled mutations including:

* changed behavioral result;
* removed evidence artifact;
* altered evidence hash;
* modified provenance edge;
* corrupted replay output;
* reduced certification scope;
* omitted mutation result;
* changed certification status;
* unauthorized normalization rule;
* altered repository revision.

Each material mutation shall cause equivalence failure.

---

# Deterministic Reproduction

Equivalent baseline and regenerated certification sets shall always produce:

* identical domain comparison outcomes;
* identical divergence classifications;
* identical materiality decisions;
* identical final equivalence result;
* equivalent comparison evidence;
* equivalent audit findings.

Repeated comparisons shall not depend upon execution order, auditor identity, or machine identity.

---

# Runtime Evidence

Every equivalence execution shall emit immutable evidence including:

* baseline certification identity;
* regenerated certification identity;
* repository identities;
* suite identities;
* environment identities;
* comparison-rule version;
* normalization-rule version;
* domain-level comparison results;
* divergence inventory;
* materiality determinations;
* behavioral comparison results;
* evidence comparison results;
* provenance comparison results;
* replay comparison results;
* certification-outcome comparison;
* final equivalence determination;
* execution timestamp;
* comparator identity.

All evidence shall be generated from the current comparison execution.

---

# Behavioral Verification

The certification suite shall independently verify:

* baseline registration;
* regenerated-set registration;
* exact comparison behavior;
* authorized normalization;
* behavioral conclusion comparison;
* evidence comparison;
* provenance comparison;
* replay comparison;
* mutation-result comparison;
* divergence classification;
* materiality determination;
* fail-closed behavior;
* deterministic equivalence outcomes;
* equivalence evidence generation.

Verification shall evaluate executable comparison behavior rather than report formatting.

---

# Required Deliverables

Execution of this order shall produce:

* submitted certification baseline manifest;
* regenerated certification manifest;
* comparison-rule specification;
* normalization-rule specification;
* domain comparison records;
* divergence inventory;
* materiality determination record;
* behavioral equivalence report;
* evidence equivalence report;
* provenance equivalence report;
* replay equivalence report;
* certification outcome equivalence report;
* final Certification Equivalence Report.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be complete only when:

* submitted and regenerated certification sets are independently registered and validated;
* all required comparison domains are evaluated automatically;
* authorized nondeterminism is normalized deterministically;
* material differences are never concealed;
* behavioral, evidence, provenance, replay, mutation, scope, and outcome differences are detected;
* controlled mutations reliably cause equivalence failure;
* equivalent certification sets produce equivalent comparison results across repeated executions;
* unresolved or unclassified differences fail closed;
* the complete comparison executes without implementation-specific auditor intervention;
* the final equivalence determination is supported by independently reproducible runtime evidence.
