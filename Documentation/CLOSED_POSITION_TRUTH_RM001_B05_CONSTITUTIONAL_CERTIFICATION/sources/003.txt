CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B05-003

# Constitutional Requirement and Traceability Audit

## Objective

Audit the complete constitutional requirement architecture governing the Closed Position Truth Office and verify that every constitutional obligation established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01 through B04** possesses atomic identity, authoritative lineage, deterministic ownership, complete participation, and bidirectional traceability.

This order shall determine whether the constitutional architecture forms one complete traceability chain from governing doctrine through future implementation obligation.

No implementation shall be modified.

No implementation discovery shall occur.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No proof generation shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional validation only.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one canonical identifier.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one authoritative source.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every constitutional requirement shall participate in complete forward and backward traceability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A constitutional statement shall not be accepted as a canonical requirement unless it establishes one independently testable obligation.

Every requirement shall be atomic.

Every aggregate statement shall be decomposed into atomic requirements.

Every duplicate requirement shall be reconciled to one canonical requirement identity.

Every requirement shall identify:

* originating doctrine;
* source clause;
* constitutional authority;
* constitutional owner;
* applicability;
* affected canonical object;
* affected lifecycle obligation;
* evidence implication;
* implementation implication;
* certification implication.

Traceability shall preserve constitutional meaning.

Traceability shall not create new constitutional authority.

Implementation obligations shall derive from constitutional requirements.

Implementation structure shall never redefine constitutional meaning.

Evidence shall prove satisfaction of requirements.

Evidence shall not substitute for missing requirements.

No constitutional requirement shall remain orphaned.

No canonical object shall exist without requirement lineage.

No lifecycle obligation shall exist without requirement lineage.

No implementation obligation shall exist without constitutional lineage.

# Atomicity Audit

A requirement shall be atomic only when it contains one independently verifiable obligation.

The audit shall identify aggregate requirements containing multiple obligations joined through:

* and;
* or;
* unless;
* except;
* where applicable;
* compound lists;
* multiple actors;
* multiple lifecycle transitions;
* multiple truth domains.

Aggregate requirements shall be decomposed while preserving:

* original authority;
* source lineage;
* shared context;
* dependency relationships;
* certification implication.

Decomposition shall not create new constitutional doctrine.

---

# Duplicate Requirement Audit

Requirements shall be considered duplicates when they govern the same:

* actor;
* obligation;
* object;
* lifecycle condition;
* authority boundary;
* evidence outcome;
* prohibited behavior.

Duplicate analysis shall distinguish:

* exact duplicate;
* semantic duplicate;
* narrower restatement;
* broader aggregate;
* supporting clarification;
* genuine independent obligation.

Duplicate requirements shall be reconciled to:

* one canonical requirement;
* one authoritative source;
* preserved secondary-source lineage;
* documented reconciliation disposition.

Duplicate reconciliation shall not discard constitutional history.

---

# Orphan Requirement Audit

A requirement shall be orphaned when it lacks one or more of:

* authoritative source;
* constitutional owner;
* affected object;
* lifecycle participation;
* evidence implication;
* implementation implication;
* certification implication.

Every orphan shall receive exactly one disposition:

* linked to authoritative lineage;
* decomposed;

No unresolved orphan may remain at constitutional freeze.

# Circular Traceability Audit

Circular traceability exists when:

* a requirement derives authority from an artifact created to satisfy that requirement;
* evidence validates the requirement that defines the evidence as valid without an independent source;
* an implementation obligation defines its own constitutional requirement;
* two requirements rely solely upon each other for authority;
* a downstream artifact becomes the source of upstream doctrine.

Circular traceability shall be prohibited unless explicitly authorized and independently grounded by constitutional doctrine.

Unresolved circular traceability shall constitute a constitutional blocker.

# Audit Finding Severity

Each finding shall receive exactly one severity:

* **BLOCKER**
* **MAJOR**
* **MINOR**
* **OBSERVATION**

A defect preventing constitutional freeze.

## MAJOR

## MINOR

## OBSERVATION

A documented condition requiring no remediation.

No finding shall be downgraded because implementation appears compliant.
