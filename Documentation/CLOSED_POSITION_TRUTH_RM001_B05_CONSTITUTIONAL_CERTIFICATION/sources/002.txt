# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B05-002

# Constitutional Consistency Audit

## Objective

Verify that the complete constitutional architecture governing the Closed Position Truth Office is internally consistent, deterministic, and free from unresolved conflict.

This order exists solely to audit every constitutional doctrine established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01 through B04** for contradictory authority, ownership, lifecycle, closure, settlement, reconciliation, evidence, historical-integrity, and traceability rules.

No implementation shall be modified.

No implementation discovery shall occur.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No proof generation shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional consistency validation only.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every constitutional statement shall possess one authoritative source.

Every constitutional obligation shall possess one governing authority.

Every constitutional responsibility shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every canonical object shall possess one owner.

Every lifecycle transition shall possess one governing authority.

Every truth element shall possess one authoritative form.

No constitutional rule shall contradict another constitutional rule.

No lower-order doctrine shall silently expand authority established by higher-order doctrine.

No interface, dependency, custody relationship, or reconciliation responsibility shall create implicit ownership.

Historical truth shall remain immutable.

Correction shall occur only through constitutionally governed successor records.

Every unresolved contradiction shall prevent constitutional freeze.

---

# Audit Scope

Audit every constitutional artifact produced under:

* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01 — Constitutional Governance, Authority, and Office Boundaries;
* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02 — Canonical Objects, Ownership, Custody, and Lifecycle;
* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03 — Closure, Settlement, Residual Quantity, and Reconciliation;
* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B04 — Temporal, Evidence, Historical Integrity, and Requirement Traceability.

The audit shall determine whether all doctrines collectively form one coherent constitutional architecture.
