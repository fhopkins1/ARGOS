# HISTORIAN-ECS003-AUDIT-002

# Independent Clean-Room Execution, Evidence Regeneration, Mutation Validation, and Final Certification Audit

## Purpose

Conduct a completely independent Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 certification audit of the Historian Office using only the submitted Repository Package and Evidence Package.

This audit shall determine whether the Historian Office independently satisfies every constitutional, implementation, behavioral, historical custody, provenance, replay, and certification requirement necessary to receive final ECS-003 certification.

This audit shall assume that every previous certification campaign, remediation campaign, modification campaign, implementation report, and audit conclusion may be incorrect.

No previous certification decision shall influence the outcome of this audit.

The auditor shall operate as an independent constitutional auditor.
