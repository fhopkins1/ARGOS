# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-003

# Fail-Closed Validation

## Objective

Independently validate that the Closed Position Truth Office certification system detects and rejects every constitutionally defective implementation produced through the controlled mutation campaign established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-001** and executed under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-002**.

For every mutation, verify the complete fail-closed consequence chain:

```text
Constitutional Requirement
        │
        ▼
Controlled Mutation
        │
        ▼
Implementation Defect
        │
        ▼
Behavioral Detection
        │
        ▼
Evidence Defect
        │
        ▼
Proof Invalidation
        │
        ▼
Certification Blocker
        │
        ▼
Certification Rejection
```

This order shall validate the certification system itself.

No constitutional doctrine shall be modified.

No implementation remediation shall occur.

No mutation shall be altered to force detection.

No mutation outcome shall be inferred.

No certification rejection shall be manually assigned.

Every fail-closed disposition shall originate exclusively from execution of the complete certification pipeline against the isolated mutated implementation candidate.

---

## Authoritative Inputs

Accept as authoritative inputs:

* the constitutional architecture established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001**;
* the unmodified implementation candidate established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002**;
* the clean-room repository and environment baseline established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-001**;
* the independently reproduced behavioral baseline established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-002**;
* the regenerated evidence, proof, and traceability baseline established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-003**;
* the Mutation Plan;
* the Mutation Registry;
* the Requirement-to-Mutation Registry;
* the Mutation Execution Registry;
* the Mutation Evidence Registry;
* the Mutation Execution Report.

Prior mutation campaigns, historical mutation results, expected blocker inventories, manually assigned outcomes, prior certification reports, and developer assertions shall not control validation.

---

## Required Validation Model

For every executed mutation, independently determine whether the certification pipeline produced each constitutionally required consequence:

1. the mutation created the registered implementation defect;
2. the affected behavioral verifier failed or otherwise exposed the defect where behavioral detection is applicable;
3. the regenerated evidence recorded the defect;
4. the affected requirement proof became NOT_PROVEN or otherwise invalid;
5. regenerated traceability connected the defect to the affected constitutional requirement;
6. an objective certification blocker was created;
7. the mutated implementation was rejected.

Every stage shall be validated independently.

A later-stage rejection shall not prove that all preceding stages operated correctly.

A verifier failure alone shall not prove complete fail-closed behavior.

A generated blocker alone shall not prove valid rejection unless the blocker possesses admissible supporting evidence and requirement lineage.

---

## Baseline Control Execution

Before validating mutated candidates, execute the complete certification pipeline against a fresh unmodified clean-room implementation candidate.

The baseline control execution shall establish:

* implementation identity;
* environment identity;
* configuration identity;
* verifier population;
* fixture population;
* execution population;
* evidence population;
* proof population;
* traceability population;
* blocker population;
* certification disposition.

The unmodified baseline shall not contain mutation-derived blockers.

Failure of the unmodified baseline shall be recorded separately and shall prevent mutation rejection from being interpreted as valid fail-closed detection where the same failure exists without the mutation.

---

## Mutation Identity Validation

For every mutation, verify:

* canonical mutation identity;
* governing requirement identity;
* governing implementation obligation;
* mutated artifact identity;
* mutation class;
* mutation operation;
* pre-mutation artifact hash;
* post-mutation artifact hash;
* isolated candidate identity;
* execution identity;
* rollback identity;
* fresh-baseline origin.

Confirm that the executed mutation matches the Mutation Registry definition.

Any unregistered, expanded, compound, contaminated, or incorrectly applied mutation shall produce a validation finding.

## Required Mutation Coverage

At minimum, validate the following constitutionally meaningful mutation classes.

### Premature Constitutional Closure

Mutate the implementation to permit authoritative closure before all constitutional closure prerequisites are satisfied.

Verify:

* premature closure behavior is exercised;
* the closure verifier fails;
* invalid closure evidence is generated;
* the closure requirement is not proven;
* a closure blocker is generated;
* certification is rejected.

### Unresolved Reconciliation Acceptance

Mutate the implementation to accept closure or completion while reconciliation remains unresolved.

Verify detection of:

* unresolved reconciliation state;
* false reconciliation success;
* missing reconciliation failure disposition;
* invalid completion or closure transition.

### Positive Residual Quantity Acceptance

Mutate the implementation to accept a positive residual quantity as constitutionally closed.

Verify:

* positive residual is preserved in raw evidence;
* zero-residual proof is invalidated;
* the residual-quantity requirement becomes NOT_PROVEN;
* a certification blocker is generated.

### Negative Residual Quantity Acceptance

Where constitutionally applicable, mutate the implementation to accept a negative residual quantity or over-closure condition.

Verify rejection through the same evidence, proof, blocker, and certification chain.

### Settlement Bypass

Mutate the implementation to permit closure without required settlement verification or valid settlement exemption.

Verify:

* missing or bypassed settlement authority;
* invalid closure transition;
* settlement proof invalidation;
* blocker creation;
* certification rejection.

### Degraded Analytical Input Acceptance

Mutate the implementation to treat degraded, insufficient, stale, or otherwise inadmissible analytical input as constitutionally sufficient.

Verify that the certification pipeline detects the invalid admissibility decision and rejects the candidate.

### Duplicate Authoritative Closure Creation

Mutate the implementation to permit more than one authoritative closure object or duplicate authoritative closure event for the same position.

Verify:

* duplicate creation is exercised;
* uniqueness or idempotency verification fails;
* duplicate evidence is preserved;
* proof is invalidated;
* a blocker is created.

### Immutable History Mutation

Mutate the implementation to overwrite, delete, or retroactively alter immutable historical state.

Verify:

* historical alteration is observable;
* immutable-history verification fails;
* original and altered state identities are preserved;
* proof is invalidated;
* certification is rejected.

### Correction Overwrite

Mutate correction handling so that a correction overwrites original authoritative history rather than creating an additive corrective object.

Verify rejection of destructive correction behavior.

### Supersession Removal

Mutate the implementation to omit required supersession relationships or permit superseded truth to remain ambiguously authoritative.

Verify:

* missing supersession lineage;
* conflicting authoritative state;
* proof invalidation;
* blocker generation.

### Stale Evidence Acceptance

Mutate evidence admissibility logic to accept evidence outside constitutionally permitted freshness limits.

Verify:

* stale evidence identity and timestamp are preserved;
* freshness verification fails;
* affected proof becomes NOT_PROVEN;
* certification is rejected.

### Conflicting Evidence Acceptance

Mutate the implementation to accept conflicting authoritative evidence without constitutional conflict resolution.

Verify:

* the conflict is preserved in raw evidence;
* conflict detection or resolution verification fails;
* proof is invalidated;
* a blocker is generated.

### Missing Evidence Acceptance

Mutate the implementation to permit closure, realized outcome generation, archival eligibility, or other authoritative disposition without required evidence.

Verify that absence of evidence cannot produce a PROVEN disposition or certification acceptance.

### Verifier Removal

Remove or disable a constitutionally required verifier within the isolated mutated candidate.

Verify:

* verifier discovery detects the omission;
* verifier coverage becomes incomplete;
* the affected requirement is NOT_PROVEN;
* a certification blocker is generated;
* the candidate is rejected.

A missing verifier shall not be treated as a passing verifier.

### Implementation Discovery Failure

Mutate implementation participation or discovery metadata so that a required implementation artifact becomes undiscoverable or is incorrectly excluded.

Verify:

* dependency-derived discovery identifies the discrepancy where possible;
* inventory reconciliation fails;
* traceability becomes incomplete;
* certification is rejected.

### Requirement Traceability Removal

Remove a required traceability relationship while preserving the underlying implementation where practical.

Verify:

* forward or backward traceability fails;
* the affected requirement or proof becomes orphaned;
* a traceability blocker is generated;
* certification is rejected.

### Proof Corruption

Mutate a regenerated proof object by changing:

* requirement identity;
* proof identity;
* disposition;
* supporting evidence reference;
* execution reference;
* provenance;
* integrity hash.

Verify:

* proof integrity or lineage validation fails;
* corrupted proof is rejected;
* certification acceptance cannot rely upon the corrupted proof.

### Replay Corruption

Mutate replay behavior so that replayed state differs semantically from original authoritative state.

Verify:

* replay equivalence fails;
* divergent state is preserved;
* affected proof is invalidated;
* a blocker is generated.

### Restart Recovery Corruption

Mutate restart or process-recovery behavior so that authoritative state is lost, duplicated, reordered, or changed after restart.

Verify:

* recovery verification fails;
* pre-restart and post-restart states are preserved;
* certification is rejected.

### Realized Outcome Ownership Corruption

Mutate ownership or mutation authority for realized outcome objects.

Verify detection of:

* unauthorized creation;
* unauthorized mutation;
* unauthorized custody transfer;
* conflicting ownership;
* ownership-proof invalidation.

### Certification Blocker Suppression

Mutate the certification system to suppress, downgrade, omit, or ignore an otherwise valid blocker.

Verify:

* the underlying defect remains observable;
* evidence and proof remain defective;
* missing blocker behavior is detected;
* the certification pipeline does not incorrectly accept the candidate;
* blocker-suppression failure itself generates a higher-order certification finding.

## Behavioral Detection Validation

For each mutation, identify all behavioral verifiers expected to exercise the affected obligation.

Validate:

* verifier identity;
* fixture identity;
* execution identity;
* mutation participation;
* asserted behavior;
* execution result;
* raw evidence;
* failure relevance.

Classify behavioral detection as:

* DETECTED_AS_EXPECTED;
* DETECTED_BY_ALTERNATE_VALID_CONTROL;
* PARTIALLY_DETECTED;
* NOT_DETECTED;
* NOT_BEHAVIORALLY_APPLICABLE.

An alternate verifier may satisfy detection only when it directly exercises the affected constitutional obligation and possesses complete evidence lineage.

Incidental failure in an unrelated verifier shall not constitute valid detection.

---

## Evidence Defect Validation

For every mutation, verify that regenerated evidence accurately records the mutated behavior or certification defect.

Validate:

* evidence identity;
* execution identity;
* mutation identity;
* evidence provenance;
* evidence integrity;
* defect observability;
* affected requirement identity;
* affected implementation artifact;
* evidence admissibility.

A mutation shall not be considered detected when the relevant defect exists only in a generated summary and is absent from raw or admissible regenerated evidence.

---

## Proof Invalidation Validation

For every affected canonical requirement, verify that the mutation causes the regenerated proof disposition to reflect the executed defect.

A constitutionally defective requirement shall not remain PROVEN.

Validate:

* affected proof identity;
* affected requirement identity;
* evidence references;
* execution references;
* proof disposition;
* proof justification;
* proof lineage;
* integrity status.

Acceptable affected dispositions are limited to those constitutionally authorized by the executed evidence.

A mutation causing a requirement to remain PROVEN despite contradictory execution evidence shall constitute a false-negative fail-closed defect.

## Certification Blocker Validation

For every mutation, determine whether the certification system generated one or more objective blockers.

Each blocker shall contain:

* canonical blocker identity;
* mutation identity;
* governing requirement identity;
* affected implementation artifact;
* affected verifier;
* affected execution;
* affected evidence;
* affected proof;
* blocker classification;
* blocker severity;
* blocker authority;
* supporting evidence references;
* generation mechanism;
* certification consequence.

A blocker shall be valid only when supported by regenerated evidence and complete lineage.

Reject blockers that are:

* manually assigned;
* inferred from the Mutation Plan;
* copied from prior campaigns;
* generated solely from expected outcomes;
* unsupported by raw evidence;
* unrelated to the mutated requirement;
* duplicated without justification;
* suppressed from the final certification disposition.

---

## Certification Rejection Validation

Verify that every constitutionally defective mutated candidate receives a rejection disposition from the same certification pipeline applied to the unmodified candidate.

The rejection shall:

* reference objective blockers;
* preserve all supporting evidence;
* identify affected requirements;
* identify affected proof objects;
* remain deterministic across repeated execution;
* survive regeneration of evidence, proof, and traceability;
* not depend upon manual intervention.

A process crash, harness failure, missing report, or inability to complete certification shall not automatically constitute valid rejection.

Technical failure shall be classified separately unless the certification constitution explicitly requires that condition to produce a fail-closed rejection with an objective blocker.

---

## Unrelated-Domain Stability

Where practical, verify that each isolated mutation affects only the intended constitutional domain and unavoidable downstream dependencies.

Compare mutated execution against the unmodified baseline to identify:

* expected affected verifiers;
* unexpected affected verifiers;
* expected proof invalidations;
* unexpected proof invalidations;
* expected blockers;
* unrelated blockers;
* excessive certification collapse.

Unexpected unrelated failures shall be preserved for accuracy validation under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-004**.

They shall not be suppressed merely because the mutated candidate was correctly rejected.

---

## Repetition and Determinism

Repeat each mutation execution sufficiently to establish deterministic fail-closed behavior.

At minimum:

* execute every mutation once through the complete certification pipeline;
* repeat every mutation whose outcome differs from its registered constitutional consequence;
* repeat every mutation exhibiting timing, ordering, concurrency, replay, recovery, or environment sensitivity;
* repeat a representative sample of all mutation classes;
* rerun the unmodified control candidate after mutation batches to detect contamination.

For each repetition, verify identical semantic outcomes for:

* behavioral detection;
* evidence defect;
* proof invalidation;
* traceability finding;
* blocker generation;
* certification rejection.

Machine-specific identifiers, timestamps, durations, and temporary paths may vary but shall not alter semantic disposition.

---

## Mutation Isolation Validation

For every mutation, verify:

* execution began from a fresh clean-room candidate;
* only the registered mutation was applied where practical;
* no prior mutation artifact remained;
* no prior mutation evidence influenced execution;
* no mutated persistence state survived rollback;
* implementation hashes returned to baseline after rollback;
* control execution after rollback remained equivalent to the original baseline.

Mutation contamination shall invalidate the affected execution.

## Required Fail-Closed Dispositions

Assign exactly one fail-closed disposition to every executed mutation:

* FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED;
* FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED_WITH_SECONDARY_VARIANCE;
* PARTIAL_FAIL_CLOSED_DETECTION;
* FAIL_CLOSED_NOT_CONFIRMED;
* INVALID_MUTATION_EXECUTION;
* BLOCKED_VALIDATION.

### FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED

The mutation created the intended constitutional defect, the defect was detected through admissible execution evidence, affected proof was invalidated, an objective blocker was generated, and the candidate was rejected.

### FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED_WITH_SECONDARY_VARIANCE

The complete fail-closed consequence chain occurred, but additional unrelated or non-semantic variance was observed.

### PARTIAL_FAIL_CLOSED_DETECTION

The mutation was detected at one or more stages, but the complete evidence-to-rejection chain did not occur.

### FAIL_CLOSED_NOT_CONFIRMED

The mutation created the intended constitutional defect but the certification system failed to detect or reject it completely.

### INVALID_MUTATION_EXECUTION

The mutation was incorrectly applied, contaminated, compound, non-isolated, or failed to create the intended defect.

### BLOCKED_VALIDATION

Validation could not complete because the certification pipeline or required clean-room capability was unavailable.

Only FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED and FAIL_CLOSED_CONFIRMED_WITH_SECONDARY_VARIANCE constitute successful rejection outcomes for this order.

## Prohibited Actions

Do not:

* modify constitutional requirements;
* remediate implementation defects;
* strengthen verifiers during the campaign;
* alter fixtures to expose a mutation;
* alter expected outcomes after execution;
* manually assign mutation rejection;
* manually generate blockers;
* suppress unexpected verifier failures;
* suppress unexpected verifier passes;
* infer proof invalidation;
* reuse prior mutation evidence;
* execute mutations against a shared contaminated candidate;
* treat process failure as automatic valid rejection;
* treat a failing verifier as complete fail-closed validation;
* issue the final mutation-campaign reconciliation disposition governed by **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-005**.

## Fail-Closed Requirements

This order shall fail closed when:

* a registered mutation is not executed;
* a mutation does not possess deterministic identity;
* a mutation is not isolated;
* the intended implementation defect cannot be demonstrated;
* an applicable behavioral verifier fails to detect the defect;
* regenerated evidence does not preserve the defect;
* affected proof remains incorrectly PROVEN;
* mutation traceability is incomplete;
* an objective blocker is not generated;
* the defective candidate is accepted;
* rejection depends upon manual intervention;
* blocker generation depends upon expected outcomes rather than execution;
* mutation results are nondeterministic;
* rollback fails;
* mutation contamination affects later executions;
* baseline control behavior cannot be distinguished from mutation behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria

This order shall complete only when:

* every registered constitutionally defective mutation has been executed against an isolated clean-room candidate;
* every mutation has been confirmed to create its intended constitutional defect;
* every applicable behavioral defect has been exercised through the complete certification pipeline;
* every mutation possesses raw execution evidence;
* every relevant evidence defect has been regenerated and preserved;
* every affected requirement proof has been correctly invalidated;
* every mutation possesses complete requirement-to-blocker traceability;
* every constitutionally defective candidate has generated at least one valid objective certification blocker;
* every defective candidate has been rejected by the certification pipeline;
* no rejection has been inferred or manually assigned;
* certification-blocker suppression has itself been detected and rejected;
* every mutation outcome possesses a deterministic fail-closed disposition;
* repeated mutation executions produce equivalent semantic outcomes;
* every mutation has been rolled back completely;
* post-rollback control executions reconcile with the unmodified baseline;
* every partial detection, missed defect, unsupported blocker, unrelated failure, contamination event, and nondeterministic result has been recorded;
* the Fail-Closed Validation Registry reconciles completely with the Mutation Findings Registry and Blocker Generation Registry;
* all deliverables have been generated deterministically;
* no constitutional doctrine, implementation baseline, verifier, fixture, or expected result has been remediated or modified;
* no final fail-closed campaign disposition or ECS-003 certification verdict has been issued.
