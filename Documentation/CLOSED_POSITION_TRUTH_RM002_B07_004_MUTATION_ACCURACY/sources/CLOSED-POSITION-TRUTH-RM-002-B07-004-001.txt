# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-001

# Independent Mutation Plan Generation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Independent Mutation Plan Generation Order establishes the authoritative procedure governing derivation of the constitutional mutation campaign for the Closed Position Truth Office implementation certification system.

This order exists solely to derive a complete, deterministic, and constitutionally justified mutation campaign from the governing constitutional baseline.

The mutation campaign shall validate the certification system itself.

The mutation campaign shall not improve implementation behavior.

Mutation planning shall never rely upon:

* prior mutation campaigns;
* historical certification failures;
* previous blocker inventories;
* historical execution results;
* manually curated mutation lists;
* developer expectations.

Every mutation shall originate exclusively from constitutional implementation obligations.

No implementation shall be modified during this order.

No mutation shall be executed.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No certification verdict shall be issued.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives its authority from:

* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001
* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002
* CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004

Every planned mutation shall represent an objectively identifiable constitutional defect.

If a mutation cannot be traced to a constitutional implementation obligation, it shall not be admitted into the mutation campaign.

---

# Objectives

Independently derive the complete mutation campaign directly from the constitutional baseline.

Demonstrate that every mutation possesses:

* constitutional authority;
* implementation obligation;
* deterministic identity;
* objective purpose;
* constitutional justification.

Construct a complete mutation inventory suitable for independent fail-closed validation.

---

# Required Planning Activities

## Phase 1 — Constitutional Requirement Enumeration

Enumerate every canonical implementation requirement possessing behavioral or certification obligations.

For every requirement record:

* canonical requirement identifier;
* governing constitutional authority;
* implementation obligation;
* implementation participant;
* governing office;
* certification obligation.

No requirement shall be excluded without constitutional justification.

---

## Phase 2 — Constitutional Failure Derivation

For every implementation obligation independently derive the minimum constitutional defect capable of violating that obligation.

Mutation derivation shall originate solely from:

* constitutional authority;
* ownership doctrine;
* lifecycle doctrine;
* closure doctrine;
* settlement doctrine;
* reconciliation doctrine;
* evidence doctrine;
* traceability doctrine;
* historical integrity doctrine.

Mutation derivation shall never originate from historical implementation failures.

---

## Phase 3 — Mutation Identification

Assign deterministic identity to every mutation.

Each mutation shall receive:

* canonical mutation identifier;
* mutation title;
* governing requirement;
* governing implementation obligation;
* governing implementation artifact;
* mutation classification;
* constitutional violation.

Mutation identifiers shall remain stable across independent reproductions.

---

## Phase 4 — Mutation Classification

Classify every mutation according to its governing constitutional domain.

Classification shall include, where applicable:

* ownership mutation;
* lifecycle mutation;
* closure mutation;
* reconciliation mutation;
* settlement mutation;
* evidence mutation;
* persistence mutation;
* traceability mutation;
* replay mutation;
* recovery mutation;
* certification mutation;
* infrastructure mutation.

Every mutation shall possess exactly one primary constitutional classification.

---

## Phase 5 — Expected Constitutional Consequence Definition

For every mutation determine the expected constitutional consequences including:

* violated requirement;
* violated implementation obligation;
* expected behavioral consequence;
* expected evidence consequence;
* expected proof consequence;
* expected certification consequence;
* expected blocker generation.

Expected consequences shall originate exclusively from constitutional analysis.

No execution assumptions shall be introduced.

---

## Phase 6 — Requirement Coverage Validation

Verify that every canonical implementation obligation possesses at least one planned mutation.

Identify:

* uncovered requirements;
* duplicate mutations;
* redundant mutations;
* orphan mutations;
* ambiguous mutations;
* overlapping constitutional violations.

Every mutation shall possess a unique constitutional purpose.

---

## Phase 7 — Mutation Campaign Completeness Assessment

Assess the completeness of the mutation campaign.

Verify:

* complete constitutional coverage;
* deterministic mutation identity;
* objective constitutional justification;
* implementation obligation mapping;
* certification obligation mapping.

No constitutionally significant implementation obligation shall remain without mutation coverage.

---

# Required Deliverables

This order shall produce:

* Mutation Plan;
* Mutation Registry;
* Requirement-to-Mutation Registry;
* Constitutional Coverage Matrix;
* Mutation Classification Registry;
* Mutation Justification Registry;
* Mutation Planning Findings Registry;
* Mutation Planning Report.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall pass only if:

* every mutation has been independently derived;
* every mutation possesses constitutional authority;
* every mutation possesses deterministic identity;
* every mutation maps to a canonical implementation obligation;
* every canonical implementation obligation possesses mutation coverage;
* no mutation depends upon historical certification knowledge;
* the mutation campaign is independently reproducible.

---

# Failure Conditions

This order shall fail immediately upon discovery of any of the following:

* mutation lacking constitutional authority;
* mutation lacking implementation obligation;
* mutation requiring historical certification knowledge;
* mutation derived from implementation expectations;
* uncovered canonical implementation obligation;
* duplicate mutation without constitutional justification;
* ambiguous constitutional ownership;
* indeterminate mutation identity.

Upon failure, all findings shall be documented and the fail-closed validation campaign shall remain blocked pending remediation.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall complete only when:

* canonical implementation requirements have been enumerated;
* constitutional failure modes have been independently derived;
* every mutation has received deterministic identity;
* every mutation has been constitutionally classified;
* expected constitutional consequences have been documented;
* complete requirement coverage has been verified;
* the mutation campaign has been assessed for completeness;
* all required deliverables have been produced.

Successful completion of this order authorizes progression to:

**CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-002-B07-004-002 — Controlled Mutation Execution.**
