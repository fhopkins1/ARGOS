# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-001

# Performance Truth Office Initial Independent ECS-003 Constitutional, Implementation, Evidence, and Proof Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the initial independent ECS-003 audit governing the Performance Truth Office.

This audit exists solely to determine whether the Performance Truth Office possesses a complete constitutional foundation and a conforming, behaviorally verified, independently reproducible implementation sufficient to serve as the authoritative enterprise source of performance truth.

The audit shall determine whether the Office correctly derives, preserves, and communicates performance truth from constitutionally admissible upstream records without:

* creating trading facts;
* modifying upstream truth;
* substituting estimates for authoritative outcomes;
* concealing uncertainty;
* fabricating benchmarks;
* rewriting historical performance;
* issuing unsupported analytical conclusions;
* exceeding its constitutional authority.

The audit shall independently evaluate:

* constitutional authority;
* office purpose;
* office boundaries;
* canonical objects;
* ownership and custody;
* lifecycle governance;
* source admissibility;
* realized and unrealized performance separation;
* benchmark governance;
* attribution governance;
* aggregation governance;
* temporal governance;
* correction and supersession;
* evidence governance;
* historical integrity;
* requirement architecture;
* dependency-derived implementation participation;
* behavioral verification;
* implementation evidence;
* requirement-level proof;
* execution-derived traceability;
* clean-room reproducibility;
* fail-closed behavior;
* certification readiness.

No constitutional doctrine shall be modified.

No implementation shall be modified.

No remediation shall occur under this order.

No certification conclusion shall be inferred from documentation, metadata, source presence, aggregate test counts, generated reports, or self-issued readiness declarations.

The audit shall fail closed.

---

# Governing Principles

Performance Truth shall be derived exclusively from constitutionally admissible upstream truth.

Performance Truth shall never create or modify:

* trades;
* orders;
* executions;
* fills;
* positions;
* closed-position records;
* authorization records;
* risk decisions;
* exit decisions;
* broker facts;
* market facts.

Performance Truth shall never convert:

* estimates into facts;
* forecasts into realized outcomes;
* incomplete data into complete truth;
* stale data into current truth;
* analytical confidence into constitutional authority.

Every authoritative performance record shall possess complete provenance.

Every performance statement shall identify its:

* subject;
* measurement basis;
* period;
* valuation basis;
* benchmark basis;
* attribution basis;
* currency basis;
* fee and cost treatment;
* data completeness;
* temporal identity;
* evidence lineage;
* correction and supersession state.

Analytical output shall remain constitutionally distinct from authoritative enterprise truth.

Every audit finding shall possess:

* canonical identity;
* governing requirement;
* affected artifact;
* objective evidence;
* severity;
* classification;
* disposition;
* remediation recommendation.

# Prohibited Audit Shortcuts

The audit shall not:

* treat source presence as behavioral proof;
* treat documentation as constitutional authority unless formally authoritative;
* treat generated registries as self-proving;
* treat aggregate test success as requirement-level evidence;
* mark unexecuted behavior as passing;
* use one unrelated execution to prove multiple unrelated requirements;
* infer evidence from expected behavior;
* infer mutation outcomes;
* accept historical evidence as independently regenerated evidence;
* suppress failed executions;
* alter the implementation candidate;
* remediate defects during audit execution;
* issue certification because the implementation appears reasonable.
