# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B05-002

# Canonical Requirement Reconciliation

## Objective

Establish the complete atomic constitutional requirement population governing the Exit Decision Office.

This order exists solely to derive, classify, identify, and reconcile every constitutional requirement established under **EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01 through B04** into one authoritative canonical requirement model.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity beyond constitutional requirement reconciliation shall occur under this order.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one canonical identity.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one authoritative source.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess deterministic traceability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A requirement shall not exist without constitutional authority.

A requirement shall not possess multiple authoritative sources.

A requirement shall not exist only through implication.

A requirement shall not combine multiple independent obligations without explicit decomposition.

A requirement shall not remain orphaned from:

* source doctrine;
* owner;
* applicability;
* evidence obligation;
* verification classification;
* certification implication.

Requirement identity shall remain separate from implementation identity.

Requirement satisfaction shall remain separate from implementation behavior.

Requirement existence shall not imply certification completion.

# 7. Requirement Conflict Reconciliation

Identify:

## Duplicate Requirements

Requirements expressing identical obligations.

Resolve by:

* merging where appropriate;
* preserving lineage;
* selecting canonical identity.

---

## Aggregate Requirements

Requirements containing multiple independent obligations.

Resolve by:

* decomposing into atomic requirements;
* assigning unique identities.

---

## Orphan Requirements

Requirements lacking:

* source authority;
* owner;
* applicability;
* traceability.

Resolve or remove.

---

## Conflicting Requirements

Requirements containing incompatible obligations.

Resolve by:

* identifying governing authority;
* assigning precedence;
* recording reconciliation decision.

---

## Missing Requirements

Identify required obligations implied by doctrine but absent from the requirement registry.

Create canonical requirements where necessary.
