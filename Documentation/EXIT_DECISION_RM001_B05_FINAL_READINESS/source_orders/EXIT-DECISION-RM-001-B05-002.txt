# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B05-002

# Canonical Requirement Reconciliation

## Objective

Establish the complete atomic constitutional requirement population governing the Exit Decision Office.

This order exists solely to derive, identify, classify, assign ownership, and reconcile every constitutional requirement established under **EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01 through B04** into one authoritative canonical requirement model.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No implementation certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every constitutional obligation shall possess one canonical requirement identity.

Every canonical requirement shall possess one authoritative source.

Every canonical requirement shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every canonical requirement shall possess complete traceability.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A constitutional requirement shall exist only through explicit constitutional authority.

A constitutional requirement shall not exist through assumption, implication, or implementation behavior.

Requirement identity shall remain independent from implementation identity.

Requirement ownership shall remain independent from implementation ownership.

Requirement satisfaction shall remain independent from certification outcome.

Every requirement shall define:

* authority source;
* applicability boundary;
* ownership;
* evidence implication;
* verification classification;
* certification implication.

No requirement shall remain:

* duplicated;
* aggregated;
* orphaned;
* conflicting;
* undefined.
