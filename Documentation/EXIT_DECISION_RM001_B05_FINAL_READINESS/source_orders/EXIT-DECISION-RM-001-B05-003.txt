# Constitutional Dependency and Traceability Reconciliation

## Objective

Establish the complete constitutional dependency reconciliation model verifying that every Exit Decision authority, requirement, object, lifecycle obligation, admissibility obligation, decision obligation, interface obligation, evidence obligation, and certification obligation participates in one deterministic constitutional dependency graph.

This order shall eliminate ambiguity regarding:

* constitutional dependency ownership;
* requirement lineage;
* object participation;
* lifecycle participation;
* interface dependency;
* evidence dependency;
* certification traceability.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity beyond constitutional reconciliation shall occur under this order.

Every dependency shall possess one constitutional purpose.

Every dependency shall possess one governing authority.

Every traceability relationship shall possess one deterministic direction.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A constitutional dependency shall exist only when justified by authoritative doctrine.

A dependency shall not imply ownership.

A consumer shall not become the owner of a producer's truth domain.

A traceability relationship shall preserve lineage from constitutional authority to certification obligation.

Every artifact shall have:

* one source authority;
* one owner;
* one applicable scope;
* one dependency classification;
* one traceability relationship.

The authoritative traceability chain shall be:

```text id="t7h3xq"

↓

↓
Admissibility Obligation
↓

↓

```

No artifact shall exist outside this chain.

No artifact shall possess an ambiguous constitutional origin.

# Traceability Integrity Registry

The following conditions shall constitute traceability failure:

## Broken Traceability

A requirement cannot identify:

* source authority;
* owner;
* affected artifact;
* evidence obligation.

## Missing Dependency

An artifact requires another artifact but no dependency exists.

## Circular Dependency

An artifact depends upon itself or a downstream artifact without constitutional authorization.

## Orphan Artifact

An artifact exists without constitutional lineage.

## Ownership Conflict

Multiple authorities claim ownership of the same constitutional responsibility.
