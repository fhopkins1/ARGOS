# Constitutional Dependency and Traceability Reconciliation

## Objective

Verify complete constitutional dependency integrity and bidirectional traceability across the entire Exit Decision constitutional architecture established under **EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01 through B04**.

This order shall reconcile every relationship between:

* constitutional authorities;
* canonical requirements;
* Exit Decision objects;
* lifecycle obligations;
* admissibility obligations;
* decision obligations;
* interface obligations;
* evidence obligations;
* certification obligations.

This order shall establish one deterministic constitutional participation model proving that every requirement, object, responsibility, dependency, and evidence obligation possesses authoritative lineage.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No implementation certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every dependency shall have one constitutional purpose.

Every traceability relationship shall have one deterministic direction.

Every artifact shall possess complete constitutional lineage.

# Constitutional Principles

A dependency shall exist only when justified by constitutional authority.

Dependency shall never imply ownership.

Participation shall never imply authority.

Consumption shall never imply custody.

Evidence custody shall never imply truth ownership.

Certification traceability shall never create constitutional authority.

Every constitutional artifact shall map to:

* authoritative source;
* canonical identity;
* responsible owner;
* applicable scope;
* evidence obligation;
* certification implication.

The authoritative constitutional traceability chain shall be:

```text
Constitutional Authority
↔
Canonical Requirement
↔

↔
Lifecycle Obligation
↔
Admissibility Obligation
↔
Decision Obligation
↔
Interface Obligation
↔
Evidence Obligation
↔
Certification Obligation
```

No constitutional requirement shall exist outside this chain.

No artifact shall remain without deterministic lineage.
