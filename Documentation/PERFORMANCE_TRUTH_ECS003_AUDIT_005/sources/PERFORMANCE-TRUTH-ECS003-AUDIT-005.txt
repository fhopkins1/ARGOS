# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-005

# Independent Verification of Audit-004A Remediation and Final Runtime Certification Audit

## Purpose

Conduct the final independent ECS-003 certification audit of the Performance Truth Office.

This audit exists solely to determine whether the remediation implemented under **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-004A** has successfully resolved every certification-blocking finding identified during **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-ECS003-AUDIT-004**.

The audit shall independently reproduce the complete runtime validation from the submitted Repository Package ZIP.

Repository-supplied evidence, self-validation reports, historical execution logs, and prior certification conclusions shall not constitute proof.

The audit shall issue exactly one certification decision:

* **PASS**
* **FAIL**

Conditional certification is prohibited.
