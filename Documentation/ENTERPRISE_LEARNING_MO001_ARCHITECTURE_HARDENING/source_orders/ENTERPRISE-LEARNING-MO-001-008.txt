## ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-008

### Constitutional Coupling and Cohesion Review

#### Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive constitutional review of the Enterprise Learning Office to verify that its architecture exhibits maximum internal cohesion and minimum external coupling.

The objective is to ensure that Enterprise Learning functions as an independent constitutional office whose responsibilities are internally consistent while depending upon other enterprise offices only through well-defined constitutional interfaces.

No architectural dependency shall introduce hidden authority, circular ownership, or constitutional ambiguity.

---

### Constitutional Review Objectives

This review shall evaluate:

* coupling to the Historian;
* coupling to Performance Truth;
* coupling to Commander;
* coupling to Analyst;
* coupling to Risk;
* coupling to Trader;
* coupling to Monitoring;
* coupling to Librarian;
* internal architectural cohesion;
* constitutional layering;
* dependency direction;
* interface minimality.

Every dependency shall possess explicit constitutional justification.

---

### Internal Cohesion Review

Verify that every constitutional responsibility contributes directly to the singular mission of Enterprise Learning:

* transforming historical evidence into enterprise knowledge;
* validating hypotheses;
* conducting experiments;
* developing learning products;
* publishing reproducible knowledge.

No responsibility unrelated to enterprise learning shall remain within the office.

---

### Historian Coupling Review

Determine whether Enterprise Learning depends upon the Historian only for:

* immutable historical records;
* provenance;
* historical custody;
* Enterprise Information Journeys;
* historical reconstruction.

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* cannot modify history;
* cannot alter provenance;
* cannot certify historical truth;
* cannot assume historical custody.

All information flow shall be read-only from the Historian.

---

### Performance Truth Coupling Review

Determine whether Enterprise Learning depends upon Performance Truth only for:

* objective outcome measurements;
* performance observations;
* certified performance metrics;
* validation targets.

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* cannot modify performance truth;
* cannot certify operational performance;
* cannot redefine enterprise metrics.

Performance Truth shall remain the exclusive owner of objective performance.

---

### Commander Coupling Review

Verify that interaction with the Commander is limited to:

* strategic learning priorities;
* research objectives;
* publication consumption;
* enterprise guidance.

Enterprise Learning shall never:

* issue operational commands;
* establish enterprise priorities;
* authorize execution;
* direct workflows.

---

### Analyst Coupling Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning and Analyst remain constitutionally distinct.

Enterprise Learning shall:

* produce reusable knowledge;
* publish learning artifacts.

Analyst shall:

* perform case-specific reasoning;
* interpret enterprise situations;
* support operational decisions.

Neither office shall duplicate the responsibilities of the other.

---

### Risk Coupling Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning supplies knowledge to Risk without:

* calculating operational risk;
* approving risk decisions;
* enforcing risk policy.

Risk shall independently determine enterprise risk using published learning products where appropriate.

---

### Trader Coupling Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning:

* publishes advisory models;
* publishes uncertainty;
* publishes explainability.

Trader shall retain exclusive authority for execution decisions.

Learning outputs shall never initiate transactions directly.

---

### Monitoring Coupling Review

Verify that Monitoring supplies operational observations while Enterprise Learning:

* consumes observations;
* performs learning;
* publishes knowledge.

Enterprise Learning shall not perform runtime monitoring.

Monitoring shall not perform learning.

---

### Librarian Coupling Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning publishes finalized learning artifacts while the Librarian retains responsibility for:

* enterprise publication storage;
* cataloging;
* retrieval services;
* long-term preservation.

Publication ownership and repository ownership shall remain constitutionally distinct.

---

### Constitutional Layering Review

Verify that architectural dependencies follow a one-way constitutional flow:

1. Operational systems generate behavior.
2. Historian preserves immutable history.
3. Performance Truth certifies objective outcomes.
4. Enterprise Learning derives knowledge.
5. Consumers evaluate published knowledge.
6. Operational offices independently determine future actions.

No reverse dependency shall allow Enterprise Learning to alter lower constitutional layers.

---

### Dependency Direction Verification

Verify that Enterprise Learning receives constitutional inputs but does not control:

* historical preservation;
* workflow execution;
* operational monitoring;
* truth certification;
* enterprise governance.

All outbound interfaces shall publish knowledge rather than exercise authority.

---

### Coupling Metrics

Evaluate each constitutional interface for:

* dependency necessity;
* interface size;
* ownership preservation;
* authority preservation;
* information flow direction;
* replaceability;
* architectural independence.

Unnecessary dependencies shall be identified for elimination.

---

### Cohesion Metrics

Evaluate internal architecture for:

* mission alignment;
* responsibility concentration;
* ownership consistency;
* lifecycle consistency;
* publication consistency;
* explainability consistency;
* scientific governance consistency.

Responsibilities serving unrelated constitutional purposes shall be identified as cohesion defects.

---

### Constitutional Defect Identification

Identify defects including:

* excessive coupling;
* circular dependencies;
* hidden authority;
* duplicated responsibilities;
* shared ownership;
* unnecessary interfaces;
* architectural leakage;
* improper dependency direction.

Every defect shall include a constitutional recommendation for simplification.

---

### Constitutional Verification

Verify that:

* Enterprise Learning possesses a single cohesive constitutional mission;
* every external dependency is minimal, deterministic, and justified;
* constitutional interfaces preserve ownership boundaries;
* architectural layering remains strictly one-directional;
* Enterprise Learning cannot acquire hidden operational authority through coupling.

---

### Deliverables

Completion of this review shall produce:

* Constitutional Coupling Assessment;
* Internal Cohesion Assessment;
* Enterprise Dependency Matrix;
* Interface Simplification Report;
* Architectural Layering Assessment;
* Dependency Direction Analysis;
* Constitutional Defect Register;
* Coupling Reduction Recommendations;
* Cohesion Improvement Recommendations.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent constitutional review demonstrates that:

* Enterprise Learning exhibits high internal cohesion around its singular constitutional mission;
* every interface with the Historian, Performance Truth, Commander, Analyst, Risk, Trader, Monitoring, and Librarian is minimal, deterministic, and constitutionally justified;
* no circular dependencies, ownership violations, hidden authority, or architectural leakage exist;
* constitutional layering is preserved through one-way dependency flow from enterprise truth toward published knowledge;
* the office achieves maximum constitutional cohesion with minimum constitutional coupling while remaining fully scalable for future enterprise growth.
