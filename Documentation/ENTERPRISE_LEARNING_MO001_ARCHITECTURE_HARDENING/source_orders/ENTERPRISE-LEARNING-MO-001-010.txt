# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-010

## Constitutional Architecture Certification Review

### Objective

Conduct the final constitutional certification review of the Enterprise Learning Office following completion of the Constitutional Architecture Hardening Campaign.

This review shall determine whether the Enterprise Learning Office possesses the simplest constitutionally complete architecture capable of governing enterprise learning while preserving complete separation from enterprise truth, historical custody, workflow execution, decision authority, performance certification, and operational control.

The review shall certify constitutional architecture only.

Behavioral implementation shall remain outside the scope of this order.

---

* ARGOS Constitution;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003;
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-004;
* Enterprise Truth Doctrine;

* ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001;

This review shall not modify constitutional architecture.

# Mission Integrity Review

Verify that the constitutional mission remains:

* complete;
* minimal;
* deterministic;
* unambiguous;
* independently understandable.

Verify that the Enterprise Learning Office exists solely to:

* transform enterprise information into reusable enterprise knowledge;
* discover statistically and scientifically supported relationships;
* construct learning artifacts;
* publish learning products;
* preserve learning evidence.

Verify that the mission excludes authority over:

* enterprise truth;
* workflow execution;
* operational decision making;
* historical preservation;
* performance certification;
* enterprise authorization;
* enterprise monitoring.

No hidden constitutional authority shall exist.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete only when:

* the constitutional mission is complete, minimal, and unambiguous;
* every constitutional responsibility possesses unique purpose and ownership;
* ownership boundaries are complete and deterministic;
* enterprise interfaces preserve constitutional independence;
* learning products represent enterprise knowledge without acquiring operational authority;
* lifecycle governance is deterministic and evidence-producing;
* explainability and scientific governance satisfy constitutional requirements;
* evidence ownership remains distinct from historical provenance;
* historical provenance remains exclusively owned by the Historian;
* constitutional boundaries prevent Enterprise Learning from exercising truth, execution, performance, monitoring, historical, or decision authority;
* constitutional cohesion is high and constitutional coupling is minimal;
* architectural determinism is fully demonstrated;
* no unresolved Critical Constitutional Defects remain;
* the architecture represents the simplest constitutionally complete governance model for enterprise learning;
* a formal certification decision authorizes one of the four constitutional dispositions, including progression to **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-002** where all constitutional certification requirements have been satisfied.
