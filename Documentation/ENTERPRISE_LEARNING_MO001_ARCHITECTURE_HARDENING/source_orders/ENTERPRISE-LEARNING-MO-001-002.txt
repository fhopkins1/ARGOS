# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-002

## Responsibility Decomposition Review

### Objective

Challenge every constitutional responsibility assigned to the Enterprise Learning Office and determine whether each responsibility is:

* necessary;
* complete;
* independent;
* deterministic;
* uniquely owned;
* free from duplication;
* appropriately bounded.

This order shall identify responsibility overlap, hidden authority, unnecessary decomposition, missing responsibility, excessive fragmentation, and constitutional ambiguity.

Every retained responsibility shall possess one unique constitutional purpose.

---

# Constitutional Review Standard

Each Enterprise Learning responsibility shall be evaluated against the following questions:

1. Is the responsibility required to fulfill the office mission?
2. Does another office already own the same constitutional purpose?
3. Can the responsibility be expressed more simply?
4. Does it conceal decision, truth, execution, custody, or certification authority?
5. Does it possess a defined object, lifecycle, owner, and output?
6. Can compliance be independently determined?
7. Does its removal create a constitutional gap?
8. Does its retention create unnecessary coupling or duplication?

Responsibilities that cannot satisfy these questions shall be modified, consolidated, transferred, or removed.

---

# Responsibilities Under Review

The review shall challenge responsibility for:

* learning dataset governance;
* hypothesis governance;
* experiment governance;
* feature-definition governance;
* model-development governance;
* learning validation;
* learning evidence generation;
* reproducibility qualification;
* explainability qualification;
* uncertainty disclosure;
* learning-product publication;
* learning-product monitoring qualification;
* retirement;
* withdrawal;
* supersession;
* historical registration.

No responsibility established under **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001** shall be presumed necessary merely because it already exists.

---

# Responsibility Classification

Every responsibility shall be classified as one of the following:

* Core Learning Responsibility;
* Supporting Learning Responsibility;
* Interface Responsibility;
* Custodial Responsibility;
* Publication Responsibility;
* Validation Responsibility;
* Prohibited Responsibility;
* Duplicate Responsibility;
* Unnecessary Responsibility;
* Missing Responsibility.

Classification shall be based upon constitutional purpose rather than implementation convenience.

---

# Core Responsibility Test

A responsibility shall qualify as a Core Learning Responsibility only when it directly governs the creation, evaluation, qualification, or publication of enterprise learning knowledge.

Core responsibilities may include:

* dataset definition;
* hypothesis formulation;
* experiment design;
* feature definition;
* model development;
* learning-product validation;
* publication qualification.

A responsibility that does not directly support a learning object or learning conclusion shall not be classified as core.

---

# Supporting Responsibility Test

A supporting responsibility shall be retained only when it is necessary to make a core responsibility:

* reproducible;
* explainable;
* auditable;
* deterministic;
* constitutionally bounded.

Supporting responsibilities may include:

* evidence generation;
* uncertainty disclosure;
* reproducibility assessment;
* lineage maintenance;
* qualification records.

Supporting responsibilities shall not become independent sources of enterprise authority.

---

# Dataset Governance Challenge

Challenge whether Enterprise Learning should own the complete governance of derived learning datasets.

Verify that dataset responsibility is limited to:

* learning-purpose definition;
* selection criteria;
* transformation rules;
* labeling;
* partitioning;
* versioning;
* qualification;
* retirement;
* supersession.

Determine whether any dataset responsibility improperly includes:

* ownership of source truth;
* alteration of historical records;
* permanent historical custody;
* authoritative source correction;
* enterprise data governance beyond learning use.

Source-record ownership and historical custody shall remain outside Enterprise Learning.

---

# Hypothesis Governance Challenge

Challenge whether hypothesis governance is constitutionally distinct and necessary.

Verify that the responsibility includes:

* registration;
* formulation;
* assumptions;
* falsification criteria;
* evidence association;
* evaluation status;
* rejection;
* publication;
* retirement;
* supersession.

Determine whether hypothesis governance duplicates:

* Analyst interpretation;
* Commander decision framing;
* Risk scenario generation;
* historical reconstruction.

Hypotheses shall remain testable learning propositions rather than decisions or authoritative conclusions.

---

# Experiment Governance Challenge

Challenge whether experiment governance contains only scientific learning responsibilities.

Verify responsibility for:

* experimental objective;
* hypothesis association;
* method;
* controls;
* input definitions;
* metrics;
* acceptance criteria;
* reproducibility;
* evidence;
* disposition.

Remove or prohibit any implied authority to:

* alter operational workflows;
* conduct unauthorized live interventions;
* direct Trader execution;
* modify risk limits;
* redefine Performance Truth;
* create historical facts.

Operational experimentation shall require separate constitutional authority.

---

# Feature Governance Challenge

Challenge whether feature-definition governance possesses a unique constitutional purpose distinct from dataset construction and model development.

Verify responsibility for:

* semantic definition;
* source mapping;
* calculation logic;
* temporal meaning;
* missing-value treatment;
* unit definition;
* versioning;
* validation;
* lineage;
* retirement.

Determine whether feature governance can be simplified without losing reproducibility or lineage.

Feature definitions shall remain derived learning objects and shall not alter source records.

---

# Model Governance Challenge

Challenge whether model-development governance is appropriately bounded.

Verify responsibility for:

* model registration;
* model design;
* training;
* validation;
* comparison;
* uncertainty assessment;
* explainability;
* reproducibility;
* publication;
* retirement;
* supersession.

Prohibit responsibility for:

* final enterprise decisions;
* automatic operational authority;
* risk authorization;
* trade execution;
* performance certification;
* truth declaration.

A model shall remain a learning product regardless of technical autonomy.

---

# Learning Validation Challenge

Determine whether learning validation is one coherent responsibility or an unnecessary collection of duplicated checks.

Validation shall be reviewed across:

* dataset validity;
* feature validity;
* experimental validity;
* model validity;
* reproducibility;
* explainability;
* uncertainty;
* publication readiness.

Where possible, validation responsibilities shall be consolidated under one constitutional qualification architecture.

Validation shall not duplicate independent audit or enterprise certification authority.

---

# Learning Evidence Challenge

Challenge whether Enterprise Learning evidence responsibility is complete but narrowly defined.

Verify responsibility for evidence generated by:

* dataset construction;
* feature generation;
* experimentation;
* model training;
* model evaluation;
* reproducibility;
* explainability;
* publication;
* retirement;
* supersession.

Determine whether the office improperly claims ownership of:

* historical evidence;
* enterprise truth records;
* Performance Truth records;
* external audit evidence;
* custody records owned by the Historian.

Learning evidence ownership shall remain distinct from permanent historical custody.

---

# Reproducibility Qualification Challenge

Challenge whether reproducibility qualification belongs within Enterprise Learning or should be entirely delegated to audit.

Enterprise Learning shall remain responsible for preparing and internally qualifying the reproducibility of its products.

Independent certification shall remain outside the office.

The review shall distinguish:

* internal reproducibility qualification;
* independent reproducibility certification;
* historical reproduction;
* operational replay.

These responsibilities shall not be conflated.

---

# Explainability Qualification Challenge

Challenge whether explainability is a separate responsibility or an inseparable publication requirement.

Determine whether explainability should be treated as:

* a standalone learning product;
* a model qualification requirement;
* a publication disclosure requirement;
* a combination of these categories.

The architecture shall avoid duplicate explainability ownership across model governance, validation, and publication.

Explainability shall remain proportional to product purpose and potential enterprise consequence.

---

# Uncertainty Responsibility Challenge

Challenge whether uncertainty disclosure is adequately decomposed.

Verify responsibility for uncertainty arising from:

* source data;
* dataset selection;
* labels;
* features;
* experiment design;
* model estimation;
* generalization;
* causal interpretation;
* publication scope.

Determine whether one unified uncertainty architecture can replace fragmented responsibilities.

Enterprise Learning shall characterize uncertainty but shall not convert uncertainty into Risk authority.

---

# Publication Responsibility Challenge

Challenge the publication lifecycle for unnecessary authority.

Verify that Enterprise Learning may:

* qualify learning products;
* assign versions;
* define intended use;
* define prohibited use;
* attach evidence;
* disclose assumptions;
* disclose uncertainty;
* publish;
* restrict;
* withdraw;
* retire;
* supersede.

Verify that publication does not authorize:

* enterprise execution;
* workflow initiation outside the office;
* decision adoption;
* risk acceptance;
* truth certification;
* mandatory operational use.

Publication shall mean release of qualified knowledge only.

---

# Monitoring Qualification Challenge

Challenge whether learning-product monitoring is a constitutional responsibility of Enterprise Learning or merely an interface obligation.

Enterprise Learning may define:

* monitored assumptions;
* model-performance indicators;
* drift criteria;
* degradation criteria;
* withdrawal thresholds.

Monitoring shall remain responsible for enterprise monitoring execution where constitutionally assigned.

The review shall prevent duplication between:

* learning-product qualification;
* ongoing enterprise observation;
* performance certification;
* operational alerting.

---

# Retirement and Supersession Challenge

Challenge whether retirement, withdrawal, invalidation, and supersession require separate responsibilities.

Determine the simplest lifecycle capable of preserving:

* product validity;
* historical continuity;
* consumer protection;
* replacement relationships;
* auditability.

Redundant terminal states shall be consolidated unless they possess materially distinct constitutional consequences.

Deletion shall not replace retirement or supersession.

---

# Historical Registration Challenge

Challenge whether submission of learning artifacts to the Historian is a responsibility, interface obligation, or custody-transfer event.

Enterprise Learning shall be responsible for submitting required artifacts and lifecycle events.

The Historian shall remain responsible for:

* acceptance into historical custody;
* historical registration;
* historical ordering;
* historical provenance;
* immutable preservation.

Enterprise Learning shall not claim historical-registration authority.

---

# Responsibility Independence Test

Each responsibility shall be tested for independence from:

* Historian custody;
* Commander authority;
* Analyst interpretation;
* Risk authority;
* Trader execution;
* Performance Truth certification;
* Monitoring observation;
* Librarian cataloging;
* enterprise audit;
* enterprise certification.

A responsibility that cannot operate without assuming another office’s authority shall be redesigned.

Dependency upon an interface shall not imply dependency upon another office’s internal implementation.

---

# Responsibility Duplication Test

The review shall identify duplication where two or more responsibilities:

* govern the same object;
* produce the same constitutional output;
* exercise the same lifecycle authority;
* require the same evidence;
* perform the same validation;
* publish the same conclusion;
* control the same interface.

Duplicated responsibilities shall be consolidated under one owner.

Interface participation shall not be mistaken for shared ownership.

---

# Missing Responsibility Test

The review shall determine whether the architecture lacks responsibility for:

* learning-object identity;
* method registration;
* assumptions;
* limitations;
* contradictory evidence;
* failed experiments;
* product defects;
* publication correction;
* consumer notification;
* use restrictions;
* dependency qualification;
* version compatibility.

Missing responsibilities shall be added only when necessary to close a constitutional gap.

No addition shall expand the office into truth, decision, execution, or historical authority.

---

# Hidden Authority Test

Every responsibility shall be examined for language that could imply authority to:

* declare truth;
* direct enterprise action;
* approve operational use;
* override decision owners;
* alter history;
* certify performance;
* determine risk acceptance;
* compel workflow execution;
* suppress contradictory evidence;
* conceal uncertainty.

Hidden authority shall be removed explicitly.

---

# Determinism Test

Every retained responsibility shall define:

* governed object;
* triggering condition;
* responsible owner;
* lifecycle authority;
* required inputs;
* permitted outputs;
* evidence obligation;
* interface dependencies;
* completion condition;
* prohibited actions.

A responsibility that depends upon undocumented discretion shall fail the review.

---

# Consolidation Review

The campaign shall evaluate whether the constitutional responsibility model can be simplified into the following coherent domains:

1. Learning Object Governance;
2. Scientific Evaluation;
3. Learning Qualification;
4. Learning Publication;
5. Learning Lifecycle Preservation.

The review shall determine whether this consolidation preserves all necessary responsibilities without creating hidden authority.

Consolidation shall be adopted only where ownership and certification remain explicit.

---

# Responsibility Decision Outcomes

Each responsibility shall receive one of the following dispositions:

* Retain Unchanged;
* Retain with Clarification;
* Consolidate;
* Divide;
* Transfer;
* Remove;
* Add as Missing Responsibility;
* Prohibit Explicitly;
* Escalate for Constitutional Resolution.

Every disposition shall include a documented constitutional rationale.

---

# Required Findings

The review shall produce findings for:

* unnecessary responsibilities;
* missing responsibilities;
* overlapping responsibilities;
* duplicate validation;
* duplicate lifecycle authority;
* hidden decision authority;
* hidden truth authority;
* hidden execution authority;
* hidden custody authority;
* fragmented scientific governance;
* fragmented publication governance;
* excessive responsibility coupling;
* ambiguous ownership.

Absence of findings shall require affirmative evidence rather than assumption.

---

# Modification Requirements

Where defects are identified, the order shall define precise modifications to:

* responsibility statements;
* object ownership;
* lifecycle authority;
* interface obligations;
* publication authority;
* evidence requirements;
* prohibited responsibilities;
* constitutional terminology.

Modifications shall reduce ambiguity and complexity without weakening constitutional safeguards.

---

# Independent Verification

An independent reviewer shall verify that:

* every retained responsibility is necessary;
* every responsibility possesses one unique purpose;
* no two responsibilities exercise the same authority;
* all required learning functions remain covered;
* no constitutional gap remains;
* no hidden authority remains;
* responsibility boundaries are independently understandable;
* responsibilities remain separable from other offices;
* the resulting architecture is simpler than the reviewed baseline.

Verification shall evaluate constitutional architecture rather than implementation behavior.

---

Execution of this order shall produce:

* complete responsibility inventory;
* responsibility classification register;
* necessity assessment;
* completeness assessment;
* duplication assessment;
* independence assessment;
* determinism assessment;
* hidden-authority assessment;
* missing-responsibility register;
* responsibility consolidation analysis;

* proposed constitutional modifications;
* Responsibility Decomposition Assessment.

---

# Completion Criteria

This order shall be complete only when:

* every Enterprise Learning responsibility has been individually challenged;
* every retained responsibility possesses one unique constitutional purpose;
* unnecessary responsibilities have been removed or recommended for removal;
* duplicate responsibilities have been consolidated;
* missing responsibilities have been identified;
* hidden decision, truth, execution, custody, and certification authority has been eliminated;
* responsibility ownership is deterministic;
* interface obligations are not mistaken for shared ownership;
* scientific governance is complete without unnecessary fragmentation;
* publication governance is complete without execution authority;
* no unresolved responsibility overlap remains;
* the resulting responsibility architecture is constitutionally simpler, more cohesive, and independently certifiable.
