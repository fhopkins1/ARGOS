# ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-006

# Learning Lifecycle Challenge

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive constitutional challenge of the learning lifecycle governing every artifact owned by the Enterprise Learning Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether the constitutional lifecycle established under **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-RM-001** represents the simplest, most deterministic, most scientifically rigorous, and most constitutionally complete lifecycle capable of governing enterprise learning.

Rather than validating the lifecycle, this order shall aggressively challenge every lifecycle state, transition, authority boundary, ownership assignment, publication mechanism, and retirement process.

The objective is to eliminate unnecessary lifecycle complexity while preserving constitutional guarantees.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order is complete only when:

* every lifecycle state has been challenged under the assumption that it may be unnecessary or incomplete;
* every transition possesses explicit constitutional authority or a documented constitutional finding;
* scientific governance has been evaluated for completeness and rigor;
* publication, retirement, and supersession preserve constitutional history and reproducibility;
* lifecycle determinism has been independently assessed;
* lifecycle scalability has been evaluated for long-term enterprise operation;
* opportunities for constitutional simplification have been identified or constitutionally disproven;
* the resulting lifecycle is demonstrably simpler, more deterministic, more scientifically rigorous, or confirmed to already represent the minimal constitutionally complete lifecycle for Enterprise Learning.

Completion of this order authorizes progression to **ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-007 — Explainability and Scientific Governance Challenge**.
