## ENTERPRISE-LEARNING-MO-001-003

### Ownership Boundary Challenge

#### Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive constitutional review of every enterprise object owned by the Enterprise Learning Office to determine whether ownership boundaries are complete, deterministic, minimal, and constitutionally justified.

The objective is to verify that Enterprise Learning owns only those objects necessary to fulfill its constitutional mission while eliminating hidden authority, overlapping ownership, and unnecessary custody responsibilities.

No modification shall weaken constitutional separation or transfer ownership without explicit constitutional justification.

---

### Constitutional Review Objectives

The review shall challenge the constitutional ownership of:

* learning datasets;
* feature definitions;
* feature stores;
* hypotheses;
* experiments;
* experiment evidence;
* trained models;
* model metadata;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty artifacts;
* publication packages;
* learning evidence;
* publication lifecycle records.

Each ownership assignment shall be independently justified.

---

### Ownership Authority Review

For every constitutional object, determine whether Enterprise Learning requires authority to:

* create;
* modify;
* validate;
* approve;
* publish;
* supersede;
* retire;
* archive.

Any authority lacking constitutional necessity shall be recommended for removal.

---

### Publication Authority Review

Verify that publication authority is limited exclusively to learning products including:

* hypotheses;
* predictive models;
* causal models;
* anomaly models;
* explainability artifacts;
* uncertainty estimates;
* supporting learning documentation.

Determine whether publication authority extends beyond enterprise knowledge into enterprise decision-making.

Any publication authority capable of directing enterprise behavior shall constitute a constitutional defect.

---

### Lifecycle Authority Review

Challenge Enterprise Learning ownership of lifecycle governance including:

* dataset creation;
* feature generation;
* experiment execution governance;
* model publication;
* model retirement;
* model supersession;
* publication retirement.

Determine whether lifecycle authority belongs exclusively to Enterprise Learning or should be constitutionally reassigned.

---

### Custody Boundary Review

Verify constitutional custody of:

* learning repositories;
* model repositories;
* experiment repositories;
* feature repositories;
* explainability repositories;
* publication repositories.

Determine whether custody duplicates responsibilities constitutionally assigned to:

* Historian;
* Librarian;
* Performance Truth;
* other enterprise offices.

Custody shall never imply ownership of historical truth.

---

### Modification Authority Review

Verify that Enterprise Learning possesses modification authority only over:

* learning products;
* learning metadata;
* learning evidence;
* publication records;
* internal lifecycle metadata.

Enterprise Learning shall not possess authority to modify:

* historical records;
* provenance;
* workflow execution;
* operational decisions;
* enterprise truth;
* performance records.

---

### Ownership Boundary Analysis

Evaluate every ownership assignment for:

* constitutional necessity;
* determinism;
* exclusivity;
* auditability;
* lifecycle consistency;
* enterprise scalability.

Every owned object shall possess one constitutional owner and one clearly defined authority boundary.

---

### Cross-Office Ownership Verification

Verify that ownership boundaries remain constitutionally separated from:

**Historian**

* historical truth;
* provenance;
* immutable records;
* custody history.

**Performance Truth**

* objective performance outcomes;
* performance certification;
* measurement truth.

**Commander**

* enterprise priorities;
* strategic objectives;
* execution intent.

**Analyst**

* analytical reasoning;
* operational interpretation.

**Risk**

* risk ownership;
* exposure assessment;
* policy enforcement.

**Trader**

* execution decisions;
* transaction authority.

**Monitoring**

* runtime observation;
* operational monitoring.

**Librarian**

* enterprise publication storage;
* document preservation;
* enterprise knowledge distribution.

No ownership overlap shall exist.

---

### Ownership Simplification Review

Determine whether constitutional ownership may be simplified by identifying:

* duplicate ownership domains;
* unnecessary custodial responsibilities;
* redundant publication authority;
* unnecessary lifecycle ownership;
* excessive governance objects.

Every simplification shall preserve constitutional completeness.

---

### Constitutional Defect Identification

Identify constitutional defects including:

* shared ownership;
* hidden ownership;
* undocumented ownership;
* conflicting lifecycle authority;
* overlapping custody;
* publication ambiguity;
* modification authority creep.

Every identified defect shall produce a recommended constitutional correction.

---

### Constitutional Verification

Verify that:

* every constitutional object possesses one exclusive owner;
* ownership authority matches constitutional mission;
* custody aligns with ownership without expanding authority;
* publication authority governs knowledge rather than enterprise decisions;
* lifecycle authority remains deterministic;
* ownership boundaries remain independent of implementation details.

---

### Deliverables

Completion of this review shall produce:

* Ownership Boundary Assessment;
* Publication Authority Assessment;
* Lifecycle Authority Assessment;
* Custody Boundary Assessment;
* Modification Authority Assessment;
* Cross-Office Ownership Matrix;
* Ownership Simplification Report;
* Constitutional Defect Register;
* Ownership Correction Recommendations.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when independent constitutional review demonstrates that:

* every Enterprise Learning object has a single, deterministic constitutional owner;
* ownership, custody, publication authority, lifecycle authority, and modification authority are complete, mutually exclusive, and independently auditable;
* no ownership overlaps with the Historian, Performance Truth, Commander, Analyst, Risk, Trader, Monitoring, or Librarian Offices;
* unnecessary ownership and governance have been eliminated while preserving constitutional completeness;
* the Enterprise Learning Office retains only the minimum ownership necessary to fulfill its constitutional mission.
