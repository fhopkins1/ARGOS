EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01-003

# Decision Authority and Execution Separation Constitution

## Objective

Establish the complete constitutional separation between Exit Decision evaluation authority, recommendation authority, decision authority, authorization authority, execution authority, settlement authority, reconciliation authority, and closed-position truth authority.

This order shall eliminate every ambiguity where a decision could be interpreted as execution authority, where authorization could be interpreted as ownership, or where execution could be interpreted as truth ownership.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every authority shall have one constitutional owner.

Every transition shall have one governing authority.

Every truth domain shall retain one authoritative custodian.

---

# Constitutional Principles

A decision shall not equal execution.

A recommendation shall not equal authorization.

An authorization shall not equal broker execution.

A broker execution shall not equal settled position truth.

A settled position shall not equal historical truth.

A historical record shall not equal performance truth.

Exit Decision shall determine whether an exit condition satisfies the constitutional criteria for producing an exit decision outcome.

Exit Decision shall not perform actions owned by authorization, trading, brokerage, position custody, settlement, reconciliation, or historical custody authorities.

No office may consume decision authority as a substitute for execution authority.

No office may consume execution authority as a substitute for truth ownership.

---

# Exit Decision Authority Constitution

## Evaluation Authority

Exit Decision shall possess authority to:

* evaluate authorized exit conditions;
* evaluate decision criteria;
* evaluate available enterprise truth inputs;
* determine whether exit criteria are satisfied;
* determine whether insufficient information prevents a decision.

Exit Decision shall not create:

* market truth;
* risk truth;
* authorization truth;
* position truth;
* performance truth;
* historical truth.

---

## Recommendation Authority

Exit Decision shall possess authority to:

* generate exit recommendations;
* document recommendation rationale;
* identify supporting evidence;
* identify uncertainty;
* defer recommendation when conditions are insufficient.

A recommendation shall not compel execution.

A recommendation shall not create authorization.

A recommendation shall not modify enterprise state.

---

## Decision Authority

Exit Decision shall possess authority to:

* issue constitutionally valid exit decisions;
* approve or reject exit conditions within assigned scope;
* defer decisions;
* cancel previously issued decisions where permitted;
* request required escalation.

An Exit Decision shall represent an enterprise decision outcome only.

An Exit Decision shall not represent:

* a broker instruction;
* an executed trade;
* a closed position;
* a settlement event.

---

# Exit Decision Non-Authority Constitution

Exit Decision shall not possess authority to:

* execute trades;
* submit broker orders;
* directly control Trader execution;
* directly communicate execution instructions unless through approved constitutional interfaces;
* create or modify authorizations;
* override Risk controls;
* override Authorization restrictions;
* mutate Position Registry state;
* modify Closed Position Truth;
* modify Historian records;
* modify Performance Truth;
* replace Commander authority.

# Risk Separation Constitution

Risk shall remain the sole authority for:

* risk calculation;
* risk constraints;
* exposure truth;
* risk acceptance;
* risk rejection.

Exit Decision may consume Risk outputs.

Exit Decision shall not:

* modify Risk outputs;
* suppress Risk constraints;
* reinterpret Risk ownership;
* create substitute risk truth.

Any disagreement between Exit Decision and Risk shall enter constitutional escalation.

---

# Authorization Separation Constitution

Authorizations shall remain the sole authority for:

* authorization issuance;
* authorization validation;
* authorization lifecycle;
* authorization expiration;
* authorization revocation.

Exit Decision may request execution only through valid authorization pathways.

Exit Decision shall not:

* self-authorize;
* extend authorization;
* consume expired authorization;
* bypass authorization validation.

---

# Trader Separation Constitution

Trader shall remain the sole authority for:

* execution preparation;
* execution submission;
* execution management;
* trade lifecycle control.

Exit Decision may provide an approved decision object.

Trader shall determine execution behavior within authorized constraints.

Exit Decision shall not:

* submit orders;
* modify executions;
* force execution outcomes;
* assume trader custody.

---

# Broker Separation Constitution

Broker shall remain the sole authority for:

* external market submission;
* broker acknowledgement;
* broker execution state;
* settlement communication.

Exit Decision shall not:

* communicate directly as a broker substitute;
* interpret broker state as position truth;
* modify broker records.

---

# Position Registry Separation Constitution

Position Registry shall remain the sole authority for:

* position identity;
* position state;
* position ownership;
* position mutation;
* position reconciliation.

Exit Decision shall consume position truth.

Exit Decision shall not:

* modify positions;
* close positions;
* declare positions closed;
* replace reconciliation authority.

---

# Historian Separation Constitution

Historian shall remain the sole authority for:

* immutable historical custody;
* event lineage;
* historical replay;
* archival integrity.

Exit Decision shall produce decision evidence.

Exit Decision shall not own historical custody.

---

# Emergency Decision Pathway Constitution

Emergency pathways may permit accelerated decision processing only.

Emergency pathways shall not permit:

* authorization bypass;
* risk bypass;
* execution bypass;
* position mutation bypass;
* historical custody bypass.

Emergency conditions shall preserve authority ownership.

Emergency authority shall define:

* activation criteria;
* authorized participants;
* duration;
* evidence requirements;
* reconciliation requirements.

---

# Decision-to-Execution Boundary Registry

The following boundaries shall remain mandatory:

| Boundary                    | Producer          | Consumer          | Separation Requirement            |
| --------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Evaluation → Recommendation | Exit Decision     | Exit Decision     | Internal decision process         |
| Recommendation → Decision   | Exit Decision     | Exit Decision     | Decision accountability preserved |
| Decision → Authorization    | Exit Decision     | Authorizations    | Authorization independent         |
| Authorization → Execution   | Authorizations    | Trader            | Execution constrained             |
| Execution → Broker          | Trader            | Broker            | Broker independence preserved     |
| Broker → Position Truth     | Broker/Settlement | Position Registry | Truth custody preserved           |
| Position Truth → History    | Position Registry | Historian         | Historical custody preserved      |

---

# Prohibited Authority Registry

The following combinations are constitutionally prohibited:

* Exit Decision + Broker Execution Authority;
* Exit Decision + Authorization Creation Authority;
* Exit Decision + Risk Override Authority;
* Exit Decision + Position Mutation Authority;
* Exit Decision + Historical Mutation Authority;
* Exit Decision + Performance Truth Authority.

No future implementation may combine these authorities without constitutional amendment.
