EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01-004

# Escalation, Conflict Resolution, and Governance Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional governance model for conflicts, overrides, escalations, exceptional conditions, and unresolved decision states involving the Exit Decision Office.

This order shall define deterministic escalation authority, conflict resolution authority, precedence rules, exception handling, and final reconciliation ownership.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify Exit Decision implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not perform certification activity.
* Not grant unauthorized execution authority.
* Not permit Exit Decision to bypass Risk, Authorizations, Trader, Broker, Position Registry, or Historian authority.

This order shall establish only constitutional governance.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

## 1. Escalation Authority Constitution

Establish that escalation exists when:

* constitutional authority ownership is ambiguous;
* decision inputs conflict;
* required dependencies are unavailable;
* authorization state is inconsistent;
* risk constraints conflict with requested exit action;
* execution state cannot be reconciled;
* historical truth conflicts with operational records;
* emergency conditions require elevated review.

Define:

* escalation initiator;
* escalation recipient;
* escalation priority;
* escalation timing;
* escalation evidence requirements;
* escalation closure authority.

---

# 2. Escalation Authority Ownership

Establish:

## Exit Decision Office

Owns:

* escalation initiation regarding exit decision ambiguity;
* escalation of unresolved decision conflicts;
* escalation of incomplete exit condition evaluation;
* escalation of decision-object integrity issues.

Does not own:

* risk resolution;
* authorization issuance;
* execution resolution;
* position truth correction;
* historical truth correction.

---

## Commander Office

Owns:

* enterprise priority conflicts;
* mission intent conflicts;
* strategic precedence decisions;
* final enterprise direction when constitutional authority permits.

Commander shall not:

* directly mutate operational truth;
* bypass authorization controls;
* bypass risk controls;
* execute broker actions.

---

## Risk Office

Owns:

* risk constraint conflicts;
* risk acceptance decisions;
* risk-state interpretation;
* risk truth correction.

Risk shall not:

* issue exit decisions;
* authorize execution;
* execute trades.

---

## Authorizations Office

Owns:

* authorization validity conflicts;
* authorization issuance;
* authorization revocation;
* authorization expiry interpretation.

Authorizations shall not:

* determine exit necessity;
* execute trades;
* modify risk truth.

---

## Trader Office

Owns:

* execution request handling;
* trade lifecycle management;
* execution-state reconciliation.

Trader shall not:

* override Exit Decision authority;
* create unauthorized exit decisions;
* bypass authorization requirements.

---

## Broker Office

Owns:

* external broker interaction;
* broker acknowledgment state;
* execution communication integrity.

Broker shall not:

* determine enterprise exit intent;
* create decision authority;
* modify position truth.

---

## Position Registry Office

Owns:

* position identity;
* position state;
* position mutation;
* reconciliation of position records.

Position Registry shall not:

* determine exit decisions;
* authorize execution.

---

## Historian Office

Owns:

* immutable historical custody;
* historical replay;
* audit reconstruction.

Historian shall not:

* alter operational truth;
* resolve live operational conflicts.

---

# 3. Conflict Resolution Constitution

## Decision Conflict

When Exit Decision outputs conflict with another office:

Resolution order:

1. Validate constitutional authority ownership.
2. Validate input provenance.
3. Validate authorization state.
4. Validate risk constraints.
5. Escalate unresolved authority conflicts.

No office may resolve a conflict by assuming another office's authority.

---

# 5. Emergency Governance Constitution

Emergency conditions may permit accelerated decision pathways.

Emergency pathways shall:

* preserve authorization separation;
* preserve risk ownership;
* preserve execution ownership;
* preserve historical custody;
* require complete post-event reconciliation.

Emergency authority shall not create permanent authority expansion.

---

# 6. Suspension Authority

Define:

The following may suspend progression within their constitutional domain:

* Exit Decision — decision generation when required inputs are invalid;
* Risk — actions violating risk constraints;
* Authorizations — actions lacking valid authorization;
* Trader — execution when execution prerequisites fail;
* Broker — external communication when broker conditions prevent safe execution.

Suspension shall not equal cancellation.

---

# 7. Cancellation Authority

Define:

Cancellation authority belongs to:

* Exit Decision for pending exit decisions not yet authorized;
* Authorizations for invalid or revoked authorization;
* Trader for unexecuted execution requests within Trader custody;
* Broker for rejected external instructions;
* Position Registry for invalid position state corrections.

No office may cancel another office's owned truth object without constitutional authority.

---

# 8. Reopening Authority

Define reopening conditions:

* new authoritative evidence;
* corrected dependency state;
* invalidated prior decision basis;
* unresolved reconciliation failure.

Reopening shall require:

* reason capture;
* authority identification;
* evidence preservation;
* historical record.

---

# 9. Final Reconciliation Authority

Establish:

Final reconciliation responsibility shall be distributed by domain:

* Exit Decision reconciles decision-state consistency.
* Authorizations reconciles authorization-state consistency.
* Trader reconciles execution-state consistency.
* Broker reconciles broker communication state.
* Position Registry reconciles position-state consistency.
* Historian reconciles historical completeness.

No single office may claim ownership of enterprise-wide truth.
