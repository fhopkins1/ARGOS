# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B01-002

# Enterprise Boundary and Responsibility Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional boundaries between the Exit Decision Office and every interacting enterprise office.

This order exists solely to define ownership, custody, authority direction, dependency relationships, mutation authority, correction authority, reconciliation authority, and evidence responsibility across all Exit Decision interactions.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every interaction shall possess one governing constitutional authority.

Every responsibility shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every custody obligation shall possess one constitutional custodian.

Every mutation authority shall possess one constitutional source.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Exit Decision shall never assume ownership of enterprise truth owned by another office.

Exit Decision shall consume authorized enterprise truth.

Exit Decision shall evaluate exit conditions using constitutionally admitted inputs.

Exit Decision shall produce exit decisions within its authorized boundary.

Exit Decision shall not create, alter, replace, or override truth domains owned by other offices.

No dependency shall imply ownership.

No consumption relationship shall imply authority transfer.

No recommendation relationship shall imply execution authority.

No execution relationship shall imply truth ownership.

No reconciliation responsibility shall imply mutation authority.

# 3. Office Boundary Definitions

## Commander

Define:

* Commander owns enterprise intent;
* Commander owns strategic directives;
* Commander does not own Exit Decision outputs;
* Commander does not directly perform Exit Decision evaluation.

Exit Decision shall consume Commander intent only through authorized interfaces.

---

## Monitoring

Define:

* Monitoring owns operational observation outputs;
* Monitoring owns monitoring truth;
* Monitoring does not own exit decisions;
* Exit Decision may consume monitoring-derived conditions.

Exit Decision shall not modify monitoring truth.

# 6. Boundary Conflict Identification

Identify and resolve:

## Shared Responsibility

Every shared responsibility shall be classified as:

* shared input;
* shared review;
* shared escalation;
* shared evidence;
* shared reconciliation.

Shared authority shall be prohibited unless explicitly authorized.

---

## Duplicated Authority

Identify any duplicate authority involving:

* exit decision creation;
* execution approval;
* risk override;
* authorization approval;
* position mutation;
* historical modification.

Each duplicate authority shall be eliminated or assigned precedence.

---

## Missing Ownership

Identify any responsibility without:

* owner;
* custodian;
* correction authority;
* reconciliation authority.

All missing ownership shall be resolved before completion.

---

## Circular Authority

Identify any circular dependency where:

* one office requires authority from another;
* the second office requires authority from the first;
* neither possesses final constitutional ownership.

All circular authority shall be eliminated unless explicitly authorized.
