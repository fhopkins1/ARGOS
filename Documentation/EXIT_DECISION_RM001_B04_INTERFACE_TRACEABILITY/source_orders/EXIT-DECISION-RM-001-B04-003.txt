EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B04-003

# Evidence and Historical Integrity Constitution

## Objective

Establish the complete constitutional governance model for Exit Decision evidence creation, ownership, custody, provenance, admissibility, retention, correction lineage, supersession lineage, and historical integrity.

This order shall define how Exit Decision evidence is generated, preserved, validated, reconciled, and transferred without allowing evidence ownership to become decision ownership, execution ownership, or truth ownership belonging to another office.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every evidence object shall possess one constitutional owner.

Every historical record shall possess one constitutional custodian.

Every evidence event shall preserve lineage.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Evidence shall originate from constitutional events.

Evidence shall not exist solely as assertion.

Evidence shall not replace source truth.

Evidence shall not create authority.

Evidence shall not transfer ownership.

Evidence shall not modify lifecycle state without authorized transition authority.

Every evidence object shall possess:

* one canonical identity;
* one producer;
* one owner;
* one custodian;
* one provenance chain;
* one admissibility basis;
* one retention obligation.

Evidence shall preserve:

* origin;
* creation authority;
* temporal context;
* relationship to the governed object;
* lifecycle position;
* correction history;
* supersession history.

Historical custody shall remain separate from operational ownership.

Historian shall preserve records.

Historian shall not alter decision authority.

---

# Evidence Authority Constitution

## Evidence Ownership Principle

The producer of an event shall create the evidence record associated with that event.

The owner of the underlying truth domain shall remain the owner of that truth.

Evidence custody shall not transfer truth ownership.

Examples:

* Exit Decision owns decision evidence.
* Risk owns risk evidence.
* Authorizations owns authorization evidence.
* Trader owns execution evidence.
* Broker owns broker interaction evidence.
* Position Registry owns position evidence.
* Historian owns historical custody.

# Evidence Admissibility Constitution

Evidence shall be admissible only when:

* producer identity is known;
* ownership is known;
* provenance is complete;
* temporal information exists;
* relationship to governed object is established;
* integrity requirements are satisfied.

Evidence shall be rejected when:

* origin is unknown;
* ownership is ambiguous;
* provenance is incomplete;
* evidence is manually asserted without source support;
* evidence duplicates conflicting records without reconciliation;
* evidence attempts to replace another office's truth.

---

# Prohibited Evidence Registry

The following evidence forms are constitutionally insufficient:

## Metadata-Only Evidence

Evidence containing only identifiers, timestamps, or status fields without supporting event context.

## Completion-Report-Only Evidence

A report claiming completion without underlying event records.

## Manually Asserted Evidence

Evidence created without originating authority or event source.

## Synthetic Evidence

Evidence generated solely to satisfy certification requirements.

## Circular Evidence

Evidence validated only by evidence derived from itself.

---

# Evidence Integrity Constitution

Every evidence object shall preserve:

* immutability after acceptance;
* identity continuity;
* provenance continuity;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* temporal integrity.

Corrections shall create new evidence records.

Corrections shall never overwrite accepted evidence.

Supersession shall create successor relationships.

Supersession shall never delete predecessor records.

---

# Historical Custody Constitution

Historian shall possess custody authority for:

* accepted evidence records;
* lifecycle history;
* decision lineage;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* archival records.

Historian shall not:

* create operational truth;
* modify decision outcomes;
* authorize execution;
* alter evidence ownership.

Historical custody shall preserve:

```text
Event
→ Evidence
→ Object
→ Lifecycle State
→ Decision Lineage
→ Final Record
```

---

# Retention Constitution

Evidence retention shall preserve:

* regulatory requirements where applicable;
* constitutional lineage;
* audit requirements;
* replay requirements;
* reconciliation requirements.

Evidence shall remain available for:

* audit;
* reconstruction;
* dispute resolution;
* certification review.

---

# Evidence Reconciliation Constitution

Evidence conflicts shall require:

* conflict identification;
* source authority determination;
* ownership verification;
* reconciliation decision;
* preserved resolution record.

Conflicting evidence shall never be silently merged.

No evidence conflict may result in loss of historical lineage.
