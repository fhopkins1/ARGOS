# EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B04-002

# Temporal, Freshness, Ordering, and Replay Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional temporal governance for the Exit Decision Office.

This order exists solely to define the authoritative temporal model governing every Exit Decision event, state transition, decision validity period, freshness requirement, ordering rule, replay behavior, duplicate handling rule, and temporal reconciliation obligation.

No implementation behavior shall be modified under this order.

No behavioral verification shall occur under this order.

No implementation proof shall be generated under this order.

No certification activity shall occur under this order.

Every temporal event shall possess one defined meaning.

Every timestamp shall possess one defined authority.

Every freshness requirement shall possess one governing owner.

Every replay behavior shall preserve constitutional truth.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Time shall never be inferred.

Freshness shall never be assumed.

Ordering shall never be assumed.

A timestamp shall not imply authority.

A newer record shall not automatically replace an authoritative record.

A delayed event shall not create a new truth state without constitutional authority.

Replay shall reproduce historical decisions without altering historical truth.

Duplicate events shall preserve lineage.

Expired decisions shall not silently reactivate.

Cancelled decisions shall not silently reactivate.

Superseded decisions shall remain historically preserved.

Execution timing shall not alter Exit Decision ownership.
