EXIT-DECISION-RM-001-B04-004

# Canonical Requirement Identity and Traceability Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional requirement identity, classification, ownership, and traceability model governing the Exit Decision Office.

This order shall create deterministic lineage between constitutional authority, requirements, objects, lifecycle obligations, admissibility obligations, decision obligations, interface obligations, evidence obligations, and future certification obligations.

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify Exit Decision implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not perform certification activity.
* Not establish implementation test requirements.
* Not replace ECS-003 certification governance.
* Not create requirements without constitutional authority.
* Not permit duplicate requirement ownership.
* Not permit orphan constitutional obligations.
* Not permit aggregate requirements without decomposition.

This order shall establish constitutional traceability only.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Canonical Requirement Identity Constitution

Establish that every constitutional requirement shall possess:

* one canonical identity;
* one originating authority;
* one constitutional source;
* one owner;
* one applicability boundary;
* one lifecycle relationship;
* one evidence implication;
* one certification implication.

No requirement shall exist without:

* authoritative origin;
* defined purpose;
* ownership;
* traceability relationship.

---

# 2. Requirement Identity Registry

Create the canonical requirement identity model:

```text
EXIT-DECISION-REQ-[DOMAIN]-[NUMBER]
```

Each requirement identity shall define:

* requirement identifier;
* requirement title;
* originating doctrine;
* originating order;
* requirement statement;
* requirement owner;
* affected objects;
* affected lifecycle states;
* affected interfaces;
* evidence obligations;
* certification disposition.

---

# 3. Requirement Source Lineage Constitution

Every requirement shall trace to:

```text
Constitutional Authority
        ↓
Doctrine Order
        ↓
Canonical Requirement
        ↓
Operational Obligation
        ↓
Evidence Obligation
        ↓
Certification Obligation
```

No requirement may bypass constitutional lineage.

No implementation artifact may become the source of constitutional authority.

---

# 4. Atomic Requirement Constitution

Establish:

A canonical requirement shall represent one independently governed obligation.

Aggregate requirements shall be decomposed into atomic requirements.

Each atomic requirement shall possess:

* unique identity;
* unique owner;
* unique authority source;
* unique applicability;
* unique traceability path.

Examples of prohibited aggregate requirements:

* "Exit Decision shall be correct."
* "Exit Decision shall be secure."
* "Exit Decision shall operate properly."

These shall be decomposed into individually governed requirements.
