# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B04

# Certification Evidence Finalization

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the final certification evidence package governing the permanent certification record of the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to independently assemble, validate, reconcile, and preserve every artifact produced throughout the constitutional remediation, implementation certification, and enterprise certification campaigns into a single immutable certification package that permanently demonstrates constitutional compliance, implementation conformance, and enterprise readiness.

This order certifies certification evidence only.

It shall not introduce new implementation behavior or modify any constitutional requirement.

This order shall be considered complete when independent audit demonstrates that:

5. integrity verification confirms the certification package is complete, internally consistent, and free from corruption or unresolved discrepancies;
6. the Certification Evidence Package is permanently preserved as the immutable constitutional certification record for the Performance Truth Office; and
7. the Performance Truth Office is certified as ready to proceed to **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B05 Constitutional Freeze and Operational Transition**.
