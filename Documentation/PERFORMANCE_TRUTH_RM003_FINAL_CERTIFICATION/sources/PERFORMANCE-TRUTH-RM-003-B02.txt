# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B02

# Enterprise Integration Certification

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the final enterprise integration certification campaign for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this certification is to independently verify that the Performance Truth Office integrates correctly with every constitutionally dependent office, that enterprise-wide behavior remains deterministic, and that constitutional guarantees are preserved across all inter-office boundaries.

Successful completion of this order certifies that the Performance Truth Office functions as a fully integrated constitutional component of the ARGOS Enterprise.

This order certifies enterprise integration only.

It shall not introduce new functionality or modify constitutional requirements.

---

This certification derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine
* Truth Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine
* Audit Doctrine
* Enterprise Certification Doctrine

Where implementation differs from constitutional doctrine, constitutional doctrine shall prevail.

# Constitutional Principles

Enterprise integration shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Every office shall retain exclusive constitutional ownership of its business objects.

2. No interface shall alter constitutional meaning.

3. Enterprise behavior shall remain deterministic.

4. Every cross-office transaction shall be completely traceable.

5. Every enterprise interaction shall generate constitutional evidence.

6. Interface failures shall fail closed.

7. No office shall bypass constitutional interfaces.

8. Enterprise replay shall reproduce identical cross-office behavior.

9. Constitutional authority shall never transfer implicitly.

10. Enterprise integration shall preserve historical truth.

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Successful constitutional integration has been independently verified with every dependent office.
2. Every enterprise interface satisfies its constitutional contract.
3. Constitutional ownership boundaries remain fully enforced across all inter-office interactions.
4. Enterprise-wide execution is deterministic and independently reproducible.
5. Complete cross-office traceability has been demonstrated for every published Performance Truth Record.
6. Every enterprise interaction generates complete, immutable constitutional evidence.
7. Interface failures, dependency failures, and ownership violations deterministically fail closed.
8. Independent auditors can reconstruct every enterprise interaction solely from repository artifacts and constitutional evidence.
9. No undocumented enterprise behavior or cross-office constitutional ambiguity remains.
10. The Performance Truth Office is certified as fully integrated with the ARGOS Enterprise and eligible to proceed to enterprise regression certification.
