# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B05

# Constitutional Freeze and Operational Transition

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the final constitutional certification, constitutional freeze, and operational transition governing the Performance Truth Office.

Its purpose is to permanently certify the Performance Truth Office as a constitutional component of the ARGOS Enterprise, establish the immutable constitutional baseline from which all future evolution shall proceed, and authorize transition of the certified office into enterprise operations.

This order concludes the complete Performance Truth remediation and certification campaign.

No new functionality shall be introduced under this order.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* Enterprise Certification Doctrine
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002
* Audit Doctrine
* Truth Doctrine
* Workflow Ownership Doctrine
* Evidence Doctrine

Nothing within this order may modify constitutional requirements.

---

# Certification Preconditions

Constitutional Freeze shall not be authorized unless independent audit confirms:

* completion of PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001;
* completion of PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002;
* completion of PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B01 through B04;
* resolution of every constitutional finding;
* resolution of every implementation finding;
* resolution of every enterprise integration finding;
* completion of all required certification evidence.

Any unresolved finding shall prohibit Constitutional Freeze.

# Constitutional Certification

The independent certification authority shall determine whether the Performance Truth Office satisfies every constitutional requirement established throughout the remediation campaign.

Certification shall verify:

* constitutional completeness;
* implementation completeness;
* deterministic behavior;
* interface conformance;
* evidence completeness;
* traceability completeness;
* reconciliation completeness;
* replay capability;
* fail-closed behavior;
* enterprise integration readiness.

Only complete constitutional compliance shall authorize certification.

# Freeze Declaration

Following establishment of the Constitutional Freeze Baseline, the Performance Truth Office shall be declared constitutionally frozen.

Following freeze:

* constitutional behavior shall remain immutable;
* certified implementation shall remain immutable;
* interface contracts shall remain immutable;
* certification evidence shall remain immutable;
* historical certification records shall remain immutable.

Direct modification of certified functionality is prohibited.

---

# Operational Authorization

Following Constitutional Freeze, the Performance Truth Office shall be authorized for enterprise operation.

Operational authorization confirms that the office may:

* calculate enterprise performance;
* publish certified Performance Truth Records;
* participate in enterprise workflows;
* participate in enterprise reconciliation;
* support enterprise audit;
* support enterprise replay;
* participate in enterprise certification activities.

Operational authorization shall not expand constitutional authority.

---

# Modification Governance

Following Constitutional Freeze, all future functional evolution shall occur exclusively through formally approved Modification Order (MO) campaigns.

Every Modification Order shall:

* identify the affected constitutional requirements;
* justify the proposed modification;
* assess enterprise impact;
* identify affected offices;
* specify implementation changes;
* require independent certification;
* establish a new certified baseline following approval.

Direct modification outside an approved MO campaign is prohibited.

The Constitutional Freeze and Operational Transition shall fail closed whenever:

* constitutional remediation remains incomplete;
* implementation certification remains incomplete;
* enterprise certification evidence is incomplete;
* unresolved audit findings remain;
* deterministic behavior cannot be demonstrated;
* certification evidence is missing;
* unauthorized implementation changes are detected;
* Constitutional Freeze Baseline cannot be reproduced;
* operational readiness cannot be independently verified.

Operational authorization shall not be granted until every certification prerequisite has been satisfied.
