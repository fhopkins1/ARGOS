# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003-B01

# Constitutional Compliance Verification

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This order establishes the Final Constitutional Compliance Verification for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this certification is to independently determine whether the Performance Truth Office fully satisfies every constitutional requirement established under **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001**, that all constitutional deficiencies identified throughout the remediation campaign have been resolved, and that no unresolved constitutional ambiguity remains.

This order constitutes the final constitutional review preceding enterprise integration certification.

This certification evaluates constitutional compliance only.

Implementation behavior, enterprise integration, regression testing, operational readiness, and constitutional freeze are governed by subsequent orders.

---

The certification shall independently determine that:

# Finding Severity

## Critical

Constitutional requirement is not satisfied.

Certification shall immediately fail.

## Major

Constitutional requirement is incomplete.

## Minor

Observations shall not affect certification status.

# Certification Decision

The certification shall produce exactly one constitutional compliance decision.

## PASS

Every constitutional requirement has been independently verified.

No unresolved Critical or Major findings remain.

The Performance Truth Office is authorized to proceed to Enterprise Integration Certification.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

All constitutional requirements are satisfied.

Minor findings remain and shall be resolved before Constitutional Freeze.

Enterprise Integration Certification may proceed only under approved certification conditions.

---

## FAIL

One or more constitutional requirements remain unsatisfied.

The office shall return to the appropriate **PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001** remediation order(s) before another Constitutional Compliance Verification may be performed.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

The certification shall immediately terminate with a **FAIL** decision whenever:

* any constitutional requirement cannot be verified;
* any constitutional invariant is violated;
* constitutional ownership is ambiguous;
* required evidence is incomplete;
* constitutional interfaces are undefined;
* lifecycle definitions are incomplete;
* reconciliation behavior is incomplete;
* traceability is broken;
* temporal integrity cannot be demonstrated;
* unresolved Critical or Major findings remain.

No office failing constitutional compliance verification shall proceed to Enterprise Integration Certification.
