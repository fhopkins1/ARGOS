# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-013

# Certification Hardening Closure

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Certification Hardening Closure for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to independently determine whether the Certification Hardening Campaign has successfully eliminated or constitutionally resolved every discovered weakness within the ECS-003 certification methodology.

Unlike prior orders in this campaign, which sought to discover weaknesses, this order evaluates the certification process itself as the object under certification.

The objective is to determine whether ECS-003 has become sufficiently objective, reproducible, independent, comprehensive, and adversarial that future certifications are resistant to omission, assumption, confirmation bias, false positives, false negatives, implementation deception, and undocumented behavior.

Successful completion of this order establishes the hardened certification baseline for all future Performance Truth certifications.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

Certification Hardening Closure shall immediately fail whenever:

* any discovered certification weakness remains unresolved;
* constitutional coverage is incomplete;
* objective proof remains insufficient;
* certification repeatability cannot be demonstrated;
* independent auditors reach materially different conclusions without constitutional justification;
* false-positive or false-negative vulnerabilities remain unresolved;
* mutation testing exposes undetected certification defects;
* doctrine contains unresolved inconsistencies;
* residual certification risk cannot be objectively justified.

The hardened certification baseline shall not be declared until every closure requirement has been independently verified.

* repeated clean-room certifications produce identical constitutional conclusions;
* independent auditors consistently reach equivalent certification decisions;
* the certification process demonstrates verified resistance to false positives, false negatives, implementation deception, mutation, and confirmation bias;
* the hardened ECS-003 doctrine has been formally established as the authoritative certification methodology for the Performance Truth Office;
* the Certification Hardening Report and Hardened Certification Baseline Declaration have been permanently preserved as constitutional certification artifacts;
* no unresolved certification-process weakness remains capable of materially reducing the integrity, objectivity, independence, or reproducibility of future ECS-003 certifications.
