# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-012

# Certification Doctrine Refinement

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional doctrine refinement campaign for the ECS-003 certification process governing the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to convert every validated certification-process weakness discovered during the Certification Hardening Campaign into permanent improvements to ECS-003, thereby increasing the objectivity, completeness, reproducibility, determinism, and independence of future constitutional certification.

This order shall refine certification doctrine only.

It shall not modify the certified implementation or alter the constitutional behavior of the Performance Truth Office except where clarification of certification requirements is constitutionally necessary.

---

# Constitutional Authority

This Modification Order derives authority from:

* ARGOS Constitution
* Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-002
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-RM-003
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-001
* PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002

* Workflow Ownership Doctrine

Nothing within this order may weaken existing constitutional guarantees.

Every refinement shall strengthen the certification process while preserving backward compatibility with previously certified constitutional behavior unless explicitly superseded through constitutional authority.

---

# Modification Scope

This campaign shall evaluate every validated certification weakness discovered throughout PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002 and determine whether ECS-003 requires amendment regarding:

* certification methodology;
* audit-control architecture;
* evidence requirements;
* proof standards;
* audit independence;
* coverage requirements;
* replay requirements;
* mutation requirements;
* reproducibility requirements;
* certification decision rules;
* documentation standards;
* auditor procedures.

Only demonstrated certification weaknesses shall justify constitutional refinement.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every doctrine refinement shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Certification shall become more objective.

2. Certification shall become more independently reproducible.

3. Certification shall become more resistant to false positives.

4. Certification shall become more resistant to false negatives.

5. Auditor discretion shall be reduced wherever constitutionally possible.

6. Every certification claim shall possess sufficient proof obligations.

7. Every refinement shall preserve deterministic certification.

8. No refinement shall weaken constitutional guarantees.

9. Every refinement shall remain independently auditable.

10. Certification doctrine shall evolve only through evidence-backed constitutional modification.

# Root Cause Verification

Before doctrine may be amended, every weakness shall undergo constitutional root-cause analysis.

The investigation shall determine whether the weakness originated from:

* incomplete constitutional doctrine;
* incomplete audit doctrine;
* incomplete evidence requirements;
* ambiguous acceptance criteria;
* inadequate proof obligations;
* hidden assumptions;
* undocumented procedures;
* excessive auditor discretion;
* incomplete reproducibility requirements;
* insufficient adversarial testing.

No refinement shall be based upon speculation.

---

# Amendment Necessity Verification

For every proposed amendment, determine whether:

* existing doctrine already resolves the weakness;
* implementation error created the appearance of a doctrine weakness;
* audit execution error created the appearance of a doctrine weakness;
* constitutional doctrine genuinely requires modification.

Doctrine shall be amended only when constitutional insufficiency has been demonstrated.

---

# Amendment Design Requirements

Every proposed ECS-003 amendment shall explicitly define:

* Amendment Identifier;
* affected doctrine;
* constitutional purpose;
* affected certification property;
* rationale;
* constitutional authority;
* normative language;
* implementation impact;
* audit impact;
* backward compatibility assessment;
* required evidence;
* acceptance criteria.

No amendment shall rely upon explanatory commentary alone.

---

# Audit-Control Refinement

Where weaknesses originate from audit controls, doctrine shall define:

* new audit controls;
* revised audit controls;
* removed audit controls;
* strengthened acceptance criteria;
* strengthened rejection criteria;
* required negative testing;
* required mutation testing;
* required replay testing;
* required clean-room verification.

Every revised control shall map directly to constitutional requirements.

---

# Evidence Requirement Refinement

Where weaknesses originate from insufficient evidence, doctrine shall strengthen:

* evidence completeness;
* evidence provenance;
* evidence immutability;
* evidence sufficiency;
* evidence reproducibility;
* evidence preservation;
* evidence traceability;
* evidence validation.

Evidence demonstrating only implementation existence shall not satisfy doctrine where correctness must be proven.

---

# Proof Requirement Refinement

For every constitutional property certified by ECS-003, doctrine shall define:

* required proof;
* supporting evidence;
* required reproduction;
* required negative proof;
* proof completeness criteria;
* proof rejection criteria.

Every certified constitutional property shall possess explicit proof obligations.

---

No constitutional requirement shall remain indirectly audited where direct verification is feasible.

* conflict-of-interest controls;
* evidence isolation;
* prior-knowledge isolation;
* standardized decision criteria.

Certification shall increasingly depend upon constitutional evidence rather than auditor experience.

* re-audit authorization;
* doctrine ambiguity;

Decision rules shall become increasingly deterministic.

# Fail-Closed Requirements

Certification doctrine refinement shall fail closed whenever:

* a proposed amendment lacks objective justification;
* a weakness has not been independently reproduced;
* root cause remains unresolved;
* amendment effectiveness has not been demonstrated;
* backward compatibility cannot be evaluated;
* refinement increases discretionary judgment;
* refinement weakens constitutional guarantees;
* amendment introduces new constitutional ambiguity.

No doctrine amendment shall be adopted under unresolved constitutional uncertainty.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold.

* Every doctrine amendment originates from a validated certification weakness.
* Certification becomes progressively more objective and reproducible.
* Auditor discretion decreases over successive doctrine revisions.
* Every constitutional property possesses explicit proof obligations.
* Every constitutional requirement possesses explicit audit coverage.
* Certification doctrine evolves through evidence rather than opinion.
* Historical certification remains reproducible across doctrine versions.
* Amendment history remains immutable and fully traceable.
* Constitutional guarantees are never weakened by doctrine refinement.
* ECS-003 remains a deterministic constitutional certification standard.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every validated certification-process weakness has been independently analyzed and classified.
2. Root-cause analysis has demonstrated which weaknesses require constitutional doctrine changes.
3. Every necessary ECS-003 amendment has been formally specified with constitutional authority, rationale, normative language, and acceptance criteria.
4. Audit controls, evidence requirements, proof obligations, coverage requirements, auditor-independence requirements, and certification decision rules have been refined where necessary.
5. Every proposed amendment has been independently validated as eliminating its originating weakness without introducing new ambiguity.
6. Backward compatibility with previously certified constitutional guarantees has been verified where applicable.
7. A permanent Constitutional Amendment Register has been established and fully populated.
8. Independent auditors can reproduce the rationale, implementation, and validation of every doctrine refinement solely from constitutional records.
9. No unresolved certification-process weakness remains that requires further ECS-003 doctrine refinement.
10. ECS-003 has been constitutionally strengthened into a more objective, reproducible, deterministic, and independently verifiable certification standard while preserving all previously established constitutional guarantees.
