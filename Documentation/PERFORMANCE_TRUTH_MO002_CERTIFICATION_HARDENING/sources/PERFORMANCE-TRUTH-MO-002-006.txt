# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-006

# Independent Auditor Equivalence

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the constitutional validation of equivalence among independent auditors applying ECS-003 certification to identical Performance Truth repository packages.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether separate constitutionally competent auditors, operating independently and without shared interpretive guidance, reach materially equivalent:

* findings;
* evidence conclusions;
* proof assessments;
* severity classifications;
* remediation requirements;
* certification decisions.

Certification shall not depend upon the identity, familiarity, preferences, or subjective judgment of the auditor.

Every material disagreement shall be treated as evidence of certification ambiguity, incomplete doctrine, insufficient evidence, defective tooling, or inadequate auditor independence until constitutionally resolved.

---

Nothing within this order may weaken constitutional guarantees or permit auditor consensus to replace objective proof.

Equivalent auditor conclusions are required, but agreement alone shall not establish correctness.

---

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* define constitutionally sufficient auditor independence;
* establish identical audit inputs for multiple auditors;
* prevent communication or result contamination between auditors;
* require independent execution of the complete certification procedure;
* compare audit coverage, findings, proofs, and decisions;
* identify every material disagreement;
* determine the cause of each disagreement;
* distinguish harmless presentation variance from constitutional divergence;
* eliminate discretionary certification paths;
* strengthen ECS-003 until equivalent evidence produces equivalent conclusions.

---

# Equivalence Principle

Independent auditor equivalence exists when separate auditors evaluating identical constitutional evidence reach the same material certification outcome through independently reproducible reasoning.

Equivalence does not require identical prose, formatting, file structure, or finding identifiers.

Equivalence requires agreement regarding:

* which constitutional requirements were tested;
* whether each requirement was satisfied;
* which defects were discovered;
* the constitutional impact of each defect;
* the evidence supporting each conclusion;
* whether remediation is required;
* the final certification decision.

Two auditors shall not be considered equivalent when they reach the same final decision while materially disagreeing about the constitutional condition of the office.

---

# Certification Scope

Equivalence validation shall cover:

* repository completeness;
* canonical object implementation;
* behavioral conformance;
* interface conformance;
* ownership;
* lifecycle behavior;
* evidence completeness;
* traceability;
* reconciliation;
* deterministic execution;
* replay;
* clean-room reproducibility;
* fail-closed behavior;
* constitutional conformance;
* finding classification;
* final certification decision.

No audit dimension may be excluded because it was previously certified.

---

# Auditor Cohort Requirements

Each equivalence exercise shall use at least two independent auditors.

A third auditor or adjudication audit shall be required whenever the first two auditors produce a material disagreement.

Each auditor shall:

* possess access only to the authorized audit package;
* receive the same constitutional baseline;
* receive the same repository package;
* receive the same approved audit tooling;
* receive the same environmental specification;
* perform the audit independently;
* generate evidence independently;
* preserve a complete audit trail;
* disclose all prior exposure to the implementation.

No auditor may inspect another auditor’s work before submitting a final independent decision.

---

# Auditor Independence Requirements

Auditor independence shall be invalidated by:

* participation in implementation development;
* participation in prior remediation of the audited package;
* access to another auditor’s findings before submission;
* access to expected outcomes;
* reliance upon developer explanation not contained in the repository package;
* use of undocumented external knowledge;
* coordinated interpretation during execution;
* pressure to preserve a prior certification result;
* undisclosed conflicts of interest.

Where complete historical independence is impossible, prior exposure shall be documented and controlled through blind reassignment, bounded audit scope, or independent replication.

---

# Common Audit Package

Every auditor shall receive an identical Common Audit Package containing:

* immutable repository package;
* package integrity manifest;
* constitutional baseline;
* applicable ECS-003 doctrine;
* approved audit procedures;
* approved tool identities and versions;
* clean-room environment specification;
* authorized dependency package;
* finding classification rules;
* required evidence schemas;
* certification decision rules.

The Common Audit Package shall exclude:

* prior audit findings;
* prior certification decisions;
* implementation-author explanations;
* suggested defect locations;
* expected test outcomes;
* unpublished remediation history.

---

# Audit Isolation

Each auditor shall operate within a separately established clean-room environment.

Isolation evidence shall verify:

* independent environment creation;
* no shared mutable storage;
* no inter-auditor communication;
* no access to prior results;
* no access to implementation authors;
* independent evidence generation;
* independent report generation;
* independent decision issuance.

A nominal declaration of independence shall not be sufficient.

---

# Equivalence Dimensions

## PT-IAE-001

### Requirement-Coverage Equivalence

Verify that each auditor maps and tests the same complete set of constitutional requirements.

Disagreement includes:

* omitted requirements;
* differently scoped requirements;
* requirements treated as implicitly satisfied;
* requirements merged in a manner that conceals missing coverage;
* requirements tested by one auditor but presumed by another.

Every requirement shall possess explicit coverage from every auditor.

---

## PT-IAE-002

### Repository-Discovery Equivalence

Verify that each auditor discovers materially equivalent:

* modules;
* services;
* dependencies;
* interfaces;
* configurations;
* generated artifacts;
* execution paths;
* undocumented behavior.

A component discovered by only one auditor shall trigger investigation into discovery completeness.

---

## PT-IAE-003

### Behavioral-Observation Equivalence

Verify that identical test conditions produce equivalent observed behavior.

Disagreement shall be investigated for:

* environmental variance;
* nondeterministic implementation;
* inconsistent tooling;
* different input construction;
* incomplete evidence capture;
* auditor interpretation.

Observed behavior shall not depend upon the auditor.

---

## PT-IAE-004

### Evidence-Assessment Equivalence

Verify that auditors reach equivalent conclusions regarding:

* evidence authenticity;
* evidence completeness;
* evidence relevance;
* chain of custody;
* provenance;
* proof sufficiency;
* reproducibility.

Evidence accepted by one auditor and rejected by another constitutes a material certification ambiguity unless the difference is objectively resolved.

---

## PT-IAE-005

### Finding-Discovery Equivalence

Verify that auditors identify materially equivalent constitutional defects.

The comparison shall account for findings that differ in wording but describe the same underlying defect.

A defect found by only one auditor shall not be dismissed as auditor preference.

---

## PT-IAE-006

### Severity-Classification Equivalence

Verify equivalent classification of findings as:

* Critical;
* Major;
* Minor;
* Observation.

Every classification shall derive from explicit constitutional impact criteria.

Disagreement regarding severity shall trigger review of doctrine, evidence, and classification rules.

---

## PT-IAE-007

### Remediation-Requirement Equivalence

Verify that auditors prescribe materially equivalent constitutional correction requirements.

Remediation may differ in implementation detail but shall agree regarding:

* property requiring correction;
* constitutional owner;
* required evidence;
* required re-audit scope;
* certification effect.

---

## PT-IAE-008

### Proof-Conclusion Equivalence

Verify that auditors agree whether each required proof establishes its claimed constitutional property.

Equivalent proof assessment requires agreement regarding:

* premises;
* evidence;
* logical sufficiency;
* hidden assumptions;
* reproducibility;
* conclusion.

---

## PT-IAE-009

### Certification-Decision Equivalence

Verify that every auditor independently issues the same final decision:

* PASS;
* CONDITIONAL PASS;
* FAIL.

Agreement on the final label shall not cure material disagreement in the supporting findings or proofs.

---

# Independent Audit Procedure

Each auditor shall independently execute:

## Phase 1

Verify package identity and integrity.

## Phase 2

Establish and record the clean-room environment.

## Phase 3

Discover the repository and dependencies.

## Phase 4

Construct an independent constitutional coverage matrix.

## Phase 5

Execute behavioral, interface, evidence, replay, reconciliation, and fail-closed verification.

## Phase 6

Generate independent findings and proof assessments.

## Phase 7

Classify all findings.

## Phase 8

Issue an independent certification decision.

## Phase 9

Seal all audit evidence and reports.

No cross-auditor comparison shall begin before all independent submissions are sealed.

---

# Equivalence Comparison Procedure

Following sealed submission, an independent comparison authority shall:

1. verify that all auditors received identical authorized inputs;
2. verify auditor independence;
3. normalize differences in terminology and formatting;
4. compare constitutional coverage;
5. compare discovered implementation scope;
6. compare observed behaviors;
7. compare evidence assessments;
8. compare findings;
9. compare severity classifications;
10. compare proof conclusions;
11. compare remediation requirements;
12. compare certification decisions;
13. identify every material disagreement;
14. determine whether equivalence has been achieved.

The comparison authority shall not silently merge or average conflicting conclusions.

---

# Disagreement Classification

Every disagreement shall receive exactly one classification.

## Presentation Variance

The auditors express the same material conclusion using different wording, organization, or identifiers.

No constitutional remediation is required.

---

## Evidence-Capture Variance

The auditors reach the same conclusion but preserve materially different evidence.

Evidence requirements shall be reviewed for completeness and standardization.

---

## Coverage Variance

One auditor evaluates a constitutional requirement or execution path omitted by another.

This constitutes an audit coverage defect.

---

## Interpretation Variance

Auditors apply the same doctrine differently.

This constitutes certification-doctrine ambiguity.

---

## Observation Variance

Auditors observe different implementation behavior from identical inputs.

This indicates nondeterminism, environmental divergence, tooling defects, or uncontrolled execution.

---

## Severity Variance

Auditors agree that a defect exists but assign materially different constitutional severity.

Classification rules are insufficient or inconsistently applied.

---

## Decision Variance

Auditors issue different certification decisions.

Certification shall fail closed until the disagreement is resolved.

---

## Unresolved Variance

Available evidence cannot determine the source or correct resolution of the disagreement.

The affected certification property shall remain unproven.

---

# Disagreement Investigation

Every material disagreement shall generate an Auditor Disagreement Record containing:

* disagreement identifier;
* participating auditor identifiers;
* affected constitutional requirement;
* affected repository component;
* each auditor’s conclusion;
* each auditor’s evidence;
* classification;
* suspected cause;
* independent reproduction result;
* doctrine impact;
* tooling impact;
* environmental impact;
* certification impact;
* required remediation;
* final resolution.

No disagreement may be resolved solely by majority vote.

---

# Adjudication Audit

A third independent audit shall be required when:

* certification decisions differ;
* severity differs between Critical or Major and a lower classification;
* one auditor identifies a potentially certifying defect omitted by another;
* observed behavior differs;
* proof sufficiency remains disputed;
* independence cannot be established;
* the cause of disagreement remains unresolved.

The adjudication auditor shall receive the original Common Audit Package without access to prior findings until the independent adjudication report is sealed.

After sealing, all three reports may be compared.

The adjudication result shall not automatically override the other auditors. The disagreement’s constitutional cause shall still be identified and corrected.

---

# Equivalence Metrics

The audit shall calculate at minimum:

* constitutional requirement coverage agreement;
* repository component discovery agreement;
* finding discovery agreement;
* severity agreement;
* proof-conclusion agreement;
* remediation agreement;
* certification-decision agreement.

Metrics may support analysis but shall not replace constitutional evaluation.

A high aggregate agreement score shall not conceal disagreement concerning a Critical or Major property.

---

# Permitted Variance

The following differences may be accepted where they do not affect constitutional meaning:

* report structure;
* prose;
* file naming;
* finding identifiers;
* order of test execution;
* equivalent test implementations;
* additional observations;
* alternative but equally sufficient evidence presentation.

Permitted variance shall not include differences concerning compliance, ownership, truth, evidence sufficiency, determinism, fail-closed behavior, severity, or certification status.

---

# Certification Defects

Independent auditor disagreement shall generate a Certification Defect Record whenever it reveals:

* incomplete ECS-003 doctrine;
* discretionary audit interpretation;
* insufficient evidence requirements;
* inconsistent finding criteria;
* incomplete test procedures;
* uncontrolled audit environments;
* invalid auditor independence;
* defective audit tooling;
* nondeterministic implementation behavior;
* false-positive or false-negative certification risk.

Each defect shall identify the required doctrine, procedure, tooling, or evidence modification.

---

# Prior Certification Impact

The order shall determine whether detected auditor variance could have affected previous Performance Truth certifications.

Prior certification impact shall be classified as:

* no material impact;
* evidence supplementation required;
* bounded re-audit required;
* complete re-certification required;
* prior certification invalidated.

Any prior certification dependent upon unresolved discretionary judgment shall not remain conclusively authoritative.

# Constitutional Invariants

## PT-IAE-INV-001

Identical constitutional evidence shall produce materially equivalent certification conclusions.

## PT-IAE-INV-002

No auditor may rely upon another auditor’s findings before issuing an independent decision.

## PT-IAE-INV-003

Auditor consensus shall not substitute for constitutional proof.

## PT-IAE-INV-004

Every material disagreement shall be preserved and investigated.

## PT-IAE-INV-005

No disagreement shall be resolved solely by majority vote.

## PT-IAE-INV-006

Every finding and certification decision shall trace to objective evidence and explicit doctrine.

## PT-IAE-INV-007

Auditor identity shall not determine certification outcome.

## PT-IAE-INV-008

Equivalent final decisions shall not conceal materially different findings or proof conclusions.

## PT-IAE-INV-009

Unresolved auditor disagreement shall render the affected constitutional property unproven.

## PT-IAE-INV-010

Every equivalence exercise shall itself be independently reproducible.

## PT-IAE-INV-011

Prior implementation familiarity shall not influence certification conclusions.

## PT-IAE-INV-012

Every discovered equivalence defect shall strengthen certification doctrine or procedure.

---

# Fail-Closed Behavior

Certification shall fail closed whenever:

* auditor independence cannot be demonstrated;
* auditors receive materially different audit inputs;
* one auditor accesses another auditor’s work before submitting;
* constitutional coverage differs materially;
* observed behavior differs under identical conditions;
* proof sufficiency remains disputed;
* Critical or Major finding discovery differs;
* severity classification materially differs;
* certification decisions differ;
* disagreement cause cannot be objectively established;
* doctrine permits multiple materially valid interpretations;
* audit tooling or environment produces auditor-dependent outcomes.

No affected certification decision or constitutional freeze shall remain authoritative until equivalence is restored through doctrine correction, procedural correction, evidence supplementation, or independent re-certification.

---

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

All participating independent auditors reach materially equivalent findings, proof conclusions, severity classifications, remediation requirements, and certification decisions.

All identified differences are limited to constitutionally immaterial presentation variance.

No unresolved material disagreement remains.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

Auditors reach equivalent constitutional and certification conclusions, but bounded Evidence-Capture or Minor Coverage Variances remain.

Each variance shall possess an approved corrective action and shall be resolved before Certification Hardening Closure.

---

## FAIL

One or more material disagreements remain regarding constitutional coverage, observed behavior, evidence sufficiency, finding discovery, severity, proof conclusions, remediation, or certification decision.

The affected certification process shall remain suspended pending remediation and independent revalidation.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. At least two constitutionally independent auditors have evaluated identical Common Audit Packages.
2. Auditor independence and clean-room isolation have been objectively demonstrated.
3. Every auditor has independently executed the complete required certification scope.
4. Every auditor has submitted sealed findings, proofs, evidence, and a final certification decision.
5. Constitutional coverage, repository discovery, observations, evidence assessments, findings, severity, remediation, proofs, and decisions have been compared.
6. Every material disagreement has received an Auditor Disagreement Record.
7. Every required adjudication audit has been completed independently.
8. No disagreement has been resolved solely through consensus or majority vote.
9. Every discovered certification defect has generated a complete Certification Defect Record.
10. Prior certifications affected by auditor variance have undergone impact review.
11. All evidence has been preserved within the Evidence Repository.
12. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
13. No unresolved auditor-dependent certification behavior remains within the hardened ECS-003 process.
