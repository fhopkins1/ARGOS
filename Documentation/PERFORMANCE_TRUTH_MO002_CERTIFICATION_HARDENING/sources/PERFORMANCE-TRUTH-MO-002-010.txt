# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-010

# Constitutional Proof Sufficiency

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Constitutional Proof Sufficiency campaign for the Performance Truth Office certification process.

Its purpose is to determine whether every constitutional proof required by Enterprise Constitutional Standard ECS-003 genuinely proves the property it claims to establish, rather than merely suggesting, assuming, or indirectly supporting that property.

This campaign assumes that certification may contain proofs that are incomplete, circular, insufficiently independent, or dependent upon hidden assumptions.

Only proofs that independently establish constitutional correctness shall remain part of the hardened ECS-003 certification doctrine.

---

Nothing within this order may weaken previously certified constitutional guarantees.

---

# Campaign Objective

The objective of this campaign is to independently evaluate every constitutional proof required by ECS-003 and determine whether it:

* establishes the claimed constitutional property;
* relies upon explicit constitutional authority;
* is independently reproducible;
* is free from hidden assumptions;
* is free from circular reasoning;
* is supported by sufficient evidence;
* remains valid under adversarial review.

A proof that merely increases confidence shall not be considered a constitutional proof.

---

# Proof Inventory

The reviewer shall construct a complete inventory of every constitutional proof required by ECS-003.

The inventory shall include proofs governing:

* constitutional compliance;
* implementation correctness;
* deterministic execution;
* canonical object integrity;
* ownership;
* lifecycle conformance;
* interface correctness;
* calculation correctness;
* evidence completeness;
* traceability;
* reconciliation;
* temporal integrity;
* replay capability;
* fail-closed behavior;
* enterprise integration;
* certification readiness.

No constitutional proof may remain undocumented.

---

# Proof Specification Verification

Every proof shall explicitly identify:

* proof identifier;
* constitutional property proven;
* governing constitutional requirement;
* required evidence;
* assumptions;
* inference steps;
* acceptance criteria;
* failure conditions.

Implicit proof definitions are prohibited.

---

# Property-to-Proof Mapping

Every constitutional property shall map to one or more independent proofs.

The mapping shall identify:

* constitutional requirement;
* supporting proof;
* supporting evidence;
* validation methodology;
* certification order;
* residual limitations.

A constitutional property without an explicit proof constitutes a certification deficiency.

---

# Proof Independence Audit

Every proof shall be examined to determine whether it depends upon:

* implementation behavior;
* prior certification conclusions;
* undocumented assumptions;
* inferred correctness;
* auditor judgment;
* toolchain behavior;
* external knowledge.

Proofs shall derive solely from constitutional evidence and reproducible reasoning.

---

# Hidden Assumption Analysis

Every proof shall identify every assumption required for validity.

Each assumption shall be classified as:

* constitutionally established;
* indirectly supported;
* operationally guaranteed;
* externally governed;
* unsupported.

Unsupported assumptions shall invalidate the proof until remediated.

---

# Circular Reasoning Audit

The reviewer shall determine whether any proof depends upon the conclusion it claims to establish.

Examples include:

* implementation validating itself;
* evidence validating itself;
* replay proving replay correctness without independent reference;
* certification reports serving as proof of certification correctness;
* generated outputs serving as their own oracle.

Circular proofs are constitutionally invalid.

---

# Evidence Sufficiency Verification

Every proof shall demonstrate that its evidence is sufficient to establish the claimed property.

Evidence shall prove:

* existence;
* correctness;
* completeness;
* determinism;
* ownership;
* causality;
* traceability;
* reproducibility.

Evidence demonstrating only execution or presence is insufficient.

---

# Proof Completeness Analysis

Each proof shall be evaluated to determine whether it proves:

* the entire constitutional property;
* only a subset of the property;
* only nominal behavior;
* only expected scenarios.

Partial proofs shall not certify complete constitutional compliance.

---

# Adversarial Proof Validation

Every proof shall be challenged through adversarial scenarios intended to invalidate its conclusion.

The reviewer shall attempt to demonstrate that:

* the proof succeeds despite constitutional failure;
* the proof fails despite constitutional correctness;
* hidden assumptions influence the outcome;
* omitted evidence changes the conclusion;
* alternate interpretations remain possible.

Proofs surviving adversarial review shall be considered constitutionally strengthened.

---

# Proof Reproducibility

Every constitutional proof shall be independently reproducible using only:

* the constitutional specification;
* certified repository package;
* required evidence;
* documented certification procedures.

No proof shall require undocumented knowledge.

---

# Proof Determinism

Repeated execution of the same proof using identical inputs shall produce:

* identical reasoning;
* identical evidence evaluation;
* identical intermediate conclusions;
* identical final conclusions.

Proof determinism shall be independent of reviewer identity.

---

# Cross-Proof Consistency

The reviewer shall verify that independent proofs do not contradict one another.

The review shall identify:

* conflicting conclusions;
* inconsistent assumptions;
* contradictory evidence requirements;
* incompatible constitutional interpretations;
* overlapping proof responsibilities.

Conflicting proofs constitute certification defects.

---

# Proof Dependency Analysis

Every proof shall explicitly identify dependencies upon:

* other proofs;
* evidence packages;
* certification artifacts;
* constitutional doctrine;
* implementation artifacts;
* enterprise offices.

Hidden proof dependencies are prohibited.

---

# Proof Minimality

The reviewer shall determine whether each proof contains unnecessary assumptions, evidence, or reasoning.

Where equivalent certainty can be achieved through a simpler proof, the simpler proof shall be preferred.

Simplification shall never reduce constitutional guarantees.

---

# Proof Mutation Analysis

Controlled mutations shall be introduced into proof inputs including:

* evidence;
* assumptions;
* inference steps;
* constitutional references;
* traceability;
* certification artifacts.

A valid proof shall either:

* detect the mutation; or
* fail deterministically.

Undetected material mutations invalidate proof sufficiency.

---

# Proof Coverage Analysis

The reviewer shall determine whether every constitutional property is proven under:

* nominal operation;
* boundary conditions;
* failure conditions;
* recovery conditions;
* concurrent execution;
* replay;
* enterprise integration.

Proofs limited to nominal behavior are incomplete.

---

# Proof Confidence Classification

Each proof shall receive one of the following determinations:

* Constitutionally Proven;
* Constitutionally Proven with Explicit Assumptions;
* Constitutionally Incomplete;
* Constitutionally Invalid.

Only Constitutionally Proven proofs may support final certification.

---

# ECS-003 Amendment Recommendations

Every identified proof deficiency shall produce one or more recommended ECS-003 amendments including:

* revised proof requirements;
* additional evidence requirements;
* new independent validation procedures;
* strengthened acceptance criteria;
* strengthened failure criteria;
* new regression certification requirements.

Every amendment shall strengthen objective certification without increasing discretionary judgment.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every constitutional property possesses at least one explicit proof;
* every proof explicitly identifies its assumptions;
* unsupported assumptions invalidate constitutional proofs;
* circular reasoning is prohibited;
* proofs are independently reproducible;
* proofs are deterministic;
* evidence is sufficient to establish the claimed property;
* independent proofs remain mutually consistent;
* proof deficiencies produce constitutional remediation rather than certification.

Violation of any invariant constitutes a certification-hardening finding.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The certification process shall refuse constitutional certification whenever:

* a required proof is missing;
* proof assumptions cannot be justified;
* evidence is insufficient;
* circular reasoning is detected;
* proof reproducibility cannot be demonstrated;
* conflicting proofs remain unresolved;
* proof mutations survive undetected;
* proof coverage is incomplete;
* constitutional certainty cannot be independently established.

Certification confidence shall never replace constitutional proof.

# Acceptance Criteria

This Modification Order shall be considered complete when independent review demonstrates that:

1. every constitutional property required by ECS-003 is supported by one or more explicit, independently reproducible constitutional proofs;
2. every proof explicitly identifies its assumptions, dependencies, evidence requirements, and acceptance criteria;
3. no proof relies upon hidden assumptions, undocumented behavior, circular reasoning, or subjective interpretation;
4. every proof has been successfully challenged through adversarial validation and remains sufficient to establish the claimed constitutional property;
5. proof coverage extends across nominal, boundary, failure, recovery, replay, and enterprise integration scenarios;
6. every identified proof deficiency has been remediated or incorporated into strengthened ECS-003 certification doctrine;
7. independent reviewers reproduce equivalent proof conclusions using identical constitutional evidence; and
8. the ECS-003 certification process demonstrates a materially stronger, more objective, and constitutionally rigorous proof architecture than the prior hardened certification baseline.
