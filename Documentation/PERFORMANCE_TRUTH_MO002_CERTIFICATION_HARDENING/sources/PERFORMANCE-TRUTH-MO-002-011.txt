# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-011

# Auditor Independence Validation

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Constitutional Auditor Independence Validation for the ECS-003 certification process governing the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether certification outcomes depend exclusively upon constitutional evidence and reproducible proof, rather than upon auditor identity, implementation familiarity, prior certification history, repository authorship, subjective interpretation, organizational pressure, or any other source of cognitive or procedural bias.

This order treats the auditor as a potential source of constitutional risk.

The objective is not to evaluate auditor competence.

The objective is to prove that the certification process remains constitutionally correct regardless of who performs the audit.

---

* ARGOS Constitution;

Nothing within this order may weaken constitutional evidence requirements or permit discretionary certification.

Certification authority shall derive solely from objective constitutional proof.

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* define constitutional auditor independence;
* identify every potential influence upon certification decisions;
* verify independence of audit inputs, execution, interpretation, and conclusions;
* eliminate dependence upon historical knowledge;
* eliminate dependence upon implementation familiarity;
* eliminate dependence upon repository authorship;
* eliminate dependence upon prior audit outcomes;
* eliminate subjective interpretation wherever objective rules are possible;
* establish objective evidence demonstrating auditor independence;
* produce a hardened constitutional independence standard for future ECS-003 audits.

---

# Constitutional Definition of Auditor Independence

An auditor is constitutionally independent when every certification conclusion can be reproduced by another competent auditor using only:

* the approved repository package;
* constitutionally authorized evidence;
* the approved audit doctrine;
* the approved certification procedures;
* the approved audit tooling.

The auditor shall require no additional information to reach a constitutionally valid certification decision.

Personal knowledge shall never substitute for constitutional evidence.

---

# Scope

The validation shall examine independence throughout:

* auditor selection;
* repository acquisition;
* audit planning;
* repository discovery;
* implementation discovery;
* behavioral verification;
* evidence evaluation;
* proof construction;
* finding generation;
* severity classification;
* remediation recommendations;
* certification decision;
* certification reporting;
* constitutional freeze recommendation.

Every stage of certification shall remain auditor-independent.

# Sources of Potential Influence

The audit shall search for influence arising from:

* prior implementation participation;
* repository authorship;
* previous certification results;
* previous remediation work;
* implementation familiarity;
* undocumented architectural knowledge;
* organizational hierarchy;
* management expectations;
* schedule pressure;
* historical PASS expectations;
* prior relationships with repository authors;
* institutional bias;
* confirmation bias;
* anchoring bias;
* expectation bias;
* availability bias;
* sunk-cost bias;
* authority bias;
* group consensus;
* undocumented tooling assumptions.

Every influence shall either be eliminated, objectively controlled, or constitutionally demonstrated to be irrelevant.

# Independence Validation Categories

## PT-AIV-001

### Repository Blindness

Verify that certification can be completed without prior knowledge of:

* repository history;
* repository authors;
* implementation evolution;
* previous defects;
* previous audits;
* prior certification status.

The repository package shall contain every artifact necessary for certification.

---

## PT-AIV-002

### Historical Independence

Verify that previous certification outcomes do not influence:

* audit scope;
* finding generation;
* proof evaluation;
* evidence requirements;
* certification conclusions.

Every audit shall begin constitutionally from first principles.

Previous PASS decisions shall not constitute constitutional evidence.

---

## PT-AIV-003

### Author Independence

Verify that implementation authorship cannot influence:

* finding severity;
* remediation requirements;
* acceptance criteria;
* certification decisions.

Repository identity shall never alter constitutional evaluation.

---

## PT-AIV-004

### Interpretation Independence

Verify that constitutional doctrine produces:

* deterministic interpretations;
* deterministic acceptance criteria;
* deterministic findings;
* deterministic certification outcomes.

Subjective interpretation shall be replaced with explicit constitutional rules whenever possible.

---

## PT-AIV-005

### Tool Independence

Verify that certification conclusions do not depend upon:

* undocumented audit scripts;
* proprietary tooling behavior;
* hidden configuration;
* manual calculations;
* undocumented automation.

Tool outputs shall remain independently reproducible.

---

## PT-AIV-006

### Evidence Independence

Verify that auditors evaluate evidence solely according to:

* constitutional relevance;
* constitutional sufficiency;
* authenticity;
* reproducibility;
* traceability.

Evidence credibility shall not depend upon its author.

---

## PT-AIV-007

### Proof Independence

Verify that constitutional proofs:

* establish the claimed property;
* possess objective premises;
* remain reproducible;
* remain logically complete.

Auditor intuition shall never complete missing proof.

---

## PT-AIV-008

### Finding Independence

Verify that findings arise solely from:

* constitutional violations;
* objective evidence;
* reproducible observations.

Auditors shall not suppress findings because previous audits passed.

---

## PT-AIV-009

### Decision Independence

Verify that certification decisions derive exclusively from:

* constitutional compliance;
* objective evidence;
* deterministic proof;
* certified audit procedures.

No external factor shall influence PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, or FAIL.

---

## PT-AIV-010

### Reporting Independence

Verify that certification reports remain faithful to audit evidence.

Reports shall not:

* omit constitutional deficiencies;
* soften findings;
* reinterpret evidence;
* elevate unsupported confidence.

Reports shall remain objective constitutional artifacts.

# Independence Challenge Procedure

Each validation shall execute the following sequence.

## Phase 1

Identify the independence property under examination.

---

## Phase 2

Identify all possible influencing factors.

---

## Phase 3

Remove one influence while preserving identical constitutional evidence.

---

## Phase 4

Repeat certification independently.

---

## Phase 5

Compare:

* findings;
* evidence;
* proof;
* severity;
* remediation;
* certification decision.

---

## Phase 6

Determine whether removal of the influence altered constitutional conclusions.

---

## Phase 7

Record constitutional implications.

---

## Phase 8

Recommend doctrine modifications whenever influence remains detectable.

# Independence Classification

Each influence shall receive exactly one classification.

## Eliminated

The influence cannot affect certification.

---

## Constitutionally Controlled

The influence exists but is objectively neutralized through doctrine or procedure.

---

## Observable but Harmless

The influence exists but demonstrably cannot alter certification outcomes.

---

## Certification Weakness

The influence measurably alters findings, proof evaluation, or certification conclusions.

A constitutional modification shall be required.

---

## Critical Certification Defect

The influence permits identical constitutional evidence to produce different certification outcomes.

Certification shall immediately fail.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

Certification shall immediately fail closed whenever:

* auditor identity influences certification;
* repository authorship influences findings;
* previous certification influences current certification;
* subjective interpretation changes constitutional conclusions;
* implementation familiarity substitutes for evidence;
* undocumented knowledge is required;
* identical constitutional evidence produces different certification outcomes;
* objective proof is incomplete;
* required independence evidence is missing;
* any Critical Certification Defect is identified.

No certification decision affected by unresolved auditor influence shall authorize constitutional freeze or enterprise certification.

---

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

Every identified influence has been eliminated, constitutionally controlled, or demonstrated to be harmless.

Certification depends solely upon objective constitutional evidence.

No unresolved Critical Certification Defects remain.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

No influence currently alters certification outcomes, but bounded Certification Weaknesses remain.

Each weakness shall possess an approved constitutional remediation plan before Certification Hardening Closure.

---

## FAIL

One or more influences alter constitutional findings, proofs, or certification decisions.

Affected certification methodology shall remain uncertified until doctrine and procedures are remediated and independently revalidated.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every potential source of auditor influence has been identified and evaluated.
2. Auditor independence has been validated across every phase of the ECS-003 certification process.
3. Repository history, authorship, prior audits, implementation familiarity, and subjective interpretation have been independently challenged.
4. Every influence has received exactly one constitutional classification.
5. Every Certification Weakness has generated a required doctrine or procedural amendment.
6. Every Critical Certification Defect has been identified, documented, and scheduled for remediation.
7. Complete independence evidence has been generated and preserved within the Evidence Repository.
8. An Auditor Independence Validation Report has been produced.
9. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
10. No unresolved auditor influence remains capable of affecting ECS-003 certification outcomes.
