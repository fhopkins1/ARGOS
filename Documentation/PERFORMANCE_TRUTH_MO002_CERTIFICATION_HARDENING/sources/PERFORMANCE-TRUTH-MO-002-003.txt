# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-003

# Evidence Sufficiency Audit

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Evidence Sufficiency Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether the evidence required by the ECS-003 certification process is constitutionally sufficient to independently prove every certified property of the Performance Truth Office.

This audit assumes that previously accepted evidence may demonstrate implementation activity without actually proving constitutional correctness.

The objective is to eliminate evidence that merely suggests correctness and replace it with evidence that objectively proves constitutional compliance.

# Audit Objectives

This audit shall determine whether certification evidence independently proves:

* constitutional correctness;
* implementation correctness;
* deterministic execution;
* constitutional ownership;
* lifecycle compliance;
* immutable historical truth;
* complete traceability;
* reconciliation correctness;
* replay correctness;
* fail-closed behavior.

Evidence shall prove constitutional properties rather than merely document implementation behavior.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Every certification claim shall be supported by evidence that is:

* objective;
* deterministic;
* independently reproducible;
* constitutionally relevant;
* complete;
* tamper-evident;
* historically preserved.

No certification decision shall depend upon undocumented knowledge or subjective interpretation.

---

# MO-003-001 Evidence Inventory

Independently identify every evidence artifact generated during ECS-003 certification, including:

* constitutional audit reports;
* implementation certification reports;
* behavioral verification reports;
* reconciliation reports;
* replay reports;
* traceability reports;
* evidence packages;
* certification summaries;
* mutation reports;
* clean-room certification reports.

Produce a complete constitutional evidence inventory.

---

# MO-003-002 Evidence-to-Claim Mapping

For every certification claim, identify the exact evidence supporting it.

Every constitutional claim shall map to explicit evidence demonstrating:

* the claimed property;
* the observed behavior;
* the constitutional requirement;
* the verification procedure.

Claims lacking explicit evidence shall constitute certification defects.

---

# MO-003-003 Correctness Proof Audit

Determine whether existing evidence independently proves:

* calculation correctness;
* publication correctness;
* reconciliation correctness;
* correction correctness;
* revision correctness;
* benchmark correctness;
* attribution correctness.

Evidence that merely records execution shall not be accepted as proof of correctness.

---

# MO-003-004 Determinism Proof Audit

Verify that evidence proves:

* identical execution under identical conditions;
* identical calculations;
* identical publications;
* identical replay;
* identical reconciliation;
* identical certification outcomes.

Evidence shall demonstrate determinism rather than assume it.

---

# MO-003-005 Ownership Proof Audit

Determine whether evidence independently proves:

* constitutional ownership;
* ownership transfer;
* authority boundaries;
* custody transitions;
* ownership invariants.

Ownership shall never be inferred solely from implementation structure.

---

# MO-003-006 Traceability Proof Audit

Verify that evidence independently proves complete provenance through:

* Workflow Execution;
* Workflow Execution Token;
* Decision Objects;
* Authorizations;
* Risk;
* Broker;
* Monitoring;
* Closed Position Truth;
* calculations;
* evidence packages;
* certification artifacts.

Broken provenance shall invalidate the supporting evidence.

---

# MO-003-007 Replay Proof Audit

Determine whether replay evidence proves:

* deterministic reconstruction;
* historical equivalence;
* calculation equivalence;
* evidence equivalence;
* certification equivalence.

Replay evidence shall demonstrate constitutional reproducibility.

---

# MO-003-008 Reconciliation Proof Audit

Verify that reconciliation evidence independently proves:

* dependency agreement;
* discrepancy detection;
* discrepancy resolution;
* reconciliation completeness;
* deterministic reconciliation outcomes.

Evidence shall demonstrate constitutional agreement rather than merely report reconciliation execution.

---

# MO-003-009 Fail-Closed Proof Audit

Determine whether evidence independently proves fail-closed behavior for:

* missing evidence;
* corrupted evidence;
* dependency failures;
* ownership violations;
* reconciliation failures;
* temporal violations;
* duplicate publication;
* invalid calculations.

Evidence shall demonstrate prevention of unsafe behavior.

---

# MO-003-010 Evidence Independence Audit

Verify that every evidence artifact can be independently interpreted without requiring:

* implementation authors;
* repository history;
* undocumented procedures;
* institutional memory;
* subjective explanation.

Evidence shall remain self-sufficient.

---

# MO-003-011 Evidence Completeness Audit

Determine whether evidence completely covers:

* every constitutional requirement;
* every behavioral requirement;
* every interface;
* every dependency;
* every failure mode;
* every certification decision.

Coverage gaps shall constitute certification deficiencies.

---

# MO-003-012 Evidence Integrity Audit

Verify every evidence artifact possesses:

* immutable identity;
* integrity verification;
* provenance;
* generation history;
* custody history;
* timestamp integrity;
* version history.

Evidence integrity shall be independently verifiable.

---

# MO-003-013 Evidence Minimality Audit

Determine whether any evidence artifact:

* duplicates another artifact;
* contributes no independent constitutional proof;
* exists solely for convenience;
* may be removed without reducing proof strength.

Evidence shall remain both sufficient and constitutionally minimal.

---

# MO-003-014 Proof Strength Classification

Classify every certification proof according to constitutional strength:

* **Class I — Conclusive Proof**

  * Independently establishes the claimed constitutional property.

* **Class II — Corroborating Proof**

  * Strengthens a claim but is insufficient independently.

* **Class III — Contextual Evidence**

  * Provides supporting context only.

* **Class IV — Informational Artifact**

  * Documents implementation without proving constitutional compliance.

Certification decisions shall rely only upon Class I proofs, with Class II proofs serving corroborative purposes. Class III and Class IV artifacts shall not independently justify certification.

# Constitutional Hardening Requirements

Every insufficient proof shall result in one or more of the following:

* strengthened constitutional evidence requirements;
* additional certification artifacts;
* improved traceability requirements;
* enhanced replay verification;
* expanded deterministic verification;
* strengthened fail-closed validation;
* refinement of ECS-003 certification doctrine.

No certification property shall remain supported by insufficient evidence.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The certification process shall immediately fail whenever:

* constitutional claims lack objective supporting evidence;
* correctness cannot be independently demonstrated;
* determinism is assumed rather than proven;
* ownership cannot be objectively verified;
* provenance is incomplete;
* replay cannot independently establish historical equivalence;
* reconciliation correctness cannot be demonstrated;
* fail-closed behavior lacks objective proof;
* evidence integrity cannot be verified;
* certification depends upon subjective interpretation.

No implementation shall receive ECS-003 certification until every constitutional certification claim is supported by constitutionally sufficient evidence.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

* every certification claim has been mapped to objective supporting evidence;
* every required constitutional property is supported by independently reproducible proof;
* evidence demonstrates correctness rather than merely implementation activity;
* determinism, ownership, traceability, reconciliation, replay, and fail-closed behavior are objectively proven;
* evidence artifacts are complete, self-sufficient, tamper-evident, and historically preserved;
* redundant evidence has been eliminated without reducing proof strength;
* every certification proof has been formally classified by constitutional strength;
* no certification decision depends upon undocumented knowledge, subjective interpretation, or insufficient evidence;
* the ECS-003 certification process demonstrates constitutionally sufficient evidence for every certified property of the Performance Truth Office.
