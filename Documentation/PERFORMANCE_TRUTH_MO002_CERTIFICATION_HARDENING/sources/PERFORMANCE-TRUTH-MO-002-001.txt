# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-001

# Certification Assumption Discovery

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the adversarial discovery, classification, and resolution of every assumption relied upon by the ECS-003 certification process used to certify the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether any certification conclusion depends upon facts, interpretations, environmental conditions, auditor judgments, repository representations, evidence claims, or procedural conventions that have not themselves been constitutionally proven.

The current certification baseline shall not be presumed complete, independent, or correct.

Every certification decision shall be treated as potentially invalid until all assumptions supporting that decision have been identified, tested, and resolved.

No certification conclusion may depend upon an unsupported assumption.

---

Nothing within this order may weaken constitutional guarantees, reduce required evidence, or increase discretionary auditor authority.

Where an existing certification conclusion depends upon an unsupported assumption, that conclusion shall be treated as constitutionally unproven until the assumption is resolved.

---

# Modification Objectives

This order shall:

* identify every explicit and implicit assumption within the ECS-003 certification process;
* identify where each assumption enters audit planning, execution, evidence evaluation, proof construction, finding classification, and certification decision-making;
* determine whether each assumption is constitutionally proven;
* identify certification conclusions dependent upon each assumption;
* test whether false certification can occur when an assumption is false;
* eliminate reliance upon unverifiable convention;
* formalize necessary assumptions as explicit certification requirements;
* remove unnecessary assumptions from certification methodology;
* establish permanent evidence of assumption resolution;
* determine whether previously issued certification decisions require revalidation.

---

# Certification Assumption Definition

For purposes of this order, a certification assumption is any condition treated as true, complete, authentic, representative, deterministic, independent, or sufficient without direct constitutional proof.

Certification assumptions include, but are not limited to:

* presumed repository completeness;
* presumed honesty of repository documentation;
* presumed correspondence between documentation and behavior;
* presumed completeness of test suites;
* presumed representativeness of test fixtures;
* presumed authenticity of evidence;
* presumed independence of auditors;
* presumed correctness of audit tooling;
* presumed determinism of clean-room environments;
* presumed equivalence between sampled and untested behavior;
* presumed completeness of constitutional traceability matrices;
* presumed absence of hidden execution paths;
* presumed validity of dependency substitutes;
* presumed correctness of generated reports;
* presumed inability of implementation authors to conceal defects;
* presumed sufficiency of passing tests;
* presumed correctness of finding severity;
* presumed equivalence between reproduced output and constitutionally correct output;
* presumed validity of prior certification results;
* presumed completeness of ECS-003 itself.

An assumption remains within scope whether it exists in doctrine, audit procedure, scripts, tooling, templates, reports, auditor practice, test design, or informal interpretation.

# Discovery Method

For every certification requirement, audit procedure, proof, finding, and decision, the auditor shall ask:

1. What must be true for this certification step to be valid?
2. Where is that condition constitutionally required?
3. What evidence proves the condition?
4. Who produced that evidence?
5. Who independently verified it?
6. Could the condition be false while the audit still passes?
7. Could repository authors manipulate the condition?
8. Could two auditors interpret the condition differently?
9. Could tooling produce a false result?
10. Does the conclusion prove the constitutional property or merely support an inference?
11. What happens when the assumption is intentionally falsified?
12. Would the certification process detect that falsification deterministically?

Any certification step lacking deterministic answers shall generate a Certification Assumption Candidate.

# Assumption Classification

Every Certification Assumption Candidate shall receive exactly one classification.

## Constitutionally Proven

The assumption is directly established by explicit constitutional doctrine and independently verified evidence.

The governing requirement and proof shall be cited.

---

## Indirectly Supported

The assumption is supported by related doctrine or evidence but is not directly proven.

Indirect support shall not authorize final certification unless the logical derivation is complete and no compliant counterexample exists.

---

## Unsupported but Necessary

The assumption is necessary to reach a certification conclusion but is neither directly proven nor necessarily derived.

The certification process shall be modified to establish or eliminate the assumption.

Affected certification conclusions shall be suspended.

---

## Unsupported and Unnecessary

The assumption is not required for constitutionally valid certification.

The audit process shall be modified to remove reliance upon it.

---

## Contradictory

The assumption conflicts with constitutional doctrine, verified evidence, or another certification requirement.

Affected certification conclusions shall fail.

---

## Indeterminate

The available doctrine and evidence cannot determine whether the assumption is valid.

Indeterminate assumptions shall be treated as unsupported.

---

# Falsification Procedure

Every assumption shall be tested through one or more controlled falsification scenarios.

Each falsification shall:

1. preserve the certified baseline;
2. alter only the condition represented by the assumption;
3. execute the affected certification procedure;
4. determine whether the audit detects the false condition;
5. determine whether certification can still pass;
6. identify the first missed detection point;
7. record the resulting false-positive or false-negative path;
8. verify whether independent auditors reach equivalent conclusions;
9. restore the baseline;
10. reproduce the result independently.

Any assumption whose falsification permits an incorrect PASS shall constitute a certification defect.

---

# False-Certification Path Analysis

For each Unsupported, Contradictory, or Indeterminate assumption, the audit shall determine whether it can produce:

* false repository completeness;
* false implementation completeness;
* false behavioral conformance;
* false interface conformance;
* false evidence completeness;
* false traceability completeness;
* false replay certification;
* false clean-room certification;
* false fail-closed certification;
* false constitutional compliance;
* false authorization for constitutional freeze.

Every false-certification path shall generate a Certification Defect Record.

---

* evidence insufficiency;

* required audit control;
* required evidence;
* required tooling changes;
* required doctrine amendment;
* re-certification requirement;

No certification defect may be closed through assertion alone.

---

# Prior Certification Impact Review

The review shall determine whether any prior PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, freeze authorization, or certification report relied upon an unsupported assumption.

For every affected decision, the review shall classify the impact as:

* no impact;
* evidence supplementation required;
* bounded re-audit required;
* complete re-certification required;
* certification invalidated.

Invalidated certifications shall not remain operationally authoritative.

# Fail-Closed Behavior

Certification shall fail closed whenever:

* a certification conclusion depends upon an unclassified assumption;
* an assumption is Unsupported, Contradictory, or Indeterminate;
* required evidence proves existence but not the claimed constitutional property;
* audit tooling cannot be independently validated;
* auditor independence cannot be demonstrated;
* falsification permits an incorrect PASS;
* clean-room isolation cannot be proven;
* prior certification relies upon an unresolved assumption;
* proof premises cannot be traced to objective evidence;
* two constitutionally competent auditors can reach incompatible decisions from identical evidence;
* certification doctrine permits discretionary resolution of a material ambiguity.

No affected certification decision, constitutional freeze, or operational authorization shall remain authoritative until the assumption is resolved and the affected property is independently re-certified.

---

# Completion Decision

This order shall produce exactly one completion decision.

## PASS

Every discovered certification assumption has been classified and resolved.

Every necessary premise is constitutionally proven.

All unsupported assumptions have been eliminated or formalized through deterministic certification requirements.

No unresolved Critical or Major certification defects remain.

---

## CONDITIONAL PASS

No unsupported assumption presently permits false certification, but bounded Minor deficiencies remain.

Each deficiency shall possess an approved owner, doctrine amendment, evidence requirement, and completion condition.

All conditions shall be resolved before Certification Hardening Closure.

---

## FAIL

One or more assumptions remain unsupported, contradictory, indeterminate, or capable of producing false certification.

Affected certification decisions shall be suspended or invalidated until remediation and independent re-certification are complete.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every phase of the ECS-003 certification process has been reviewed for explicit and implicit assumptions.
2. Every discovered assumption has an immutable Certification Assumption Candidate Record.
3. Every assumption has received exactly one constitutional classification.
4. Every Constitutionally Proven assumption possesses direct doctrine and independently verified evidence.
5. Every Indirectly Supported assumption has been formally derived or reclassified as unsupported.
6. Every unsupported necessary assumption has generated an explicit certification-process modification.
7. Every unsupported unnecessary assumption has been removed from audit methodology.
8. Every assumption has been subjected to controlled falsification.
9. Every false-certification path has generated a Certification Defect Record.
10. All prior certifications potentially affected by unsupported assumptions have undergone impact review.
11. Complete traceability exists between assumptions, audit procedures, evidence, proofs, findings, and certification decisions.
12. All evidence and resolutions have been preserved within the Evidence Repository.
13. Exactly one completion decision—**PASS**, **CONDITIONAL PASS**, or **FAIL**—has been issued.
14. No unsupported certification assumption remains capable of contributing to an ECS-003 PASS decision.
