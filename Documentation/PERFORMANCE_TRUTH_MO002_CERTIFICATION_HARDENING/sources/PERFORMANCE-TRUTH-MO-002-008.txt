# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-008

# False Positive Resistance

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the False Positive Resistance Audit for the Performance Truth Office.

The purpose of this order is to determine whether the ECS-003 certification process can be deceived into certifying implementations that are constitutionally non-compliant.

This audit assumes that certification is vulnerable until proven otherwise.

The objective is to deliberately construct defective implementations that appear constitutionally compliant and determine whether the certification process rejects every defect through objective constitutional evidence.

Every successful false-positive certification constitutes a constitutional defect in the certification process rather than an implementation defect.

# Audit Objectives

This audit shall determine whether ECS-003 rejects implementations containing:

* constitutional violations;
* incomplete implementations;
* hidden behavioral defects;
* deceptive evidence;
* incomplete traceability;
* incomplete reconciliation;
* replay inconsistencies;
* ownership violations;
* deterministic failures;
* fail-closed failures.

Certification shall reject every defective implementation regardless of its apparent completeness.

---

# Constitutional Principles

False-positive resistance shall satisfy the following principles:

* objective verification;
* adversarial certification;
* implementation independence;
* deterministic audit behavior;
* evidence-based certification;
* constitutional conservatism.

Certification shall prefer rejection over unsupported acceptance whenever constitutional certainty cannot be established.

---

# MO-008-001 Defective Implementation Catalog

Construct a catalog of intentionally defective implementations representing realistic constitutional failures including:

* omitted behavior;
* incorrect behavior;
* hidden behavior;
* undocumented behavior;
* partially implemented behavior;
* inconsistent behavior;
* deceptive behavior.

The catalog shall represent the complete false-positive challenge suite.

---

# MO-008-002 Constitutional Defect Injection

Independently inject constitutional defects including:

* ownership violations;
* lifecycle violations;
* truth ambiguity;
* reconciliation omissions;
* temporal inconsistencies;
* missing fail-closed behavior;
* invalid publication behavior.

Certification shall reject every injected defect.

---

# MO-008-003 Behavioral Defect Injection

Construct implementations exhibiting:

* incorrect calculations;
* incorrect attribution;
* incorrect benchmark comparisons;
* incorrect revisions;
* incorrect correction propagation;
* inconsistent replay;
* inconsistent reconciliation.

Observable behavior shall reveal the defect.

---

# MO-008-004 Evidence Deception Analysis

Construct repositories containing:

* fabricated evidence;
* incomplete evidence;
* duplicated evidence;
* misleading evidence;
* evidence unrelated to observed behavior;
* internally inconsistent evidence.

Certification shall validate evidence rather than accept its existence.

---

# MO-008-005 Traceability Deception Analysis

Construct implementations where:

* provenance is incomplete;
* lineage is fabricated;
* dependency chains terminate early;
* historical reconstruction fails;
* certification artifacts disagree with traceability.

Broken provenance shall prevent certification.

---

# MO-008-006 Replay Deception Analysis

Construct implementations capable of:

* successful initial execution;
* incorrect replay;
* inconsistent historical reconstruction;
* replay divergence under repeated execution.

Certification shall independently verify replay equivalence.

---

# MO-008-007 Hidden Dependency Analysis

Introduce hidden implementation dependencies including:

* undocumented configuration;
* external services;
* hidden data sources;
* undocumented initialization;
* implicit execution order;
* environment-specific behavior.

Certification shall reject implementations requiring undocumented dependencies.

---

# MO-008-008 Mutation Concealment Analysis

Construct repositories where defects are intentionally concealed through:

* conditional execution;
* dead code;
* alternate execution paths;
* configuration switches;
* environment-dependent behavior;
* test-specific implementations.

Certification shall evaluate constitutional behavior rather than implementation appearance.

---

# MO-008-009 Incomplete Test Detection

Construct implementations that satisfy existing certification tests while leaving constitutional behavior unimplemented.

Verify that certification detects:

* untested behavior;
* uncovered constitutional requirements;
* incomplete implementations;
* missing edge-case handling.

Certification shall not rely solely on positive-path testing.

---

# MO-008-010 Auditor Confirmation Bias Analysis

Conduct certification using intentionally misleading but plausible supporting artifacts.

Verify that auditors rely exclusively upon:

* constitutional doctrine;
* objective evidence;
* deterministic reproduction;
* observable implementation behavior.

Prior certification history shall not influence conclusions.

---

# MO-008-011 Certification Decision Consistency

Require independent auditors to certify every defective implementation.

Verify that:

* every auditor rejects identical defects;
* rejection reasoning is equivalent;
* constitutional findings are consistent;
* certification outcomes remain deterministic.

Differences in rejection shall be investigated.

---

# MO-008-012 Defect Detection Coverage

For every injected constitutional defect, identify:

* the certification activity that detected it;
* supporting evidence;
* governing constitutional requirement;
* certification decision.

Every detectable defect shall possess explicit audit coverage.

---

# MO-008-013 False Positive Root Cause Analysis

For every defective implementation that is incorrectly certified:

Determine:

* why certification failed;
* which constitutional requirement was insufficient;
* which evidence requirement failed;
* which audit procedure was incomplete;
* which ECS-003 doctrine requires modification.

Every false positive shall generate constitutional hardening.

---

# MO-008-014 Certification Hardening Verification

Following implementation of corrective modifications, repeat the complete false-positive certification campaign.

Verify that:

* every previously accepted defective implementation is rejected;
* no new false-positive paths exist;
* certification behavior remains deterministic;
* audit evidence remains objective and reproducible.

Certification shall demonstrate measurable improvement over its previous baseline.

# Constitutional Hardening Requirements

Every successful false-positive certification shall require one or more of the following:

* strengthened constitutional doctrine;
* expanded audit coverage;
* additional certification tests;
* enhanced evidence requirements;
* improved traceability verification;
* strengthened replay validation;
* refined auditor procedures;
* amendments to ECS-003 certification doctrine.

A false positive shall never be resolved solely by implementation changes if the certification methodology permitted its acceptance.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The ECS-003 certification process shall immediately fail whenever:

* a constitutionally defective implementation receives certification;
* constitutional defects cannot be objectively detected;
* evidence deception cannot be distinguished from valid evidence;
* replay inconsistencies remain undetected;
* hidden dependencies escape certification;
* traceability defects remain unreported;
* certification depends upon undocumented assumptions;
* auditor conclusions rely upon subjective interpretation;
* defect detection coverage cannot be demonstrated.

Certification shall never issue a PASS whenever constitutional compliance cannot be objectively proven.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

* a comprehensive catalog of intentionally defective implementations has been independently certified against ECS-003;
* every constitutional, behavioral, evidence, traceability, replay, ownership, and fail-closed defect is deterministically rejected;
* certification demonstrates resistance to deceptive repositories, fabricated evidence, hidden dependencies, and concealed implementation defects;
* independent auditors consistently reject identical defective implementations using equivalent constitutional reasoning;
* every successful false-positive path has been eliminated through constitutional hardening of the certification process;
* repeat certification confirms that previously accepted defective implementations are now rejected;
* no unresolved false-positive certification path remains capable of granting ECS-003 certification to a constitutionally non-compliant Performance Truth implementation.
