# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-002

# Audit Coverage Analysis

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the certification-hardening audit responsible for determining whether every constitutional requirement governing the Performance Truth Office is explicitly, independently, and sufficiently examined by the ECS-003 certification process.

The objective is to identify every unaudited, partially audited, indirectly audited, redundantly audited, ambiguously audited, or falsely assumed constitutional requirement.

This order shall not evaluate whether the implementation is compliant.

It shall evaluate whether the certification process is capable of reliably determining compliance.

Every constitutional requirement shall possess explicit audit coverage, defined proof obligations, deterministic acceptance criteria, and independently reproducible evidence.

# Modification Scope

This audit shall examine the complete certification architecture governing the Performance Truth Office, including:

* constitutional requirement discovery;
* audit-control design;
* test coverage;
* proof coverage;
* evidence coverage;
* negative-test coverage;
* mutation coverage;
* lifecycle coverage;
* interface coverage;
* failure coverage;
* replay coverage;
* reconciliation coverage;
* certification-decision coverage.

The audit shall include every requirement established under RM-001, every implementation-certification obligation established under RM-002, every enterprise-certification obligation established under RM-003, and every applicable hardening requirement established under MO-001.

---

# Constitutional Principles

The audit shall satisfy the following principles.

1. Every constitutional requirement shall map to one or more explicit audit controls.

2. Every audit control shall map back to an authoritative constitutional requirement.

3. No requirement shall be considered covered solely because adjacent behavior is tested.

4. No requirement shall be considered covered solely by documentation review.

5. Positive-path evidence alone shall not establish complete coverage.

6. Coverage shall include success, rejection, failure, correction, replay, and boundary behavior where applicable.

7. Audit coverage shall be independently reproducible.

8. Duplicated audit effort shall not conceal uncovered requirements.

9. Coverage metrics shall distinguish execution from proof.

10. Unproven coverage shall be classified as absent.

Controls lacking explicit constitutional mappings shall be classified as unsupported audit activity.

# Bidirectional Coverage Matrix

The auditor shall construct a bidirectional traceability matrix mapping:

* every constitutional requirement to its audit controls; and
* every audit control to its constitutional requirements.

The matrix shall expose:

* requirements with no controls;
* controls with no requirements;
* requirements covered only indirectly;
* requirements covered by incomplete controls;
* duplicated controls;
* conflicting controls;
* controls lacking deterministic acceptance criteria;
* controls lacking sufficient evidence.

A requirement shall not be considered covered merely because it appears in a checklist.

---

# Coverage Classification

Every constitutional requirement shall be classified as one of the following:

* Fully and Directly Audited;
* Fully Audited Through Multiple Necessary Controls;
* Partially Audited;
* Indirectly Audited;
* Documentation-Only Reviewed;
* Evidence-Only Reviewed;
* Duplicatively Audited;
* Contradictorily Audited;
* Unaudited;
* Not Auditable as Currently Written.

Only requirements classified as Fully and Directly Audited or Fully Audited Through Multiple Necessary Controls shall satisfy certification-hardening requirements.

---

# Direct Coverage Requirements

Direct audit coverage shall independently exercise the constitutional property being certified.

Direct coverage shall require, as applicable:

* observable execution;
* deterministic expected results;
* constitutional evidence;
* independent reproduction;
* negative testing;
* fail-closed testing;
* provenance verification;
* state verification before and after execution.

Proxy evidence shall not satisfy direct coverage unless doctrine explicitly establishes that the proxy necessarily proves the claimed property.

---

# Positive Behavior Coverage

For every permitted constitutional behavior, verify that the certification process tests:

* valid preconditions;
* authorized execution;
* required state transition;
* required output;
* required evidence;
* ownership preservation;
* downstream effects;
* historical preservation.

Successful execution without proof of the applicable constitutional guarantees shall constitute incomplete coverage.

---

# Prohibited Behavior Coverage

For every prohibited constitutional behavior, verify that the certification process attempts the prohibited action and proves deterministic rejection.

The audit shall include, as applicable:

* unauthorized creation;
* unauthorized mutation;
* illegal lifecycle transition;
* duplicate publication;
* orphaned object creation;
* invalid ownership transfer;
* unsupported calculation;
* uncertified publication;
* evidence-free certification;
* historical deletion.

A prohibition shall not be considered audited unless attempted or constitutionally proven unreachable.

---

# Failure Coverage

For every constitutionally defined failure condition, verify that the certification process examines:

* detection;
* classification;
* fail-closed response;
* publication suppression;
* certification suppression;
* failure evidence;
* historical preservation;
* recovery eligibility;
* downstream observability.

Testing only the existence of an error response shall not establish fail-closed coverage.

---

# Lifecycle Coverage

For every canonical object, verify that audit controls cover:

* creation;
* validation;

* correction;
* revision;
* supersession;
* revocation where applicable;
* archival;
* historical replay.

The audit shall verify both permitted and prohibited transitions.

Unexercised lifecycle states shall be classified as coverage gaps.

# Coverage Depth Requirements

For each constitutional requirement, the auditor shall determine whether existing controls cover:

* nominal behavior;
* boundary behavior;
* invalid behavior;
* adversarial behavior;
* failure behavior;
* recovery behavior;
* replay behavior;
* cross-office behavior;
* historical behavior.

A requirement tested only under nominal conditions shall be classified as partially audited.

---

# Duplicate Audit Analysis

Duplicated audit effort shall be identified and classified as:

* necessary independent corroboration;
* necessary layered control;
* redundant execution;
* redundant evidence review;
* conflicting duplication;
* duplication masking a coverage gap.

Redundant controls may be consolidated only where coverage strength is preserved.

No control shall be removed solely to simplify the audit.

---

# Indirect Coverage Analysis

Every claim of indirect coverage shall identify:

* the directly tested property;
* the property inferred;
* the constitutional rule making the inference valid;
* the proof that no counterexample exists.

Where the inference depends on implementation architecture, convention, or assumption, the requirement shall be classified as not directly covered.

---

# Audit Blind-Spot Discovery

The auditor shall search for requirements that escape coverage because they are:

* distributed across multiple doctrine sections;
* expressed as invariants rather than actions;
* embedded in definitions;
* implied by ownership;
* triggered only during failure;
* triggered only during correction or revision;
* dependent upon cross-office sequencing;
* dependent upon historical depth;
* dependent upon concurrency;
* difficult to observe directly.

Every discovered blind spot shall become an explicit audit obligation.

---

# Adversarial Coverage Validation

The auditor shall construct defective implementations that satisfy existing tests while violating selected constitutional requirements.

For each successful deception, determine:

* which requirement was violated;
* which audit controls passed;
* why the controls failed to detect the defect;
* what coverage addition is required;
* whether similar requirements are exposed.

Every successful deception shall constitute proof of a coverage defect.

---

# Constitutional Remediation Requirements

Every coverage defect shall result in one or more of the following:

1. Creation of a direct audit control.

2. Expansion of an incomplete audit control.

3. Addition of negative or adversarial testing.

4. Addition of fail-closed verification.

5. Addition of evidence-sufficiency requirements.

6. Addition of independent reproduction requirements.

7. Clarification of an ambiguous constitutional requirement.

8. Removal or consolidation of misleading duplicated controls.

9. Proof that the requirement is necessarily covered by another control.

No coverage defect may be closed through unsupported auditor judgment.

# Fail-Closed Certification Requirements

Certification shall fail closed whenever:

* any material constitutional requirement lacks direct or constitutionally sufficient audit coverage;
* any required control lacks deterministic acceptance criteria;
* any certification claim lacks sufficient evidence;
* any prohibited behavior lacks rejection coverage;
* any failure mode lacks fail-closed coverage;
* any material traceability edge remains unaudited;
* any adversarial defect can evade certification;
* any requirement remains unauditable due to unresolved ambiguity.

No aggregate coverage score may override a material coverage failure.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold.

* Every constitutional requirement has explicit audit coverage.
* Every audit control has explicit constitutional authority.
* Coverage is bidirectionally traceable.
* Existence evidence is not automatically correctness evidence.
* Positive-path testing does not substitute for negative testing.
* Documentation review does not substitute for behavioral verification.
* Duplicated controls do not conceal missing controls.
* Material audit gaps prevent certification.
* Coverage conclusions are independently reproducible.
* No constitutional requirement is certified through assumption alone.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This order shall be considered complete when:

1. Every applicable constitutional statement has been decomposed into atomic, auditable requirements.
2. Every requirement has been mapped to one or more explicit audit controls.
3. Every audit control has been mapped to authoritative constitutional requirements.
4. Unaudited, partially audited, indirectly audited, duplicated, and contradictory coverage has been identified.
5. Positive, prohibited, failure, recovery, replay, historical, and enterprise behavior coverage has been evaluated where applicable.
6. Every canonical object, lifecycle, interface, evidence obligation, traceability edge, and fail-closed requirement has explicit coverage.
7. Adversarial defective implementations have been used to test whether the existing audit can be deceived.
8. Every discovered coverage defect has been remediated or constitutionally disproven.
9. Certification fails closed whenever any material requirement lacks sufficient coverage.
10. Independent auditors can reproduce the complete coverage analysis and reach an equivalent certification conclusion without relying upon undocumented assumptions.
