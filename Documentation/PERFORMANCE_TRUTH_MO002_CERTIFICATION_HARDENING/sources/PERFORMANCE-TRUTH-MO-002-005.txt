# PERFORMANCE-TRUTH-MO-002-005

# Audit Mutation Testing

## Doctrine Statement

### Purpose

This Modification Order establishes the Audit Mutation Testing campaign for the Performance Truth Office certification process.

Its purpose is to determine whether the ECS-003 audit process can reliably detect intentional, controlled corruption introduced into the artifacts, evidence, implementation, documentation, and conclusions used to certify the office.

This order shall assume that a certification process capable of passing mutated artifacts is constitutionally defective.

Every material mutation shall either produce deterministic audit failure or be proven constitutionally irrelevant.

---

---

# Campaign Objective

The objective of this campaign is to test the certification process rather than the Performance Truth implementation.

The campaign shall systematically introduce controlled mutations into certification-relevant artifacts and determine whether the audit process:

* detects the mutation;
* identifies the affected requirement;
* rejects invalid evidence;
* preserves audit traceability;
* reaches the correct certification decision;
* produces deterministic findings.

An undetected material mutation constitutes a certification-process defect.

---

# Mutation Scope

Mutation testing shall include:

* constitutional evidence;
* source code;
* configuration;
* dependency manifests;
* repository manifests;
* schemas;
* canonical objects;
* truth records;
* timestamps;
* ownership records;
* traceability records;
* reconciliation artifacts;
* replay artifacts;
* audit documentation;
* certification reports;
* package integrity records.

No artifact relied upon by certification shall be presumed mutation-resistant.

---

# Mutation Governance

Every mutation shall be:

* intentional;
* isolated where practicable;
* uniquely identified;
* reproducible;
* evidence-backed;
* reversible;
* mapped to an expected audit outcome.

Uncontrolled corruption shall not constitute valid mutation testing.

---

# Baseline Establishment

Before mutation testing begins, the auditor shall establish a verified clean certification baseline.

The baseline shall include:

* repository package identity;
* artifact inventory;
* cryptographic integrity records;
* expected certification findings;
* expected certification decision;
* audit toolchain versions;
* dependency versions;
* environmental configuration.

Mutation results shall be compared exclusively against this verified baseline.

---

# Mutation Catalog

A complete Mutation Catalog shall be established.

Each mutation shall identify:

* mutation identifier;
* target artifact;
* original state;
* mutated state;
* affected constitutional requirement;
* expected detection mechanism;
* expected audit finding;
* expected certification consequence;
* observed result.

No mutation result shall be accepted without catalog entry.

---

# Evidence Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate constitutional evidence through:

* removal;
* truncation;
* duplication;
* reordering;
* substitution;
* identifier alteration;
* provenance alteration;
* timestamp alteration;
* signature or hash alteration;
* scope alteration.

The audit shall reject evidence that no longer proves the claimed constitutional property.

---

# Source Code Mutation Testing

The campaign shall introduce controlled source-code mutations affecting:

* calculation logic;
* validation rules;
* ownership enforcement;
* reconciliation behavior;
* publication behavior;
* replay behavior;
* evidence generation;
* fail-closed behavior;
* lifecycle transitions.

The audit shall detect every mutation that changes constitutional behavior.

---

# Manifest Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate:

* repository manifests;
* dependency manifests;
* module inventories;
* configuration inventories;
* interface inventories;
* generated-artifact listings.

Mutations shall include:

* missing entries;
* additional hidden entries;
* incorrect versions;
* altered paths;
* invalid identities;
* stale dependency declarations.

The audit shall detect divergence between manifests and actual repository contents.

---

# Documentation Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate constitutional and implementation documentation through:

* removal of requirements;
* altered definitions;
* conflicting interface contracts;
* incorrect lifecycle descriptions;
* omitted dependencies;
* altered evidence obligations;
* inconsistent terminology.

The audit shall identify conflicts between documentation, implementation, and constitutional authority.

---

# Certification Report Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate certification reports through:

* altered findings;
* omitted failures;
* changed severity;
* changed certification outcomes;
* unsupported PASS declarations;
* deleted evidence references;
* false closure statements.

The certification process shall detect conclusions unsupported by underlying evidence.

---

# Truth Record Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate Performance Truth Records through:

* value alteration;
* identity alteration;
* ownership alteration;
* source-reference alteration;
* attribution alteration;
* revision-chain alteration;
* benchmark-reference alteration;
* certification-state alteration.

Every constitutionally material mutation shall invalidate the affected record and produce deterministic audit failure.

---

# Timestamp Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate:

* Event Time;
* Effective Time;
* Observation Time;
* Calculation Time;
* Publication Time;
* Correction Time;
* Certification Time;
* Archival Time.

Mutations shall include:

* inversion;
* duplication;
* truncation;
* precision alteration;
* impossible ordering;
* invalid effective windows.

Temporal mutations shall be detected through constitutional temporal validation.

---

# Ownership Mutation Testing

The campaign shall alter ownership records through:

* ownership removal;
* duplicated ownership;
* unauthorized transfer;
* conflicting ownership;
* owner substitution;
* unowned object creation.

The audit shall reject every mutation that violates constitutional ownership.

---

# Traceability Mutation Testing

The campaign shall alter provenance and traceability through:

* broken links;
* substituted identifiers;
* missing workflow references;
* missing decision references;
* missing authorization references;
* incorrect execution references;
* incomplete evidence chains;
* circular traceability.

The audit shall detect every material provenance defect.

---

# Reconciliation Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate reconciliation inputs and outputs through:

* value discrepancies;
* missing records;
* duplicate records;
* altered source authority;
* false reconciliation success;
* suppressed discrepancy findings;
* inconsistent correction state.

The audit shall reject false reconciliation conclusions.

---

# Replay Mutation Testing

The campaign shall mutate replay dependencies including:

* historical configuration;
* benchmark versions;
* calculation versions;
* revision history;
* dependency versions;
* event ordering;
* source evidence.

The audit shall detect any mutation that prevents exact historical reconstruction.

---

# Fail-Closed Mutation Testing

The campaign shall intentionally mutate fail-closed behavior so that the implementation:

* continues after missing evidence;
* publishes incomplete calculations;
* ignores dependency failure;
* accepts ownership ambiguity;
* suppresses reconciliation failure;
* accepts invalid timestamps;
* proceeds after repository corruption.

The audit shall detect every fail-open mutation.

---

# Compound Mutation Testing

The campaign shall apply combinations of mutations to determine whether defects can conceal one another.

Compound mutations shall include:

* evidence corruption combined with report alteration;
* source-code mutation combined with documentation mutation;
* ownership mutation combined with traceability mutation;
* reconciliation mutation combined with timestamp mutation;
* replay mutation combined with dependency-manifest mutation.

Certification shall remain resistant to coordinated deception.

---

# Mutation Equivalence Analysis

Some mutations may not alter constitutional behavior.

For each surviving mutation, the auditor shall determine whether it is:

* constitutionally equivalent;
* unreachable;
* redundant;
* masked by stronger controls;
* insufficiently tested.

A mutation shall not be classified as harmless without objective evidence.

---

# Detection Mechanism Verification

For every detected mutation, the campaign shall identify the exact detection mechanism.

Detection mechanisms may include:

* integrity validation;
* schema validation;
* behavioral testing;
* evidence verification;
* traceability reconstruction;
* replay comparison;
* reconciliation analysis;
* independent calculation;
* cross-artifact consistency checking.

Incidental detection shall not substitute for a defined certification control.

---

# Mutation Survival Analysis

Every mutation not detected by the audit shall be classified as a surviving mutation.

Each surviving mutation shall produce:

* certification defect finding;
* affected constitutional requirement;
* failed detection mechanism;
* false-certification risk assessment;
* required audit remediation;
* permanent regression test.

No material surviving mutation may remain unresolved.

---

# Deterministic Audit Outcome

Repeated audit execution against the same mutated repository package shall produce:

* identical mutation detection;
* identical findings;
* identical severity;
* identical evidence references;
* identical certification decision.

Non-deterministic mutation results constitute certification-process failure.

---

# Independent Reproduction

An independent auditor shall reproduce a representative subset of the mutation campaign using only:

* the clean baseline package;
* the Mutation Catalog;
* the audit doctrine;
* the required tooling and instructions.

Independent reproduction shall confirm equivalent findings and certification decisions.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

* every material mutation is detected;
* every detected mutation maps to an explicit constitutional requirement;
* every surviving mutation produces certification remediation;
* every mutation result is independently reproducible;
* every material artifact is included in mutation coverage;
* certification reports cannot contradict underlying evidence;
* fail-open mutations always cause certification failure;
* compound mutations cannot conceal constitutional defects;
* identical mutated packages produce identical audit outcomes.

Violation of any invariant constitutes certification-hardening failure.

---

# Fail-Closed Requirements

The certification process shall issue a failing determination whenever:

* a material mutation is detected;
* artifact integrity cannot be established;
* mutation scope cannot be reconstructed;
* expected detection controls are absent;
* a certification report conflicts with source evidence;
* a surviving mutation could permit false certification;
* repeated audit outcomes differ;
* independent reproduction fails;
* mutation evidence is incomplete.

Certification shall not proceed while any material mutation remains unresolved.

---

---

# Acceptance Criteria

This Modification Order shall be considered complete when independent review demonstrates that:

1. every certification-relevant artifact class has been subjected to systematic mutation testing;
2. every constitutionally material mutation causes deterministic audit failure;
3. every detected mutation is mapped to the affected constitutional requirement and supporting evidence;
4. certification reports, evidence, manifests, implementation artifacts, truth records, reconciliation artifacts, and replay artifacts cannot be altered without detection;
5. compound mutations cannot conceal constitutional defects or produce false certification;
6. every surviving mutation has been remediated, incorporated into permanent audit regression coverage, or objectively proven constitutionally equivalent;
7. repeated certification of identical mutated packages produces identical findings and decisions;
8. independent auditors can reproduce the mutation results from the delivered campaign materials; and
9. the ECS-003 certification process demonstrates materially greater resistance to artifact corruption, audit deception, and false-positive certification than the prior hardened certification baseline.
