# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03-002

# Settlement Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional governance for settlement participation in the creation of authoritative Closed Position Truth. 

This order exists solely to define settlement purpose, ownership, authority, admissibility, verification, exemption, failure behavior, evidence obligations, and constitutional interaction with closure determination.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional doctrine only.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Settlement shall verify financial completion associated with an executed position.

Settlement shall not independently create authoritative Closed Position Truth.

Settlement shall not independently establish constitutional closure.

Settlement shall not override:

* incomplete execution;
* unresolved fill reconciliation;
* nonzero residual quantity;
* reconciliation failure;
* missing mandatory closure evidence;
* failed closure admissibility.

Settlement shall satisfy exactly one constitutionally admissible disposition:

* **Verified**
* **Constitutionally Exempt**

Any other settlement condition shall prevent constitutional closure when settlement participation is required.

Settlement evidence shall remain distinct from:

* broker execution truth;
* position truth;
* reconciliation truth;
* closure truth;
* performance truth.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Settlement Purpose Constitution

Define settlement as the constitutional verification of financial completion following execution.

Settlement participation shall determine whether required financial obligations associated with the position have been:

* completed;
* verified;
* reconciled where required;
* constitutionally exempted.

Settlement shall serve as one closure prerequisite where applicable.

Settlement shall not become:

* execution authority;
* reconciliation authority;
* residual-quantity authority;
* closure authority;
* truth-creation authority.

---

Closed Position Truth shall consume constitutionally admissible settlement results.

# 5. Settlement Verification Constitution

Define the requirements for settlement verification.

Settlement verification shall establish that:

* the settlement identity is valid;
* the settlement scope matches the position;
* the settlement quantity matches reconciled execution;
* the settlement amount is supported by authoritative evidence;
* the settlement status is final for closure purposes;
* the settlement evidence possesses valid provenance;
* no unresolved settlement discrepancy remains.

For every verification define:

* verifier;
* source evidence;
* verification timestamp;
* verification result;
* verification rationale;
* reconciliation obligation;
* historical preservation obligation.

Verification shall fail closed.

Missing or insufficient evidence shall not produce a Verified state.

# 7. Settlement Exemption Constitution

Establish deterministic governance for constitutionally exempt settlement.

A settlement exemption shall be permitted only when:

* the position or instrument is constitutionally exempt from ordinary settlement;
* the exemption authority is explicitly defined;
* the exemption scope matches the position;
* the exemption remains temporally valid;
* complete exemption evidence exists;
* no reconciliation conflict exists.

For every exemption define:

* exemption identity;
* exemption authority;
* governing doctrine;
* applicability;
* effective time;
* expiration;
* evidence;
* revocation conditions;
* reconciliation requirements.

An exemption shall not:

* waive execution completion;
* waive zero-residual verification;
* waive reconciliation success;
* waive closure evidence;
* authorize degraded truth.

* missing settlement evidence;

Settlement failure shall never be silently converted into an exemption.

# 9. Settlement and Closure Interaction Constitution

Establish the constitutional relationship:

```text
Execution Completion
        │
        ▼
Fill Reconciliation
        │
        ▼
Zero Residual Quantity
        │
        ▼
Reconciliation Success
        │
        ▼
Settlement Verified
        or
Settlement Constitutionally Exempt
        │
        ▼
Closure Admissibility
        │
        ▼
Constitutional Closure
```

Settlement participation shall be necessary where constitutionally required.

Settlement participation shall never be sufficient by itself.

A settlement result shall not cure failure of any other closure prerequisite.

# 11. Settlement Correction and Supersession Constitution

Define constitutional correction of settlement-related records.

Corrections shall:

* preserve the original record;
* identify the error;
* identify the correcting authority;
* provide admissible evidence;
* create a successor record;
* preserve predecessor-successor lineage;
* trigger closure reassessment where required.

Supersession shall never:

* overwrite prior settlement history;
* reuse prior identity;
* erase failed or disputed states;
* silently alter prior closure evidence.

Where previously accepted settlement evidence is invalidated, the resulting Closed Position Truth shall be reviewed under the governing correction and supersession doctrine.
