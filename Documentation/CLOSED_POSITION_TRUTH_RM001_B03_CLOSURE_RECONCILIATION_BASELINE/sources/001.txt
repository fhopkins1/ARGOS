# CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03-001

# Constitutional Closure Doctrine Order

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional doctrine governing when a trading position becomes constitutionally closed and when authoritative Closed Position Truth may be created. 

This order shall define constitutional closure, closure prerequisites, closure authority, closure admissibility, closure evidence, closure determination, closure finality, prohibited closure conditions, and deterministic failure behavior.

This order shall not modify constitutional authority established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B01**.

This order shall not modify the canonical object or lifecycle model established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02**.

This order shall not modify implementation behavior.

This order shall not perform behavioral verification.

This order shall not generate implementation proof.

This order shall not execute implementation certification activity.

# Constitutional Requirements

## 1. Establish Constitutional Closure Definition

Define constitutional closure as the authoritative determination that a trading position has permanently satisfied every constitutional condition required for recognition as closed.

A position shall become constitutionally closed only when all required closure conditions have been affirmatively established through constitutionally admissible evidence.

Constitutional closure shall not be inferred from:

* execution submission;
* execution acknowledgement;
* one or more fills;
* apparent zero quantity;
* settlement alone;
* broker classification alone;
* analytical estimation;
* elapsed time;
* operational convenience.

Authoritative Closed Position Truth shall not exist before constitutional closure.

---

## 2. Establish Mandatory Closure Criteria

Define the complete mandatory criteria for constitutional closure.

At minimum, constitutional closure shall require:

* authoritative position identity;
* execution completion;
* complete fill reconciliation;
* residual position quantity equal to zero;
* successful execution reconciliation;
* successful position reconciliation;
* settlement verified or constitutionally exempt;
* mandatory closure evidence present;
* closure evidence constitutionally admissible;
* no unresolved contradiction;
* no unresolved closure exception;
* closure determination issued by the constitutional authority.

Failure of any mandatory condition shall prohibit constitutional closure.

No criterion may be silently waived, inferred, or replaced.

* no pending executable quantity remains;
* no uncertain execution state remains.

## 7. Establish Zero-Residual Requirement

Define residual position quantity equal to zero as a mandatory closure condition.

Zero-residual verification shall require:

* authoritative opening quantity reference;
* all constitutionally relevant position changes;
* reconciled executed quantity;
* authoritative current position state;
* deterministic quantity calculation;
* absence of unresolved quantity discrepancy.

Residual quantity greater than zero shall prohibit constitutional closure.

Residual quantity less than zero shall constitute a constitutional inconsistency and shall prohibit closure.

Apparent zero quantity unsupported by reconciliation shall not satisfy the requirement.

## 9. Establish Settlement Participation Requirement

## 13. Establish Prohibited Closure Conditions

Create the Prohibited Closure Registry.

Constitutional closure shall be prohibited when any of the following exists:

* incomplete execution;
* unresolved execution status;
* incomplete fill reconciliation;
* residual quantity greater than zero;
* residual quantity less than zero;
* reconciliation failure;
* unresolved identity mismatch;
* settlement neither verified nor constitutionally exempt;
* missing mandatory evidence;
* stale evidence;
* inadmissible evidence;
* contradictory upstream evidence;
* unresolved duplicate execution;
* unresolved closure exception;
* analytical degradation used as substitute evidence;
* attempted reliance on manually asserted completion;
* attempted historical overwrite.

Every prohibited condition shall possess deterministic rejection, deferral, exception, or escalation behavior.

## 16. Establish Closure Finality Doctrine

Define constitutional closure as irreversible.

* the position shall not reopen;
* the authoritative Closed Position Record shall not be mutated;
* prior truth shall remain accessible;
* correction shall occur only through successor records;
* supersession shall preserve complete lineage;
* archival shall preserve authority and auditability.

A later-discovered error shall not reverse historical closure through mutation.

It shall initiate the constitutionally governed correction and supersession process.
