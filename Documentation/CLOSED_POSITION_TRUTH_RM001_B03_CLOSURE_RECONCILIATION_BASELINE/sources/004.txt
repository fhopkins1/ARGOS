CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03-004

# Reconciliation Constitution

## Objective

Establish the authoritative constitutional doctrine governing reconciliation for the Closed Position Truth Office.

This order shall define deterministic reconciliation governance between all participating enterprise offices and establish the constitutional requirements by which authoritative Closed Position Truth may be created, rejected, corrected, superseded, or withheld.

This order shall establish reconciliation as the constitutional process by which enterprise truth is validated prior to creation of authoritative Closed Position Truth. 

---

# Constitutional Constraints

This order shall:

* Not modify implementation behavior.
* Not execute behavioral verification.
* Not perform implementation certification.
* Not generate implementation proof.
* Not permit reconciliation to modify upstream truth.
* Not permit reconciliation to infer missing evidence.
* Not permit reconciliation to create authoritative truth while unresolved conflicts remain.
* Not permit analytical conclusions to satisfy reconciliation requirements.

Reconciliation shall validate constitutional closure.

Reconciliation shall not replace authoritative upstream ownership.

---

# Required Constitutional Definitions

# 1. Constitutional Reconciliation Doctrine

Establish reconciliation as:

> The deterministic constitutional process that validates agreement among all required authoritative upstream truth sources before creation of authoritative Closed Position Truth.

Reconciliation shall verify:

* execution completeness;
* execution identity;
* fill completeness;
* position reconciliation;
* residual quantity;
* settlement status;
* realized outcome derivation;
* closure admissibility;
* evidence sufficiency.

Failure of any mandatory reconciliation requirement shall prohibit constitutional closure.

---

# 2. Constitutional Participants

## Position Registry

Provides:

* authoritative position identity;
* authoritative remaining quantity;
* authoritative position state.

Owns:

* position truth.

Closed Position Truth shall not modify Position Registry truth.

---

## Broker

Provides:

* execution records;
* execution acknowledgements;
* fill records;
* settlement evidence.

Owns:

* broker-originated facts.

Closed Position Truth shall consume broker evidence without altering broker-owned truth.

---

## Trader

Provides:

* execution lifecycle;
* execution request history;
* execution reconciliation participation.

Trader shall not determine constitutional closure.

---

## Exit Decision

Provides:

* exit decision identity;
* decision authority;
* decision lineage.

Exit Decision shall not determine reconciliation success.

---

## Performance Truth

Consumes:

* authoritative realized outcomes.

Performance Truth shall not participate in reconciliation decisions.

---

## Historian

Receives:

* finalized reconciliation history;
* immutable reconciliation lineage.

Historian shall not modify reconciliation outcomes.

---

# 3. Reconciliation Scope

Constitutional reconciliation shall establish agreement regarding:

* position identity;
* execution identity;
* execution quantity;
* fill quantity;
* residual quantity;
* settlement disposition;
* realized outcome;
* closure evidence;
* constitutional closure eligibility.

Every required reconciliation domain shall either:

* reconcile successfully; or
* produce a constitutionally defined failure.

No undefined intermediate state shall exist.

---

# 4. Reconciliation Evidence

Every reconciliation shall possess:

* reconciliation identity;
* participating authorities;
* evidence references;
* reconciliation timestamp;
* governing authority;
* reconciliation result;
* exception references, if applicable.

Evidence shall originate only from constitutionally admissible upstream sources.

Missing required evidence shall prohibit reconciliation success.

---

# 5. Source Precedence Constitution

When conflicting information exists, precedence shall be:

1. Position Registry authoritative position truth.
2. Broker authoritative execution facts.
3. Trader execution lifecycle records.
4. Settlement verification.
5. Closed Position Truth reconciliation determination.
6. Performance Truth derived analytics.
7. Analytical or diagnostic records.

Derived records shall never override authoritative source records.

---

# 6. Reconciliation Success Criteria

Successful reconciliation shall require:

* complete execution reconciliation;
* complete fill reconciliation;
* zero residual quantity;
* valid settlement state or constitutional exemption;
* complete admissible evidence;
* no unresolved authority conflicts;
* no unresolved identity conflicts;
* no unresolved quantity conflicts.

Only after successful reconciliation may constitutional closure be established.

---

# 7. Reconciliation Failure Criteria

Reconciliation shall fail upon:

* unresolved execution discrepancies;
* unresolved quantity discrepancies;
* unresolved identity mismatch;
* unresolved settlement requirement;
* conflicting authoritative evidence;
* stale mandatory evidence;
* duplicate unresolved execution;
* missing required evidence.

Failure shall prohibit creation of authoritative Closed Position Truth.

---

# 8. Exception Constitution

Every reconciliation exception shall define:

* exception identity;
* exception category;
* affected authority;
* affected evidence;
* severity;
* governing owner;
* recovery requirements;
* reconciliation impact.

Exception records shall remain constitutionally distinct from authoritative Closed Position Truth.

---

# 9. Correction Constitution

When reconciliation identifies an error after authoritative truth exists:

* predecessor truth shall remain immutable;
* successor truth shall be created through correction;
* reconciliation lineage shall remain complete;
* evidence shall be preserved.

Correction shall not overwrite prior reconciliation history.

---

# 10. Supersession Constitution

When authoritative truth must be replaced:

* predecessor reconciliation shall remain immutable;
* successor reconciliation shall reference predecessor;
* supersession authority shall be recorded;
* complete lineage shall be preserved.

Supersession shall never remove historical reconciliation evidence.

---

# 11. Reconciliation Lineage

Every reconciliation shall preserve:

* predecessor relationships;
* successor relationships;
* participating authorities;
* evidence lineage;
* correction lineage;
* supersession lineage;
* closure determination;
* realized outcome derivation.

Complete reconstruction shall be possible from authoritative records.

---

# 12. Reconciliation Determinism

Demonstrate that:

* reconciliation always produces one deterministic outcome;
* every dependency possesses one constitutional owner;
* every source possesses deterministic precedence;
* every failure possesses deterministic handling;
* every correction preserves historical integrity;
* every supersession preserves lineage.

No reconciliation outcome shall depend upon subjective interpretation.
