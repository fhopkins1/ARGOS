CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03-003

# Residual Quantity Constitution

## Objective

Establish deterministic constitutional doctrine governing residual position quantity, zero-quantity verification, negative residual handling, partial closure, quantity reconciliation, duplicate execution handling, and the relationship between residual quantity and authoritative Closed Position Truth. 

This order shall define the complete constitutional model determining whether the enterprise position quantity has reached zero and whether the position is eligible to proceed toward constitutional closure.

No implementation shall be modified.

No behavioral verification shall occur.

No implementation certification shall occur.

This order governs constitutional doctrine only.

Every residual quantity determination shall possess one authoritative source population.

Every residual quantity determination shall possess one governing authority.

Every quantity calculation shall be reproducible from constitutionally admissible evidence.

No authoritative Closed Position Truth shall exist while unresolved residual quantity remains.

---

# Constitutional Principles

Residual quantity shall represent the authoritative unresolved position quantity remaining after reconciliation of all constitutionally admissible position and execution evidence.

Residual quantity shall be deterministically derived.

Residual quantity shall never be assumed.

Residual quantity shall never be inferred solely from:

* execution intent;
* submitted order quantity;
* broker acknowledgement;
* analytical estimation;
* expected fills;
* settlement initiation;
* trader completion assertion.

Residual quantity equal to zero shall be required for constitutional closure.

Residual quantity greater than zero shall prohibit constitutional closure.

Residual quantity less than zero shall constitute a constitutional inconsistency.

A zero residual quantity shall not independently establish constitutional closure.

Zero quantity shall remain one mandatory closure condition among all conditions established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B03**.

---

# Residual Quantity Definition

Residual quantity shall be defined as the authoritative position quantity remaining after reconciliation of:

* authoritative opening quantity;
* constitutionally admitted executions;
* execution corrections;
* execution cancellations;
* execution reversals;
* position adjustments;
* corporate actions where applicable;
* duplicate suppression;
* quantity-unit normalization.

The canonical relationship shall be:

```text
Residual Quantity
=
Authoritative Position Quantity
−
Net Constitutionally Admissible Closing Quantity
±
Authorized Quantity Adjustments
```

The calculation shall preserve:

* position identity;
* instrument identity;
* quantity unit;
* direction;
* execution lineage;
* adjustment lineage;
* calculation time;
* doctrine version.

No calculation may combine quantities possessing incompatible identity, unit, scope, or provenance.

---

# Residual Quantity Ownership Constitution

## Position Registry Authority

Position Registry shall retain ownership of authoritative current position quantity and position-state truth.

Position Registry shall possess authority over:

* open-position quantity;
* position quantity mutation;
* position identity;
* active position state;
* authoritative quantity corrections within its domain.

## Closed Position Truth Authority

Closed Position Truth shall possess authority to:

* consume authoritative Position Registry quantity;
* reconcile position quantity against execution evidence;
* determine whether zero residual quantity has been constitutionally demonstrated;
* record the residual quantity determination supporting closure;
* reject closure when quantity evidence is insufficient or contradictory.

Closed Position Truth shall not:

* mutate Position Registry quantity;
* create open-position truth;
* alter broker fills;
* create execution facts;
* silently correct upstream quantity records.

## Broker Authority

Broker shall retain ownership of broker-originated fill quantities, cancellations, corrections, reversals, and execution facts.

## Trader Authority

Trader shall retain ownership of execution workflow and execution-management records.

Consumption of Broker or Trader evidence shall not transfer ownership to Closed Position Truth.

# Zero-Quantity Verification Constitution

Zero residual quantity shall be verified only when:

* authoritative starting quantity is established;
* all relevant executions are identified;
* all admitted executions are reconciled;
* duplicates are excluded;
* cancellations are excluded where no execution occurred;
* corrections are incorporated;
* reversals are incorporated;
* authorized adjustments are incorporated;
* quantity units are normalized;
* calculated residual quantity equals exactly zero under the governing precision doctrine;
* Position Registry truth does not contradict the calculated result;
* no unresolved quantity exception remains.

Zero-quantity verification shall produce:

* calculation evidence;
* source evidence inventory;
* reconciliation result;
* precision determination;
* verification authority;
* verification timestamp.

A zero value without supporting lineage shall not constitute zero-quantity verification.

---

# Quantity Precision Constitution

Every quantity calculation shall use the constitutionally authorized precision for the governed instrument.

Precision doctrine shall define:

* canonical quantity unit;
* minimum quantity increment;
* permissible decimal precision;
* rounding prohibition or authorized rounding rule;
* comparison method;
* normalization method.

Rounding shall not conceal a nonzero residual quantity.

A quantity may be treated as zero only when it equals zero under the instrument's authoritative quantity representation.

An immateriality threshold shall not be used unless separately established by explicit constitutional doctrine.

---

# Positive Residual Quantity Constitution

Residual quantity greater than zero shall mean the position remains constitutionally open in whole or in part.

Positive residual quantity shall:

* prohibit constitutional closure;
* prohibit creation of authoritative Closed Position Truth;
* create or preserve an unresolved residual condition;
* require continued reconciliation or authorized resolution;
* preserve all supporting evidence.

Positive residual quantity shall not be reclassified as closed because:

* the remaining quantity is economically small;
* the remaining quantity is operationally inconvenient;
* liquidation is expected;
* settlement is complete for executed quantity;
* analytical systems treat the position as closed;
* execution intent covered the full position.

---

# Negative Residual Quantity Constitution

Residual quantity less than zero shall constitute a constitutional inconsistency.

Negative residual quantity may indicate:

* over-execution;
* duplicated execution evidence;
* incorrect opening quantity;
* incorrect position identity;
* incorrect direction;
* missing reversal;
* missing correction;
* unit-conversion failure;
* unauthorized short exposure;
* upstream data corruption.

Negative residual quantity shall:

* prohibit constitutional closure;
* produce a Closure Exception Record;
* require source reconciliation;
* preserve conflicting evidence;
* identify the responsible upstream authority;
* require escalation when ownership or authority is disputed.

Negative residual quantity shall never be normalized to zero without authoritative correcting evidence.

---

# Partial Closure Constitution

Partial closure shall mean that a valid portion of an open position has been executed while a nonzero quantity remains.

Partial closure shall not create authoritative Closed Position Truth for the original position unless the position model constitutionally supports partition into independently governed position identities.

Where constitutional partition is authorized:

* each resulting position identity shall be unique;
* quantity allocation shall be deterministic;
* predecessor lineage shall be preserved;
* Position Registry shall govern the partition;
* no quantity may be duplicated or omitted;
* each resulting position shall satisfy closure independently.

Where constitutional partition is not authorized, the position shall remain open until residual quantity equals zero.

---

* cause classification;

Residual resolution may include constitutionally authorized:

* additional execution;
* execution correction;
* execution reversal;
* Position Registry correction;
* identity correction;
* quantity-unit correction;
* corporate-action reconciliation.

Closed Position Truth shall record resolution status but shall not perform actions owned by Trader, Broker, Position Registry, or another upstream authority.

# Quantity Reconciliation Constitution

Quantity reconciliation shall compare:

* Position Registry quantity;
* Broker execution quantity;
* Trader execution records;
* execution corrections;
* execution reversals;
* authorized adjustments;
* prior reconciliation records.

Reconciliation shall determine whether these sources produce one consistent residual quantity.

A reconciliation shall succeed only when:

* all required sources are present;
* identities align;
* quantities align;
* directions align;
* units align;
* duplicates are resolved;
* corrections are resolved;
* reversals are resolved;
* no material contradiction remains.

Reconciliation failure shall prohibit zero-quantity verification.

# Duplicate Execution Constitution

Duplicate execution evidence shall not be counted more than once.

Duplicate detection shall use constitutionally sufficient identifiers, including where applicable:

* broker execution identity;
* order identity;
* fill identity;
* position identity;
* instrument identity;
* execution time;
* quantity;
* price;
* correction or reversal relationship.

Duplicate handling shall:

* preserve every received record;
* identify the authoritative execution event;
* exclude duplicate quantity from net calculation;
* preserve duplicate-detection evidence;
* prevent creation of parallel execution lineage.

A repeated message concerning one execution shall not create additional executed quantity.

---

# Corrected Execution Constitution

Where Broker or another authoritative source corrects an execution:

* the original execution record shall remain preserved;
* the correction relationship shall be explicit;
* only the currently authoritative execution quantity shall participate in residual calculation;
* correction lineage shall remain complete;
* prior residual calculations affected by the correction shall become superseded, not overwritten.

Closed Position Truth shall not create execution corrections.

It shall consume corrections from the authoritative source.

---

# Reversed Execution Constitution

A reversed execution shall be incorporated only when:

* reversal authority is established;
* the original execution is identified;
* reversal scope is explicit;
* reversal evidence is admissible;
* quantity effect is deterministic.

A reversal shall not erase the original execution event.

The residual calculation shall include the net constitutional effect of the execution and reversal.

---

# Duplicate Closure Request Constitution

Duplicate closure requests shall not create duplicate Residual Quantity Records or duplicate Closed Position Truth.

A duplicate closure request shall:

* reference the existing closure process;
* preserve request evidence;
* produce a deterministic no-op, acknowledgement, or reconciliation action;
* never restart completed constitutional closure;
* never generate a second authoritative closure identity.

---

# Stale Quantity Evidence Constitution

Quantity evidence shall possess explicit temporal validity.

Stale evidence shall not establish residual quantity when newer authoritative evidence may exist.

Where quantity evidence is stale:

* zero-quantity verification shall fail;
* constitutional closure shall be prohibited;
* a quantity evidence exception shall be recorded;
* fresh evidence shall be required from the authoritative owner.

Freshness shall never be inferred from successful transmission or recent receipt.

---

# Quantity Conflict Constitution

Where sources produce conflicting quantity values:

* the conflict shall be preserved;
* source ownership shall be identified;
* precedence shall be applied only within each source's authority domain;
* the responsible source owner shall correct its own truth;
* Closed Position Truth shall not select a convenient value;
* closure shall remain prohibited until reconciliation succeeds.

No majority vote, confidence score, or analytical estimate shall resolve authoritative quantity conflict.

---

# Replay and Idempotency Constitution

Reprocessing the same authoritative quantity evidence under the same doctrine version shall produce the same residual quantity.

Replay shall preserve:

* source population;
* identity relationships;
* duplicate dispositions;
* correction lineage;
* reversal lineage;
* quantity precision;
* calculation result.

Replay shall not:

* count duplicate executions;
* alter zero-quantity status;
* create a second authoritative record;
* reactivate resolved exceptions;
* alter historical calculations without a new correction or supersession event.

---

# Correction and Supersession Constitution

If authoritative quantity evidence changes after a Residual Quantity Record has been established:

* the prior Residual Quantity Record shall remain immutable;
* a new Residual Quantity Record shall be created;
* predecessor-successor lineage shall be explicit;
* the correction source shall be identified;
* affected closure determinations shall be re-evaluated;
* no authoritative Closed Position Truth shall be overwritten.

Where an authoritative Closed Position Record is affected, correction shall proceed through the successor and supersession doctrine established under **CLOSED-POSITION-TRUTH-RM-001-B02**.

A closed position shall not reopen through mutation of historical truth.
